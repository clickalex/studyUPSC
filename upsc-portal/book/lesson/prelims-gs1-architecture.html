Architecture: Temple Styles, Forts & Monuments

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Four exam arenas: stupas & caves, temple styles, Indo-Islamic, modern-colonial. Every question is a match-the-pair.

1. Stupas & caves

  • Stupa anatomy: anda (dome) on medhi, harmika railing-box, chhatra umbrellas, vedika railing, torana gates (Sanchi's four — Satavahana-era carvings: Jatakas, aniconic Buddha).
  • Sequence: Ashoka core (Sanchi-1, Bharhut, Piprahwa relic-claims) → Sunga railings → Satavahana gates (Sanchi) → Amaravati (white-marble narrative, Satavahana-Ikshvaku) → Gandhara dharmarajika (Kanishka's relic chambers).
  • Caves: Barabar (Ashoka, Lomas Rishi chaitya-arch facade — earliest) → western chaitya-vihara schools (Bhaja, Kondane, Karle — Karle chaitya grandest, Nashik Pandav leni inscriptions of Gautamiputra/Ushavadata) → Ajanta (Hinayana + Mahayana phases: 9,10,12,13 early; 1,2,16,17 Vakataka) → Ellora (Buddhist-Hindu-Jain; Kailasa temple 16 — Rashtrakuta Krishna I, monolithic) → Elephanta (Shiva panels — Rashtrakuta attribution) → Jain rows (Gwalior, Ellora 30-34).

3. Indo-Islamic arc

  • Sultanate: Qutub Minar (Iltutmish completes; Aibak begins), Alai Darwaza (Alauddin — first true arch + dome finishing), Tughlaq battered walls + sloping, Khirki mosque (Khan-i-Jahan), Lodi octagonal tomb-gardens (first garden tombs), Jaunpur Sharqui, Bengal curved-roof (Adina mosque, Pandua), Gujarat white-marble blends.
  • Mughal: Humayun's tomb (first grand garden tomb, charbagh), Purana Qila, Akbar: Fatehpur Sikri (Buland Darwaza; Panch Mahal, Diwan-i-Khas pillar; red sandstone + Gujarati-Chaulukya brackets), Allahabad/Agra forts, Shah Jahan: Taj (1632-53; pietra dura, double dome), Jama Masjid, Red Fort (Diwan-i-Am/Khas, Rang Mahal), Shalimar (Lahore); Sikh: Harmandir Sahib (gold leaf — Ranjit Singh era); Rajput: Amber, Mehrangarh, Udaipur palaces.
  • Colonial: classical revival (Victoria Memorial — Indo-Saracenic hybrid is different: Chepauk, Gateway of India, Lutyens' New Delhi blend — Viceroy's House dome).

MCQ hooks

  • First true arch/dome → Alai Darwaza; first garden tomb → Humayun's; charbagh peak → Taj.
  • Pietra dura (parchin kari) = Taj inlay; panch mahal = Fatehpur Sikri.
  • Kailasa (Ellora 16) carved top-down monolith = Krishna I.
  • Stupa 'anda-harmika-chhatra' order asked verbatim.
  • Konark wheels = sundial function; jagamohana type = deul pair (Odisha: rekha deul + pidha deul).
  • Hoysala star platform + soapstone = Vesara terminal glory.

Indo-Islamic (arch, dome, minaret, pietra dura, jali, charbagh)

  • Sultanate: Qutb complex (Aibak-Iltutmish; Alai Darwaza 1311), Tughlaq batter (Tughlaqabad, Firoz Shah Kotla), Lodi octagonal tombs & double dome (Lodi Gardens, Sikandar Lodi).
  • Provincial: Bengal (Adina mosque Pandua; bangla roof), Jaunpur (Atala — propylon screens), Gujarat (Jama Masjid Ahmedabad; Sidi Saiyyed jali; Sarkhej), Malwa (Mandu — Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal), Bijapur (Gol Gumbaz — 2nd largest dome; Ibrahim Rauza), Golconda (Charminar 1591), Bidar (Gawan madrasa).
  • Mughal: Humayun's tomb (charbagh prototype), Fatehpur Sikri (red sandstone; Buland Darwaza; Salim Chishti tomb marble), Agra fort, Akbar's tomb Sikandra, Itimad-ud-Daula (pietra dura), Taj Mahal (Ustad Ahmad Lahauri; 1632–53), Red Fort & Jama Masjid Delhi, Badshahi Lahore, Bibi ka Maqbara (1660s).
  • Sikh: Harmandir Sahib 1589–1604 (Guru Arjan; gold Ranjit Singh 1830). Rajput: Chittor (Kirti Stambh Jain 12th c., Vijay Stambh 1448 Rana Kumbha), Kumbhalgarh (36-km wall), Amber, Mehrangarh, Jaisalmer, Hawa Mahal 1799 (Pratap Singh; Lal Chand Ustad), Jantar Mantar (Jai Singh II; 5 — Delhi, Jaipur (UNESCO), Ujjain, Varanasi, Mathura lost). Stepwells: Rani ki Vav (11th c., UNESCO 2014), Chand Baori (Abhaneri, 9th c.), Adalaj (1499).

Colonial

  • Indo-Saracenic (Chepauk, Victoria Memorial (Emerson), Gateway of India (Wittet, 1924), Madras High Court, Mysore Palace (Irwin)), Gothic revival (CST Mumbai — Stevens, UNESCO 2004; Bombay University), neoclassical (Raj Bhavan Kolkata, Town Hall Mumbai), New Delhi (Lutyens — Rashtrapati Bhavan (Sanchi-style dome); Baker — Secretariats, Parliament House 1927), Art Deco Marine Drive (UNESCO 2018), Portuguese Goa churches (Bom Jesus — Xavier relics; Se Cathedral), French Pondicherry.

UNESCO recent (India total 43 as of 2024)

  • 2021 Dholavira & Ramappa (Kakatiya Rudreshwara) · 2023 Santiniketan & Hoysala ensembles · 2024 Moidams (Charaideo, Ahom) · Tentative list items keep appearing in news (Maratha Military Landscapes — inscribed 2025).

Traps

  • Gopuram dominance grows later (Pandya/Nayaka), not in early Pallava temples.
  • Konark is a Sun temple of the Eastern Gangas (Narasimhadeva I) — not Chola.
  • Gol Gumbaz is Bijapur (Adil Shahi), Charminar Golconda (Qutb Shahi).
  • Hoysala temples use soapstone (chloritic schist); Khajuraho sandstone; Dilwara marble.

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

Match-pair drills.

Pairs flash

  • Lomas Rishi chaitya-arch → Barabar (Ashoka) · grandest chaitya → Karle.
  • Sanchi toranas → Satavahana carvings · Amaravati → white marble narrative.
  • Kailasa Ellora → Krishna I (Rashtrakuta), top-down · Elephanta Shiva → Rashtrakuta line.
  • Kandariya → Chandelas · Konark → Eastern Gangas · Modhera → Solankis.
  • Brihadeshwara → Rajaraja I 1010 · Gangaikondacholapuram → Rajendra I.
  • Pattadakal → Chalukya (UNESCO) · Belur-Halebidu → Hoysala star plans.
  • Alai Darwaza → first true arch+gate dome · sloping walls → Tughlaqs.
  • First garden tomb → Humayun's · Buland Darwaza → Fatehpur Sikri (Akbar).
  • Pietra dura → Taj · Panch Mahal → Fatehpur Sikri · Peacock throne → Shah Jahan.
  • Adina mosque → Bengal Pandua · curved roofs → Bengal style · Victoria Memorial → Indo-Saracenic.

Style-order flash

  • Stupa anatomy: medhi → anda → harmika → chhatra; gates = torana, railing = vedika.
  • Nagara = shikhara/amalaka; Dravida = vimana/gopuram; Vesara = hybrid/star.
  • Odisha temple pair: rekha deul (sanctum) + pidha deul (hall).

Trap flash

  • Qutub Minar begun by Aibak, finished by Iltutmish (Firoz repaired top).
  • Shore Temple = Narasimhavarman II (Rajasimha), not Mamalla I.
  • Ajanta ≠ Ellora: Ajanta = Buddhist only; Ellora = tri-faith.
  • First true arch in Sultanate masonry = Alai Darwaza (Alauddin).
