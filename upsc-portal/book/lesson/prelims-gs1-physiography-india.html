Lesson 1.2.5

Physiography: Himalayas, Peninsular Plateau, Plains & Coasts

In this lessonDetailed StudyRevision DigestKey Points & MindmapDiagrams, Maps & FlowchartsPrevious Year Questions

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Five divisions: Himalaya, Northern Plains, Peninsula, Coasts-Islands, Desert. Passes, doabs, gaps — direct ask zone.

1. The Himalaya

  • North-to-south: Trans-Himalaya (Karakoram-Ladakh-Zanskar; K2 Godwin-Austen) → Great Himalaya (Himadri; Everest-Sagarmatha; Kanchenjunga = India's highest) → Lesser/Middle (Himachal; Dhauladhar-Pir Panjal-Mussoorie-Nag Tibba) → Siwalik/Duns (outer; Bhabar belt below).
  • Regional: Punjab Himalaya (Indus-Sutlej), Kumaon (Sutlej-Kali), Nepal (Kali-Teesta), Assam (Teesta-Dihang). Syntaxial bends: Nanga Parbat (west), Namcha Barwa/Dihang (east). Origin: Tethys closure, India-Asia collision (~50-40 Ma), phased uplift.
  • Major passes: Zoji la-Banihal (J&K), Shipki la (HP-Sutlej entry), Mana-Niti (Uttarakhand), Nathu la-Jelep la (Sikkim), Bomdi la-Diphu (Arunachal).

2. Northern Plains

  • Depositional mega-plain (Indus-Ganga-Brahmaputra); belts: Bhabar (porous gravel; streams vanish) → Terai (marshy re-emergence) → Bhangar (older alluvium, kankar) → Khadar (newer flood-refreshed).
  • Sections: Punjab plain (doabs — Bist-Jalandhar, Bari, Rechna, Chaj, Sind Sagar), Ganga plain (upper-middle-lower; Ganga-Yamuna doab), Brahmaputra valley (Majuli = largest river island).

3. Peninsular plateau

  • Oldest stable block (Archean gneiss); Aravallis (oldest fold range, heavily eroded; Guru Shikhar-Mount Abu), Vindhya-Satpura east-west trend, Maikal-Mahadeo; Deccan Traps (end-Cretaceous flood basalt) under most of Maharashtra.
  • Western Ghats (Sahyadri — continuous, sheer west scarp; Anaimudi highest; gaps: Thal-Bhor, Palghat, Shencottah), Eastern Ghats (discontinuous — Nallamala, Mahendragiri end), Anaimudi 2695 m (Anaimalai), Nilgiri junction (Doddabetta).
  • Plateaus: Malwa (Chambal head), Chotanagpur (minerals; Ranchi), Meghalaya (Garo-Khasi-Jaintia; Shillong peak), Chhattisgarh basin; mineral belts (Chotanagpur-Bastar-Dharwar-Ballari).

4. Coasts, islands, desert

  • West coast: Konkan (Maharashtra-Goa), Kanara (Karnataka), Malabar (Kerala; backwaters-kayals) — narrow, estuaries (Narmada-Tapi), Mumbai High offshore. East coast: Utkal (Odisha), Coromandel (TN-AP) — wide, deltas (Mahanadi-Godavari-Krishna-Kaveri), lagoons (Chilika-Pulicat).
  • Islands: Andaman-Nicobar (~570; Barren-Narcondam volcanoes; Arakan-Yoma arc), Lakshadweep (coral atolls; Minicoy), Gulf of Mannar biosphere.
  • Thar desert: arid-marusthali; longitudinal dunes; barchan (crescent), seif, parabolic; Luni = only major SW-flowing river; Rann (Great-Little) salt flats; Indira Gandhi Canal greened the Sriganganagar belt.

MCQ hooks

  • Order west→east: Karakoram → Great → Lesser → Siwalik (Trans-Himalaya northmost).
  • Passes with states: Zoji la-Banihal (J&K), Shipki la (HP), Nathu-Jelep (Sikkim), Bomdi la (Arunachal).
  • Bhabar-Terai-Bhangar-Khadar sequence moving away from the hills.
  • Anaimudi (2695, Anaimalai) vs Doddabetta (Nilgiri) vs Guru Shikhar (Aravalli).
  • Majuli = Brahmaputra; kayals = Kerala; Chilika = Odisha lagoon.
  • Luni drains SW in Thar; Rann = salt desert (Kutch).

Revision Digest

Condensed for the last mile before the exam.

Prelims GS-I › Geography › Indian Geography. Map-anchored facts: ranges, passes, peaks, hills, coastal plains and islands.

Frame

  • Area 32.87 lakh km² (7th; 2.4% of world land; 17.7% population); lat 8°4′–37°6′ N, long 68°7′–97°25′ E; N–S 3,214 km, E–W 2,933 km; Tropic of Cancer through 8 states (Gujarat, Rajasthan, MP, Chhattisgarh, Jharkhand, WB, Tripura, Mizoram); IST 82°30′ E (Mirzapur); land frontier 15,200 km (Bangladesh 4,096 longest → China 3,488 → Pakistan 3,323 → Nepal 1,751 → Myanmar 1,643 → Bhutan 699 → Afghanistan 106); coastline 7,516 km (Gujarat longest ~1,600; then Andhra/TN; new 2024 measurement 11,098 km); southernmost point Indira Point (Great Nicobar; 6°45′), mainland Kanyakumari (8°4′); Indira Col northernmost.

Himalayas (young fold; Tethys; ~2,400 km arc, 150–400 km wide; syntaxial bends Nanga Parbat & Namcha Barwa)

  • Trans-Himalaya: Karakoram (K2/Godwin Austen 8,611 — India's highest incl. PoK; Siachen, Baltoro glaciers), Ladakh, Zanskar, Kailash.
  • Greater/Himadri (avg 6,000 m; Everest 8,849 Nepal, Kangchenjunga 8,586 — India's highest, Sikkim; Nanda Devi 7,816 highest wholly in India; Kamet, Nanga Parbat 8,126, Namcha Barwa 7,756).
  • Lesser/Himachal (Pir Panjal — longest; Dhauladhar; Mussoorie; Nag Tibba; Mahabharat (Nepal); hill stations Shimla, Mussoorie, Nainital, Darjeeling; margs Gulmarg/Sonamarg, bugyals; Kashmir & Kangra valleys; karewas — saffron).
  • Outer/Shiwalik (900–1,100 m; duns — Dehradun, Kotli, Patli; Churia in Nepal); bhabar → terai → khadar/bhangar.
  • Regional (Sir Sidney Burrard): Punjab/Kashmir (Indus–Sutlej), Kumaon (Sutlej–Kali; Nanda Devi, Badrinath, Kedarnath, Gangotri), Nepal (Kali–Tista; Everest, Kangchenjunga), Assam (Tista–Dihang; Namcha Barwa). Purvanchal: Patkai Bum, Naga hills (Saramati 3,826), Manipur hills, Mizo/Lushai hills (Blue Mt/Phawngpui), Barail range, Mishmi, Dafla, Abor, Miri; Meghalaya plateau (Garo-Khasi-Jaintia; Shillong peak; part of peninsular block; Mawsynram).
  • Passes: Karakoram (Ladakh–Xinjiang), Khardung La, Zoji La (Srinagar–Leh; Zojila tunnel), Banihal (Jawahar tunnel), Pir Panjal (Mughal road), Rohtang & Atal tunnel (Manali–Lahaul), Baralacha La, Shipki La (Sutlej; HP–Tibet trade), Mana & Niti (Uttarakhand), Lipulekh (Kailash route; India–Nepal–China trijunction), Nathu La & Jelep La (Sikkim–Tibet), Bomdila, Se La (Tawang), Diphu (India–Myanmar–China trijunction), Pangsau (Stilwell Rd), Chang La, Debsa, Bum La, Thang La, Aghil, Lanak La (Aksai Chin), Umling La (highest motorable ~19,024 ft).
  • Glaciers: Siachen (76 km, Karakoram; longest outside polar regions in India), Baltoro, Biafo, Hispar, Batura, Gangotri (30 km; Gaumukh), Zemu (Sikkim), Milam, Pindari, Bara Shigri (HP largest), Drang-Drung; ~9,575 glaciers; Third Pole; GLOF (South Lhonak 2023 Sikkim, Chorabari 2013).

Northern plains (7 lakh km²; 2,400 km; alluvium up to 2,000 m thick)

  • Belts: bhabar (pebbles; streams disappear), terai (marshy, re-emergent), bhangar (old; kankar; barind Bengal, bhur sands), khadar (new). Regional: Punjab-Haryana (doabs — Bist Jalandhar, Bari, Rechna, Chaj, Sindh Sagar; dhaya, bet, chos), Ganga plain (upper/middle/lower; bhur, tal, diara, chaur), Brahmaputra valley (char islands; Majuli), Rajasthan bagar/rohi; Sundarbans delta largest. Ganga–Brahmaputra largest delta.

Peninsular plateau (oldest — Gondwana; Archaean)

  • Central Highlands: Aravalli (oldest fold; Guru Shikhar 1,722 m Mt Abu), Malwa plateau (lava; Chambal ravines), Bundelkhand, Baghelkhand, Vindhya (escarpment; N of Narmada), Satpura (block; Dhupgarh 1,350 highest MP; Amarkantak; Mahadeo, Maikal hills), Chota Nagpur (Ranchi, Hazaribagh, Rajmahal; Parasnath 1,366; Damodar coal; "Ruhr of India"), Meghalaya plateau (Garo/Khasi/Jaintia; Nokrek), Rajmahal–Garo gap (Malda gap).
  • Deccan: lava traps (Cretaceous–Eocene; 5 lakh km²); Western Ghats/Sahyadri (1,600 km; Anamudi 2,695 m highest peninsular — Anaimalai; Doddabetta 2,637 Nilgiri; Kalsubai 1,646 Maharashtra; Kudremukh, Pushpagiri, Mullayanagiri (Karnataka highest 1,930); passes/ghats — Thal (Mumbai–Nashik), Bhor (Mumbai–Pune), Pal (Kolhapur–Ratnagiri), Palakkad/Palghat gap (30 km; Coimbatore–Palakkad), Shencottah/Senkottai (Madurai–Kollam), Goa gap, Amba, Naneghat, Kumbharli; UNESCO 2012; 39 sites; biodiversity hotspot) vs Eastern Ghats (discontinuous; older; Arma Konda/Jindhagada 1,690 AP highest; Mahendragiri (Odisha 1,501), Nallamala (Srisailam; GIB? no—tigers), Palkonda, Velikonda, Seshachalam (Tirumala; red sanders), Javadi, Shevaroy (Yercaud), Kollimalai, Pachaimalai, Nilgiri junction); Nilgiri meet point (Doddabetta; Ooty); Cardamom (Elamalai) & Anaimalai & Palani (Kodaikanal) south of Palghat gap; Deccan plateau proper (Maharashtra, Karnataka (Malnad/Maidan), Telangana), Dandakaranya, Bastar, Garhjat hills (Odisha), Karnataka plateau (Bababudan — iron; Kudremukh; Jog falls on Sharavati).
  • Hills in news: Similipal, Niyamgiri (Dongria Kondh; bauxite), Gandhamardan (bauxite), Nallamala (uranium), Shevaroy, Agasthyamalai (biosphere), Anamalai, Sathyamangalam, Amrabad, Kudremukh (iron mining ban 2005), Aravalli (illegal mining; SC 2024 definition — >100 m), Malwa (Deccan traps), Lonar (meteorite crater lake; Ramsar 2020).

Coasts & islands

  • West coast (narrow; submerged; estuaries; Konkan (Daman–Goa), Kanara/Karavali (Goa–Mangaluru), Malabar (Kerala; kayals/backwaters — Vembanad, Ashtamudi; Kuttanad below sea level); Kutch & Kathiawar (Gujarat; Rann; Gir; Girnar), lagoons, ports natural (Mumbai, Marmagao, Kochi, New Mangalore), high tidal ranges Khambhat); East coast (wide; emergent; deltas — Mahanadi, Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri; Utkal (Odisha; Chilika largest brackish lagoon; Bhitarkanika), Northern Circars (AP; Kolleru freshwater lake; Pulicat lagoon — Sriharikota barrier), Coromandel (TN; NE monsoon rain), Pulicat, Point Calimere, Vedaranyam; ports artificial (Chennai, Vizag natural, Paradip, Ennore, Tuticorin); Marina beach; Dhanushkodi.
  • Andaman & Nicobar (572 islands; 37 inhabited; Ten Degree Channel A–N; Duncan Passage (South Andaman–Little Andaman), Coco channel (Myanmar); Saddle Peak 732 m highest (N Andaman); Barren (active), Narcondam; Indira Point; Great Nicobar (Campbell Bay; Galathea — transhipment project); Baratang mud volcano; Cellular jail; Ross → Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose Dweep, Neil → Shaheed, Havelock → Swaraj; tribes — Great Andamanese, Onge, Jarawa, Sentinelese (North Sentinel), Shompen (Nicobar), Nicobarese; Car Nicobar; Katchal 2004 tsunami).
  • Lakshadweep (36 islands; 32 km²; coral atolls; 10 inhabited; Minicoy (southernmost; Mahl; Nine Degree Channel separates from rest; Eight Degree Channel — Maldives), Kavaratti capital, Agatti airport, Bangaram, Pitti (bird sanctuary), Bitra smallest; entirely ST; new tourism push 2024).
  • Other: Majuli (river island; Brahmaputra; district 2016), Bhola/Salsette (Mumbai), Diu, Sriharikota (barrier island; Pulicat; SDSC-SHAR), New Moore/Purbasha (disappeared), Pamban/Rameswaram (Adam's bridge/Ram Setu; Pamban bridge new vertical-lift 2024), Elephanta, Vypin, Willingdon (artificial; Kochi), Sagar (Gangasagar), Nachugunta, Katchatheevu (ceded to Sri Lanka 1974; in news 2024), Abdul Kalam (Wheeler; DRDO), St Mary's (basalt columns; Udupi), Netrani, Munroe (Kerala backwaters), Umananda (smallest inhabited river island; Guwahati), Sambhar? (lake, not island).

Traps

  • Highest peak in India: Kangchenjunga (K2 is in PoK-administered Karakoram — official Indian claim lists K2 as highest). Highest wholly in India: Nanda Devi. Highest peninsular: Anamudi (not Doddabetta).
  • Meghalaya plateau is peninsular (not Himalayan) — separated by Malda/Garo–Rajmahal gap.
  • Palghat gap = Western Ghats break (Kerala–TN); Shencottah gap further south.
  • Ten Degree Channel: Andaman–Nicobar; Nine Degree: Lakshadweep–Minicoy; Eight Degree: Minicoy–Maldives.
  • Chilika = largest brackish lagoon; Vembanad = longest lake; Wular = largest freshwater (J&K); Kolleru freshwater (AP); Loktak (phumdis; Manipur); Sambhar largest inland salt lake; Pangong/Tso Moriri high-altitude (Ladakh).

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

Division-anchor flash.

Himalaya flash

  • N→S: Karakoram/Ladakh → Great (Himadri) → Lesser (Himachal) → Siwalik-Dun.
  • India's highest peak → Kanchenjunga (Sikkim) · syntaxial bends → Nanga Parbat + Namcha Barwa.
  • Passes → Zoji la-Banihal (J&K) · Shipki la (HP) · Mana-Niti (Uttarakhand) · Nathu-Jelep (Sikkim) · Bomdi la (Arunachal).

Plains flash

  • Belt order from hills → Bhabar (gravel) → Terai (marsh) → Bhangar (old alluvium) → Khadar (new).
  • Doabs → Bist (Beas-Sutlej), Bari (Ravi-Beas), Rechna (Chenab-Ravi), Chaj (Chenab-Jhelum) · Majuli → Brahmaputra island.

Peninsula flash

  • Aravalli = oldest fold, eroded (Guru Shikhar) · Deccan Traps = flood basalt · Sahyadri continuous-west, E-Ghats discontinuous.
  • Peaks → Anaimudi 2695 (Anaimalai), Doddabetta (Nilgiri), Guru Shikhar (Mount Abu) · gaps → Thal-Bhor, Palghat, Shencottah.
  • Plateaus → Malwa, Chotanagpur (minerals), Meghalaya (Khasi), Chhattisgarh.

Coast-island flash

  • West → Konkan-Kanara-Malabar: narrow, estuaries, kayals · East → Utkal-Coromandel: wide, deltas.
  • Lagoons → Chilika (Odisha), Pulicat (AP-TN), Vembanad (Kerala) · islands → Andaman-Nicobar (volcanic arc), Lakshadweep (atolls).

Desert flash

  • Thar → longitudinal dunes · barchan = crescent · Luni = SW-draining · Rann = salt flats · Indira Gandhi Canal → Sriganganagar belt.

Trap flash

  • Everest is in Nepal; Kanchenjunga = India's highest (Sikkim).
  • Terai = marshy re-emergence (gravel = Bhabar).
  • Palghat gap = between Anaimalai and Palni-Cardamom line (Coimbatore-Palakkad axis).
  • E-Ghats continuity breaks at the Mahanadi-Krishna mouths — 'discontinuous' keyword.
  • Andaman = Arakan-Yoma extension (not coral).
