Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Class-X level maths at speed: percent-ratio core, arithmetic set, DI reading discipline.

1. Percent-ratio core

  • Commutative percent: x% of y = y% of x (instant 18% of 50 = 9); fraction-percent anchors: 1/8 = 12.5%, 1/6 ≈ 16.67%, 1/7 ≈ 14.28%, 1/12 ≈ 8.33%.
  • Successive change: a + b + ab/100 (two 10% rises = 21%); percentage-change = (new − old)/old × 100 with old as base — base-swap is the classic trap.
  • Income-expenditure ratios: assume LCM values; part-whole from ratios (3:5 → 3/8, 5/8).

2. Arithmetic set

  • Profit-loss: markup on CP → discount on MP; SP/CP ratio method ((100+P)/100); successive discounts add via a+b+ab/100 (not simple sum).
  • SI linear; CI compounding; 2-year CI − SI = P(r/100)²; halves-doubling (rule of 72 ≈ 72/r years).
  • TSD: distance constant → speed × time trade (speed ×1.25 → time ÷1.25); average speed for equal distances = 2ab/(a+b) (never (a+b)/2); trains: relative speed on opposite directions.
  • Time-work: LCM-of-days method (work = units); pipes: fill − empty; efficiency ∝ 1/time; men-days: M₁D₁H₁/W₁ = M₂D₂H₂/W₂.
  • Averages-mixtures: alligation (cheaper:dearer = d−m : m−c); replacement formula for repeated dilution.

3. Data interpretation discipline

  • Read title-units-footnotes first; per-capita vs total traps; percentage-point vs percent change distinction.
  • Approximate aggressively — options are far apart; compute only what the question asks; DI often needs no exact arithmetic (rank-compare questions).
  • Charts: bar = compare, line = trend, pie = share (angles: share × 3.6°), table = precision lookups.

4. Probability & counting

  • P = favourable/total; complement: P(at least one) = 1 − P(none); independent: multiply; mutually exclusive: add.
  • Permutations with repetition (letters of a word), combinations = selection (nCr), circular arrangements (n−1)!; dice-card standard setups memorised.

MCQ hooks

  • x% of y = y% of x — instant mental math.
  • Successive changes: a+b+ab/100 — discounts trap (not simple sum).
  • Equal-distance average speed = 2ab/(a+b) — never arithmetic mean.
  • CI−SI (2 yr) = P(r/100)² — formula ask.
  • Alligation ratio = (d−m):(m−c) — mixture ask.
  • P(at least one) = 1 − P(none) — complement first.
  • Percentage-point ≠ percent change — DI trap.

Revision Digest

Condensed for the last mile before the exam.

Core formula rail (memorise+practise)

  • %↔fraction: 1/8=12.5% · 1/6≈16.67% · 1/7≈14.28% · 1/9≈11.11% · 1/11≈9.09%.
  • CI vs SI spread over 2 yrs = P×(r/100)².
  • Speed: km/h × 5/18 = m/s; average speed (equal distances) = 2ab/(a+b).
  • Work: combined = LCM method; efficiency ratios invert time.
  • Trains: crossing pole = own length; crossing bridge = length+bridge; relative speeds — opposite add, same subtract.
  • Boats: downstream = b+w; upstream = b−w; still-water speed = (d+u)/2.
  • Averages: inclusion/replacement adjust via total-difference method.
  • Ratios: a:b and b:c → a:b:c by b-matching.
  • Profit/loss: SP = CP×(1±p); successive discounts = 1−Π(1−dᵢ); MP vs discount vs profit triangles.
  • Divisibility: 3/9 digit-sum · 4 last two digits · 8 last three · 11 alternate-sum · 7 split-3-digit trick.
  • HCF/LCM: product = HCF×LCM (two numbers); prime factors via division chains.

Drill Set A (one-minuteers)

  1. 15% of 240 → 36 · 2. 3/8 of 160 → 60 · 3. √1764 → 42 · 4. 12% of ₹2,500 → 300 · 5. If 40% of x = 96 → x = 240.

Drill Set B (two-minuteers)

  1. Two numbers ratio 3:5; sum 64 → 24 & 40.
  2. Simple interest doubles money in 8 yrs → 12.5% p.a.
  3. Pipe A 4h, Pipe B 6h, together → 2.4 h (LCM=12; 3+2=5 units/hr; 12/5).
  4. Train 180 m at 54 km/h crosses pole in → 54×5/18=15 m/s; 12 s.
  5. CP ₹500; 20% loss → SP ₹400. CP unknown, SP ₹450 at 10% loss → CP ₹500.
  6. Avg of 7 numbers = 50; remove 68 & 85 → total drop 153; new total 350−153=197 over 5 → 39.4.
  7. Successive 20% & 10% discounts → net 28% (1−0.8×0.9).
  8. A does 1/3 work in 5 days → full in 15 d; B in 10 → combined 6 days.
  9. LCM(15,20,24) = 120; HCF: gcd(15,20) = 5 and gcd(5,24) = 1 → HCF = 1. Working: 15=3·5 · 20=2²·5 · 24=2³·3 → LCM = 2³·3·5 = 120.
  10. Population: 2,000 grows 10%/yr → after 2 yrs 2,420.

Drill Set C (DI-lite table)

Table: (Units produced: Mon 120, Tue 150, Wed 180, Thu 130, Fri 210; defective %: 5, 4, 6, 5, 5)

  • Total produced 790; total defective = 6+6+10.8+6.5+10.5=39.8; % defective of total ≈ 5.04%.
  • Best quality day (lowest % defective): Tuesday (4%).
  • Conversions that punish: "defective in thousands" interpretations — read units line twice.

Shape & mensuration (CSAT-level only)

  • Rectangle/square/triangle area · circle πr² & circumference · cylinder (2πr(h+r) TSA; πr²h volume) · cone (1/3 πr²h) · sphere (4πr²; 4/3 πr²h) · cuboid diagonals √(l²+b²+h²).
  • Path-around problems (garden frames), wire-to-circle conversions, tiled-floor counts.

Probability & counting (light)

  • Basics P = favourable/total; coins/dice/cards deck facts (52 cards; 4 suits; 12 face cards; 26 red).
  • Combinatorics: nCr selections of committee questions; "at least one" = 1 − P(none).

Number system traps

  • "Between 100 and 200, numbers divisible by 7": count via floor division (200−100 bracket careful endpoints inclusivity).
  • Remainder cycles: 3^n mod 4 alternates 3,1,3,1…
  • "Five-digit numbers ending in 6 divisible by 6?" — test both even + digit-sum.

Time-wasters caught early

  • Multi-step ratio back-solves → verify by plugging options instead.
  • Percentage-of-percentage chains ≥3 steps → convert to fractions before rounding.
  • Mensuration decimals — carry π=22/7 until last multiplication for clean integers.

Previous Year Questions

What the examiner has actually asked.

CSAT-pattern arithmetic + data drills; key with shortcut at the end.

Set 1 — arithmetic

  1. Q1. A price rises 20% then falls 20%. Net change: (a) 0% (b) 4% fall (c) 4% rise (d) 2% fall
  2. Q2. CP ₹250, marked ₹400, discount 20%. Profit %: (a) 28% (b) 25% (c) 20% (d) 32%
  3. Q3. A car travels to a city at 60 km/h and returns at 40 km/h. Average speed: (a) 50 (b) 48 (c) 45 (d) 52
  4. Q4. SI on ₹8,000 at 5% for 3 years vs CI — CI − SI (2 years) on same principal: (a) ₹20 (b) ₹40 (c) ₹60 (d) ₹8
  5. Q5. 12 men finish a job in 20 days; 8 men will finish it in: (a) 30 (b) 26 (c) 24 (d) 32

Set 2 — DI & probability

  1. Q6. Wheat output: 2021 = 100 mt, 2024 = 120 mt. Percentage-point annual rise if uniform: (a) 6.67% (b) 20% (c) 6% (d) 5%
  2. Q7. A pie chart's sector is 54°. Its share: (a) 15% (b) 18% (c) 12% (d) 20%
  3. Q8. Ratio 3:5; total 64. Larger part: (a) 36 (b) 40 (c) 42 (d) 44
  4. Q9. Two dice. P(sum = 8): (a) 5/36 (b) 1/6 (c) 1/9 (d) 7/36
  5. Q10. P(rain) = 0.3 on each of two independent days. P(rain at least once): (a) 0.51 (b) 0.49 (c) 0.6 (d) 0.09

Answers

1-b (successive: +20−20−4 = −4%) · 2-a (SP 320; 70/250 = 28%) · 3-b (2ab/(a+b) = 48) · 4-a (P(r/100)² = 8000×0.0025 = ₹20) · 5-a (240 man-days ÷ 8) · 6-a (20 points ÷ 3 years ≈ 6.67 pp/yr) · 7-a (54/3.6 = 15%) · 8-b (5/8 × 64) · 9-a ((2,6)(3,5)(4,4)(5,3)(6,2) = 5/36) · 10-a (1 − 0.7² = 0.51).

Drill the anchors daily: successive-change formula, 2ab/(a+b), P(r/100)², 1 − P(none).
