Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Scheme → ministry → launch year → headline number. High-frequency prelims zone; verify current figures each cycle.

1. Poverty & rural

  • MGNREGA 2005 (100 days/household; wage-material 60:40; unemployment allowance; social audit (Sec 17); demand-driven (DBT wages via NREGASoft)). PMAY-G/U (rural pucca homes; urban CLSS component), DAY-NRLM (women SHGs — Ajeevika lineage; Lakhpati Didi target 3cr), PM-KISAN 2019 (₹6,000/yr, landholding farmer families, exclusion caps), PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (NFSA top-up; made free 2023), Ayushman Bharat: PM-JAY (₹5 lakh/family secondary-tertiary) + HWC/AB-HWC now Ayushman Arogya Mandirs.

2. Financial inclusion & pension

  • PMJDY 2014 (zero-balance, RuPay, overdraft ₹10k), PMJJBY (life ₹2L) + PMSBY (accident ₹2L) + APY (pension) trio 2015, MUDRA (Shishu ≤50k, Kishore 50k-5L, Tarun 5-10L; now Tarun-Plus 20L), PM SVANidhi (street vendors); Stand-Up India (SC-ST-women entrepreneurs ₹10L-1cr).

3. Health-education-skill

  • PM Matru Vandana (₹5,000 in instalments; 2017), POSHAN Abhiyaan 2018 (now POSHAN 2.0 — Mission Saksham Anganwadi), PM POSHAN (mid-day meal rename 2021). Education: Samagra Shiksha (2018 school integration), NEP 2020 (5+3+3+4, FLN NIPUN), PM SHRI (14,500 exemplar schools), PM Vidyalaxmi (education loans). Skill: PMKVY 4.0, DGT apprenticeships (NAPS).

4. Water-energy-urban

  • Jal Jeevan Mission 2019 (har ghar jal tap target; under Jal Shakti), PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (per-drop-more-crop micro-irrigation), eNAM (mandi e-platform), FPO scheme (10,000 FPOs), Ujjwala (LPG to poor women; 8cr+ connections), PM Surya Ghar (rooftop solar 1cr homes 2024), Saubhagya (electrification 2017), AMRUT 2.0 + Smart Cities Mission (100 cities, 2023-24 sunset), SBM 2.0 (ODF-plus-plus), PM-eBus Sewa.
  • Newer 2023-24 banners: PM Vishwakarma (artisans, 18 trades), PM-JANMAN (PVTGs, 11 interventions), PM PVTG mission, Mission Karmayogi (civil-service capacity), Aspirational Districts (112, NITI), Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM health IDs).

MCQ hooks

  • MGNREGA = 2005 act, FY2006; social audit Sec 17; wage:material 60:40.
  • PM-KISAN ₹6,000 (not ₹5,000/7,000); DBT via PFMS.
  • MUDRA tiers order Shishu-Kishore-Tarun; new Tarun-Plus 2024.
  • JJM 2019 tap water; Smart Cities = 100, launched 2015; SBM = 2 Oct 2014.
  • Ayushman Bharat duo: PM-JAY (insurance) + Arogya Mandirs (care).
  • NEP 2020 structure 5+3+3+4 (not 10+2).
  • Aspirational Districts = NITI, 112 districts.

How to remember funding patterns

  • 100% Centre (Central Sector): PM-KISAN, PMJJBY/PMSBY (premium by beneficiaries), PMGKAY, Ujjwala, PM Vishwakarma, PMMY, Stand-Up, DBT-LPG, PM-SVANidhi, PM Internship, PM-Surya Ghar, PLI, FAME, Semicon, PM Gati Shakti, Atal Pension (govt co-contribution), NSAP (100% — grants), MPLADS, Namami Gange, PM-JANMAN (mostly).
  • 60:40 (90:10 NE & Himalayan; 100% UTs without legislature): PMAY-G/U, NHM, Samagra Shiksha, PM POSHAN (materials), JJM, SBM, PMGSY (60:40 since 2015–16), PMKSY, PMFBY (90:10 NE), NRLM, ICDS/POSHAN 2.0, PMMVY, PM SHRI, RKVY, Ayushman PM-JAY, DAY-NULM, Post-matric SC (60:40 from 2021).
  • MGNREGA — wages 100% Centre; material 75:25; admin 6%; VB-G RAM G — 60:40 total.
  • Core of the core (100% for scheduled castes/tribes/minority, e.g., MGNREGA, NSAP, umbrella schemes for SC/ST/OBC/minorities) vs core (60:40) vs optional; CSS rationalised (Shivraj Chouhan sub-group 2015 — 66 → 28; 2021 — 75 CSS umbrella).
  • Years: 2014 — Jan Dhan, SBM, Make in India, Digital India(2015), Saansad Adarsh Gram; 2015 — PMJJBY/PMSBY/APY, Mudra, Smart Cities, AMRUT, PMAY-U, Skill India, BBBP, Sukanya, Soil Health Card, PMKSY, Ujala, UDAY, Gold schemes; 2016 — PMFBY, Ujjwala, Stand-Up, Startup, e-NAM, PMAY-G, PMGDISHA, UDAN; 2017 — PMMVY, Saubhagya, PMKSY-Sampada; 2018 — Ayushman, Aspirational Districts, Samagra Shiksha, GOBAR-Dhan, PM-AASHA, Ayushman HWCs; 2019 — PM-KISAN, JJM, PM-KUSUM, PM-KMY, PM-SYM, ONORC, Study in India; 2020 — PM SVANidhi, AIF, PMMSY, Garib Kalyan Rojgar, SVAMITVA, Atmanirbhar Bharat (₹20 lakh cr), PMGKAY, PLI; 2021 — Gati Shakti, e-Shram, RDSS, ABDM, PM-ABHIM, POSHAN 2.0, Mission Shakti/Vatsalya, PM MITRA, PM-DAKSH; 2022 — Agnipath, PM-DevINE, Amrit Sarovar (75/district; 68,000+), PM-SHRI, Mission LiFE, 5G; 2023 — PM Vishwakarma, PM-JANMAN, PM Vikas, Green Hydrogen, Aspirational Blocks, Vibrant Villages, MY Bharat, PM-PRANAM, GOBARdhan, ONDC push, Sagar Parikrama; 2024 — PM Surya Ghar, PM Internship, PMAY 2.0, PM-eBus, Digital Agri Mission, Dharti Aaba, National Mission on Natural Farming, Mission Mausam, Bio-E3, PM Vidyalaxmi, Ayushman 70+, Unified Pension Scheme, One Nation One Subscription; 2025 — PM Dhan-Dhaanya, Nuclear Energy Mission, Makhana Board, Cotton mission, Pulses mission, Urban Challenge Fund, PM VBRY, National Sports Policy, Manufacturing Mission, Deep-tech fund, Nutrition & Health, Gyan Bharatam, Bharat Trade Net, National Critical Mineral Mission, India Semiconductor Mission 2.0, RDI scheme ₹1 lakh cr (July 2025), Employment Linked Incentive, PMKSY-2, MSME thresholds revision, SHANTI Act, VB-G RAM G, Income-tax Act 2025.

Traps

  • PM-KISAN = 100% Central Sector; PM-KMY = pension (LIC); PM-KUSUM under MNRE; PMFBY premium 2/1.5/5%; AIF till 2032–33.
  • MGNREGA wage 100% Centre; social audit is statutory (s 17); unemployment allowance by states; VB-G RAM G Act (Dec 2025) — 125 days, 60:40.
  • NFSA 75/50; AAY 35 kg/household; PHH 5 kg/person; PMGKAY free till Dec 2028; ONORC 2019 (2022 full).
  • Ayushman 70+ universal since Oct 2024; ABHA ≠ Aadhaar; NHM extended; Ayushman Arogya Mandir (renamed Nov 2023).
  • PMJDY OD ₹10,000; RuPay accident cover ₹2 lakh (post-Aug 2018 accounts); Mudra Tarun Plus ₹20 lakh; Stand-Up ₹10 lakh–1 cr; APY 18–40 (non-taxpayers since Oct 2022).
  • Aspirational Districts — NITI (112); Blocks 500; Smart Cities — MoHUA (closed Mar 2025); AMRUT 2.0 to 2026; JJM to 2028; SBM-G II to 2026.
  • BBBP — Jan 2015 Panipat (WCD + Health + Education); PMMVY ₹5,000 (first), ₹6,000 (second girl); Sukanya — 10 yrs age; 8.2%.
