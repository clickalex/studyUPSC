Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Syllabus slot: GS 3 (Mains) · Internal security challenges, extremism, terrorism, organised crime, security forces, border management + Disaster & disaster management Note: One pair of linked domains — UPSC tends to draw 2–4 questions across this combined envelope yearly.

1. Internal security architecture

  • Constitutional basis: Entry 1–2 (Public Order, Police) are STATE subjects (List II); Union retains Entry 2A (deployment of armed forces in aid of civil power), CISF/industrial security, intelligence coordination.
  • Structures: MHA (with internal-security divisions), NIA (2008 Act), NATGRID, MAC/sMAC intelligence grid, NSG (post-26/11 hubs), CAPFs: BSF (Pakistan & Bangladesh borders), CRPF (largest), CISF, ITBP (China border), SSB (Nepal-Bhutan), Assam Rifles dual-control (MoD ops/MHA admin).
  • Reforms lineage: police commissions (Gore 1971, Ribeiro, Padmanabhaiah, Malimath) — Prakash Singh 2006 SC directives (state security commissions, fixed DGP tenure, separation of investigation/law & order) — mostly unimplemented → Model Police Act 2015 draft.

2. Theatre-wise threats

2.1 Left-Wing Extremism (LWE/Maoism)

  • Origin Naxalbari 1967; peak spread "Red Corridor"; current contraction to ~38 districts (down from 200+) — govt target: elimination by March 2026 (security ops + development).
  • Instrument mix: Surrender-cum-rehab, Aspirational Districts Programme-ish convergence, Special Infrastructure Scheme, fortified police stations, anti-Naxal ops (intensified Bastar campaigns 2024–25; Operation Samadhan playbook), 5G towers in Dandakaranya.
  • Remaining hotspots: Bastar division (Chhattisgarh) — intensified 2024–25 ops (incl. killing of top cadres); Jharkhand pockets; Malkangiri-Koraput tri-junction.

2.2 Northeast insurgency & ethnic conflict

  • Post-accords: Bodo (Jan 2020), Bru-Reang (Jan 2020), Karbi Anglong (Sep 2021), DNLA/Assam settlements, ULFA pro-talks (Dec 2023) → reduced violence; Naga framework pending (NSCN-IM '15 framework; NSCN-K Khaplang faction abrogation 2019).
  • Manipur ethnic violence (May 2023): Meitei-Kuki faultline — valley(hills) governance split demands (ST status demand by Meitei; hill-areas autonomy under Art 371C) — persistence into 2024–25 (President's Rule imposed Feb 2025) — repeat PYQ-worthy case study in ethnic federalism & border spillover (Chin-Kuki civil-war refugees from Myanmar).
  • Other lines: interstate (Assam-Mizoram 2021 VS Assam-Arunachal 2022 resolutions); border fencing debates (Myanmar FMR scrapped 2024).

2.3 Kashmir

  • Article 370 dilution + 35A removal (Aug 2019) → J&K reorganization as UT (2019) → delimitation 2022 → assembly elections 2024 (NC-led govt; statehood restoration demand persistent) → Pahalgam terror attack Apr 2025 → Operation Sindoor (May 2025) — cross-LOC strikes on terror infrastructure; doctrine of treating terror as "act of war" (post-Sindoor PM speech, May 2025) — Indus Waters Treaty kept in "abeyance", calibrated escalation posture. (Specifics keep evolving — anchor answers to official statements and current affairs at exam time.)
  • Trends: hybrid militants, narco-terror synergy (drone drops post-2021), social-media recruitment (counter: 4G restoration graded post-2019).

2.4 Terror financing & transnational links

  • FATF gray-list watch for Pakistan (exits 2022—but monitoring continues); India on FATF "regular follow-up" (2024 mutual evaluation).
  • Hawala + crypto-terror convergences; UAPA strengthening (amendments 2019 individual designation); khadi courts & special NIA courts.

2.5 Organised crime & narcotics

  • Narco routes: Golden Crescent (Afghan products via Pakistan-Iran sea route — 2025 surge of Gujarat coast busts incl. 2024 Delhi mega-meth seizure), Golden Triangle via Myanmar-Manipur; synthetic drug labs (Mephedrone hubs) — Narco Coordination Centre (NCORD) + MANAS/Seizure portals.
  • Smuggling: drones (BSF counter-drone purchases), gold/electronics; wildlife trafficking clusters (Siliguri-Guwahati corridor).

6. Disaster management — the statutory frame

  • Disaster Management Act 2005 → three-tier structure: NDMA (PM chair) / SDMA / DDMA; NDRF (12→16+ battalions), NDRF vs state SDRF rules; National Disaster Management Plan 2016 (Sendai-aligned, with heatwave + biological edges); PM's 10-point agenda.
  • Financing: NDRF/SDRF windows (15th FC ₹1.6 lakh crore for 2021–26); parametric insurance pilots.
  • Sendai Framework 2015–30: 4 priorities (understand risk, strengthen governance, investing in resilience, build back better).

Hazard archetypes (for Mains answers)

  • Himalayan compound events: Chamoli (Feb 2021), Joshimath subsidence (Jan 2023 — carrying capacity), Sikkim GLOF (Oct 2023, South Lhonak flash after monsoon storm + Teesta III dam burst) → GLOF early-warning gaps.
  • Cyclones: Arabian Sea intensification (Cyclone Biparjoy Mocha Dana Remal); improving IMD accuracy (Mission Mausam ₹2,000 cr).
  • Urban floods: Chennai 2015·2023; Bengaluru 2022; Mumbai drainage audit story.
  • Heatwaves: HAPs scale + wet-bulb risks; 2024–25 attribution studies (WWA).
  • Landslide/infrastructure slope failures: Wayanad (July 2024, ~300 deaths; landslide atlas programme NRSC).

Tools & tech

  • Mission Mausam (₹2,000 cr, approved Sep 2024) — radars, satellites, AI models for nowcasting; Doppler radar grid expansion to ~100%.
  • Emergency support function (ESF) 12 clusters; Incident Response System (IRS) training; CBDRR schools (Aapda Mitra volunteers ~100k target).
  • Rebuild-better financing: risk insurance pools + parametric cues (Kalaignar-style crop insurance pilots at district level in hybrid schemes).

  • Hybrid terror / drones / narco convergence.

9. The Security Answer Method: Architecture First, Theatre Second, Reform Always

Section 1 maps the architecture (List-II police, MHA–NIA–grids–NSG–CAPFs, the Prakash-Singh-to-Model-Act lineage); this section makes it the answer method — because security answers fail structurally (theatre-description without architecture, threats without instruments, critique without reform) more often than factually. The method runs: constitutional hook (public order-and-police as State subjects — Entry 1–2, List II — the Union acting through Entry 2A deployment, intelligence coordination, and central forces — the federalism frame that opens every internal-security answer: the State owns order, the Union owns coordination-plus-surge) → institutional map (MHA's divisions; NIA (2008 Act — the post-26/11 federal-investigation answer — scheduled offences, special courts); NATGRID–MAC–sMAC (the intelligence grid — data-fusion plus multi-agency centres — the connect-the-dots machinery); NSG (hubs post-26/11 — the federal contingency force); CAPFs (BSF–CRPF–CISF–ITBP–SSB–Assam-Rifles — each with its border-or-role: BSF Pakistan–Bangladesh, ITBP China, SSB Nepal–Bhutan, Assam Rifles Myanmar-dual-control, CRPF the internal-surge bulk, CISF the installation-guard) — drawn as the web, one sketch per answer) → theatre analysis (§2's five: LWE-contraction, NE-accords-plus-Manipur, Kashmir-post-Sindoor, terror-finance, narco-organised — each: trend-honest, instrument-mapped, datum-anchored) → reform close (the lineage: Gore-1971 → Ribeiro → Padmanabhaiah → Malimath → Prakash Singh 2006 (security commissions, DGP tenure, investigation–law-and-order separation — mostly unimplemented, honestly admitted) → Model Police Act 2015 draft → the live levers: SMART-policing (CCTNS-interoperable, ICJS-linked), forensic capacity (the conviction-rate bottleneck — BNS–BNSS–BSA 2023-24 codes' forensic mandates), police-population ratios and vacancy arithmetic, community-policing pilots). Architecture-first disciplines every theatre: no threat described without its counter-instrument named, no instrument named without its reform-gap admitted.

The federalism stress-tests (§7's grid extended) deserve their standing paragraph: general-consent withdrawals (CBI's federalism friction — States withdrawing DSPE consent — investigation federalised by permission); NIA's concurrent reach (scheduled offences overriding the State veto — the post-26/11 settlement: terror as national crime); CAPF deployment politics (aid-to-civil-power requisitions — the \"forces as ordered, control as disputed\" tension); and the intelligence-sharing doctrine (MAC–sMAC–NATGRID as the technical fix for the pre-26/11 silos — Kargil-Review-to-Naresh-Chandra lineage: reform born of failure, audited by the next failure). The security answer's close is always reform-sequenced (first fill vacancies-and-forensics, then separate investigation, then commission-oversight — ordering as judgment) — threats described, architecture mapped, gaps admitted, sequence proposed: the method that converts the security envelope's 2–4 yearly questions into bankable marks.

10. Theatres in Depth: LWE Endgame, Manipur's Federal Lesson, Post-Sindoor Kashmir

Section 2 sketches five theatres; this section argues three — the ones whose examination weight (PYQ-worthy, §2's own flag) demands depth. LWE endgame (Naxalbari 1967 → Red-Corridor peak → ~38 districts → the March-2026 elimination target): argue the contraction's mechanics (security: Operation Samadhan playbook — fortified stations, Bastar-2024–25 intensified campaigns, top-cadre neutralisations; development: Special Infrastructure Scheme, 5G-towers-in-Dandakaranya, Surrender-cum-rehab economics, Aspirational-Districts convergence — the clear-hold-build sequence executed with unusual patience) against the honest caveats (contraction-measured-in-districts versus presence-measured-in-armed-cadres; the tri-junction spillovers — Malkangiri-Koraput, Jharkhand pockets; the post-elimination governance bet: elimination-without-rights risks resurgence — FRA-titles, PESA-compliance, mining-consent as the peace's foundations — the answer's second half: security wins the quarter, development wins the decade). Manipur (May 2023 → persistence → President's Rule February 2025): argue the federal anatomy (Meitei valley-demand (ST status) versus Kuki–Naga hill-autonomy (Article 371C's Hill Areas Committee — the constitutional asymmetry straining); governance-split (buffer zones, separate administrations-in-practice — the State's monopoly fraying); border-spillover (Myanmar-civil-war Chin-Kuki refugees, FMR-scrapped-2024, fencing-resisted-by-Mizoram–Nagaland — the international-inside-the-domestic); the PYQ-frame: ethnic federalism's stress-test — autonomy-within-unity (Sixth-Schedule-plus models) versus partition-by-stealth — the answer refusing both (neither majoritarian-imposition nor ethnic-secession, but asymmetric-federalism renegotiated: 371C empowered, district-councils funded, border managed humanely).

Post-Sindoor Kashmir (370-dilution-2019 → UT → delimitation-2022 → assembly-elections-2024 → Pahalgam April 2025 → Operation Sindoor May 2025 (cross-LoC strikes on terror infrastructure; the \"act of war\" doctrine articulated; Indus Waters Treaty in \"abeyance\"; calibrated-escalation posture — specifics evolving, anchored to official statements per §2's own discipline)): argue the doctrine-shift (terror treated as warfare, not crime-plus-diplomacy — the response-ladder redefined: strike-plus-treaty-leverage-plus-posture — with the escalation-management corollary: calibration as the doctrine's second half — signalling resolve while bounding war) alongside the normalisation ledger (elections-held, statehood-demanded, hybrid-militancy plus narco-drops plus social-media-recruitment as the threat-mutation — drones-post-2021, 4G-graded-restoration as the connectivity-counterinsurgency). The terror-finance-and-narco coda (§2.4–2.5): FATF regular-follow-up-2024 (compliance maintained, vigilance priced), UAPA-2019 individual designation, hawala-plus-crypto convergence; Golden-Crescent-via-sea (Gujarat-coast 2025 busts, Delhi-2024 mega-meth) plus Golden-Triangle-via-Manipur plus mephedrone-labs — NCORD–MANAS coordination, BSF counter-drones, the Siliguri-wildlife corridor — the convergence answer (terror-finance-narco-cyber as one enforcement web: §7's hybrid-triangle). Three theatres argued, each: mechanics → caveats → frame — depth where the marks cluster.

11. Borders, Coasts, Cyber: The Perimeter as a System

Sections 3–5 map borders (six-neighbour table), coasts (post-26/11 stack), and cyber (coordinator-plus-CERT-plus-NCIIPC-plus-I4C); this section systems the perimeter — because border/coast/cyber questions reward the candidate who shows the seams (where perimeters meet and gaps open). Land borders (§3's grid as the drill-sheet — force × length × live-issue, memorised cold): Indo-Pak (~3,323 km, BSF — drone-drops, LC-infiltration, post-Sindoor posture); Indo-China (3,488 km, ITBP-plus-Army — LAC-standoff-since-2020, buffer-zones, October-2024 patrolling-resumption agreement, Vibrant-Villages-II-2025 plus BRO-record-spend — Atal-and-Sela-tunnels, Shinku-La — infrastructure-as-deterrence); Indo-Nepal (~1,751 km, SSB — open-border misuse, Kalapani-Lipulekh dispute — the map-in-the-textbooks versus the ground-in-Kalapani); Indo-Bhutan (~699 km, SSB — the quiet border, tea-belt porosity); Indo-Bangladesh (~4,096 km, BSF — riverine fencing-gaps, cattle-smuggling, post-Hasina-August-2024 tensions — the regime-change border: minority-safety narratives, custodial frictions); Indo-Myanmar (~1,643 km, Assam Rifles — FMR suspended December 2024, fencing-pushed versus Naga–Mizo–Kuki-resisted, refugee-inflows — the fence-versus-kinship dilemma). The management doctrine (SMART-fencing — CIBMS pilots: sensors-lasers-cameras integrated; Border Haats as soft-perimeter; PM-Gati-Shakti border-roads — the hard–soft–smart triad) applied border-by-border: fence the hostile (Pak, Myanmar-push), develop the contested (China — villages-plus-roads), manage the open (Nepal, Bhutan — regulated-openness, not walls), complete the long (Bangladesh — riverine-tech plus diplomacy).

Coasts (§4 — the post-26/11 architecture: NC3I/Navy network, coastal-radar-chain Phase-I-plus-II, Sagar-Kavach drills, CISF-ports, ICG-toward-200-ships-by-2030, AI-surveillance-2024–25 procurement — the sea-leg of the perimeter: gap-honest (fishing-vessel tracking — the 26/11-lesson half-learnt: transponders-and-licensing progressing, dark-vessels persisting; island-territories — Great-Nicobar-transhipment's security-strategic overlap with the environment note, ANC tri-command — the Indian-Ocean fulcrum). Cyber-and-new-domains (§5 — the doctrine-still-forming: National Cyber Security Coordinator (PMO) plus CERT-In (incident-response, the 6-hour-reporting mandate) plus NCIIPC (critical-information-infrastructure — power, banking, the protected-list) plus I4C (MHA's cybercrime coordination — fraud-stack reporting, the monthly-threat picture) plus DPDP-2023-as-half-architecture plus RBI-cyber-resilience-norms — the strategy-evolving-into-operation, honestly admitted): the answer-frame (threats: state-sponsored (power-grid probes), criminal (UPI-fraud industrialisation, ransomware-on-hospitals), terror-adjacent (crypto-hawala rails); instruments: coordinator-plus-CERT-plus-NCIIPC-plus-I4C; gaps: strategy-to-operation, workforce-scale, private-sector-mandate depth; the space–undersea-cable–semiconductor extensions — new domains named: ASAT-deterrence, cable-chokepoints, chip-supply). The perimeter synthesis: land fenced-and-developed by doctrine, sea networked-and-drilled with gaps admitted, cyber coordinated-but-strategy-pending — three perimeters, one seams-analysis, the examination's border-grid answered cold.

12. Disaster Management Masterclass: Statute, Sendai, Archetypes, and the DDMA

Section 6 frames the statute (DM Act 2005 — NDMA–SDMA–DDMA, NDRF, NDMP-2016, 10-point agenda, Sendai, finance, archetypes, tech); this section masters it — because disaster questions (the §7 PYQ patterns: Sendai-translation, DDMA-clarity, archetype-application) reward the candidate who descends from framework to field. Statute-to-field: DM Act 2005 (the three-tier pyramid — NDMA chaired by PM (policy-plus-guidelines), SDMAs (State plans-plus-funds), DDMAs (the district engine — Collector-chaired: DDMP plans, control rooms, mock drills, relief disbursement — §7's role-clarity PYQ answered here: the DDMA prepares (vulnerability-maps, resource-inventories, IRS-training), responds (ESF-12 clusters activated, NDRF–SDRF–Aapda-Mitra deployed), recovers (damage-assessment, build-back-better enforcement — the Collector as the disaster's accountable face); NDRF (12→16+ battalions — the special-response bulk — versus SDRFs' uneven State capacity — the federal-response gap honestly admitted); NDMP 2016 (Sendai-aligned — heatwave-plus-biological edges — the plan's existence versus the DDMP-implementation variance — plans written, drills uneven); PM's 10-point agenda (risk-mapping, building-codes, insurance, women's leadership — the doctrine's checklist). Sendai translation (§7 — the four priorities domesticated: understand-risk (landslide atlas NRSC, GLOF inventories, Mission-Mausam observation), strengthen-governance (DDMAs empowered, IRS institutionalised), invest-in-resilience (mitigation funds, resilient infrastructure — CDRI's export), build-back-better (reconstruction codes enforced — the Kerala-2018, Chamoli-2021 rebuild audits).

Archetype application (§6's five, each: mechanism → Indian case → gap → instrument): Himalayan-compound (Chamoli-February-2021 rock-ice avalanche; Joshimath-January-2023 subsidence — carrying-capacity breached; Sikkim-October-2023 South-Lhonak GLOF plus Teesta-III burst — early-warning gaps → GLOF-EWS push, tunnel-dam review); cyclones (Arabian-Sea intensification — Biparjoy–Mocha–Dana–Remal — IMD-accuracy improving, Mission-Mausam-₹2,000-crore radars-satellites-AI → zero-casualty doctrine (Odisha's proof: 1999-super-cyclone-10,000-dead to recent near-zero — evacuation-plus-shelters-plus-forecasts); urban-floods (Chennai-2015-and-2023, Bengaluru-2022, Mumbai-drainage-audits — concretised-watersheds, encroached-paleochannels → sponge-city norms, wetland-notified buffers); heatwaves (HAPs-23+-States, wet-bulb thresholds, WWA-attribution-2024–25 → cool-roofs, work-hour mandates, insurance-linkage); landslides (Wayanad-July-2024 ~300 dead — the Western-Ghats ESA saga's human cost → atlas-plus-EWS-plus-settlement-regulation). Finance-and-tech (NDRF–SDRF windows — 15th-FC-₹1.6-lakh-crore-2021–26 — the corpus; parametric-insurance pilots, Kalaignar-style district crop-pilots — the payout-without-survey frontier; Mission Mausam's Doppler-100-per-cent; ESF-12, IRS-training, Aapda-Mitra-~100k-volunteers — CBDRR-schools — the community-last-mile).
