Lesson 1.1.8

Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526)

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Five dynasties 1206–1526. Exam anchors: institutional terms (iqta, dagh), market reforms, architecture firsts, traveller visits.

1. The five dynasties

DynastySpanAnchors
Mamluk (Slave)1206-90Aibak (Lahore; Arhai-din-ka-jhompra), Iltutmish (Delhi shift; iqta system; tanka-jital bimetallic; Chihalgani 'the Forty'; completes Qutub; iqta-tul-mash), Razia (1236-40, only woman), Balban (divine-right theory 'zil-i-Ilahi', spy system, chihalgani crushed, Persian etiquette sijda-paibos)
Khalji1290-1320Jalaluddin murdered by Alauddin; Chittor 1303 (Padmini tradition); Devagiri; Deccan raids (Malik Kafur — Hoysalas, Pandyas); market reforms: 4 markets, price control, dagh (brand) + chehra (descriptive roll), grain state-monopoly, land-measure (biswa), first permanent standing-pay army, first Muslim conquest of South fully; Ziauddin Barani chronicles
Tughlaq1320-1414Ghiyasuddin (Tughlaqabad), Muhammad bin Tughlaq: token currency (bronze, 1330 — failed forgery), Daulatabad shift (reversed), khurasan & qarachil expeditions, agricultural loans (diwan-i-amir-kohi), Ibn Battuta resident; Firoz Shah: canals, jizya re-imposed, diwan-i-khairat + diwan-i-istibqa, employment bureau, Firoz Shah Kotla (Ashokan pillar shifted here), slavery regulation, madrasa; Timur sacks Delhi 1398 → Sayyids
Sayyid1414-51Khizr Khan (claimed Prophet descent); Mubarak Shah first Sayyid to issue coins; last = Alauddin Alam Shah → Bahlul Lodi bloodless
Lodi1451-1526Bahlul (first Afghan; 'khan-i-khanan' humility), Sikandar (Agra founded 1504; measurement reform — gaz-i-sikandari), Ibrahim (nobles rebel; Daulat Khan + Alam Khan invite Babur; Panipat-1 1526 ends sultanate)

2. Administration, economy, society

  • Wizarat (finance), diwan-i-ariz (military), diwan-i-risalat (foreign/religious), diwan-i-insha (correspondence); iqtadari = revenue-assignment instead of salary; khalisa (crown land), inam, waqf; central vs muqti audit.
  • Society: ulema influence, sufis (Chishti: Moinuddin Ajmer, Nizamuddin Auliya, Nasiruddin Chirag; Suhrawardi: Bahauddin Multani; Firdausi in Bihar (Sharfuddin Yahya Maneri)), Bhakti seeds (Ramanuja line, later Ramananda); slavery institutional; caste-Hindu jizya shifts by regime.
  • Economy: tanka (silver) + jital (copper); Alauddin's biswa measurement; Firoz's 4 canals (Sutlej, Yamuna, Ghaghara lines); guilds (shahna-i-mandi controller under Alauddin).

3. Art, architecture, travellers

  • Arch firsts: true arch + dome (Alai Darwaza), double-dome experiments (Lodi), octagonal tomb type (Lodi), sloping walls (Tughlaq), Qutub Minar phases, Hauz Khas madrasa (Firoz), Khirki mosque, Jaunpur Atala (Sharquis), Bengal Adina (Sikandar Shah), Mandu Jahaz Mahal.
  • Literature: Barani (Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi, Fatwa-i-Jahandari), Amir Khusrau (polymath — khayal attribution, tarana claims; Nuh Siphir), Isami (Futuh-us-Salatin), Yahya Sirhindi; Sanskrit court patronage under the Sultanate was limited.
  • Travellers: Ibn Battuta (M.B. Tughlaq — Rihla), Megasthenes is Mauryan (trap), Marco Polo (Pandyas 1292), Nicolo Conti (Vijayanagara, 1420s), Abdur Razzaq (Vijayanagara, Deva Raya II), Athanasius Nikitin (Bahmani 1470s).

MCQ hooks

  • Market reforms = Alauddin (4 markets, sultan's control, Shahna-i-Mandi).
  • Token currency + capital shift = M.B. Tughlaq pair.
  • First true arch = Alai Darwaza (Alauddin); first garden tomb trend = Lodi.
  • Chihalgani break = Balban; iqta institutionaliser = Iltutmish.
  • Ibn Battuta → M.B. Tughlaq; Barani → Firoz-era history.
  • Panipat-1 (1526) ends Lodi, starts Mughal — double hook.

Dynasties & rulers (five, in order)

DynastyYearsRulers & hooks
Mamluk / Slave1206–90Aibak (Qutb Minar begun, Quwwat-ul-Islam, Adhai-din-ka-Jhonpra; died playing chaugan 1210) · Iltutmish (real founder; capital Lahore→Delhi; silver tanka & copper jital; Iqta system; Chahalgani/Turkan-i-Chahalgani (40); mansab of Caliph 1229; completed Qutb Minar) · Raziya 1236–40 (first woman ruler; Yakut) · Balban (1266–87; Zil-i-Ilahi, sijda & paibos, Persian Nauroz; blood-and-iron; crushed Chahalgani; Diwan-i-Arz military dept)
Khalji1290–1320Jalaluddin · Alauddin (1296–1316) — market control (Diwan-i-Riyasat, Shahna-i-Mandi), first standing army with dagh (branding) & chehra (descriptive roll), land revenue 50% (kharaj), ghari & charai taxes, Mongol repulse, Deccan campaigns (Malik Kafur; Devagiri, Warangal, Dwarasamudra, Madurai), Chittor 1303 (Padmini legend), Siri fort, Alai Darwaza 1311, Hauz Khas; Sikandar-i-Sani; Amir Khusrau
Tughlaq1320–1414Ghiyasuddin (Tughlaqabad; canals) · Muhammad bin Tughlaq (1325–51) — capital to Daulatabad 1327, token copper/brass currency 1329–30, Doab tax hike, Qarachil & Khurasan plans, Diwan-i-Kohi (agriculture), Ibn Battuta (qazi of Delhi, Rihla) · Firoz Shah (1351–88) — jizya on Brahmins, canals (Yamuna–Hisar), Diwan-i-Khairat, Diwan-i-Bandagan (slaves), 300 towns (Firozabad, Jaunpur, Hisar, Fatehabad), moved two Ashokan pillars (Topra, Meerut), Futuhat-i-Firozshahi, abolished 24 taxes (kept 4: kharaj, khams, jizya, zakat + haqq-i-sharb irrigation) · Timur's invasion 1398 (Nasiruddin Mahmud)
Sayyid1414–51Khizr Khan (Timur's deputy; Rayat-i-Ala) · Mubarak (Yahya Sirhindi's Tarikh-i-Mubarakshahi)
Lodi1451–1526Bahlul (first Afghan dynasty) · Sikandar (1489–1517) — founded Agra 1504, Gaz-i-Sikandari measurement, Gulrukhi poetry, destroyed temples yet Persian–Sanskrit translations (Farhang-i-Sikandari) · Ibrahim — Panipat 21 April 1526

Administration vocabulary

  • Wazir (Diwan-i-Wizarat, finance) · Ariz-i-Mumalik (Diwan-i-Arz) · Diwan-i-Insha (correspondence) · Diwan-i-Risalat (religious/foreign) · Sadr-us-Sudur · Qazi-ul-Quzat · Barid (intelligence) · Muqti/Wali (iqta holder) · Khut, Muqaddam, Chaudhari (rural intermediaries) · Amir-i-Dad.
  • Land types: khalisa, iqta, inam/waqf/milk.

Culture & architecture

  • Indo-Islamic firsts: arch & dome (true arch: Balban's tomb), Alai Darwaza first true dome with scientific arch; Tughlaq batter/sloping walls; Lodi octagonal tombs & double dome (Sikandar Lodi's tomb, Lodi Gardens).
  • Literature: Amir Khusrau (Tughlaqnama, Khazain-ul-Futuh, Nuh Sipihr; "Tuti-i-Hind"; qawwali, tabla/sitar attributions), Ziauddin Barani (Tarikh-i-Firozshahi, Fatawa-i-Jahandari), Minhaj-us-Siraj (Tabaqat-i-Nasiri), Isami (Futuh-us-Salatin), Shams Siraj Afif.
  • Sufi silsilas: Chishti (Moinuddin Ajmer, Bakhtiyar Kaki, Farid Ganj-i-Shakar, Nizamuddin Auliya, Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Dehli), Suhrawardi (Bahauddin Zakariya Multan; accepted state service), Firdausi (Sharfuddin Yahya Maneri), Qadiri, Naqshbandi (Baqi Billah, Sirhindi — later).

Traps

  • Iltutmish, not Aibak, is the "real founder" and issued the pure Arabic-legend tanka.
  • MbT's token currency was copper/brass, on a silver-tanka standard — failed by forgery.
  • Firoz Tughlaq's jizya on Brahmins was new; Hindus in general already paid it.

Pairs flash

  • Iqta + tanka-jital + Chihalgani → Iltutmish · zil-i-Ilahi spy net → Balban.
  • dagh-chehra + 4 markets + Shahna-i-Mandi → Alauddin · Malik Kafur → Deccan raids.
  • Token currency + Daulatabad + diwan-i-amir-kohi → M.B. Tughlaq · Rihla → Ibn Battuta.
  • Canals + jizya + Firoz Shah Kotla pillar → Firoz Shah · Timur 1398 → Sayyid rise.
  • Agra 1504 + gaz-i-sikandari → Sikandar Lodi · Panipat-1 → Ibrahim vs Babur 1526.
  • Alai Darwaza → first true arch · octagonal tombs + garden trend → Lodis · sloping walls → Tughlaq.
  • Tarikh-i-Firoz Shahi + Fatwa-i-Jahandari → Barani · Futuh-us-Salatin → Isami.
  • Chishti line → Moinuddin → Nizamuddin → Nasiruddin Chirag · Suhrawardi → Bahauddin Multani.
  • Marco Polo → Pandyas · Abdur Razzaq + Nicolo Conti → Vijayanagara · Nikitin → Bahmani.
  • Nizamuddin Auliya → Chishti Delhi · Amir Khusrau → his disciple, khayal-tarana claims.

Trap flash

  • Razia's dates 1236-40; first woman ruler claim vs Rudramadevi (Kakatiya) — both asked.
  • Megasthenes = Chandragupta Maurya era, NOT Sultanate traveller.
  • Qutub Minar: Aibak begun, Iltutmish finished, Firoz repaired; Alai Minar = Alauddin's unfinished rival.
  • Iqta = revenue assignment (not hereditary by rule); khalisa = crown land.
  • Alauddin's land measure introduced biswa; Sher Shah perfects it later (Mughal frame).
