Services & Infrastructure

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Services engine + infrastructure pipeline. Pipeline-number and scheme-name recall.

1. Services economy

  • ~55% of GVA; IT-BPM ~7.5-8% of GDP, exports ~$200bn (NASSCOM industry body); GCC boom (1,600+ centres); startups (3rd-largest ecosystem band); ONDC open network.
  • Tourism: Swadesh Darshan 2.0 (destinations) + PRASHAD (pilgrimage); medical-value travel; hospitality rebound. Logistics: cost ~13-14% → 7.5% target; LPI 2023: India 38th; LEADS = domestic survey; PM Gati Shakti multimodal layer.
  • Finance-professional: GIFT IFSC, education-skill exports (NEP internationalisation).

2. Infrastructure pipeline

  • NIP (2019): ₹111 lakh crore (FY20-25); NMP monetisation (₹6 lakh crore, 2022-25); NIIF funds (2015); PM Gati Shakti (Oct 2021; GIS master-plan); central capex thrust (~3.4% GDP band).
  • Transport: DFCs Eastern + Western (largely operational), Bharatmala, UDAN routes, Vadhavan port (2024), metro expansion (25+ cities), NW-1 Haldia-Varanasi cargo-cruise.
  • Energy-urban: PM Surya Ghar rooftop (2024; 1 crore homes), PM-KUSUM pumps, RDSS (2021 DISCOM reform), green corridors + OSOWOG, AMRUT 2.0 + SBM-U 2.0, PM-eBus Sewa (10,000), SVAMITVA property cards, CCTS carbon market (2023 framework).

MCQ hooks

  • Services ~55% GVA; IT exports ~$200bn; GCC 1,600+.
  • NIP ₹111L cr (2019) vs NMP ₹6L cr (2021) — acronym pair.
  • Gati Shakti = 2021 platform; NLP = 2022 policy.
  • LPI 38th (2023); LEADS = domestic index.
  • DFC duo operational; Vadhavan 2024; UDAN regional air.
  • Surya Ghar 2024; RDSS 2021; PM-eBus 10,000; CCTS 2023.
