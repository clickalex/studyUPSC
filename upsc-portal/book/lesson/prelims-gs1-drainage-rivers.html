Lesson 1.2.6

Drainage System & Major Rivers

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Himalayan vs peninsular systems, interlinking, disputes, dams. River-atlas recall is the skill.

1. Two systems

  • Himalayan (perennial, snow-fed, antecedent): deep gorges, dendritic-braided reaches, large deltas.
  • Peninsular (mostly rain-fed, seasonal, graded): east-flowing giants (Mahanadi-Godavari-Krishna-Kaveri → Bay) + west-flowing rift rivers (Narmada-Tapi-Sabarmati-Mahi-Luni → Arabian Sea) + interior Yamuna tributaries (Chambal-Betwa-Ken-Son) + independent east-coast streams (Subarnarekha-Baitarani).

2. Indus & Ganga systems

  • Indus: rises near Mansarovar (Tibet), enters India in Ladakh; Indus Water Treaty 1960 (World Bank-brokered): India = Ravi-Beas-Sutlej (eastern rivers); Pakistan = Indus-Jhelum-Chenab (western). Jhelum source = Verinag; Sutlej enters via Shipki la.
  • Ganga: Bhagirathi (Gangotri) + Alaknanda → Devprayag confluence; Panch Prayag chain; right-bank Yamuna (largest tributary — Chambal-Sind-Betwa-Ken join it), left-bank Ghaghara-Gandak-Kosi (sorrow of Bihar) + Ramganga-Son (south bank, from Amarkantak). Delta: Bhagirathi-Hooghly distributary (Kolkata port); Sundarbans.
  • Brahmaputra: Tsangpo (Tibet) → Dihang (Arunachal; Siang-Dibang-Lohit) → Assam valley (Majuli island) → Jamuna (Bangladesh).

3. Peninsular detail

  • Godavari (largest peninsular; Trimbakeshwar-Nashik; tributaries Manjira-Pranhita-Indravati-Sabari; Polavaram project), Krishna (Mahabaleshwar; Bhima-Tungabhadra-Musi; Nagarjuna Sagar-Almatti), Kaveri (Talakaveri-Coorg; Bhavani-Amaravati-Hemavati; Mettur dam; delta rice bowl; Cauvery dispute — 2007 tribunal award, 2018 SC modification), Mahanadi (Sihawa; Hirakud long dam).
  • Narmada (Amarkantak; rift estuary; Sardar Sarovar-Indira Sagar; Jabalpur marble rocks-Dhuandhar falls), Tapi (Multai; Ukai; Surat estuary), Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni (Aravalli source; turns saline downstream), Periyar-Pamba (Kerala).

4. Management & disputes

  • Interlinking: Ken-Betwa first (Panna tiger-reserve section); disputes: Cauvery (TN-KA), Krishna (MH-KA-AP-TS), Mahadayi/Mandovi (KA-GO-MH), Ravi-Beas/SYL (Punjab-Haryana-Rajasthan), Vansadhara (AP-OD).
  • Frame: Art 262 — Parliament may exclude court jurisdiction; Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956 (tribunals; 2019 single-tribunal proposal); Namami Gange (2014), Jal Shakti ministry (2019), Atal Bhujal (2020). Flood-prone: Kosi-Bihar, Brahmaputra-Assam.

MCQ hooks

  • IWT 1960: eastern rivers (Ravi-Beas-Sutlej) = India; western (Indus-Jhelum-Chenab) = Pakistan.
  • Devprayag = Bhagirathi + Alaknanda; Kosi = sorrow of Bihar; Damodar = once sorrow of Bengal.
  • Polavaram = Godavari; Hirakud = Mahanadi; Mettur = Kaveri; Sardar Sarovar = Narmada; Tehri = Bhagirathi.
  • Rift-valley west-flowers: Narmada + Tapi.
  • Ken-Betwa = first interlink; Cauvery dates 2007/2018 pair.
  • Art 262 + ISRWD Act 1956 tribunal regime.

Classification

  • Himalayan (perennial; antecedent gorges; meanders) vs Peninsular (seasonal; older; graded; Narmada, Tapi, Mahi, Sabarmati, Periyar westward — rift/estuary; Godavari, Krishna, Kaveri, Mahanadi eastward — deltas). Drainage patterns: dendritic (Ganga), trellis (Singhbhum), radial (Amarkantak), rectangular (Vindhya), centripetal (Loktak), parallel (W Ghats streams), deranged. Water divide: Delhi ridge–Aravalli–Sahyadri (Bay of Bengal 77% vs Arabian Sea 23%). Antecedent rivers: Indus, Sutlej, Brahmaputra (cut Himalaya); superimposed: Damodar, Subarnarekha, Chambal, Banas.

Indus system (2,880 km; India 1,114; Indus Waters Treaty 1960 — India: Ravi-Beas-Sutlej; Pakistan: Indus-Jhelum-Chenab; India held IWT "in abeyance" April 2025)

  • Source Bokhar Chu glacier near Mansarovar (Kailash; Singi Khamban — lion's mouth); enters India Demchok; Ladakh (Leh); Nanga Parbat gorge; Karachi. Tributaries: Jhelum (Verinag; Wular; Srinagar; Kishanganga/Neelum — Kishanganga project; Tulbul/Wular barrage; joins Chenab at Trimmu), Chenab (Chandra+Bhaga at Tandi; largest tributary; Baglihar, Salal, Dulhasti, Ratle, Pakal Dul, Kiru, Kwar), Ravi (Rohtang; Chamba; Shahpurkandi barrage; Ranjit Sagar/Thein; Madhopur), Beas (Beas Kund/Rohtang; Kullu, Manali; Pong dam/Maharana Pratap Sagar; Pandoh; Harike confluence), Sutlej (Rakas Tal Tibet; Shipki La; longest tributary; Bhakra-Nangal (Gobind Sagar), Nathpa Jhakri; Harike; Hussainiwala); trans-Himalayan tributaries — Shyok (Siachen; Nubra), Zanskar, Gilgit, Kabul (Pakistan), Dras, Shigar; Panjnad; Ujh multipurpose; Renuka (Giri). IWT: Permanent Indus Commission; Neutral Expert vs Court of Arbitration (2023 dispute).

Ganga system (2,525 km; longest in India; basin 26% area; National River 2008; Namami Gange 2014; NMCG)

  • Bhagirathi (Gaumukh/Gangotri; Tehri dam on Bhagirathi–Bhilangana) + Alaknanda (Satopanth/Bhagirath Kharak glaciers; Badrinath; Panch Prayag: Vishnuprayag (Dhauliganga), Nandprayag (Nandakini), Karnaprayag (Pindar), Rudraprayag (Mandakini — Kedarnath), Devprayag (Bhagirathi → Ganga)); Haridwar (enters plains); Farakka barrage (1975; Bangladesh treaty 1996, 30-yr expiring 2026); Bhagirathi–Hooghly (India) vs Padma (Bangladesh) → Meghna → Bay.
  • Left-bank: Ramganga (Corbett; Kalagarh), Gomti (Lucknow; Jaunpur; only wholly plains-fed via Fulhar jheel), Ghaghara/Karnali (Tibet Mapchachungo; Sharda/Kali (Kalapani; Pancheshwar), Rapti (Gorakhpur); largest by volume), Gandak (Nepal Kali Gandaki; Valmiki; Triveni; Sonepur; Gandak barrage), Burhi Gandak, Kosi ("sorrow of Bihar"; seven streams — Sapta Kosi; Arun (Tibet); Kosi barrage (Bhimnagar, Nepal 1954 treaty); 2008 breach; avulsions), Mahananda (Darjeeling; last left-bank in India).
  • Right-bank: Yamuna (Yamunotri/Bandarpunch; longest tributary 1,376 km; Delhi, Agra, Mathura; joins Prayagraj; tributaries Chambal (Janapav Mhow; Gandhi Sagar, Rana Pratap Sagar, Jawahar Sagar, Kota barrage; ravines; ghariyal sanctuary), Sindh, Betwa (Vindhya Raisen; Ken–Betwa link first ILR; Panna tiger reserve; Rajghat, Matatila), Ken, Tons, Hindon; Hathnikund/Tajewala, Okhla, Lakhwar, Renuka), Son (Amarkantak; Bansagar; Rihand (Govind Ballabh Pant Sagar); Dehri; 784 km), Damodar (Chota Nagpur; "sorrow of Bengal"; DVC 1948 (first multipurpose, TVA model); Maithon, Panchet, Tilaiya, Konar; Barakar; joins Hooghly), Punpun, Karmanasa, Tamsa/Tons (Sirsa).
  • Cities: Haridwar, Kanpur (tanneries), Prayagraj (Sangam), Varanasi, Patna, Bhagalpur (Vikramshila Gangetic dolphin sanctuary; national aquatic animal 2009; first dolphin census 2025 — 6,327), Kolkata. Ganga Expressway; Arth Ganga; GIS-based Ganga basin management; Clean Ganga Fund; sewage (STP) targets; Ganga Vilas cruise (Varanasi–Dibrugarh 2023).

Brahmaputra system (2,900 km; India 916 km; largest volume; Yarlung Tsangpo — Chemayungdung/Angsi glacier near Mansarovar; flows east 1,200 km in Tibet; Namcha Barwa U-turn (Great Bend; China's Medog mega-dam 2024–25); enters Arunachal as Siang/Dihang; joins Dibang & Lohit at Sadiya → Brahmaputra; Assam braided 700 km; Majuli; Dhubri → Bangladesh as Jamuna; joins Padma at Goalundo; Meghna (Barak) at Chandpur)

  • Tributaries: north — Subansiri (largest; Lower Subansiri HEP 2,000 MW), Kameng/Jia Bhoreli, Manas (Bhutan; Ramsar? no — Manas NP), Sankosh, Dhansiri (N), Raidak, Teesta (Sikkim; Gajoldoba; joins in Bangladesh; Teesta water dispute; Sikkim GLOF Oct 2023 — Chungthang dam); south — Lohit, Dibang, Dhansiri (S), Kopili, Burhi Dihing, Disang, Dikhou, Kulsi (dolphin). Barak (Manipur; Cachar; Surma–Kushiyara; Tipaimukh). Floods/erosion (Brahmaputra Board 1980); Bogibeel rail-road bridge (2018, longest 4.94 km), Dhola-Sadiya/Bhupen Hazarika bridge (9.15 km, longest river bridge in India), Kaziranga (flood refuge), Dibru-Saikhowa, Umananda, NW-2 (Dhubri–Sadiya), Pandu port; hydropower race (Siang Upper Multipurpose 11,000 MW proposed).

Water disputes, tribunals & links

  • Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956 (Art 262; Sarkaria → single permanent tribunal Bill 2019); tribunals: Krishna (I 1969 Bachawat; II 2004 Brijesh Kumar), Godavari (1969), Narmada (1969), Ravi-Beas (1986; SYL canal — Punjab-Haryana; SC 2002/2004; Punjab Termination Act 2004 struck 2016), Kaveri (1990), Vansadhara (2010), Mahadayi (2010; award 2018), Mahanadi (2018); Pennaiyar (2023 constituted); Godavari–Cauvery link; Ken–Betwa link (foundation Dec 2024; ₹44,605 cr; Daudhan dam; Panna); Par–Tapi–Narmada (shelved); Damanganga–Pinjal; National Water Policy 2012 (draft 2019 Mihir Shah); Interlinking of Rivers (NPP 1980; 30 links — 14 Himalayan, 16 peninsular); Jal Jeevan Mission (2019; 15 cr+ tap connections, ~80% by 2025); Jal Shakti Abhiyan: Catch the Rain; Atal Bhujal; Dam Safety Act 2021 (NDSA); National Waterways Act 2016 (111 NWs; NW-1 Prayagraj–Haldia 1,620 km; NW-2 Brahmaputra; NW-3 West Coast canal; NW-4 Godavari–Krishna; NW-5 Brahmani–Mahanadi delta); Central Water Commission; Composite Water Management Index (NITI); groundwater — CGWB (over-exploited blocks ~14%; Punjab-Haryana-Rajasthan-Delhi worst), Atal Jal; Ramsar sites 89 (2025) — TN most (20); largest Sundarbans; smallest Renuka; 2024 additions (Nagi & Nakti (Bihar), Tawa, Karaivetti, Longwood Shola; 2025 — Khichan, Menar (Rajasthan), Udhwa (Jharkhand), Gokul Jalashay & Therthangal/Sakkarakottai (TN)).

Traps

  • Longest river in India by length inside: Ganga (2,525); longest tributary: Yamuna; largest volume: Brahmaputra; longest peninsular: Godavari; largest west-flowing: Narmada.
  • Narmada/Tapi make estuaries, not deltas; Mahi crosses the Tropic of Cancer twice.
  • Kaveri's Kabini rises in Kerala (Wayanad), Bhavani in Nilgiris; Krishna's Bhima passes Pune-Solapur (Ujjani).
  • Kosi barrage is in Nepal (1954 treaty); Farakka on the Ganga feeds the Hooghly (1996 treaty till 2026).
  • Sutlej rises at Rakas Tal; Indus at Bokhar Chu; Brahmaputra at Angsi/Chemayungdung — all near Mansarovar-Kailash.

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

River-anchor flash.

Indus flash

  • IWT 1960 → Ravi-Beas-Sutlej = India · Indus-Jhelum-Chenab = Pakistan · broker = World Bank.
  • Jhelum source → Verinag · Sutlej entry → Shipki la · Indus rises near → Mansarovar (Tibet).

Ganga flash

  • Ganga born → Devprayag (Bhagirathi + Alaknanda) · largest trib → Yamuna (right bank).
  • Left-bank trio → Ghaghara, Gandak, Kosi (sorrow of Bihar) · Son = south-bank, from Amarkantak.
  • Distributary → Bhagirathi-Hooghly (Kolkata) · Tehri dam on Bhagirathi.

Brahmaputra flash

  • Tibet name → Tsangpo · India entry → Dihang (Arunachal) · Majuli = largest river island · Bangladesh name → Jamuna.

Peninsular flash

  • Largest peninsular → Godavari (Trimbakeshwar; Indravati trib) · Krishna → Mahabaleshwar (Tungabhadra-Bhima) · Kaveri → Talakaveri (Mettur; rice-bowl delta).
  • Rift-valley west-flowers → Narmada (Amarkantak; Sardar Sarovar; Jabalpur marble) + Tapi (Ukai) · Luni = Thar · Periyar = Kerala.
  • Chambal-Betwa-Ken → Yamuna system (ravines) · Damodar → once sorrow of Bengal (DVC 1948).

Dispute-dam flash

  • Dams → Hirakud (Mahanadi), Polavaram (Godavari), Nagarjuna Sagar (Krishna), Mettur (Kaveri), Sardar Sarovar (Narmada), Tehri (Bhagirathi), Bhakra (Sutlej), Ukai (Tapi).
  • Disputes → Cauvery (TN-KA), Krishna (4 states), Mahadayi (KA-GO-MH), SYL-Ravi-Beas (PB-HR-RJ) · frame → Art 262 + ISRWD Act 1956.
  • Interlink → Ken-Betwa first (Panna TR section).

Trap flash

  • Godavari basin = largest peninsular (not Krishna).
  • Narmada flows WEST (rift) — direction trap with Chambal (north into Yamuna).
  • Kosi = left-bank Ganga tributary (not Brahmaputra).
  • Indus rises in Tibet — enters India at Ladakh.
  • Cauvery: tribunal 2007 + SC 2018 — date-swap trap.
