Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Paper 1: the science core (foundations, cognition, personality, social psychology). Paper 2: applications (well-being, disorders, organisational, educational, community). Compact syllabus, experiment-friendly examples, and a direct GS-4 bridge — but the marking rewards textbook precision plus Indian case application.

7. How Paper 1 Foundations and Cognition Are Won: Experiments as Evidence

Psychology Paper 1 is won with named experiments — the candidate who cites design-plus-finding in two lines per study outscores the candidate who summarises conclusions, because the mark scheme credits empirical texture. The foundations block runs the paradigm relay: structuralism (Wundt's Leipzig laboratory, 1879 — introspection's elements) → functionalism (James — consciousness as adaptation) → behaviourism (Watson → Skinner — observable behaviour only; Pavlov's dogs — classical conditioning by contiguity; Skinner's boxes — operant shaping by reinforcement schedules) → the cognitive turn (Miller's magical number, Neisser's Cognitive Psychology, 1967 — mind as information processing) → biological–evolutionary–cultural syntheses. Research methods need experiment-anatomy precision: independent–dependent variables, random assignment, control versus experimental groups, correlation's causation limits (the third-variable and directionality problems — deployed on every \"screens cause depression\" headline), and ethics (informed consent, deception debriefing, Milgram–Zimbardo as the cautionary canon). Biological bases (neuron–synapse–neurotransmitter mechanics; localisation — Broca–Wernicke, the split-brain Sperry–Gazzaniga studies; endocrine links — HPA axis, cortisol) and perception (absolute–difference thresholds — Weber–Fechner; Gestalt laws — proximity, similarity, closure, figure–ground; attention bottlenecks — Broadbent's filter, Treisman's attenuation, Deutsch–Norman late selection — the cocktail-party phenomenon as the test case) complete the science floor.

The cognition block (learning, memory, thinking) is the most experiment-dense in the paper and must be learned as study-pairs. Learning: Pavlov (classical — UCS–UCR–CS–CR, extinction, generalisation, Little Albert's fear conditioning — Watson–Rayner 1920) versus Skinner (operant — reinforcement schedules — fixed/variable ratio/interval — shaping, the Premack principle) versus Bandura (observational — the Bobo doll studies — attention–retention–reproduction–motivation; self-efficacy as the applied bridge) versus Tolman (cognitive maps — latent learning, the rat-maze rebuke to strict behaviourism). Memory: the Atkinson–Shiffrin pipeline (sensory–STM–LTM) superseded by Baddeley's working memory (phonological loop, visuospatial sketchpad, central executive, episodic buffer); forgetting (Ebbinghaus's curve, interference, retrieval failure, motivated forgetting); reconstructive memory (Loftus — misinformation effect, lost-in-the-mall — deployed on eyewitness-testimony reform, the one-experiment-one-policy habit that marks toppers). Thinking: heuristics-and-biases (Kahneman–Tversky — availability, representativeness, anchoring — Thinking, Fast and Slow as the accessible spine; deployed on administrative decision-making — the GS-4 bridge), problem-solving (Newell–Simon, insight — Köhler's Sultan, functional fixedness — Duncker's candle), creativity (Guilford's divergent production, Torrance tests, Amabile's intrinsic-motivation principle). One model-box diagram per answer (memory pipeline, appraisal flow, conditioning paradigms) is the visual discipline — drawn, labelled, cited in text.

The failure mode to avoid from the first week is hallucinated studies: psychology marking punishes invented experiments more harshly than absent ones. Carry a finite experiment bank (thirty studies, each two lines — design plus finding), revise it weekly, and deploy only banked studies. Precision about what was actually shown — samples, controls, effect sizes where famous — is the difference between science and storytelling.

8. How Paper 1 Individual and Social Blocks Are Won

The individual block (motivation–emotion, personality, intelligence) runs as theory triads with a measurement tail. Motivation: drive theory (Hull — homeostasis) versus incentive versus humanistic (Maslow's hierarchy — physiological to self-actualisation, the pyramid every answer must be able to critique — Wahba–Bridwell's weak-evidence verdict, the cultural-bias objection) versus self-determination theory (Deci–Ryan — autonomy, competence, relatedness; intrinsic-versus-extrinsic — the overjustification effect — deployed on education and workplace design). Emotion: James–Lange (body first — \"we are afraid because we run\") versus Cannon–Bard (simultaneous arousal and experience — thalamic theory; the too-slow-viscera objection) versus Schachter–Singer (arousal plus cognitive label — the adrenaline experiment — deployed on crowd behaviour and misattribution-of-arousal effects: Dutton–Aron's suspension bridge) versus Lazarus (appraisal-first — primary–secondary evaluation — the stress-theory bridge to Paper 2). Personality: the trait line (Allport's cardinal–central–secondary; Cattell's 16PF; Eysenck's PEN — extraversion–neuroticism–psychoticism; the Big Five — OCEAN — Costa–McCrae, the cross-cultural replication verdict) versus Freud's structure (id–ego–superego, psychosexual stages, defence mechanisms — repression to sublimation) versus humanistic (Rogers — unconditional positive regard, the fully functioning person; Maslow — self-actualisers) versus social-cognitive (Bandura–Mischel — reciprocal determinism, the person–situation debate, CAPS). Intelligence: Spearman's g versus Thurstone's PMA versus Guilford's SOI versus Cattell–Horn fluid–crystallised versus Gardner (multiple intelligences — linguistic to bodily-kinesthetic, the education-deployment favourite) versus Sternberg (triarchic — analytic–creative–practical) versus emotional intelligence (Mayer–Salovey ability model, MSCEIT; Goleman's mixed model — the GS-4 EI syllabus in full, carried once and deployed twice) — with measurement (Binet–Simon to WAIS–Stanford-Binet; Flynn effect — three IQ points per decade; heritability — the 40–80 per cent range and its SES-moderation — Scarr–Rowe; culture-fair testing debates — the Indian adaptation literature) as the tail every intelligence answer must wag.

The social block is the paper's most quotable and most abused: attitudes (formation — mere exposure, conditioning, observational; persuasion — Yale–Hovland, ELM central–peripheral — Petty–Cacioppo; cognitive dissonance — Festinger's $1–$20 experiment — deployed on every post-decision-rationalisation question), social cognition (schemas, heuristics, attribution errors — fundamental attribution error — Jones–Harris; self-serving bias; actor–observer — deployed on administrator–citizen misperception), and groups (Asch conformity — line judgments, 37 per cent conformity rate; Milgram obedience — 65 per cent to 450 volts, the agency-theory explanation, the ethical firestorm; Zimbardo's Stanford prison — roles and deindividuation, the 2018 archival critique honestly noted; groupthink — Janis's Bay-of-Pigs symptoms and remedies; social facilitation–loafing — Triplett to Latané; leadership — trait, behavioural Ohio–Michigan, contingency — Fiedler, transformational — Bass). Language (Chomsky–Skinner — the poverty-of-stimulus versus reinforcement debate; Whorf; bilingualism effects) and the computer-applications unit (testing software, simulation, AI–psychology interfaces) close Paper 1. The social block's rule is that classic studies need year-plus-finding precision (Asch 1951, Milgram 1963, Zimbardo 1971, Festinger–Carlsmith 1959) — loose dating signals loose reading.

9. How Paper 2 Applied Psychology Is Won: Disorders, Work, and Indian Programmes

Paper 2's rule is absolute: theory without an Indian programme looks imported — every applied answer must land on a scheme, survey or institution. The well-being block runs stress–coping–health: Selye's General Adaptation Syndrome (alarm–resistance–exhaustion) versus Lazarus's transactional appraisal (primary–secondary–reappraisal — the model-box diagram to draw); coping (problem-focused versus emotion-focused — Folkman; hardiness — Kobasa; social support buffering); health psychology (Type A–B, psychoneuroimmunology, lifestyle interventions — the ICMR–NPCDCS NCD-prevention architecture as deployment); and positive psychology (Seligman's PERMA — positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, accomplishment; Fredrickson's broaden-and-build; the mindfulness evidence base — Kabat-Zinn MBSR, meta-analytic verdicts honestly qualified). The disorders block needs descriptive precision plus diathesis–stress causation: anxiety (GAD, panic, phobias, OCD — the working models), mood (major depression, bipolar I–II — Beck's cognitive triad, learned helplessness — Seligman–Maier, the serotonin-story revision honestly noted), schizophrenia spectrum (positive–negative symptoms, dopamine hypothesis to neurodevelopmental revision), personality disorders (clusters A–B–C, the dimensional DSM-5 Section III turn) — each with the Indian epidemiology anchor (NMHS 2015–16: 10.6 per cent adult prevalence, 70–92 per cent treatment gap; NCRB suicide series — 1.7 lakh in 2022, the student–farmer–daily-wager breakdowns; the Mental Healthcare Act 2017 — advance directives, nominated representatives, decriminalised attempt — Section 309 IPC effectively neutralised, now BNS continuity).

Therapies run as a ladder of evidence: CBT (Beck–Ellis — cognitive restructuring, behavioural experiments — the first-line verdict for anxiety–depression); behavioural (exposure hierarchies, systematic desensitisation — Wolpe; token economies); humanistic (Rogers — empathy–congruence–UPR, the common-factors verdict — Wampold); psychodynamic (transference, brief-psychodynamic evidence); mindfulness-based (MBCT for relapse prevention — Teasdale–Williams–Segal); rehabilitation and community care (halfway homes, day-care, the DMHP district architecture). The programme layer is the deployment: Tele-MANAS (October 2022 — 14416/1-800-891-4416 toll-free counselling, NIMHANS-nodal cells in every State); Manodarpan (education-ministry student counselling); workplace EAPs and the Shops-and-Establishments mental-health advisories; the community-deinstitutionalisation debate (Erwadi's 2001 fire as the horror-anchor, the Supreme Court's rehabilitation orders). Work–organisational psychology (selection — validity coefficients, structured interviews; training evaluation — Kirkpatrick's four levels; motivation — Herzberg two-factor, Vroom expectancy, Adams equity; leadership — the Paper 1 theories applied; culture — Schein's artefacts–espoused–assumptions; ergonomics; consumer behaviour — nudges, Thaler–Sunstein) lands on HRM practice and the gig-work stress literature. Educational (learning-styles myth-busting — Pashler's no-evidence verdict, deployed against the \"visual learner\" industry; giftedness — Terman–Renzulli; inclusive education — RTE–RPwD architecture; teacher effectiveness — Rosenthal–Jacobson Pygmalion) and community–environmental (crowding — Calhoun to Indian slum-density studies; noise; pro-environment behaviour — Cialdini norms, the Swachh Bharat behavioural-architecture case) plus the applied frontier (sports — Yerkes–Dodson, imagery; military selection — SSB's psychological battery; terrorism–deradicalisation — Moghaddam's staircase, the Indian surrender–rehabilitation policies; media — cultivation — Gerbner, social-media mental-health evidence) reward the candidate whose programme literacy matches theory command.

10. The Precision Strategy: Experiment Bank, Programme Literacy, and the GS-4 Bridge

Psychology's syllabus is compact, its marking strict, and its toppers distinguished by two finite assets: the experiment bank (thirty studies, two lines each — design plus finding — revised weekly, deployed one-per-answer, never hallucinated) and the programme sheet (one page per domain — mental health, education, work, community — each carrying the scheme, the survey figure and the Act, refreshed quarterly from NMHS/NCRB/NIMHANS releases). The answer formula from the host — definition → theory → classic experiment → Indian application → critique — should be enforced mechanically: the definition proves textbook command, the experiment proves empirical literacy, the Indian programme proves applied relevance, and the critique (one limitation, honestly stated — WEIRD samples, replication qualifications, the Rosenhan–Zimbardo archival revisions) proves scientific maturity. Diagrams (one model-box per answer — memory pipeline, appraisal flow, therapy ladder, Big Five radar) supply the visual texture examiners credit. The PYQ map shows the heavily taxed zones (learning–memory, motivation–emotion, personality–intelligence, attitudes–groups, stress–disorders–therapies) and the stable shapes (\"describe and evaluate,\" \"compare,\" \"apply to Indian context\") — preparation follows the map, and the twelve-week cadence (foundations, cognition, individual, social, Paper 2 well-being–disorders, work–education–community, PYQ-plus-bank) is realistic because the syllabus is genuinely compact.

The GS-4 bridge is the optional's compounding engine: attitude (formation–change–dissonance — the GS-4 attitude unit in full), emotional intelligence (Mayer–Salovey–Goleman — the GS-4 EI unit in full), values–morality (Kohlberg–Gilligan — the ethics-development spine), motivation–leadership (the governance-application vocabulary), and stress–resilience (the administrator-wellbeing frame) — five GS-4 units carried once in the optional and deployed twice. The Essay draws empathy–psychology prompts (happiness, resilience, crowd behaviour, media effects) and the Interview draws opinion-structuring (\"is social media harming youth?\" answered with evidence-hierarchies, not slogans). The failure modes, in order: hallucinated studies (the fatal one — invented findings destroy credibility); imported Paper 2 (Western theory with no Indian programme); critique-free answers (textbook summaries that never evaluate — the \"evaluate\" in the question unanswered); diagram poverty (model-boxes omitted where expected); and stale data (pre-2020 figures in a Tele-MANAS–Manodarpan world). Psychology rewards the candidate who treats it as a laboratory science with Indian field sites: the experiment named precisely, the programme named currently, the judgment stated scientifically.

Trap watch. Pavlov is classical conditioning (contiguity) and Skinner is operant (consequences) — Little Albert (Watson–Rayner 1920) is classical, the Bobo doll (Bandura 1961–63) is observational, not operant. James–Lange (body first) versus Cannon–Bard (simultaneous) versus Schachter–Singer (arousal-plus-label, 1962) versus Lazarus (appraisal first) — keep the emotion quartet ordered. The Big Five (OCEAN, Costa–McCrae) is the trait consensus; Eysenck's PEN and Cattell's 16PF are its predecessors. Gardner (multiple) and Sternberg (triarchic) are the anti-g pluralists; Spearman's g and Cattell–Horn fluid–crystallised defend the hierarchy. Asch (1951, 37%) is conformity and Milgram (1963, 65% to 450V) is obedience — rates and years travel with names. Festinger–Carlsmith ($1–$20, 1959) is dissonance; Dutton–Aron (1974, suspension bridge) is misattributed arousal; Loftus (misinformation) proves reconstruction. NMHS 2015–16 (10.6% prevalence, 70–92% treatment gap) is the epidemiology anchor; the Mental Healthcare Act is 2017; Tele-MANAS (October 2022, 14416) and Manodarpan are the programme pair. Pashler's verdict (no evidence for matching instruction to \"learning styles\") busts the visual-learner industry; Pygmalion (Rosenthal–Jacobson) is teacher-expectancy effects. The Mayer–Salovey ability model (MSCEIT) and Goleman's mixed model are distinct EI theories — the GS-4 EI unit needs both, kept separate.

Paper 1 — pattern questions

Q1

Distinguish top-down and bottom-up processing with perceptual examples. (10 marks)

Approach: define data-driven vs concept-driven → examples: reading degraded text (context fill — top-down) vs feature detection in novel stimuli (bottom-up) → interactionist evidence (word-superiority effect) → diagram the dual arrows.

Q2

Explain Baddeley's working-memory model and its evidence. (15 marks)

Approach: components — central executive, phonological loop, visuospatial sketchpad, episodic buffer → evidence: dual-task interference, phonological-similarity effect, word-length effect → upgrade from multistore account → applications: arithmetic, comprehension.

Q3

Compare James-Lange, Cannon-Bard and Schachter-Singer theories of emotion. (15 marks)

Approach: sequence diagrams (body→feeling; parallel; arousal+label) → supporting evidence each (spinal-cord cases; thalamic role; epinephrine experiment) → appraisal addition (Lazarus) → verdict: multi-route contemporary synthesis.

Q4

Evaluate the Big-Five against Freudian personality theory. (10 marks)

Approach: traits — dimensional, cross-cultural stability, psychometric base vs Freud — dynamic, developmental, defence mechanisms (clinical richness, low testability) → bridges: attachment research revives early-experience claims → use-case split: prediction vs depth-explanation.

Q5

How do Milgram's findings translate to bureaucratic wrongdoing? (10 marks)

Approach: design+finding (65% to 450V under authority) → agentic-state reading → moderators (proximity, dissent-peers, legitimate institution) → GS-4 translation: tone-at-top, note-making culture, whistle-blower protection as experimental remedies.

Paper 2 — pattern questions

Q6

Explain Lazarus's cognitive-appraisal model of stress with a workplace case. (10 marks)

Approach: primary (threat/challenge) + secondary (resource check) appraisal → reappraisal route → case: merger announcement — threat-framing vs opportunity-framing outcomes → intervention: cognitive restructuring + EAPs.

Q7

Outline CBT's logic in treating depression; evidence of efficacy? (15 marks)

Approach: cognitive-triad → automatic-thoughts → schema level → technique ladder (activity scheduling → thought records → behavioural experiments) → evidence: equal-to-pharmacotherapy in moderate cases, relapse-edge (metanalysis line) → Indian access angle: digital CBT, Manodarpan counselling lattice.

Q8

Discuss psychological contributions to deradicalisation. (10 marks)

Approach: radicalisation path (identity-quest, grievance, network pull) → counters: EXIT-style programmes (mentoring, family involvement), cognitive-opening, dignity-economic ladders → ethics: persuasion vs manipulation boundary → evaluation caution: small-N evidence.

Q9

How can nudges and norms design pro-environment behaviour? (10 marks)

Approach: social-norm feedback (opower-style home-energy reports), defaults (green tariff), commitment+public pledge → Indian cases: LED UJALA diffusion, Swachh Bharat social-proof messaging → limits: reactance, rebound effects → design principles.

How to practise

  • Map 8 years of papers: hot nodes — memory, emotion, personality, attitudes (P1); stress, therapy, IO, community (P2).
  • Weekly: 3 answers with mandatory named-experiment + one diagram; maintain the 20-experiment bank.
