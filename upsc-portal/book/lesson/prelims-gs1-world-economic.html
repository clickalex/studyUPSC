Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Agriculture types, resource belts, industry regions, trade arteries — the human-economy map.

1. Agricultural systems

  • Types: subsistence-intensive (monsoon Asia), plantation (equatorial: tea-India-Sri Lanka-Kenya, coffee-Brazil-Colombia, rubber-SE Asia, cocoa-Ivory Coast), Mediterranean (citrus-olives-vines: Spain-Italy-Greece-California-Chile-Cape), commercial grain (US-Canada prairies, Argentina pampas, Australia wheat belt), dairy (NW Europe, Denmark-Netherlands, NZ), mixed farming, shifting (Amazon-Congo-NE India jhum), ranching (US-Brazil-Australia), viticulture zones.
  • Food-security geography: breadbaskets (Ukraine 'granary', US Midwest), rice bowls (Mekong delta-Thailand-Vietnam), palm (Indonesia-Malaysia ~85%), soy (Brazil-US-Argentina).

2. Mineral-energy belts

  • Oil-Gas: Persian Gulf (~48% proven reserves; Saudi-Iran-Iraq-Kuwait-UAE), Russia-Caspian, Venezuela (Orinoco), shale revolution (US), LNG-Qatar-Australia; OPEC+ (1960 Baghdad founding; OPEC=13, OPEC+ adds Russia etc).
  • Hard minerals: iron (Australia-Brazil exports; China biggest consumer), copper (Chile-Peru-DRC-Zambia), lithium ('lithium triangle' Chile-Argentina-Bolivia + Australia; DRC? — cobalt is DRC), cobalt (DRC ~70%), nickel (Indonesia-Philippines), rare-earths (China dominant; MP Materials-US revival), uranium (Kazakhstan-Canada-Australia), diamonds (Botswana-Russia-Canada).

3. Industry regions & trade

  • Industrial regions: N-American manufacturing belt (Great Lakes), European axis (UK midlands-Ruhr-N Italy), East Asian (Japan Pacific belt, Korea SE coast, China coastal SEZs), emerging (India corridors, SE Asia-Vietnam, Mexico nearshoring).
  • Trade arteries: Suez (~12-15% global trade; 2021 Ever Given; 2024 Houthi rerouting), Malacca (busiest strait), Hormuz (~20% oil), Panama (drought limits 2023-24), Bab-el-Mandeb, Danish straits; ports: Shanghai #1, Singapore #2 transhipment; container alliances (2M-Ocean), India: JNPT-Mundra pair.
  • Economic blocs: EU single market, USMCA, RCEP (world's largest — without India), AfCFTA (2021 trading start), GCC; development divide: Global North-South, G20-IMF-WB frames, chokepoint risk era (Red Sea crisis).

MCQ hooks

  • Persian Gulf = ~48% oil reserves; OPEC founded 1960 Baghdad.
  • Lithium triangle = Chile-Argentina-Bolivia; cobalt = DRC; rare earths = China.
  • Mediterranean agriculture = citrus-vine-olive (winter rain).
  • Suez bypass (Cape) adds ~10-14 days; Hormuz = 20% oil flow.
  • Shanghai = world #1 container port; Singapore = transhipment hub.
  • RCEP = largest trade bloc by GDP-pop (India outside).

Traps

  • Producer ≠ exporter: Kenya exports most tea (India/China consume); Indonesia exports most coal (China produces most); Russia exports most wheat (China grows most); Vietnam exports 2nd coffee but Brazil grows most; India exports most rice but China grows most.
  • Reserves ≠ production: Venezuela (oil reserves) vs USA (production); Bolivia (lithium reserves) vs Australia (production); Guinea (bauxite reserves) vs Australia (production); Australia (uranium reserves) vs Kazakhstan (production).
  • Largest diaspora India (18.5 mn); largest remittance recipient India ($129 bn 2024); largest migrant host USA; largest refugee host now Iran; largest displacement crisis Sudan.
  • China's population declining (2022 onward); India overtook in April 2023 (UN estimate). South Korea lowest TFR; Niger highest.

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

Belt-anchor flash.

Agriculture flash

  • Plantation → tea (India-Sri Lanka-Kenya), coffee (Brazil-Colombia), rubber (SE Asia), cocoa (Ivory Coast) · Mediterranean → citrus-olive-vine, winter rain.
  • Dairy → NW Europe-NZ · grain → prairies-pampas-Australia · palm → Indonesia-Malaysia (~85%) · jhum → Amazon-Congo-NE India.

Mineral flash

  • Oil reserves → Persian Gulf ~48% · OPEC 1960 Baghdad (13 members; OPEC+ with Russia).
  • Copper → Chile-Peru · cobalt → DRC · lithium triangle → Chile-Argentina-Bolivia (+Australia) · nickel → Indonesia.
  • Rare earths → China dominance · uranium → Kazakhstan · iron exports → Australia-Brazil.

Industry-trade flash

  • Regions → Great Lakes belt, Ruhr-Rhine, Japan Pacific, China SEZ coast, Vietnam-Mexico rise.
  • Arteries → Suez (~12-15% trade; 2021 Ever Given; 2024 reroute), Malacca busiest, Hormuz 20% oil, Panama drought caps.
  • Ports → Shanghai #1, Singapore #2 · blocs → EU, USMCA, RCEP (India out), AfCFTA.

Trap flash

  • Mediterranean agriculture = WINTER rain (not summer) — Koppen Cs link.
  • DRC = cobalt king; lithium is the triangle — metal-country swap trap.
  • Suez = sea-level canal (no locks) vs Panama locks.
  • India is OUT of RCEP but IN in IPEF partially — membership traps.
  • OPEC ≠ OPEC+ (Russia et al join the +).
