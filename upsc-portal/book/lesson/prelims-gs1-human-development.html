Human Development, Poverty & Employment

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

HDI family + India's poverty-line evolution + welfare architecture. Index-publisher-value recall.

1. The UNDP family

  • HDI (1990; Mahbub ul Haq-Amartya Sen lineage): geometric mean of health (life expectancy), education (mean + expected years of schooling), income (GNI-pc PPP, log). IHDI = inequality-discounted; GII = reproductive health + empowerment + labour market; GDI = female/male HDI ratio; MPI (2010, UNDP + OPHI, Alkire-Foster) = 3 dimensions x 10 indicators; PHDI (2020+) = planet-pressure-adjusted.
  • India: medium-human-development band; rank ~130/193 (2025 report); policy-note: SL-Bhutan above India in recent reports — comparative line.

2. India's poverty-line evolution

  • Alagh (1979) → Lakdawala (1993) → Tendulkar (2009 report; 2011-12 base: 21.9% poor; added education-health, MRP consumption) → Rangarajan (2014; ~29.5%) → MPI era: NITI MPI: 24.85% (NFHS-4 2015-16) → 14.96% (NFHS-5 2019-21); NITI 2023 discussion-paper extrapolation ~11%. World Bank extreme line $2.15/day (2017 PPP) — international-poverty-line claims contested.
  • Method note: calorie anchors 2400/2100 kcal (rural-urban) legacy; capability approach (Sen); multidimensional counting.

3. Welfare architecture

  • NFSA 2013: 67% coverage (75% rural + 50% urban); PHH 5 kg/person/month; AAY 35 kg/household; ONORC (2019) ration portability; ePOS-TPDS digitisation; PMGKAY free-food phase (2020+).
  • Targeting stack: SECC 2011 (deprivation scoring), PMJDY-DBT (~₹44 lakh crore cumulative band), Ujjwala-PMAY-pensions (NSAP: IGNOAPS-IGNWPS-IGNDPS), MGNREGA indexation.

MCQ hooks

  • HDI = life + schooling + GNI-pc (not GDP); geometric-mean aggregation.
  • MPI = 2010, UNDP+OPHI; India MPI = NITI (NFHS data).
  • Tendulkar 2009 report / 2011-12 prices = 21.9%; Rangarajan 2014 ~29.5%.
  • NITI MPI 24.85 → 14.96% (NFHS-4→5).
  • NFSA = 67%; PHH 5 kg; AAY 35 kg/hh; ONORC 2019.
  • PHDI = planet-adjusted; GDI = f/m ratio — index-name precision.
