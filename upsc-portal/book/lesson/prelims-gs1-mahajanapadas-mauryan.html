Lesson 1.1.13

Mahajanapadas, Buddhism, Jainism & Mauryan Empire

In this lessonDetailed StudyRevision DigestKey Points & MindmapDiagrams, Maps & FlowchartsPrevious Year Questions

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

The making of the first empire: 16 states → Magadha → Nandas → Maurya → Ashoka's dhamma. UPSC loves ruler→measure and edict→place pairs.

1. The 16 Mahajanapadas (6th c. BCE)

  • Source: Anguttara Nikaya (Buddhist) + Bhagavati Sutra (Jain list varies). Monarchies (Magadha, Kosala, Kashi, Vatsa...) vs ganasanghas/republics (Vajji — Licchavis of Vaishali, Malla, Shakyas of Kapilavastu).
  • Why Magadha wins: iron ore (Jharkhand belts), fertile Ganga valley, elephants, riverine trade, ambitious rulers, capitals Rajagriha → Pataliputra (Udayin — confluence point).

2. Magadhan rise

DynastyKey rulersNotes
HaryankaBimbisara (contemporary of Buddha/Mahavira — matrimony with Kosala/Lichchavi), Ajatashatru (Vaisali war; war-engines mahashilakantaka, rathamushala), Udayin (founds Pataliputra)c. 544–412 BCE
ShishunagaShishunaga (destroys Avanti power), Kalashoka (2nd Buddhist Council, Vaishali 383 BCE)c. 412–345
NandaMahapadma ('second Parashurama', destroyer of Kshatriyas), Dhana Nanda (Alexander's era; unpopular taxation)c. 345–322

3. Alexander (326 BCE)

  • Crossed Indus 326; Hydaspes (Jhelum) battle vs Porus (Puru) — impressed, restored him; army mutinied at Beas (Hyphasis); retreat via Makran; died 323 Babylon. Effects: trade routes, Greek colonies, Seleucus successor.

4. Mauryan Empire (c. 322–185 BCE)

  • Chandragupta (with Kautilya — Arthashastra): 305 Seleucus pact — marriage alliance, Arachosia/Kandahar ceded, Megasthenes ambassador (Indica). Jain tradition: became Bhadrabahu follower, died at Shravanabelagola.
  • Bindusara: 'Amitraghata'; Deccan up to Mysore (except Kalinga); Ajivika leanings.
  • Ashoka (268–232): Kalinga 261 (13th Rock Edict — remorse, dhamma-vijaya instead of bheri-ghosha); officials: dhamma mahamattas (14th year); tours; edicts in Prakrit (Brahmi script — most; Kharoshthi NW: Shahbazgarhi, Mansehra; bilingual Greek-Aramaic: Kandahar); 14 Major Rock Edicts (Kalsi, Girnar, Dhauli, Jaugada...), 7 Pillar Edicts (Delhi-Topra, Delhi-Meerut, Lauriya-Araraj...); Sarnath lion capital = national emblem; chakra = flag wheel.
  • Administration: saptanga (7 limbs), 18 tirthas (mantrin top), provinces at Ujjain, Taxila, Tosali, Suvarnagiri (+Pataliputra); army per Megasthenes (6,00,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry, 9,000 elephants); revenue: bhaga = 1/6, sita lands, punch-marked silver panas (karshapana family); espionage (gudhapurusha), urban officials (nagaraka, 30-member? committees per Arthashastra).
  • Art: monolithic polished pillars, stupas (Sanchi core), Barabar caves (Lomas Rishi — first), Yaksha-Yakshini sculpture (Didarganj).
  • Decline: 185 Pushyamitra (Brahmanical reaction + economic-fiscal + succession readings).

MCQ hooks

  • 2nd Buddhist Council = Vaishali, Kalashoka's time (1st = Rajgriha, Ajatashatru).
  • Kharoshthi edicts = only Shahbazgarhi & Mansehra; rest Brahmi.
  • Bilingual (Greek-Aramaic) = Kandahar only.
  • Tosali = eastern province (Kalinga); Suvarnagiri = southern.
  • 'Devanam Piya' = beloved of gods (Ashoka's title; named only in Maski, Gujarra, Nettur, Udegolam — Minor REs).
  • Megasthenes = Indica (fragments via Strabo/Arrian); Deimachos (Bindusara, Antiochus I), Dionysius (Ashoka, sent to Ptolemy II of Egypt).

Revision Digest

Condensed for the last mile before the exam.

Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Ancient History. Heaviest-weighted ancient block: religion facts + Ashokan edicts + Mauryan administration.

Sixteen Mahajanapadas (c. 6th BCE; Anguttara Nikaya, Bhagavati Sutra)

  • Monarchies: Magadha (Rajgir/Girivraja → Pataliputra), Kosala (Shravasti; Prasenajit), Vatsa (Kaushambi; Udayana), Avanti (Ujjain/Mahishmati; Pradyota), Anga (Champa), Kashi (Varanasi), Chedi (Shuktimati), Matsya (Viratnagar), Shurasena (Mathura), Ashmaka (Potali/Paudanya — only one south of Vindhyas, Godavari), Gandhara (Taxila), Kamboja (Rajpur/Hatak).
  • Republics (gana-sanghas): Vajji/Vrijji (Vaishali; Licchavis), Malla (Kusinara & Pava), Kuru (Indraprastha), Panchala (Ahichchhatra & Kampilya).
  • Magadha's rise: Haryanka (Bimbisara — Gandhaka army, matrimonial; Ajatashatru — war engines rathamusala, mahashilakantaka; Vaishali conquest; Udayin founded Pataliputra) → Shishunaga (Avanti annexed; Vaishali capital) → Nandas (Mahapadma — ekarat, sarvakshatrantaka; Dhana Nanda vs Alexander's time) → Mauryas.
  • Persian: Cyrus, Darius I 516 BCE (Gandhara 20th satrapy; Kharosthi script origin). Alexander 326 BCE — Hydaspes (Jhelum) vs Porus; returned from Beas; died Babylon 323.

Buddhism

  • Siddhartha b. Lumbini 563 BCE (Shakya; Suddhodana, Mahamaya; Gautami); Mahabhinishkramana 29; enlightenment Bodh Gaya (Uruvela) at 35; first sermon Sarnath — Dhammachakrapravartana (Panchavargiya); Mahaparinirvana Kusinara 483 BCE (Malla); symbols: lotus/bull (birth), horse (renunciation), bodhi tree, wheel, stupa.
  • Doctrine: Four Noble Truths, Eightfold Path (Ashtangika marga), Middle Path, Pratityasamutpada, anatta, anicca, dukkha; Three Jewels; Nirvana; no soul/god.
  • Councils: I Rajgir 483 (Mahakassapa; Ajatashatru; Vinaya-Upali, Sutta-Ananda) · II Vaishali 383 (Sabakami; Kalashoka; Sthaviravadins vs Mahasanghikas) · III Pataliputra 250 (Moggaliputta Tissa; Ashoka; Abhidhamma; Kathavatthu) · IV Kundalvana, Kashmir 1st c. CE (Vasumitra/Ashvaghosha; Kanishka; Hinayana–Mahayana split; Mahavibhasha).
  • Texts: Tripitaka (Pali) — Vinaya, Sutta (5 Nikayas incl. Jataka in Khuddaka), Abhidhamma; Milindapanho (Nagasena–Menander); Buddhacharita (Ashvaghosha); Mahayana — Nagarjuna (Madhyamika, shunyata), Asanga–Vasubandhu (Yogachara); Vajrayana (Tantric; Bihar-Bengal, Palas). Women admitted at Vaishali (Gautami; Ananda's plea).

Jainism

  • 24 Tirthankaras; Rishabhanatha (bull) first; Parshvanatha (23rd; 4 vows; snake; Varanasi); Mahavira (24th; b. Kundagrama/Vaishali 540 BCE; Siddhartha & Trishala (Licchavi); kaivalya at Jrimbhikagrama, river Rijupalika, sal tree, age 42; Jina/Nirgrantha; died Pavapuri 468 BCE, age 72; lion symbol).
  • Five vows (Mahavira added brahmacharya); triratna — right faith, knowledge, conduct; anekantavada/syadvada (sapta-bhangi); jiva in everything; extreme ahimsa; sallekhana; no creator god, but souls & gods exist.
  • Councils: I Pataliputra c. 300 BCE (Sthulabhadra; 12 Angas compiled; Bhadrabahu to south with Chandragupta → Shvetambara–Digambara split) · II Vallabhi 512 CE (Devardhi Kshamashramana; final canon in Ardha-Magadhi Prakrit).
  • Patrons: Bimbisara, Ajatashatru, Chandragupta Maurya, Kharavela (Hathigumpha; Kalinga Jina), Chalukya Kumarapala, Amoghavarsha; Shravanabelagola (Gomateshwara/Bahubali 983 CE, Ganga minister Chamundaraya).

Mauryan Empire (321–185 BCE)

  • Chandragupta (321–297): Nanda overthrow (Chanakya/Kautilya Arthashastra, Vishakhadatta Mudrarakshasa), Seleucus 305 (Herat, Kandahar, Kabul, Makran; 500 elephants; Megasthenes Indica), Jain, died Shravanabelagola. Bindusara (Amitraghata; Deimachus; Ajivika). Ashoka (268/273–232): Kalinga 261 BCE (8th year), Dhamma, Dhamma-mahamattas (14th year), missions (Mahinda & Sanghamitta to Sri Lanka — Tissa; Yavana kings named in RE XIII: Antiochus, Ptolemy, Antigonus, Magas, Alexander), Devanampiya Piyadasi; name "Ashoka" in Maski, Gujarra, Nittur, Udegolam minor rock edicts.
  • Edicts: 14 Major Rock Edicts (Girnar, Kalsi, Shahbazgarhi & Mansehra (Kharosthi), Dhauli & Jaugada (separate Kalinga edicts; no RE XI–XIII), Sopara, Yerragudi, Sannati); 7 Pillar Edicts (Topra/Delhi has all 7; Lauriya-Nandangarh, Lauriya-Araraj, Rampurva, Allahabad/Kaushambi (Queen's edict; Schism), Sanchi, Sarnath); Bhabru/Bairat (Buddhist texts); Lumbini/Rummindei (tax reduced to 1/8; bali exempt); Nigali Sagar (Konakamana stupa); Kandahar bilingual Greek–Aramaic; Lampaka Aramaic. Brahmi deciphered James Prinsep 1837.
  • Administration (Arthashastra + Megasthenes): Saptanga state; Mantriparishad; tirthas (18 high officials); provinces (Taxila, Ujjain, Tosali, Suvarnagiri; kumaras); samaharta (revenue), sannidhata (treasury), rajukas (rural justice, Ashoka), yuktas, pradeshikas; espionage (sanstha, sanchara); Megasthenes' 30-member city council (6 boards of 5); 7 castes (Megasthenes; erroneous); sita (crown land), bhaga 1/6.
  • Art: Sarnath lion capital (national emblem), Rampurva bull, Sankisa elephant, Vaishali single lion; polished Chunar sandstone; Barabar & Nagarjuni caves (Lomas Rishi; Ajivikas); Sanchi stupa core; Didarganj Yakshi (Mauryan polish); Dhauli rock elephant; woodwork palace (Kumrahar 80-pillared hall).
  • Decline 185 BCE: Pushyamitra Shunga killed Brihadratha; theories — brahmanical reaction (Haraprasad Sastri), financial crisis (D.D. Kosambi), pacifism (H.C. Raychaudhuri), weak successors, centralised structure (Romila Thapar).

Traps

  • Mahavira died at Pavapuri, Buddha at Kusinara; both were Kshatriya princes.
  • Third Buddhist council = Ashoka; fourth = Kanishka; Jain Vallabhi council = 512 CE.
  • Kalinga war in the 8th regnal year; Ashoka's personal name appears in Maski-type minor edicts, not the major ones.

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

Pair-recall sheet.

Pairs flash

  • 16 list source → Anguttara Nikaya · republics → Vajji, Malla, Shakya.
  • Pataliputra founder → Udayin · siege machines → Ajatashatru.
  • 2nd Council → Vaishali, Kalashoka · Nanda epithet → 'second Parashurama' (Mahapadma).
  • Alexander's battle → Hydaspes/Jhelum × Porus · turn-back → Beas.
  • Arthashastra → Kautilya · Indica → Megasthenes · Seleucus treaty → 305 BCE.
  • Kalinga war → 261 BCE, 13th RE · dhamma officials → mahamattas.
  • Provinces → Ujjain, Taxila, Tosali, Suvarnagiri · script NW → Kharoshthi.
  • Named Ashoka edicts → Maski, Gujarra, Nettur, Udegolam · bilingual → Kandahar.
  • Lion capital → Sarnath · first caves → Barabar (Lomas Rishi) · Didarganj → Yakshini.
  • Tax → bhaga 1/6 · coin → karshapana/pana · spy → gudhapurusha · city head → nagaraka.

Order flash

  • Haryanka → Shishunaga → Nanda → Maurya (dynasties of Magadha).
  • Maurya line: Chandragupta → Bindusara → Ashoka → (Dasaratha, Samprati...) → Brihadratha (killed by Pushyamitra Sunga, 185 BCE).

Trap flash

  • Ashoka in Buddhist texts only (Divyavadana, Ashokavadana, Mahavamsa) — name absent from his own major edicts.
  • Kalinga = 13th RE content, but Kalinga edicts = separate Dhauli/Jaugada RE set (RE 11-13 replaced).
  • Sanchi stupa core = Ashoka; railing/doors later (Sungas/Satavahanas).
  • Punch-marked coins pre-Mauryan (janapadas) — not invented by Mauryas.
