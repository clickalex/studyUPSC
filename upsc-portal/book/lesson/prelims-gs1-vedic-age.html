Lesson 1.1.12

Vedic Age & Later Vedic Period

In this lessonDetailed StudyRevision DigestKey Points & MindmapDiagrams, Maps & FlowchartsPrevious Year Questions

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Two Vedic ages, one text ladder. UPSC tests text→content, god→period, term→meaning.

1. Early Vedic / Rigvedic (c. 1500–1000 BCE)

  • Homeland: Sapta-Sindhu (Indus + Saraswati belt). Rigveda's oldest mandalas = II–VII (family books); X (Purusha sukta — varna origin).
  • Economy: pastoral — cow = wealth unit (go-dhana); godhuli = distance; agriculture minor; nishka = gold ornament (later coin-name).
  • Polity: tribe (jana) based; raja = war-leader elected-ish; sabha & samiti check king; officers: senani, gramani, purohita; bali voluntary tribute (no regular tax yet).
  • Society: dasa varna (early class colour), women visible (Lopamudra, Ghosha, Apala; no child marriage, widow could remarry).
  • Religion: personified nature — Indra (~250 hymns, war/rain), Varuna (rita), Agni (messenger), Soma; worship = hymn + sacrifice, no temples/idols.

2. Later Vedic (c. 1000–600 BCE)

  • Texts: Yajur, Sama, Atharva + Brahmanas + Aranyakas + early Upanishads; geography pushes east ( Videha, Kuru-Panchala).
  • Economy: iron (shyama ayas / krishna ayas) → plough, rice-wheat; occupations hereditary; nishka/satamana as value units.
  • Polity: janapadas; hereditary kingship; grand rites — rajasuya (consecration), vajapeya (chariot race), ashvamedha (imperial claim); sabha declines to elite council.
  • Society: varna hardens (Brahmana–Kshatriya–Vaishya–Shudra); 4 ashramas codified (Jabala Upanishad); women's status drops (no early-Vedic participation).
  • Religion: Prajapati (creator) tops; Rudra, Vishnu rise; sacrifice becomes mechanical (Brahmana literature = rule-books).
  • Reaction: Upanishads (c. 800–500 BCE) — atmabrahma monism (Yajnavalkya–Maitreyi dialogues).

MCQ hooks

  • Gayatri mantra = RV 3.62.10 (the Savitri; composed in the gayatri meter).
  • 'Ayas' = metal generally in RV; iron specifically appears later Vedic.
  • Upaveda pairs: Ayurveda–Rig, Dhanurveda–Yajur, Gandharvaveda–Sama, Shilpaveda–Atharva.
  • Purusha sukta (RV X) = first varna statement; Vaishya term appears first here.
  • Atharvaveda = folk beliefs, medicine, magic — 'Veda of magical formulae'.
  • Uttaramerur ≠ Vedic (that's Chola); Vedic assemblies = sabha/samiti/vidatha/gana.

Revision Digest

Condensed for the last mile before the exam.

Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Ancient History. Text-classification and social-term questions dominate.

Sources & chronology

  • Early/Rig Vedic c. 1500–1000 BCE (Sapta Sindhu — Punjab, Afghanistan); Later Vedic c. 1000–600 BCE (Ganga-Yamuna doab; Kuru-Panchala); archaeology: Painted Grey Ware (PGW) = later Vedic; iron (shyama/krishna ayas) from ~1000 BCE (Atranjikhera).
  • Boghaz-Koi (Hittite–Mitanni) inscription 1400 BCE — Indra, Mitra, Varuna, Nasatya.

  • Six darshanas (later): Samkhya (Kapila), Yoga (Patanjali), Nyaya (Gautama), Vaisheshika (Kanada), Mimamsa (Jaimini), Vedanta (Badarayana).

Rig Vedic society & polity

  • Tribal (jana), rajan not hereditary rigidly; assemblies sabha, samiti, vidatha, gana; purohita (Vasishtha, Vishvamitra), senani; bali voluntary; Dasarajna — Battle of Ten Kings (Sudas of Bharatas on the Parushni/Ravi).
  • Pastoral: cow = wealth (gavishti = war), gomat; barley (yava); horse chariot; rivers: Sindhu, Vitasta (Jhelum), Asikni (Chenab), Parushni (Ravi), Vipas (Beas), Shutudri (Sutlej), Sarasvati (most sacred), Drishadvati, Kubha (Kabul), Gomati (Gomal); Ganga once, Yamuna thrice.
  • Gods: Indra (250 hymns; Purandara), Agni (200), Varuna (rita), Soma, Surya, Savitri, Pushan, Ushas, Aditi (goddesses minor); no temples/idols.
  • Women: Apala, Ghosha, Lopamudra, Vishvavara — hymn composers; sabha attendance; no child marriage; widow remarriage/niyoga.
  • Varna: Purusha Sukta (10th mandala) — first mention of four varnas.

Later Vedic changes

  • Territorial kingdoms (rashtra; janapada), hereditary kingship, grand sacrifices rajasuya, vajapeya, ashvamedha; titles samrat, ekrat; ratnins (12 jewels); sabha-samiti decline, vidatha disappears; bali compulsory, bhaga, shulka.
  • Agriculture central (rice vrihi, wheat godhuma); varna rigid; gotra appears; four ashramas (Jabala Upanishad first); women's status falls (Aitareya — daughter a misery; but Gargi & Maitreyi in Brihadaranyaka); Shatapatha Brahmana — Videgha Mathava's eastward move; Prajapati, Vishnu, Rudra rise; Pushan becomes Shudra god.
  • Upanishads (108 traditional; 13 principal) — atman-brahman, karma, moksha; tat tvam asi (Chandogya), aham brahmasmi (Brihadaranyaka), Satyameva Jayate (Mundaka).

Traps

  • Gayatri is in the Rig (3rd mandala), addressed to Savitri; Nasadiya & Purusha Sukta in the 10th.
  • Atharva is outside the trayi; Shatapatha is a Yajur Brahmana; Chandogya is Sama.
  • Iron appears in the later Vedic age (PGW), not Rig Vedic (which had copper/bronze — ayas).

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

Fact pairs for last-hour revision.

Pairs flash

  • Oldest Veda → Rigveda · family mandalas → II–VII · latest → X (Purusha sukta).
  • Iron → shyama ayas · plough → sira/ langala · god-of-rain-early → Indra.
  • Sacrifices → rajasuya (coronation), vajapeya (race), ashvamedha (empire).
  • Tax early → bali (voluntary) · officer → senani/gramani/purohita.
  • Brahmana pairs → Aitareya-Rig · Satapatha-Yajur · Tandya/Panchavimsha-Sama · Gopatha-Atharva.
  • Upanishad count → 108 (Muktika); principal → 11-13; Brihadaranyaka = largest.
  • Women RV seers → Lopamudra, Ghosha, Apala, Vishwavara.
  • Upaveda → Ayur/Dhanur/Gandharva/Shilpa × Rig/Yajur/Sama/Atharva.
  • Soma = plant juice oblation · Yajna = fire ritual · Rita = cosmic order (Varuna).
  • Kuru-Panchala = Later-Vedic heartland (Brahmana literature zone).

Early → Later shift (6 keywords)

  • Pastoral → agrarian-iron · tribe → janapada · elected-ish → hereditary · sabha strong → elite · fluid varna → rigid · nature-gods → Prajapati-ritualism.

Trap flash

  • No temples/idols in Vedic worship (idol-yajna = later Puranic).
  • 'Dasa varna' ≠ caste system yet (Arya vs Dasa, colour-based).
  • Ashrama-4 codified in Upanishad era, not Rigvedic.
  • Sita (furrow) term later; Vedic taxes = bali (offering), bhaga (king's share).
