Mughal Empire (1526–1707)

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

1526–1857. Six greats + later decline. Exam anchors: battles, reforms (mansab-zabt), culture outputs, traveller accounts, decline mechanics.

1. The Great Mughals

2. Later Mughals & decline mechanics

  • Bahadur Shah I (civil-war ender), Jahandar-Farrukhsiyar (sayyid brothers), Muhammad Shah 'Rangeela' (Nadir Shah 1739 — Delhi sack, Battle of Karnal, Delhi sack, Peacock Throne + Koh-i-noor carried off), Ahmad Shah ( Abdali invasions begin), Alam II (Buxar 1764 shadow rule; EIC pension), Akbar II, Bahadur Shah 'Zafar' II (1857 figurehead → Rangoon exile).
  • Decline motors: jagir crisis, mansab inflation, Deccan drain, successor wars, regional powers (Nizam, Bengal, Awadh, Marathas, Sikhs, Rohillas), foreign raids (Nadir, Abdali — Panipat-3 1761 Marathas vs Abdali), EIC fiscal capture (Plassey 1757, Buxar 1764, Diwani 1765).

3. Economy, society, culture

  • Zabti standard; dahsala base; kharif-rabi crops; karkhana state workshops; silver rupiya (Sher Shah lineage); rahdari (transit) vs zam (land) tax heads; Thomas Roe → Surat factory 1613 trade seeds.
  • Society: caste-panchayat continuities; sati-practice noted by European travellers; women: Nur Jahan exception, harem administration, zanana economy; slavery limited courtly.
  • Culture: Persian chronicle tradition (Abul Fazl, Lahori's Badshahnama), Braj-Bhasha court verse (Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khanan), Sikh canon completion 1708, Rajput miniatures + Mughal atelier; architecture: charbagh apex, pietra dura, double dome, red sandstone → marble transition; music: Tansen line, qawwali formalisation, sitar-sarod genealogies; travellers: Roe (EIC), Tavernier-Bernier-Manucci-Thevenot (17th c economy detail), Peter Mundy (famine).

MCQ hooks

  • Battle triple: Panipat-1 1526, Khanwa 1527, Chausa-Kannauj 1539-40 → Sher Shah interregnum.
  • Din-i-Ilahi 1582 = Akbar; sulh-i-kul policy; Ibadat Khana 1575 Fatehpur.
  • Mansab zat-sawar + dahsala (Todarmal) = Akbar twins.
  • Nur Jahan junta under Jahangir; Mansur = his painter; Roe = his embassy.
  • Nadir Shah 1739 = Peacock Throne + Koh-i-noor; Abdali = Panipat-3 1761.
  • Buxar 1764 + Diwani 1765 = EIC fiscal capture of a fading Alam II.

Revision Digest

Condensed for the last mile before the exam.

Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Medieval History. Battles, buildings, books and revenue terms — the four B-R pillars.

Rulers & battles

  • Babur (1526–30): Panipat I 21 Apr 1526 (tulughma, araba; Ibrahim Lodi), Khanwa 1527 (Rana Sanga; jihad, tamgha abolished), Chanderi 1528, Ghaghra 1529; Baburnama (Turki); Kabuli Bagh mosque Panipat.
  • Humayun (1530–40, 1555–56): Chausa 1539 & Kannauj 1540 vs Sher Shah; regained Delhi 1555 (Machhiwara, Sirhind); died 1556 (library fall); Gulbadan's Humayunnama; Dinpanah.
  • Sur interregnum (1540–55): Sher Shah — rupiya (178 grains), dam, Grand Trunk Road repair & sarais, patta-qabuliyat, land measurement (sikandari gaz, zabt seeds), Purana Qila, Sasaram tomb, Rohtasgarh; died Kalinjar 1545; Tarikh-i-Sher Shahi (Abbas Khan Sarwani).
  • Akbar (1556–1605): Panipat II 1556 (Hemu; Bairam Khan regent till 1560), Chittor 1568, Ranthambore 1569, Haldighati 1576 (Man Singh vs Pratap), Gujarat 1572–73 (Buland Darwaza), Bengal 1576, Kabul, Kashmir 1586, Sindh, Kandahar 1595, Deccan (Ahmednagar 1600 — Chand Bibi). Abolished pilgrim tax 1563, jizya 1564; Ibadat Khana 1575; Mahzar 1579; Din-i-Ilahi/Tauhid-i-Ilahi 1582; Sulh-i-kul; Mansabdari 1571–95 (zat & sawar); Dahsala/Ain-i-Dahsala 1580 (Todar Mal; zabti; polaj, parauti, chachar, banjar); Navratnas; Fatehpur Sikri; Akbarnama/Ain-i-Akbari (Abul Fazl), Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh (Badauni, critical).
  • Jahangir (1605–27): Nur Jahan (junta), Khusrau revolt (Guru Arjan executed 1606), Mewar treaty 1615 (Amar Singh), Kangra 1620, Roe embassy 1615–19, Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri, 12 ordinances, chain of justice; painting zenith (Mansur, Abul Hasan, Bishandas); Itimad-ud-Daula tomb (first pietra dura; Nur Jahan).
  • Shah Jahan (1628–58): Taj Mahal 1632–53, Red Fort/Shahjahanabad 1639–48, Jama Masjid, Peacock throne, Moti Masjid Agra; Ahmednagar annexed 1636, Kandahar lost 1649, Balkh–Badakhshan failure; Portuguese Hughli 1632; war of succession 1657–58 (Dharmat, Samugarh — Dara defeated).
  • Aurangzeb (1658–1707): jizya reimposed 1679; Bijapur 1686, Golconda 1687; Shivaji Agra 1666; Sambhaji executed 1689; Jat (Gokula), Satnami 1672, Sikh (Guru Tegh Bahadur 1675), Rajput (Marwar 1679–) revolts; Fatawa-i-Alamgiri; Bibi ka Maqbara (Azam Shah); Badshahi Mosque Lahore; Moti Masjid Delhi; died Ahmednagar 1707.

Administration terms

  • Wakil, Diwan (wazir), Mir Bakhshi (military; mansab), Mir Saman/Khan-i-Saman (household), Sadr-us-Sudur · Provinces: subedar, diwan, bakhshi, faujdar (sarkar), kotwal (city), amil/amalguzar (pargana revenue), qanungo, patwari.
  • Revenue systems: zabti/dahsala, batai/ghalla-bakhshi (crop-sharing), kankut, nasaq. Jagirdar vs zamindar; madad-i-maash grants; ijara (revenue farming, late).
  • Mansab: 10 to 5,000 (nobles), 7,000+ princes; du-aspa sih-aspa (Jahangir), month-scale (Shah Jahan).

Culture

  • Painting: Humayun brought Mir Sayyid Ali & Abdus Samad; Akbar — Hamzanama, Daswant, Basawan; Jahangir — naturalism; Aurangzeb — decline, dispersal to Rajput/Pahari.
  • Architecture: Humayun's tomb (1565–72, Mirak Mirza Ghiyas; first garden tomb, charbagh), Fatehpur Sikri (Panch Mahal, Diwan-i-Khas, Buland Darwaza 1602 after Gujarat), Agra fort; Jahangir — Akbar's tomb Sikandra; Shah Jahan — white marble, pietra dura, bulbous domes.
  • Literature: Persian translations (Mahabharata → Razmnama, Ramayana, Atharvaveda; Dara Shukoh — Sirr-i-Akbar Upanishads, Majma-ul-Bahrain); Tulsidas Ramcharitmanas (Akbar's time); Bihari, Keshavdas; Abdur Rahim Khan-i-Khanan.

Traps

  • Jizya: abolished 1564 (Akbar), reimposed 1679 (Aurangzeb).
  • Pietra dura first appears in Itimad-ud-Daula's tomb (Jahangir), perfected in Taj.
  • Rupiya is Sher Shah's, mansab is Akbar's, du-aspa sih-aspa is Jahangir's.

Pairs flash

  • mansabdari zat-sawar → Akbar · dahsala/zabti → Todarmal · Ain-i-Akbari → Abul Fazl · Muntakhab-ut-Tawarikh → Badayuni.
  • Fatehpur Sikri + Buland Darwaza + Salim Chishti → Akbar · navaratna list recall.
  • Itmad-ud-Daula (pietra dura first tomb) + Chain of Justice + Tuzuk + Mansur + Roe → Jahangir (Nur Jahan junta).
  • Taj 1632-53 + Red Fort + Peacock Throne + Moti Masjid → Shah Jahan · Samugarh 1658 → succession.
  • jizya 1679 + Fatawa-e-Alamgiri + Tegh Bahadur 1675 + 27-yr Deccan → Aurangzeb · Bijapur 1686 + Golconda 1687.
  • Rangeela + Nadir Shah 1739 → Muhammad Shah · Panipat-3 → Abdali vs Marathas 1761 · Zafar → Rangoon 1857.
  • Tavernier-Bernier-Manucci-Thevenot → 17th-c travellers · Peter Mundy → famine records.

Trap flash

  • Baburnama in Chagatai Turkic (not Persian); Persian translation by Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khanan (1589, on Akbar's orders).
  • Itmad-ud-Daula tomb = Jahangir era (Nur Jahan built for father), not Shah Jahan.
  • mansab sawar = cavalry rank; zat = personal — don't swap.
  • Diwani (1765) granted by Shah Alam II after Buxar — not by Farrukhsiyar (his = farman 1717 trade).
  • Panipat-3 = Marathas vs Abdali (no Mughal army on field as party).
