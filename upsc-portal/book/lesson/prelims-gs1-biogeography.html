Biogeography: Soils & Natural Vegetation

In this lessonDetailed StudyRevision DigestKey Points & MindmapDiagrams, Maps & FlowchartsPrevious Year Questions

Detailed Study

The full treatment of this lesson.

Biomes, diversity metrics, island theory, soil-vegetation links. Light-read area with trick MCQs.

1. Biomes & vegetation belts

  • Whittaker's frame: temperature x precipitation → biome. Sequence pole-to-equator: tundra → taiga (boreal coniferous) → temperate deciduous-grassland → Mediterranean scrub → desert → tropical rainforest (max NPP) → savanna.
  • India forest types (Champion-Seth 1968, 16 major groups): tropical evergreen (Western Ghats-NE-Andaman), tropical deciduous (most extensive — moist then dry), tropical thorn (NW), montane (oak-conifer above), littoral-swamp (mangroves), alpine. Forest Survey of India (ISFR biennial): forest+tree cover ~25% of geographic area (verify latest ISFR figure).

2. Biodiversity metrics & hotspots

  • Metrics: alpha (within habitat), beta (between-habitat change), gamma (landscape total). Endemism: species confined to an area (India: high in Himalaya-Western Ghats-Andaman). India's 4 hotspots (of 36 global): Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats-Sri Lanka, Sundaland (Nicobars).
  • Species roles: keystone (ecosystem-defining: tigers, sea otters), flagship (iconic: panda), umbrella, indicator; IUCN categories: EX-EW-CR-EN-VU-NT-LC-DD-NE; Red List updates annual.
  • Island biogeography (MacArthur-Wilson 1967): species richness rises with area, falls with isolation; immigration-extinction balance; SIBA-conservation design basis.

3. Soil-vegetation geography

  • Soil classification (ICAR 8-fold): alluvial (largest; khadar-new vs bangar-old), black/regur (cotton; Deccan basalt, moisture-retentive, self-ploughing), red (ferruginous, weathered crystalline), laterite (intense leaching; brick-tea-cashew), arid (calcified; kankar layers), saline-usara (alkaline; irrigation mismanagement), peaty-organic (Kerala), forest-mountain soils.
  • Soil-vegetation coupling: laterite ↔ degraded deciduous; black ↔ dry savanna-ish zones; alluvium ↔ agriculture belt; erosion (sheet-rill-gully-ravine — Chambal badlands) + conservation (contour bunding, watershed, agroforestry).

MCQ hooks

  • India's 4 hotspots; Andaman-Nicobar chain sits under the Sundaland hotspot.
  • Champion-Seth classification 1968; tropical deciduous = most extensive type.
  • Alpha-beta-gamma definitions verbatim.
  • Black soil = regur = Deccan basalt; laterite = leaching (not lava).
  • Khadar = newer alluvium (flood-refreshed) vs bangar older.
  • MacArthur-Wilson = island theory (area up, richness up).

Soil formation & profile

  • Factors (Jenny): parent material, climate, relief, organisms, time (Cl-O-R-P-T). Processes: weathering → humification → eluviation/illuviation, laterisation (hot-wet; silica leached, Fe/Al oxides stay), podzolisation (cool-wet coniferous; acidic, ash-grey), calcification (semi-arid; CaCO₃), salinisation/alkalisation (arid; capillary rise), gleisation (waterlogged). Profile: O (organic) → A (topsoil, humus; eluviation) → E → B (illuviation; subsoil) → C (regolith) → R (bedrock). Texture triangle (sand-silt-clay; loam best); pH (6.5–7.5 ideal); soil health card (12 parameters — NPK, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, OC); NBSS&LUP (Nagpur).

India's vegetation (Champion & Seth 1968 — 16 types; ISFR 2023: forest cover 7,15,343 km² = 21.76%, tree cover 3.41%, total 25.17%; MP largest area, Mizoram highest %; Lakshadweep ~90%; mangroves 4,992 km² — WB 42%)

  • Tropical evergreen (>200 cm; W Ghats, NE, A&N; rosewood, ebony, mahogany, aini), semi-evergreen; tropical moist deciduous (100–200 cm; most widespread; teak, sal, shisham, sandalwood; Chhota Nagpur, Ghats foothills); tropical dry deciduous (70–100 cm; teak, tendu, palas, khair; UP, MP, peninsular interior); thorn/scrub (<70 cm; acacia, babul, khejri, ber, date; Rajasthan, Gujarat, Deccan rain shadow); montane (wet temperate — Nilgiri sholas; Himalayan — oak, chestnut, pine, deodar, silver fir, spruce, birch; alpine — juniper, rhododendron); littoral/mangrove (Sundarbans — Heritiera fomes (sundari), Rhizophora, Avicennia; Bhitarkanika, Pichavaram, Godavari-Krishna, Gulf of Kutch; pneumatophores, viviparity). Forest Survey of India (Dehradun; biennial ISFR); National Forest Policy 1988 (33% target); Van Mahotsav (July); Green India Mission; CAMPA; Nagar Van; Mangrove — MISHTI 2023; Great Indian Bustard grasslands; sacred groves (Meghalaya law kyntang, Kerala kavu, Rajasthan oran, Karnataka devarakadu).

Traps

  • Black soil is rich in Ca, Mg, K, Fe but poor in N, P, humus — same NPH deficiency as most Indian soils.
  • Laterite forms under alternating wet-dry tropical conditions, and hardens like brick (Latin later).
  • Khadar = new & fertile; bhangar = old & kankar-rich (not the reverse).
  • Chernozem = temperate grasslands; latosol = rainforest; podzol = taiga.
  • ISFR 2023 (released Dec 2024): forest+tree cover 25.17%; forest cover alone 21.76%.

Key Points & Mindmap

Skeleton facts and recall triggers.

Term-anchor flash.

Biome flash

  • Max NPP → tropical rainforest · taiga = boreal conifer · savanna = grass+scattered trees · Mediterranean = winter-rain scrub.
  • India forests → Champion-Seth 1968; tropical deciduous largest; mangrove = littoral-swamp type.

Diversity flash

  • Alpha → within-habitat · beta → between-habitat · gamma → landscape total.
  • Hotspots (36 global) → India 4: Himalaya, Indo-Burma, W-Ghats-Sri Lanka, Sundaland.
  • IUCN order → EX, EW, CR, EN, VU, NT, LC (+DD, NE).
  • Keystone → ecosystem-defining · flagship → charismatic · indicator → health signal.

Island flash

  • MacArthur-Wilson 1967 → richness ↑ with area, ↓ with isolation · SIBA application in reserve design.

Soil flash

  • Alluvial largest (khadar new / bangar old) · black = regur, cotton, basalt, self-ploughing · red = crystalline weathering.
  • Laterite → leaching, brick-cashew-tea · arid → kankar · saline-usara → irrigation mismanagement · peaty → Kerala.
  • Ravines → Chambal badlands; gully erosion severe.

Trap flash

  • Laterite ≠ volcanic origin (it's a leaching product).
  • Gamma diversity ≠ 'global' — it's landscape scale.
  • Hotspot criteria: endemic plants ≥1500 + habitat loss ≥70%.
  • Black soil self-ploughing = crack-swell on wetting-drying — not tillage.
