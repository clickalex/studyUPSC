Section 1 maps the architecture (List-II police, MHA–NIA–grids–NSG–CAPFs, the Prakash-Singh-to-Model-Act lineage); this section makes it the answer method — because security answers fail structurally (theatre-description without architecture, threats without instruments, critique without reform) more often than factually. The method runs: constitutional hook (public order-and-police as State subjects — Entry 1–2, List II — the Union acting through Entry 2A deployment, intelligence coordination, and central forces — the federalism frame that opens every internal-security answer: the State owns order, the Union owns coordination-plus-surge) → institutional map (MHA's divisions; NIA (2008 Act — the post-26/11 federal-investigation answer — scheduled offences, special courts); NATGRID–MAC–sMAC (the intelligence grid — data-fusion plus multi-agency centres — the connect-the-dots machinery); NSG (hubs post-26/11 — the federal contingency force); CAPFs (BSF–CRPF–CISF–ITBP–SSB–Assam-Rifles — each with its border-or-role: BSF Pakistan–Bangladesh, ITBP China, SSB Nepal–Bhutan, Assam Rifles Myanmar-dual-control, CRPF the internal-surge bulk, CISF the installation-guard) — drawn as the web, one sketch per answer) → theatre analysis (§2's five: LWE-contraction, NE-accords-plus-Manipur, Kashmir-post-Sindoor, terror-finance, narco-organised — each: trend-honest, instrument-mapped, datum-anchored) → reform close (the lineage: Gore-1971 → Ribeiro → Padmanabhaiah → Malimath → Prakash Singh 2006 (security commissions, DGP tenure, investigation–law-and-order separation — mostly unimplemented, honestly admitted) → Model Police Act 2015 draft → the live levers: SMART-policing (CCTNS-interoperable, ICJS-linked), forensic capacity (the conviction-rate bottleneck — BNS–BNSS–BSA 2023-24 codes' forensic mandates), police-population ratios and vacancy arithmetic, community-policing pilots). The federalism stress-tests (§7's grid extended) deserve their standing paragraph: general-consent withdrawals (CBI's federalism friction — States withdrawing DSPE consent — investigation federalised by permission); NIA's concurrent reach (scheduled offences overriding the State veto — the post-26/11 settlement: terror as national crime); CAPF deployment politics (aid-to-civil-power requisitions — the \"forces as ordered, control as disputed\" tension); and the intelligence-sharing doctrine (MAC–sMAC–NATGRID as the technical fix for the pre-26/11 silos — Kargil-Review-to-Naresh-Chandra lineage: reform born of failure, audited by the next failure). The security answer's close is always reform-sequenced (first fill vacancies-and-forensics, then separate investigation, then commission-oversight — ordering as judgment) — threats described, architecture mapped, gaps admitted, sequence proposed: the method that converts the security envelope's 2–4 yearly questions into bankable marks.
