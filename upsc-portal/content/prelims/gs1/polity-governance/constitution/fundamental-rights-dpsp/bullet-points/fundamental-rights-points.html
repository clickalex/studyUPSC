Fundamental Rights & DPSP — Bullet Points

Article-map flash.

FR flash

  • 14 equality-before-law · 15 non-discrimination · 16 opportunity · 17 untouchability · 18 titles.
  • 19 six-freedoms · 20 conviction safeguards · 21 life+privacy · 21A education · 22 arrest.
  • 23 begar-traffic · 24 child labour · 25-28 religion · 29-30 minorities · 32 remedies (SC).

Writ flash

  • Habeas corpus → detention · mandamus → public duty · prohibition → during case (superior→inferior) · certiorari → quash after · quo warranto → public office claim.
  • HC writs (226) cover legal rights too — wider than SC (32, FRs only).

DPSP flash

  • Socialist → 38, 39, 39A legal aid, 41, 42, 43 · Gandhian → 40 panchayats, 43 cottage, 46, 47, 48 cows · Liberal → 44 UCC, 45 ECCE, 50 sep judiciary, 51 peace.
  • 42nd additions → 39A, 43A, 48A, 50 · 44th changed 38(2) · 43B via 97th (cooperatives).

Cases flash

  • Champakam Dorairajan 1951 → FR dominance · Golaknath 1967 → FRs unamendable · Kesavananda 1973 → basic structure · Minerva 1980 → 42nd partial strike · Maneka 1978 → due process · Puttaswamy 2017 → privacy.

Trap flash

  • Art 21A = 86th (2002), NOT 42nd; 11th FD same 86th amendment.
  • Right to property: FR before 44th (1978) → now 300A constitutional legal right.
  • DPSP non-justiciable but 'fundamental in governance' (Art 37).
  • FDs apply to citizens only; Art 19 = citizens, but 14-21 apply to persons — mixed map.
