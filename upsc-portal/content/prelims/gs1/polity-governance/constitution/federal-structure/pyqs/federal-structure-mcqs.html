Federal Structure & Centre–State Relations — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Under Article 249, Parliament can legislate on a State List subject if:

  1. (a)The Lok Sabha passes a resolution by two-thirds majority
  2. (b)The Rajya Sabha passes a resolution by two-thirds of members present and voting
  3. (c)Two or more states request it
  4. (d)The President issues an ordinance
Show answer
Correct: (b)RS resolution, one year, renewable.
Q2

Consider Article 252: 1. Parliament legislates on a State List matter at the request of two or more state legislatures. 2. Such a law applies only to the requesting states and any state that later adopts it. 3. The state legislatures may amend or repeal the law.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Only Parliament may amend/repeal (e.g., Wildlife Act 1972).
Q3

Match the timeline with the emergency: A. Approval within one month by special majority — 1. Article 360 · B. Approval within two months by simple majority, maximum three years — 2. Article 352 · C. Approval within two months, no maximum duration — 3. Article 356

  1. (a)A-2 B-3 C-1
  2. (b)A-1 B-2 C-3
  3. (c)A-3 B-2 C-1
  4. (d)A-2 B-1 C-3
Show answer
Correct: (a)352 / 356 / 360 timelines.
Q4

Decisions of the GST Council are taken by a majority of not less than three-fourths of weighted votes, where the Centre's vote carries a weight of:

  1. (a)One-fourth
  2. (b)One-third
  3. (c)One-half
  4. (d)Two-thirds
Show answer
Correct: (b)Centre one-third, states two-thirds.
Q5

Consider the Inter-State Council: 1. It is established under Article 263 by an order of the President. 2. It was set up in 1990 on the recommendation of the Sarkaria Commission. 3. The Union Home Minister is its chairman.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)PM chairs the ISC; Home Minister chairs zonal councils.
Q6

The 15th Finance Commission recommended vertical devolution to states of:

  1. (a)32%
  2. (b)41%
  3. (c)42%
  4. (d)50%
Show answer
Correct: (b)41% (42% minus 1% for J&K/Ladakh).
Q7

Which of the following is administered under the Sixth Schedule?

  1. (a)Nagaland
  2. (b)Arunachal Pradesh
  3. (c)Mizoram
  4. (d)Manipur
Show answer
Correct: (c)Mizoram has three ADCs; Nagaland/Arunachal/Manipur have Art 371 provisions.
Q8

Consider taxes under Article 268:

  1. (a)Levied and collected by the Union and assigned to states
  2. (b)Levied by the Union but collected and appropriated by states
  3. (c)Levied and collected by the Union and shared with states
  4. (d)Levied, collected and retained by the Union
Show answer
Correct: (b)e.g., stamp duties on bills of exchange.
Q9

In S.R. Bommai (1994), the Supreme Court held that: 1. A proclamation under Article 356 is subject to judicial review. 2. The state assembly can be dissolved only after both Houses of Parliament approve the proclamation. 3. Majority of a government must be tested on the floor of the House.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q10

Consider the Supreme Court's 2023 judgment on Article 370: 1. It held Article 370 to be a temporary provision. 2. It struck down the creation of the Union Territory of Ladakh. 3. It directed restoration of statehood to Jammu and Kashmir.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (c)Ladakh UT creation was upheld.
