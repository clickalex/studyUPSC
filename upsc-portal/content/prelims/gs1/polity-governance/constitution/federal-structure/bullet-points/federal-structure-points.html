Federal Structure — Bullet Points

Article-anchor flash.

Lists flash

  • List I ~97 (defence, banking, currency) · List II ~66 (police, health, agriculture) · List III ~52 (education, forests, criminal law) · residuary → Union (248).
  • State-list intrusion routes → 249 (RS 2/3), 250 (emergency), 252 (consent), 253 (treaties), 356.
  • 254 → Union prevails; presidential-assent state law can locally prevail.

Administrative flash

  • 256-257 → directions · 258 → entrustment · 262 → water-dispute tribunals (Cauvery-Krishna) · 263 → Inter-State Council (1990, Sarkaria).
  • Zonal councils → 1956 SRA statutory (5 + NE) · All-India Services → 312 (IAS-IPS-IFoS only).

Financial flash

  • 268-270 → levy-collect pattern · 271 → surcharges out of pool · 280 → Finance Commission (5-yearly) · 275/282 → grants.
  • GST Council 279A → FM chairs; states 2/3 + Centre 1/3; 3/4 threshold.
  • 292/293 → borrowing; states need Centre consent if indebted.

Cases & commissions flash

  • S.R. Bommai 1994 → 356 judicial review + floor test · Kesavananda → federalism inside basic structure.
  • Sarkaria 1983-88 → 356 last resort, IC · Punchhi 2007-10 → Governor norms, localised emergency.

Trap flash

  • Residuary: India = Union; USA = states — comparison trap.
  • Inter-State Council ≠ Zonal Councils (constitutional vs statutory).
  • Finance Commission recommends devolution; NITI Aayog is not a statutory distributor.
  • Education sits in Concurrent List (since 42nd), not State — common error.
