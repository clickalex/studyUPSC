Constitutional Amendments — Bullet Points

Number-year-change flash.

Classic flash

  • 1st 1951 → Ninth Schedule, 15(4) · 7th 1956 → states reorg · 24th 1971 → FRs amendable · 25th → 31C.
  • 36th 1975 → Sikkim state · 42nd 1976 → mini-constitution (words + FDs + tribunals) · 44th 1978 → property out of FRs, 'armed rebellion'.
  • 52nd 1985 → anti-defection · 61st 1989 → vote 18 · 69th 1991 → Delhi NCT · 73rd-74th 1992 → PRIs/municipalities.

Modern flash

  • 86th 2002 → 21A + FD-11 · 91st 2003 → cabinet 15% · 97th 2011 → cooperatives · 99th 2014 → NJAC (struck) · 100th 2015 → Bangladesh LBA.
  • 101st 2016 → GST · 102nd 2018 → NCBC · 103rd 2019 → EWS 10% · 104th 2020 → SC-ST seats extended, Anglo-Indian nomination ends · 105th 2021 → SEBC state power · 106th 2023 → women's reservation.

Procedure flash

  • Simple majority outside 368 (states, citizenship, salaries) · special majority most · +½ ratification for President election, Union-state power, Seventh Schedule, HC-SC, 368 itself.
  • No joint sitting for amendment; assent obligatory (24th).

Trap flash

  • 91st = cabinet-size cap (15%), NOT anti-defection (52nd).
  • Delhi NCT = 69th (1991), not 70th.
  • FDs added by 42nd (10) + 86th (11th) — count trap.
  • IR Coelho 2007: Ninth Schedule laws post-24 Apr 1973 face basic-structure review.
  • 106th women's quota: awaits delimitation census — 'implementation' phrasing matters.
