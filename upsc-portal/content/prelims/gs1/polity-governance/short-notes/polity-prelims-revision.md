## Constitution skeleton
- **Borrowed features**: UK → parliamentary, rule of law, single citizenship, cabinet, bicameralism; Ireland → DPSP, RS nomination, presidential election method; USA → FRs, judicial review, impeachment, removal of SC/HC judges, VP office; Canada → federal with unitary bias, SC appointment method; Australia → Concurrent List, joint sitting; USSR → FDs, justice ideals; Germany → emergency provisions; France → Republic, liberty-equality-fraternity (added via 42nd, preamble); SA → amendment procedure + RS election; Japan → procedure established by law; **Govt of India Act 1935** → federation, PSC, governor, emergency, judiciary skeleton (~250 provisions source).

## Preamble-42nd-44th drill
- 42nd (1976): SOCIALIST·SECULAR·INTEGRITY added; **FDs added (Art 51A, 10 duties — 86th added 11th in 2002, age 6-14 for education)**.
- 44th (1978): right to property removed from FR (Art 300A legal right now); "internal disturbance" → "armed rebellion"; Press freedom guard via Art 19 clarity.

## FRs vs DPSPs vs FDs
- FR: Part III (12–35); enforceable; 32 = remedies writs.
- DPSP: Part IV (36–51); non-enforceable; salient: 39(b)(c) resource distribution; 44 UCC; 45 ECE; 48A env; **47 liquor prohibition**; 43A workers' participation (42nd Amdt).
- FDs: Part IVA (51A).

## Union executive
- President: Art 52; indirect election (Art 54); value of MLA vote = state population/ MLAs ÷1000 (1971 census base currently; frozen till 2026); qualification-age 35; impeachment Art 61 (quasi-judicial 2/3rd procedure).
- PM: Art 74(1) council to aid/advice (42nd: binding); PM chairs NITI.
- VP: ex-officio RS Chair; Art 63; removed by RS resolution + LS concurrence.

## Parliament mechanics
- Sessions, quorum 1/10th; **motion lines**: no-confidence (Art 75(3)), cut motions (policy/economy/token), privilege, calling-attention vs adjournment, short-duration (Rule 176 RS) vs half-hour.
- Bills: money bill Art 110 (Speaker certifies), finance (Art 117 joint), ordinary; joint sitting Art 108 (LS dominance); RS saver-clauses Art 249/312.
- Committees: DRPCs (17+7); financial trio (PAC — opposition-convention chair; Estimates; CoPU).

## Judiciary grid
- SC: Art 124–147; appointment collegium (99th Amdt/NJAC struck 2015); writs under **Art 32** ("heart & soul" — Ambedkar line).
- HC: Art 214+; writs Art 226 (wider than 32).
- Writs: habeas corpus, mandamus, prohibition, certiorari, quo warranto.
- Tribunals: Art 323A/323B lineage; NGT (2010 statute) — not constitutional tribunals.

## Federal & emergency
- Federal features: dual polity; union bias: residuary (Centre), Art 249 RS resolutions, Art 356 president's rule.
- Emergency types: Art 352 National (armed-rebellion after 44th), 356 President's Rule, 360 Financial.
- **44th Amdt** floor: Art 20/21 cannot be suspended even during emergency.
