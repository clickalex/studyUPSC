Election Commission (Art 324–329)

  • Art 324 superintendence, direction, control of elections to Parliament, state legislatures, President, VP (not local bodies — SEC 243K/ZA); Art 325 one general roll; 326 adult suffrage (18 — 61st); 327/328 Parliament/state powers; 329 bar on court interference (delimitation final; election petitions to HC (RPA 1951 s 80) — 45 days; SC appeal); Art 324(5) — CEC removal like SC judge; ECs removable on CEC recommendation; conditions can't be varied to disadvantage; Anoop Baranwal Mar 2023 (5-judge) — PM + LoP + CJI committee until law → CEC & Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act Dec 2023 — PM, Union Minister, LoP (LS) (largest opposition leader); status = Cabinet Secretary (originally SC judge); immunity for acts in office; challenge pending SC (Mar 2024 refused stay); first under Act: Gyanesh Kumar & Sukhbir Singh Sandhu (Mar 2024); Gyanesh Kumar 26th CEC (Feb 2025; first under new Act; till Jan 2029); ECs Sukhbir Sandhu & Vivek Joshi (Feb 2025); Rajiv Kumar 25th CEC (2022–Feb 2025); Sukumar Sen first; T.N. UPSC & SPSC (Arts 315–323)

  • UPSC (Art 315; UPSC (Members) Regulations 1969; Chairman Preeti Sudan (Aug 2024) → Ajay Kumar (May 2025, ex-Defence Secretary); members ~10; conditions by President; removal — President on SC inquiry (Art 317) for misbehaviour; directly if adjudged insolvent, engaged in paid employment, infirm; suspension pending inquiry; SC advice binding; post-tenure bar (Art 319) — Chairman UPSC ineligible for any government employment; members eligible only for UPSC chair/SPSC chairman; SPSC chairman → UPSC chairman/member or other SPSC chairman; functions (Art 320) — all-India, Central, Union services exams; assist joint recruitment; consulted on recruitment methods, promotions/transfers principles, disciplinary matters, legal cost reimbursement, extraordinary pension, temporary appointments >1 yr; exclusions — reservation (Art 335), SC/ST backward class claims, chairmanship of tribunals/commissions, diplomatic posts, Group C/D (regulations); advisory (not binding; State of UP v Manbodhan Lal 1957); report to President — Parliament with memorandum of non-acceptance (Art 323); Art 321 Parliament extends functions (local authorities, corporations); expenses charged on CFI (322); JSPSC (Art 315(2) — Parliament by law on states' request; none exists at present); Art 318 conditions; Art 319 post-office restrictions; Constitutional history — Lee Commission 1924 → Public Service Commission 1926 (Sir Ross Barker); Federal PSC 1935 (GoI Act); UPSC 26 Jan 1950; DoPT administers; CSE — ~13 lakh applicants; 1,129 vacancies (CSE 2024); Shakti Dubey topper 2024 (Prayagraj; Biochemistry; PSIR); CSAT 2011; ethics paper 2013; optional one 2013; Interview 275; Mains 1,750; Lateral entry (2018; 45 posts ad Aug 2024 withdrawn — reservation row); Mission Karmayogi (iGOT; Capacity Building Commission 2021 — Adil Zainulbhai); Baswan Committee 2016 (age); Hota 2004; Y.K. Alagh 2001; Kothari 1976; Satish Chandra 1989; Nigavekar 2012 (CSAT); Puja Khedkar 2024 (fraud; candidature cancelled; UPSC registrar FIR; Aadhaar-based verification, facial recognition 2025)**.

Finance Commission (Art 280) — see federal-structure card; CAG (Art 148–151)

  • CAG: single; President; oath; 6 yrs/65; removal like SC judge; ineligible for further government office; salary = SC judge (charged); audit of Union/state accounts, CFI/CF/Public Account, government companies (Companies Act 143(6)), autonomous bodies (Sec 14/15 of DPC Act 1971 — substantially financed >₹25 lakh & 75%), PSUs (supplementary audit), not audit of secret service expenditure (certificate from executive); compiles state accounts (Union accounts to Controller General of Accounts since 1976 departmentalisation); reports — Union to President → Parliament (PAC), states to Governor → assembly; audits appropriation, finance, propriety (discretionary), performance/efficiency (3E); "friend, philosopher and guide of PAC"; Art 148(6) expenses charged; 149 duties by Parliament — CAG (DPC) Act 1971 (amended 1976 — accounts separated); 150 form of accounts (President on CAG advice); 151 reports; Ambedkar — "most important officer"; Indian Audit & Accounts Department; K. Sanjay Murthy (Nov 2024; 15th CAG) after G.C. Murmu (2020–24); CAG audits — 2G (₹1.76 lakh cr presumptive), Coalgate ₹1.86 lakh cr; 2023 — Bharatmala, Dwarka Expressway (₹250 cr/km), Ayushman Bharat (7.5 lakh on one number), Delhi liquor policy 2025 (₹2,026 cr loss; Delhi assembly Feb 2025 tabled 14 reports), Toll plazas; 2025 — Rajasthan, Karnataka; International role — CAG of India is/was external auditor of WHO (2020–23), FAO (2020–25), IAEA (2022–27) and served on the UN Board of Auditors (2014–20); India chaired ASOSAI 2024–27; Comptroller role — India's CAG only auditor (unlike UK where Comptroller controls issue); Public Accounts Committee reviews; "Audit of Accounts of the Union Government" — Departmental Accounts; e-audit; "Audit Diwas 16 Nov" (2021); Recent — CAG reports on MGNREGA (2024), PMGSY, Ayushman Bharat (2023), Swadesh Darshan, Char Dham, Indian Railways (2024), DDA; access to GSTN data for GST audit.

Traps

  • EC: CEC removable like SC judge; ECs only on CEC's recommendation; 2023 Act committee = PM + Union Minister + LoP (no CJI); EC does not conduct panchayat/municipal elections (SEC — Art 243K) but President/VP elections are conducted by EC; MCC not statutory; EC can register but not deregister parties (except fraud); Election petitions → HC (Art 329(b)); delimitation orders not justiciable (329(a)).
  • UPSC removal — President after SC inquiry (misbehaviour) — also for SPSC (not Governor); SPSC retirement 62, UPSC 65; UPSC advice not binding; JSPSC by Parliament; expenses charged.
  • CAG — audits government companies via Companies Act, not autonomous bodies unless substantially funded; no audit of secret service expenditure; Union accounts compiled by CGA (1976), state accounts by CAG; CAG is not Comptroller in practice; reports go to President/Governor, examined by PAC; PAC chairman opposition; CAG cannot be re-employed in government.
  • NCSC/NCST/NCBC — all Art 338/338A/338B, three-year tenure by rules; NCBC constitutional since 2018 (102nd); 342A states' own OBC list restored by 105th (2021); Rohini (Art 340) report 2023 not implemented; NCM/NCW/NHRC statutory; NCSK non-statutory; Special Officer Linguistic Minorities — Art 350B (7th Amdt), HQ Allahabad; no fixed tenure.
