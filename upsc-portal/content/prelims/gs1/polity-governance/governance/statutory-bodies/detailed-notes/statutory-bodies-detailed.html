Statutory, Regulatory and Executive Bodies — Detailed Notes

Created by acts (statutory), executive resolutions (executive), or dedicated regulatory acts. Match body → act → mandate.

1. Rights & oversight commissions

  • NHRC (PHRA 1993): chair ex-CJI or SC judge; quasi-judicial recommendations; 1-year limitation; can't probe armed forces; 2019 amendment: 3-year tenure, woman member added. SHRCs mirror.
  • CIC (RTI 2005): CIC + ≤10 ICs; second-appeal body; 2019 amendment: Centre decides tenure-salary (previously 5y fixed) — federalism critique anchor. Lokpal (2013 act, functional 2019): chair + ≤8 (50% judicial); jurisdiction PM with safeguards; Lokayuktas in states (state act variance).
  • CVC (2003 act; origins 1964 Santhanam): superintendence of DSPE/CBI corruption work; Central Bureau of Investigation = executive-resolution body (1963, DSPE Act 1946 base) — classic exam pair. NCPCR/NCW (2007/1992 acts); NCSC is constitutional (Art 338).

3. Executive/policy bodies

  • NITI Aayog (1 Jan 2015, cabinet resolution) — replaced Planning Commission (1950); no fund-allocation power; Governing Council (CMs + LG admins); indices (SDG, innovation, water). NDC (1952) — status post-NITI debated. National Statistical bodies: MoSPI; NSC controversies (jobs-data 2019).
  • NGT (2010 act): zonal benches; 30-day filing; civil-environment adjudication; 2021 chair-eligibility tweaks upheld with caveats.
  • Fast-track+tribunal framework: Tribunal Reforms Act 2021 (4-year tenure; Madras HC critique).

MCQ hooks

  • CBI = executive resolution (1963) under DSPE Act 1946; CVC = statutory (2003).
  • Lokpal 2013 act; PM jurisdiction with safeguards; 8 members max, 50% judicial.
  • NITI Aayog = executive resolution 1 Jan 2015 (no act!).
  • NGT = 2010 act, zonal principal bench Delhi.
  • SEBI-SAT, TRAI-TDSAT appeal pairs.
  • NHRC can't investigate armed forces; 1-year bar; 2019 amendment tenure 3y.
