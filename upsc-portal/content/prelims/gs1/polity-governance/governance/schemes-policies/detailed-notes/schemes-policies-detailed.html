Flagship Schemes and Policies — Detailed Notes

Scheme → ministry → launch year → headline number. High-frequency prelims zone; verify current figures each cycle.

1. Poverty & rural

  • MGNREGA 2005 (100 days/household; wage-material 60:40; unemployment allowance; social audit (Sec 17); demand-driven (DBT wages via NREGASoft)). PMAY-G/U (rural pucca homes; urban CLSS component), DAY-NRLM (women SHGs — Ajeevika lineage; Lakhpati Didi target 3cr), PM-KISAN 2019 (₹6,000/yr, landholding farmer families, exclusion caps), PM Garib Kalyan Anna Yojana (NFSA top-up; made free 2023), Ayushman Bharat: PM-JAY (₹5 lakh/family secondary-tertiary) + HWC/AB-HWC now Ayushman Arogya Mandirs.

2. Financial inclusion & pension

  • PMJDY 2014 (zero-balance, RuPay, overdraft ₹10k), PMJJBY (life ₹2L) + PMSBY (accident ₹2L) + APY (pension) trio 2015, MUDRA (Shishu ≤50k, Kishore 50k-5L, Tarun 5-10L; now Tarun-Plus 20L), PM SVANidhi (street vendors); Stand-Up India (SC-ST-women entrepreneurs ₹10L-1cr).

3. Health-education-skill

  • PM Matru Vandana (₹5,000 in instalments; 2017), POSHAN Abhiyaan 2018 (now POSHAN 2.0 — Mission Saksham Anganwadi), PM POSHAN (mid-day meal rename 2021). Education: Samagra Shiksha (2018 school integration), NEP 2020 (5+3+3+4, FLN NIPUN), PM SHRI (14,500 exemplar schools), PM Vidyalaxmi (education loans). Skill: PMKVY 4.0, DGT apprenticeships (NAPS).

4. Water-energy-urban

  • Jal Jeevan Mission 2019 (har ghar jal tap target; under Jal Shakti), PM Krishi Sinchayee Yojana (per-drop-more-crop micro-irrigation), eNAM (mandi e-platform), FPO scheme (10,000 FPOs), Ujjwala (LPG to poor women; 8cr+ connections), PM Surya Ghar (rooftop solar 1cr homes 2024), Saubhagya (electrification 2017), AMRUT 2.0 + Smart Cities Mission (100 cities, 2023-24 sunset), SBM 2.0 (ODF-plus-plus), PM-eBus Sewa.
  • Newer 2023-24 banners: PM Vishwakarma (artisans, 18 trades), PM-JANMAN (PVTGs, 11 interventions), PM PVTG mission, Mission Karmayogi (civil-service capacity), Aspirational Districts (112, NITI), Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM health IDs).

MCQ hooks

  • MGNREGA = 2005 act, FY2006; social audit Sec 17; wage:material 60:40.
  • PM-KISAN ₹6,000 (not ₹5,000/7,000); DBT via PFMS.
  • MUDRA tiers order Shishu-Kishore-Tarun; new Tarun-Plus 2024.
  • JJM 2019 tap water; Smart Cities = 100, launched 2015; SBM = 2 Oct 2014.
  • Ayushman Bharat duo: PM-JAY (insurance) + Arogya Mandirs (care).
  • NEP 2020 structure 5+3+3+4 (not 10+2).
  • Aspirational Districts = NITI, 112 districts.
