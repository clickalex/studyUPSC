Governance, Bodies & Schemes — Prelims Revision

Constitutional vs statutory vs executive (the ever-green table)

  • Constitutional: Election Commission (Art 324) · UPSC/SPSCs (315–323) · CAG (148) · Finance Commission (280) · GST Council (279A) · AG (76)/Advocate General (165) · NCSC (338) · NCST (338A) · NCBC (338B) · Inter-State Council (263) · Zonal Councils (statutory! States Reorganisation Act 1956 — perennial trap) · Scheduled Areas machinery (5th/6th Sched; Tribes Advisory Council 244; DPCs).
  • Statutory: NHRC (Protection of Human Rights Act 1993) · NCPCR (CPCR Act 2005) · NCW (1990 Act) · NCBC-origin (pre-2018 statutory) · Lokpal (Lokpal & Lokayuktas Act 2013) · CIC/SIC (RTI 2005) · CVC (2003) · NGT (2010) · CCI (2002 Act) · NALSA (1987 Act) · UIDAI (Aadhaar Act 2016) · UGC/AICTE (statutory respective) · TRAI · SEBI(1992) · IRDAI · RBI (statutory 1934) · ARCs (SARFAESI lineage).
  • Executive-only (resolution): NITI Aayog (2015 replacing PC) · NDMA (note: created via executive order Dec 2005 — DM Act 2005 made it statutory—trap, treat as statutory now) · PM's office groupings · National Development Council (dormant since 2014-era reset) · IN-SPACe (executive-resolution regulator for space).

Bodies' tenure/appointment levers

  • NHRC chair = ex-CJI; members incl SC judge sitting etc; appointment committee PM+LoP both Houses+HM.
  • CVC: 4-yr/65; CVC-chair selection PM+HM+LoP-LS.
  • CBI: DSPE 1946 lineage; director fixed-minimum 2 years (Vineet Narain + Lokpal 2013 amendments; DoPT-pooled selection of PM+CJI/ nominee judge+LoP trio); state consent cessions narrative (general consent withdrawals by many states 2018–2022 → litigation queries).
  • ED: under Finance Ministry (Revenue dept), PMLA anchor 2002; director-tenure extensions contested in SC (2021+) vs later amendments.
  • Lokpal: chair + up to 8 members (50% judicial); appointments since 2019 set; 2023-24 staffing updates; search-committee structure.

Local-programmes tracker (selected bench hits)

  • PMAY-G (rural) house completions scale ~2 crore+ mark crossed · PMAY-U 2.0 (2024) 3 crore additional households across both versions package lines.
  • Jal Jeevan Mission (2019; target Har Ghar Jal 2024 → extended run to 2028; JJM 2.0) — tap-water households from ~17% to ~75%+ figures claimed band.
  • Swachh Bharat 2.0: ODF++ protocols, WWT plant capex ridge.
  • PM-KISAN: ₹6,000/yr in 3 instalments to eligible landholding farmers (14th–19th instalment cadence through 2024-26).
  • MGNREGA: 100 days; social-audit sets (art: Andhra model best-practice); wage rates notified per state — check the current year's notification.
  • Ujjwala 2.0 / PMUY: 10+ crore LPG connections cumulative — subsidy transitions to DBT PAHAL.
  • POSHAN Abhiyaan → PM POSHAN (mid-day meal to pre-school inclusion 2021); Anaemia Mukt Bharat run-rate challenge.
  • Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY (~₹5 lakh family/yr, ~55 crore people footprint), ABDM digital-health mission, Ayushman Vaya Vandana 70+ universal cover (2024).
  • e-Shram portal (unorganised worker registry ~30 crore login scale) · PM SVANidhi street-vendor microloans · Stand-Up India · PM Mudra Yojana (loans tiers Shishu/Kishor/Tarun → Tarun Plus category ₹20 lakh added 2024).
  • PLI 14 sectors · PM Gati Shakti (NMP) · Bharatmala/Sagarmala · Dedicated Freight Corridors (Eastern fully operational 2023; Western phases).
  • Mission Amrit Sarovar: 75-ponds-per-district drive (2022) — counted ~68k+ created band.
  • Vibrant Villages Programme I/II (2023-25) — border-village development push; coverage expansion added after the Oct-2024 border updates.
  • SVAMITVA drone-mapping property cards (target ~6.62 lakh villages).
  • Kisan e-Mitra AI grievance-hotline; Krishi-DSS geospatial agriculture-stack (Sept 2024 unveils).

RTI & transparency quicks

  • RTI 2005; 100 RTI portals state-rolls; RTI Amendment 2019 (IC tenure/salary via rules) — pattern-litigation; DPDP 2023 Sec-8(1)(j) rewrite debates.

Delimitation/election-line CA

  • Census 2027 two-phase (house-listing 2026 → enumeration 2027; self-enumeration digital pilot); frozen seat-counts delimitation post-2026 census → women's 33% (106th Amdt) runway; J&K delimitation completed 2022 as pilot narrative.

Traps

  • Zonal Councils = statutory (not constitutional);
  • NITI ≠ constitutional; NDC = executive (dormant).
  • NCBC is constitutional post-2018; NCW & NCPCR statutory; NHRC statutory (not constitutional).
  • NDMA under DM Act 2005 — statutory authority despite "Mission-style" naming.
  • Lokpal covers PM with safeguards (not an exemption).
