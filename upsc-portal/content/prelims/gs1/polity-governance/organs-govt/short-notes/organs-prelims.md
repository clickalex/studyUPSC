# Organs of Government — Prelims Revision

## President mechanics
- Election: elected **MPs of both Houses + elected MLAs of states & UTs with legislatures (Delhi, Puducherry, J&K when leg active)**; proportional representation by single transferable vote (secret ballot).
- MLA vote value = State population (1971 census snapshot) ÷ elected MLAs ÷ 1000.
- Term: 5 yrs; eligible re-election; resignation to VP; vacancy window 6 months max fill.
- Impeachment (Art 61): charge by 1/4 members of either House → 14-day notice → 2/3 pass charging House → other House investigates → 2/3 removal; **quasi-judicial** process.
- Disputed-election adjudicator = SC (Art 71).
- Nomination power (RS 12 Art 80); ordinance Art 123 (max life 6wk+6mo; Krishna Kumar Singh re-promulgation doctrine).

## Vice-President
- Art 63–71; ex-officio **Chairman of Rajya Sabha**; removed by RS resolution (effective 14 days' notice) + LS agreement; election by both Houses **members** (elected+nominated — unlike President, nominated MPs vote).

## Prime Minister & Council
- Art 74(1) — CoM to "aid and advise" (42nd made binding except presidential discretion to reconsider).
- Art 75 — collective responsibility to LS; individual responsibility to President; ministries 15% cap (91st Amdt).
- Oath via President; convention of majority-party leader invite; caretaker conventions on no-confidence loss.

## Parliament numbers & process
- Composition: LS 543 elected max (current 543 seats; constituency freeze till post-2026 delimitation → 2026+ census delimitation) + RS 245 (233 elected + 12 nominated).
- LS seats allocated by census 1971 base currently (87th Amdt rationalised intra-state boundaries on 2001 figures without changing totals).
- Speaker: from LS members; removal by 14-day-notice effective-majority (of all the then members per Kihoto... actually Art 94: passed by "a majority of all the then members"); Deputy-Speaker position (Art 93; Om Birla-era vacancy discourse).
- Quorum = **1/10th of total membership (Art 100(3))**.
- Adjournment motion = LS-only censure-matter device; **No-confidence = LS-only (Art 75(3))**, first by Acharya Kripalani 1963; Calling-attention vs Short-duration (Rule 176) distinctions.
- Joint sitting Art 108 — Speaker presides; exclusion: Money Bills (110), Constitution amendments (368 deadlock rule per Shankari Prasad-era interpretation).
- **Money Bill (Art 110)**: Speaker certificate final; RS 14-day recommendations only; Adj- etc abstain.
- **Parliamentary privilege**: Arts 105 (MPs) & 194 (MLAs): freedom of speech + publication; codification stalled; Keshav Singh (1965) reference beat.

## Supreme Court
- Art 124–147; appointment → collegium (CJI + 4 senior-most); NJAC struck (2015); seniority debate in Khehar misread.
- Jurisdiction: original (Art 131 centre-state etc), writ (Art 32), appellate (133/134/136-SLP), advisory (Art 143).
- Language: SC = English per Art 348 until Parliament provides otherwise; translation projects push to vernacular.

## High Courts
- Art 214–231; HC writ Art 226 — "for any other purpose" makes it wider than Art 32's FR-restriction.
- Judges: appointed by President (CJI+Governor+senior-most advice per collegium extension); removal process same as SC (Art 124(4) applied via 217(1)(b)).
- Power of superintendence over subordinate courts & tribunals in territory (Art 227).
- **Subordinate courts**: district judges Art 233–237 (consultation with HC); not Ex-Parte removal.

## Local-bodies skeleton
- 73rd: 3-tier (village GP/intermediate/district); reservation floors: **SC/ST population-proportional + OBC via state law + minimum 1/3 women** (states push to 50%); State Election Commission (Art 243K) + State Finance Commission (243I).
- 74th: Nagar Panchayat / Municipal Council / Municipal Corporation; Ward committees >3 lakh population; District Planning Committee (243ZD) & Metropolitan Planning Committee (243ZE).
- Anti-defection not auto-apply? Tenth-schedule extends only when state laws bring it within (state-specific).

## Election Commission & offshoots
- Art 324–329; CEC + ECs (since Oct 1993 three-member); **CEC & Other ECs (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act 2023** replaces Court-vision; Anoop Baranwal (Mar 2023) had recommended a PM+LCoP+CJI trio — 2023 Act enacted PM+CabMin+LoP panel with seniority-dominated pool-basis selection; tenure protection differs CEC vs ECs → separation-of-power litigation pending contours.

## AG/CAG/AG-states quick
- AG (Art 76): chief law officer; right of audience all courts; not full CO member; sits in Houses w/o vote.
- CAG (Art 148–151): audits centre+states+PSUs; reports President→Parliament (PAC agenda-setter); **6-year/65-age** tenure; can't hold office-after.
- State AG Art 165 mirror.

## Traps
- Speaker-can-preside-joint-sitting vs 108 nuance (Deputy Speaker can if Speaker absent — Deputy Chairman never presides a joint sitting (Art 118)).
- Nominated members: vote in VP election but **not** in Presidential or impeachment proceedings (VP case = all members; President = **elected only**).
- Article 143 advisory opinions aren't binding (St Xavier's & Keshav Singh among referred history).
- Anti-defection: Tenth Schedule — Speaker decision judicially reviewable only **after** decision (Kihoto Hollohan).
