Parliament: Bills, Sessions and Procedures — Detailed Notes

Composition, bill taxonomy, joint sitting, committees, privileges. Bill-type comparison is the eternal MCQ.

1. Composition & officers

  • President + RS (245 max; 233 elected by MLAs + 12 nominated — arts, science, literature, social service) + LS (530 states + 13 UTs; 543 filled; 2 Anglo-Indian nominated seats ended by 104th). Term 5y (extendable during emergency, Art 83). Quorum: 1/10th. RS = permanent house; 1/3 retire biennially (6-year terms).
  • Officers: Speaker-Deputy Speaker (LS), Chairman = Vice-President (RS), Deputy Chairman RS. Speaker: casting vote, Tenth-Schedule decisions (Kihoto: reviewable), money-bill certificate final, presides joint sittings. Pro-tem Speaker for new-Ls oath; Lok Sabha secretariat under Speaker.

  • Money-bill definition (110): only matters in 110(a)-(g) (taxes, borrowing, CFI custody-appropriation, charged expenditure...). Speaker's certificate final — challenged over Aadhaar Act 2016 + Finance Acts (Rojer Mathew 2019 → 7-judge reference).
  • Charged vs non-charged expenditure (112): charged (President-emoluments, judges, debt, decrees) = discussed, not voted.

3. Sessions, motions, accountability

  • Sessions: Budget (Feb-May), Monsoon (Jul-Sep), Winter (Nov-Dec); summoning gap ≤6 months (85). Question Hour (first hour: starred oral, unstarred written, short-notice urgent), Zero Hour (after QH, Indian innovation).
  • Motions: adjournment, calling attention, censure (individual), no-confidence (LS only, whole CoM), privilege, point of order.
  • Financial procedure: Budget = Annual Financial Statement (112); demands for grants voted (LS); cut motions — policy ₹1, economy specified-sum, token ₹100; Appropriation Bill (Art 114; 121 bars discussion of conduct of judges); Guillotine on last demands day; CFI-CPF (266(1)-(2)), Contingency 267.
  • Committees: PAC (22, Opposition-chair convention), Estimates (30, LS-only), Public Undertakings (22), DRSCs (24, since 1993), BAC, Privileges. Legislative stages: introduce (first), consider (second + committee), passing (third) → other house → President (assent / withhold / return non-money; 201 for state bills reserve).
  • Anti-defection: Tenth Schedule — para 2 (voluntary giving-up / whip), para 4 merger 2/3; Speaker decides with 90-day outer window (Keisham Meghachandra 2020). Privileges (105): free speech in House; Sita Soren 2024: bribery-immunity rejected (P.V. Narasimha Rao overruled).

MCQ hooks

  • Money bill: LS-only + President rec + RS 14 days + no joint sitting + Speaker certifies.
  • Joint sittings (108): 3 historically — Dowry Prohibition 1961, Banking 1978, POTA 2002.
  • Cut motions: policy ₹1, token ₹100; Guillotine = pending demands voted without debate.
  • RS: 12 nominated, 1/3 biennial retirement, never dissolved; MLA open-ballot since 2003.
  • Anti-defection: Speaker, 90-day guideline; merger para 4 = 2/3.
  • Quorum 1/10; summoning gap ≤6 months; Zero Hour post-Question Hour.
