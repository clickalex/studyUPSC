# Polity — Prelims PYQs

> Pattern-faithful MCQs. Exact-PYQ nature hedged; answers original.

**Q1.** Which amendment substituted "armed rebellion" for "internal disturbance" in Article 352?
(a) 42nd (b) 44th (c) 52nd (d) 73rd

**Model answer:** (b). The 44th (1978) also restricted Art 356 abuse and shielded Arts 20–21.

**Q2.** With reference to Money Bills:
1. The Speaker's certification is final.
2. Rajya Sabha can amend and return within 14 days.
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both (d) Neither

**Model answer:** (a). RS can only **recommend** amendments within 14 days; LS may accept/reject.

**Q3.** [PYQ-pattern] The "procedure established by law" clause in Article 21 was borrowed from:
(a) UK (b) USA (c) Japan (d) Ireland

**Model answer:** (c). USA's phrase is "due process of law" — the standard PYQ distinction.

**Q4.** Which DPSP directs equitable distribution of material resources?
(a) Art 38 (b) Art 39(b) & 39(c) (c) Art 44 (d) Art 48

**Model answer:** (b). 39(b) resource distribution; 39(c) prevents concentration of wealth; 44 is UCC; 48 is agri/animal husbandry modernisation.

**Q5.** A no-confidence motion can be introduced in:
(a) Either House (b) Lok Sabha only (c) Rajya Sabha with LS concurrence (d) Neither — only censure motions exist

**Model answer:** (b). Art 75(3) makes the Council collectively responsible to Lok Sabha only.

**Q6.** 73rd and 74th Amendments deal respectively with:
(a) Panchayats and Municipalities
(b) Municipalities and Panchayats
(c) Panchayats and Cooperatives
(d) Official languages and Cooperatives

**Model answer:** (a). Both are 1992 amendments; cooperatives came via the **97th (2011)**.

**Q7.** The doctrine of Basic Structure was first articulated in:
(a) Golaknath (b) Kesavananda Bharati (1973) (c) Minerva Mills (1980) (d) Waman Rao

**Model answer:** (b). Minerva Mills later added the "balance between FR and DPSP" facet.

**Q8.** Which body's members hold office "during the pleasure of the President"?
(a) Governors (b) CAG (c) Election Commissioners (d) SC judges

**Model answer:** (a). Governors serve at President's pleasure (Art 156); the others have fixed tenures/removal processes.

**Q9.** The GST Council is constituted under:
(a) Art 263 (b) Art 280 (c) Art 279A (d) Art 312

**Model answer:** (c). Art 279A via 101st Amendment; Art 263 is the inter-state council, 280 is Finance Commission.

**Q10.** PIL and suo motu jurisdiction questions revolve around Articles:
(a) 32 and 226 (b) 124 and 214 (c) 280 and 324 (d) 352 and 356

**Model answer:** (a). SC writs under Art 32; HC writs under Art 226 (wider as to subject matter).
