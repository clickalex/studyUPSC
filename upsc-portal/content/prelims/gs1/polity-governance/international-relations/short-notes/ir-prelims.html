International Relations — Prelims Revision

UN system drill

  • Founding 1945 (Charter signed San Francisco June; in force 24 Oct); 6 principal organs: GA, UNSC, ECOSOC, Trusteeship Council, ICJ (The Hague), Secretariat.
  • UNSC = 15 members: P5 (US, UK, France, Russia, China) with veto + 10 elected for 2-year terms (regional slots: Africa 3, Asia-Pacific 2, Latin America 2, WEOG 2, Eastern Europe 1). India terms: most recent 2021-22 (8th).
  • GA resolutions non-binding (except budget/internal); UNGA 2023 themes etc; UNSC reform groups: G4 (India, Germany, Japan, Brazil), L.69 (developing bloc), Coffee Club/Uniting for Consensus (Italy, Pakistan etc).
  • Agencies location pairing: UNESCO Paris · UNICEF NY · WHO Geneva · FAO Rome · ILO Geneva · IAEA Vienna · WMO Geneva · IMO London · ICAO Montreal · UPU Bern (oldest level) · ITU Geneva · UNIDO Vienna · WIPO Geneva · IFAD Rome.

Global financial-eco bodies

  • IMF (1944 Bretton Woods; quota votes; SDR basket currencies: USD, EUR, CNY (added 2016), JPY, GBP — weights revised 2022) · World Bank group (IBRD+IDA+IFC+MIGA+ICSID) — Ajay Banga President (2023) · WTO (1995, Marrakesh replacing GATT; dispute-appellate paralysis since 2019 member-blocking; MC13 Abu Dhabi 2024 modest outcomes (fisheries phase-2 partial, e-commerce moratorium extension)).
  • ADB Manila · AIIB Beijing (India largest borrower profile) · NDB Shanghai (BRICS bank, K V Kamath first president; HQ membership added BD-UAE-Uruguay-Egypt etc).
  • FATF Paris — Pakistan gray listed (2018) → removed Oct 2022; India mutual evaluation 2024 regular-follow-up classification.

Groupings membership micro-drill

  • G20 1999 formed; leaders-summit from 2008; members = 19 countries + EU (full members: Argentina, Australia, Brazil, Canada, China, France, Germany, India, Indonesia, Italy, Japan, Korea, Mexico, Russia, Saudi, South Africa, Türkiye, UK, US + EU) → AU became 21st member 2023 New Delhi summit; sequence Indonesia'22 → India'23 → Brazil'24 → South Africa'25 → USA'26.
  • BRICS: Brazil-Russia-India-China-South Africa ('11) → expanded 2024 Jan: Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE (+ Saudi invite nuances; later partner-country tier incl. Indonesia full member Jan 2025 with Uzbekistan/Kazakhstan/Malaysia etc partner-list overlay — verify at exam).
  • SCO: China, Russia, India, Pakistan, Central Asia 4 (Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Uzbekistan) + Iran (2023) & Belarus (2024) full; Iran full'23 Saudi? no.
  • QUAD: India-US-Japan-Australia (revived 2017).
  • I2U2: India-Israel-UAE-US (2022).
  • IMEC: India-Middle East-Europe Economic Corridor (announced G20 Delhi Sep 2023 signatories: India, UAE, Saudi, EU, France, Germany, Italy, US).
  • BIMSTEC: Bay of Bengal 7 (BD, Bhutan, India, Myanmar, Nepal, Sri Lanka, Thailand); HQ Dhaka; 6th summit: Bangkok, April 2025 — confirm venue text before quoting.
  • ASEAN: 10 members (MY-SG-TH-ID-PH-BN-VN-LA-MM-KH); Timor-Leste full member 2025; India = dialogue partner + EAS core state.
  • IPEF (2022; India observer in trade pillar negotiation then partial joiners — supply-chain pillar signed): 14 nations.
  • NATO 32 members (Finland 2023, Sweden 2024 latest); Shanghai line — not related.
  • CDRI (CDRI HQ New Delhi; 2019 launch) · ISA (IISA HQ Gurugram; 121-signatory story; India-France 2015) · IBCA (International Big Cat Alliance; India HQed, 2023 letter) — India-anchored institutional exports trio.
