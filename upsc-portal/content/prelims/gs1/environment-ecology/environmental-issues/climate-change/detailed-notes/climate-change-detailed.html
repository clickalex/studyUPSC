Climate Change Regime — Detailed Notes

UNFCCC → Kyoto → Paris chain, IPCC basics, COP milestones, India's NDC-Panchamrit, carbon markets.

1. Treaty architecture

  • UNFCCC (Rio 1992; entered force 1994; Annex-I developed vs Non-Annex-I; CBDR-RC principle); Kyoto Protocol (1997; 2005 force; binding targets for Annex-I; flexible mechanisms: CDM, JI, emissions trading; first period 2008-12, second 2013-20 Doha); Paris Agreement (2015; force 2016; 'well below 2°C, pursue 1.5°C'; NDCs every 5 years; global stocktake every 5 years; ratchet mechanism; art 6 market mechanisms).
  • COP milestones: COP21 Paris; COP26 Glasgow (2021: Glasgow Climate Pact — 'phase-down' unabated coal, phase-out inefficient subsidies, rulebook completion, art-6 operationalised); COP27 Sharm (2022: loss-and-damage fund agreed); COP28 Dubai (2023: first Global Stocktake — 'transition away from fossil fuels', tripling renewables-doubling efficiency, loss-damage fund operational launch); COP29 Baku (2024: NCQG — $300bn/yr by 2035 new collective goal, carbon-market art-6 finalisation); COP30 Belém (2025, Brazil) in pipeline.

2. Science & assessment

  • IPCC (1988; WGI science, WGII impacts, WGIII mitigation; AR6 cycle 2021-23 — 1.5°C pathway framing, 'brief window'); GHG basket: CO2-CH4-N2O-F-gases; GWP comparisons (CH4 ~28-80 depending horizon); RF concept; CMIP models.
  • Impacts-terms: tipping elements (AMOC, ice sheets, permafrost), ocean acidification, SLR thermal-expansion logic; adaptation vs mitigation vs resilience vocabulary.

3. India: NDC, Panchamrit, markets

  • Panchamrit (COP26 Glasgow, PM): 500 GW non-fossil capacity by 2030; 50% energy from renewables by 2030; 1 bn tonne emission-reduction by 2030; 45% emissions-intensity cut by 2030 (from 2005); net-zero by 2070. NDC updated 2022 (45% intensity + 50% RE-share formalised).
  • Schemes: National Solar Mission; green-energy corridors; PLI solar; PM-KUSUM (solar pumps); PM Surya Ghar rooftop (2024); Lifestyle for Environment (LiFE, 2022); International Solar Alliance (HQ Gurugram, 2015-20 treaty); CDRI (coalition disaster-resilient infrastructure, 2019); LeadIT (Sweden-India industry transition); green hydrogen mission tie-in.
  • Carbon market: Energy Conservation (Amendment) Act 2022 → Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS 2023; offset + compliance tracks; BEE administrator); PAT scheme legacy (perform-achieve-trade); ECBC; green bonds (sovereign 2022-23).

MCQ hooks

  • UNFCCC 1992 Rio; Kyoto binding Annex-I; Paris NDC-ratchet (2°C/1.5°C).
  • Glasgow = phase-down coal (wording ask); Sharm = loss-damage; Dubai = GST 'transition away'; Baku = NCQG $300bn.
  • Panchamrit numbers: 500 GW, 45% intensity, 1 bn t, net-zero 2070.
  • ISA HQ Gurugram; CDRI 2019; LiFE 2022 — initiative-year pairs.
  • IPCC AR6 (2021-23); GWP: methane vs CO2 horizon logic.
  • CCTS 2023 (Energy Conservation Act, amended 2022).
  • Art 6 = markets; GST = 5-yearly stocktake.
