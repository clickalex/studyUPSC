Environmental Issues — Prelims Drill

Climate change mechanics

  • GHGs: CO2 · CH4 (GWP-100 ≈ 27–30) · N2O (≈273) · HFCs · PFCs · SF6 · NF3 (Kyoto basket+ Montreal-siblings); water vapour = feedback not anthropogenic forcing.
  • IPCC: formed 1988 (WMO+UNEP); AR6 (2021–23): observed ~1.1°C warming; likely 1.5°C breach-window early-2030s on current policies; WG I (physical), II (impacts), III (mitigation).
  • UNFCCC: launched Rio-1992 → Kyoto Protocol 1997 (CDM/JI/ET mechanisms; Doha amendment) → Paris 2015 (NDCs, net-zero language, 5-year ratchet, GST cycles).
  • COP beats: COP26 Glasgow 2021 (Methane Pledge 100+ signatories; "phase down" coal wording) → COP27 Sharm el-Sheikh 2022 (Loss & Damage fund agreed) → COP28 Dubai 2023 (first Global Stocktake; "transition away from fossil fuels"; L&D operationalised) → COP29 Baku 2024 (NCQG $300 bn/yr-by-2035 vs $1.3 tn ask; India registered dissent at adoption plenary) → COP30 Belém Nov 2025 (Amazon venue; Brazil lead; TFFF tropical-forest facility launched — check final-decision text before quoting) → COP31 2026 (Australia-Türkiye race resolution pending).
  • India NDC (updated 2022): net-zero 2070; 2030: 500 GW non-fossil capacity, 50% RE electricity-capacity share, 45% emission-intensity cut (vs 2005), 1 bn-tonne CO2 reduction.
  • Domestic instruments: NAPCC 8 missions (Solar; Enhanced Energy Efficiency; Sustainable Habitat; Water; Sustaining the Himalayan Ecosystem; Green India; Sustainable Agriculture; Strategic Knowledge) + LiFE (COP-26 announcement; lifestyle mission); CCTS 2023 carbon market (compliance + offset, MoP/BEE registry); PAT legacy.

Ozone & air

  • Vienna Convention 1985 → Montreal Protocol 1987 (CFC phase-out — "most successful environmental treaty" tag) → Kigali Amendment 2016 (HFC phase-down; India ratified 2021).
  • Ozone hole sits over Antarctica (polar stratospheric clouds chemistry); Arctic weaker episodes.
  • Air pollutants pairs: PM2.5/PM10 (fine/coarse) · SOx-NOx (acid rain precursors — converts sulfates/nitrates) · CO (incomplete-combustion) · O3 (secondary, photochemical; tropospheric ozone = pollutant; stratospheric = shield) · NH3 (PM2.5 ammonium chemistry 2020s focus) · lead (phase-out done) · VOC+NOx → smog.
  • NCAP (2019): updated target up to 40% PM reduction by 2026 vs 2017 base for non-attainment cities; CAQM (2021) runs NCR-GRAP staged curbs.
  • Stubble-burning: Pusa bio-decomposer lines; SMG (crop-residue management) machines subsidised.

Water & waste

  • EIA: 2006 notification (screening/scoping/public hearing/appraisal); SEIAA state level; exempt classes criticism; 2020 draft-EIA controversies.
  • Waste rules (2016 refresh suite): MSW, HWM (2016), BMW (2016+2018 tweaks), EPR plastic (2022), e-waste (2022), battery-waste (2022), EOL tyres (2023+), used-oil (2023), C&D 2016; single-use plastic ban July 2022 (19 items).
  • Land/water figures: Ganga pollution hotspots via CPCB; Namami Gange (2015) NMCG statutory 2016; wetlands rules 2017; Vanadium? no.
  • Pesticides: Endosulfan ban (Kerala Kassargod saga, SC 2011 ratified global Stockholm 2011); glyphosate restrictions state-level; Insecticides Act 1968 → Pesticide Management Bill pending saga.

National programmes

  • Green India Mission (10 Mha target), CAMPA fund (2016 Act — compensatory afforestation money routing), Green Credit Programme (2023 rules implementation critiques 2024), Nagar Van Yojana, National Clean Air Programme, Heat Action Plans (23+ states), Coastal Regulation Zone notifications (CRZ 2011 → 2019 update), Island Protection Zone (2019).

Nuclear & radiation basics

  • Ionizing sources: uranium/radon homes gossip; AERB regulator (1983; statutory upgrade bill narrative); NPCIL operating fleet; Bhabha 3-stage.

Traps

  • Kyoto basket = 6 gases; Montreal covers ozone-depleters; NCAP ≠ legislation.
  • L&D Fund = climate (Paris/Article 8-linked); biodiversity fund = GBF/DSI Cali Fund (2024), not "climate fund".
  • Tropospheric ozone = harmful; stratospheric = beneficial.
  • LiFE is a mission (2022 announcement; NAPCC-adjacent) — not one of the original 8 NAPCC missions.
