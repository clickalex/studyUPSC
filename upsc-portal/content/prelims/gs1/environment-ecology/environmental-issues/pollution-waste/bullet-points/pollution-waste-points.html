Pollution & Waste — Bullet Points

Rule-year flash.

⚡ Air flash

  • AQI 6 bands × 8 pollutants · NCAP 2019 (131 cities, 40% by 2026) · CAQM 2021 NCR · GRAP I-IV.
  • PM2.5 deep-lung · SAFAR forecast · CAAQMS monitors · stubble-decomposer push.

⚡ Water-soil-noise flash

  • DO-BOD-COD + coliform · 351 priority stretches (2022 band) · eutrophication-HABs.
  • As-F groundwater belts · Noise Rules 2000 (silence 100 m; night lower dB).

⚡ Waste flash

  • SWM 2016 segregation · PWM 2021-22: SUP ban 1 Jul 2022 + 4-category EPR.
  • E-Waste 2022 EPR targets · BMW 2016 CBWTF · Batteries 2022 EPR · C&D 2016.
  • Remedies → bio vs phyto remediation · WtE · circular-economy baskets.

⚡ Trap flash

  • NCAP (plan) vs CAQM (statutory body) — instrument-type pair.
  • SUP ban = 19 items (not all plastics) — scope trap.
  • DO inversely related to BOD — direction trap.
  • E-waste rule year = 2022 (not 2016) — rule-date swap.
  • Silence zone = 100 m radius (hospitals-schools-courts) — distance ask.
