Environmental Acts and Conventions — Detailed Notes

India's statutory stack + global convention grid. Instrument-year-scope triples.

1. Indian acts

  • Water Act 1974 (first environment act; CPCB-SPCB creation); Air Act 1981 (1987 amendment: noise-air authority widening); EP Act 1986 (post-Bhopal umbrella act; sec 3 rule-making; EIA notification 1994→2006 under it); WPA 1972 (schedules; 2002 tightening; 2022 CITES-alignment); Forest Conservation Act 1980 → 2023 Van (Sanrakshan evam Samvardhan) Adiniyam (de-reservations: 100-km border strategic projects, zoos-ecotourism exemptions, forest-land definition narrowing — asked as changes).
  • Biological Diversity Act 2002 (NBA-SBB-BMC; 2023 amendment: decriminalisation + AYUSH fast-track + foreign-controlled-company reclassification); NGT Act 2010 (tribunal); Wildlife-transit rules; EIA 2006: category A-B1-B2, scoping-EAC, public consultation, 4-stage process; draft-EIA-2020 controversy (lapsed-pending).

2. Global conventions

  • Species-habitat line: Ramsar 1971 (wetlands; Montreux record), CITES 1975 (appendices trade), CMS Bonn 1979 (migratory), CBD 1992 (Rio; + Cartagena biosafety 2000, Nagoya ABS 2010; Aichi targets 2011-20 → Kunming-Montreal GBF 2022: 30×30), World Heritage 1972.
  • Chemicals-waste line: Montreal 1987 (ODS; London-Copenhagen-Kigali 2016 amendments), Basel 1989 (transboundary waste; plastic-waste amendment 2019), Stockholm 2001 (POPs), Rotterdam 1998 (PIC), Minamata 2013 (mercury).
  • Land-climate line: UNCCD 1994 (land degradation neutrality; COP14 New Delhi 2019), UNFCCC 1992 → Paris 2015 (see climate topic).

MCQ hooks

  • Water 1974 → Air 1981 → EP 1986 (Bhopal-triggered umbrella) — act-year chain.
  • EIA 2006 under EP Act; categories A-B; 4 stages.
  • FCA 1980 → Van Adiniyam 2023 (changes list).
  • Ramsar 1971 vs CITES 1975 vs CMS 1979 — subject-year triple.
  • CBD Rio 1992; Cartagena = biosafety; Nagoya = ABS; GBF 30×30 (2022).
  • Basel = hazardous waste movement; Stockholm = POPs; Rotterdam = PIC; Minamata = Hg.
  • Kigali = HFC phase-down (Montreal amendment).
