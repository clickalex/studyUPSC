Environmental Acts & Institutions — Prelims Drill

Acts timeline (chronological spine)

  1. Wildlife (Protection) Act 1972 — schedules I–VI (2022 amendment rationalised them into four: I & II animal-protection tiers, III plants, IV vermin — provisions took effect April 2023); protected areas NPs/WLS; community reserves; the NBWL is chaired by the PM.
  2. Water (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act 1974 — CPCB/SPCBs born; consent-to-operate regime.
  3. Air (Prevention & Control of Pollution) Act 1981.
  4. Environment (Protection) Act 1986 — post-Bhopal umbrella; Section 5 powers (directions incl closure), Section 3 central rules engine; EIA/CRZ/waste rules hang under it.
  5. Public Liability Insurance Act 1991 (immediate relief).
  6. Biological Diversity Act 2002 — NBA Chennai + SBBs + BMCs; benefit-sharing; 2023 amendment relaxed penalties→monetary exemptions debate.
  7. Scheduled Tribes & Other Traditional Forest Dwellers (Recognition of Forest Rights) Act 2006 — FRA (IFR/CFR/CR titles; Gram Sabha consent the consent-centre) — near-certain pairing with FCA questions.
  8. National Green Tribunal Act 2010 — Delhi + 4 zonal benches (Bhopal, Pune, Kolkata, Chennai); applies 7 listed acts incl EPA/WPA?— WPA not in its schedule — key trap.
  9. Compensatory Afforestation Fund Act (CAMPA) 2016 — fund routing centre-state.
  10. Forest (Conservation) Act 1980 (+ 2023 Amendment) — forest-land diversion prior-approval; 2023 carve-outs (linear projects near borders, PS Ecotourism-y sites, Zoos/safaris, defence RFI list) + NPV rationalisation; Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam rename narration.

Convention ledger (city-year pairing)

  • Stockholm '72 = UNEP foundation conference (not convention).
  • Ramsar 1971 (Iran) wetlands: criteria: 1–9; Montreux Record = threatened register (Chilika removed 2002; Keoladeo placed 1990s).
  • CITES 1973 (Washington) — appendices I/II/III; CoPs: 19 Panama 2022; CoP20 2025 Samarkand (Uzbekistan).
  • CMS Bonn 1979 — migratory species; India hosted CoP13 Gandhinagar 2020.
  • Vienna 1985 → Montreal 1987 → Kigali 2016 (HFCs; India ratified 2021).
  • Basel 1989 (hazardous waste transboundary) · Rotterdam 1998 (PIC prior-informed consent) · Stockholm 2001 (POPs) — trio administered jointly. Minamata 2013 (mercury; India ratified 2018).
  • CBD 1992 (Rio) — COP15 split-phase Montreal 2022 → Kunming-Montreal GBF (30×30; 23 targets/4 goals; DSI → Cali Fund 2024); Nagoya Protocol 2010 (ABS — India ratified 2014-ish); Cartagena 2000 (biosafety; LMO trade).
  • UNFCCC Kyoto Paris chain (see issues sheet).
  • UNCCD 1994 — desertification; India hosted COP14 (Sep 2019, Greater Noida); land-degradation-neutrality target 2030 (India pledge ~26 mn ha restoration).

Committees & awards

  • Stockholm+50 (2022), Rio+20 (2012); UNEP Champions of the Earth awardees from India (Modi'18 program narrative + grassroots etc); Tyler Prize & Earthshot tracks; "Stockholm Water Prize" etc — niche setters.
  • Kigali/year/date pins beat all: Ramsar 1971 · CITES 1973 · Bonn 1979 · Vienna 1985 · Montreal 1987 · Basel 1989 · Rio trio 1992 · Cartagena 2000 · Stockholm-POPs 2001 · Nagoya 2010 · Minamata 2013 · Kigali 2016 · Paris 2015 · K-M GBF 2022.

Institutions quicks

  • MoEFCC apex ministry · CPCB (1974-act body) · CPCB regional· NBWL · CZA · GEAC (genetic-engineering appraisal) · NTCA (Project Tiger's statutory nodal body since WPA amendment 2006) · AERB · CAQM (NCR airshed commission 2021 — statutory by Act 2021) · State Boards & BMCs · NGRBA (2009→2016 NMCG authority status).
  • Judiciary pillars: Vellore Citizens' Welfare Forum (1996) = sustainable development/precautionary/polluter-pays triad case · M C Mehta line (Taj trapezium, Ganga, vehicular) · T N Godavarman continuing mandamus (forests definition debate) · M K Ranjitsinh v UoI (Mar 2024) — climate right Arts 14/21 (GIB powerline case origin) · Great Nicobar NGT-matters 2023–25 chronicling.

Traps

  • WPA 1972 was amended 2022 (schedules compacted) — citation style: "I/II protection tiers."
  • NGT's schedule lists 7 statutes — WPA is not among them (WPA wildlife disputes go elsewhere) — classic trap.
  • FRA 2006 consent sits with Gram Sabha; FCA clearances often precede settlement of FRA claims — tension point in PYQs.
  • CITES CoP20 = Samarkand (not Baku/Belém mix-ups with climate COPs).
  • Ramsar has no headquarters-city-as-treaty-name (its Iranian city-only convention) — don't confuse with "Montreux Record" the register name.
