Environment & Ecology — Prelims PYQs

10 questions
Q1

[PYQ-pattern] Which of the following is under "Right to environment" interpretation lineage?

  1. (a)Art 48A only
  2. (b)Art 21 via M C Mehta line + Art 48A/51A(g)
  3. (c)Fundamental Duties only
  4. (d)Art 19(1)(a)
Show answer
Correct: (b)Judicially read into Art 21; restated by M K Ranjitsinh (2024) via Arts 14+21.
Q2

The Keoladeo National Park vs Montreux Record:

  1. (a)First Indian site; listed 1981, Montreux 1990 due water shortage
  2. (b)listed 1981; NOT on Montreux
  3. (c)world's highest Ramsar elevation
  4. (d)marine category
Show answer
Correct: (a)It was placed on Montreux Record in 1990 — classic shade trap.
Q3

Which hydrologic feature corresponds to the Bhagirathi-Hooghly formation?

  1. (a)Ganga distributary below Farakka
  2. (b)Yamuna tributary
  3. (c)Brahmaputra channel
  4. (d)Indus canal
Show answer
Correct: (a)Downstream distributary story; Farakka barrage moment.
Q4

Consider pairs:

  1. Great Nicobar Biosphere Reserve — UNESCO MAB list
  2. Sundarbans NP — World Heritage
  3. Simlipal NP — Ramsar site
  1. (a)1 and 2 only
  2. (b)2 and 3 only
  3. (c)1 and 3 only
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Great Nicobar BR is on UNESCO's MAB world list (2013); Sundarbans NP is WH (1987) — Simlipal is NOT Ramsar; it's a biosphere reserve.
Q5

The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework's headline coverage goal is:

  1. (a)20×20
  2. (b)30×30
  3. (c)Aichi-17
  4. (d)Paris-2°C
Show answer
Correct: (b)Protect/restore 30% land & sea by 2030.
Q6

The first "living root bridge" heritage protection mention in Indian courts touched which state? (hedged prelims-style)

  1. (a)Meghalaya (root-bridge custodianship recognition trend)
  2. (b)Odisha
  3. (c)Kerala
  4. (d)Uttarakhand
Show answer
Correct: (a)Meghalaya Khasi root bridges; recognition narrative via MAB landscape overlaps.
Q7

Minamata Convention concerns emissions/uses of:

  1. (a)Lead
  2. (b)Mercury
  3. (c)Cadmium
  4. (d)Arsenic
Show answer
Correct: (b)Mercury; India ratified 2018.
Q8

India's CCT Scheme (2023) is administered primarily by:

  1. (a)MoEFCC alone
  2. (b)Ministry of Power with BEE/Grid-Controller roles
  3. (c)Ministry of Finance
  4. (d)NITI Aayog
Show answer
Correct: (b)Anchored by MoP with BEE as registry administrator.
Q9

The Nilgiri langur / Nilgiri tahr pairings correspond to:

  1. (a)Silent Valley NP & Eravikulam NP respectively (Kerala)
  2. (b)Bandipur & Nagarhole (Karnataka)
  3. (c)Ranthambore & Sariska (Rajasthan)
  4. (d)Sunset point & Dooars (WB)
Show answer
Correct: (a)Both Kerala icons; Silent Valley episode-anchor for older PYQs.
Q10

The Diclofenac ban story is connected with the collapse of:

  1. (a)House sparrow
  2. (b)Vulture populations (Gyps species)
  3. (c)Great Indian Bustard
  4. (d)Olive ridley turtle
Show answer
Correct: (b)NSAID livestock drug caused renal failure in vultures; ban (2006) + captive-breeding (BNHS Pinjore) storyline.
