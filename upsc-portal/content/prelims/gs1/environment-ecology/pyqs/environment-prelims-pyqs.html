Environment & Ecology — Prelims PYQs

Q1. [PYQ-pattern] Which of the following is under "Right to environment" interpretation lineage? (a) Art 48A only (b) Art 21 via M C Mehta line + Art 48A/51A(g) (c) Fundamental Duties only (d) Art 19(1)(a)

Model answer: (b). Judicially read into Art 21; restated by M K Ranjitsinh (2024) via Arts 14+21.

Q2. The Keoladeo National Park vs Montreux Record: (a) First Indian site; listed 1981, Montreux 1990 due water shortage (b) listed 1981; NOT on Montreux (c) world's highest Ramsar elevation (d) marine category

Model answer: (a). It was placed on Montreux Record in 1990 — classic shade trap.

Q3. Which hydrologic feature corresponds to the Bhagirathi-Hooghly formation? (a) Ganga distributary below Farakka (b) Yamuna tributary (c) Brahmaputra channel (d) Indus canal

Model answer: (a). Downstream distributary story; Farakka barrage moment.

Q4. Consider pairs:

  1. Great Nicobar Biosphere Reserve — UNESCO MAB list
  2. Sundarbans NP — World Heritage
  3. Simlipal NP — Ramsar site

(a) 1 and 2 only (b) 2 and 3 only (c) 1 and 3 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

Model answer: (a). Great Nicobar BR is on UNESCO's MAB world list (2013); Sundarbans NP is WH (1987) — Simlipal is NOT Ramsar; it's a biosphere reserve.

Q5. The Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework's headline coverage goal is: (a) 20×20 (b) 30×30 (c) Aichi-17 (d) Paris-2°C

Model answer: (b). Protect/restore 30% land & sea by 2030.

Q6. The first "living root bridge" heritage protection mention in Indian courts touched which state? (hedged prelims-style) (a) Meghalaya (root-bridge custodianship recognition trend) (b) Odisha (c) Kerala (d) Uttarakhand

Model answer: (a). Meghalaya Khasi root bridges; recognition narrative via MAB landscape overlaps.

Q7. Minamata Convention concerns emissions/uses of: (a) Lead (b) Mercury (c) Cadmium (d) Arsenic

Model answer: (b). Mercury; India ratified 2018.

Q8. India's CCT Scheme (2023) is administered primarily by: (a) MoEFCC alone (b) Ministry of Power with BEE/Grid-Controller roles (c) Ministry of Finance (d) NITI Aayog

Model answer: (b). Anchored by MoP with BEE as registry administrator.

Q9. The Nilgiri langur / Nilgiri tahr pairings correspond to: (a) Silent Valley NP & Eravikulam NP respectively (Kerala) (b) Bandipur & Nagarhole (Karnataka) (c) Ranthambore & Sariska (Rajasthan) (d) Sunset point & Dooars (WB)

Model answer: (a). Both Kerala icons; Silent Valley episode-anchor for older PYQs.

Q10. The Diclofenac ban story is connected with the collapse of: (a) House sparrow (b) Vulture populations (Gyps species) (c) Great Indian Bustard (d) Olive ridley turtle

Model answer: (b). NSAID livestock drug caused renal failure in vultures; ban (2006) + captive-breeding (BNHS Pinjore) storyline.
