Biodiversity of India: Hotspots & Protected Areas — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Which of the following global biodiversity hotspots extend into Indian territory? 1. Himalaya 2. Indo-Burma 3. Sundaland 4. Western Ghats–Sri Lanka

  1. (a)1, 2 and 4
  2. (b)1 and 4
  3. (c)2, 3 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (d)Nicobar Islands = Sundaland.
Q2

Consider the following statements: 1. A National Park cannot have its boundaries altered except by a resolution of the State Legislature. 2. Grazing of livestock can be permitted inside a Wildlife Sanctuary by the Chief Wildlife Warden. 3. Community Reserves are declared on land owned by the government.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Community Reserves are on private/community land.
Q3

Match the following superlatives: 1. Largest National Park — Hemis 2. First Biosphere Reserve — Nilgiri 3. Largest Tiger Reserve — Nagarjunsagar-Srisailam 4. State with most Ramsar sites — Uttar Pradesh Which of the above are correctly matched?

  1. (a)1, 2 and 3
  2. (b)1, 2 and 4
  3. (c)2, 3 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)Tamil Nadu (20) has the most Ramsar sites.
Q4

As per the India State of Forest Report 2023, forest cover as a percentage of India's geographical area is about:

  1. (a)19.5%
  2. (b)21.8%
  3. (c)25.2%
  4. (d)33%
Show answer
Correct: (b)21.76%; with tree cover 25.17%.
Q5

Consider the following statements about the Biological Diversity Act, 2002: 1. The National Biodiversity Authority is headquartered in Chennai. 2. People's Biodiversity Registers are prepared by Biodiversity Management Committees at the local body level. 3. The 2023 amendment exempted registered AYUSH practitioners from benefit-sharing obligations for codified traditional knowledge.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q6

The Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2022:

  1. (a)Increased the number of schedules from four to six
  2. (b)Reduced the number of schedules to four and added one for CITES-listed species
  3. (c)Removed elephants from Schedule I
  4. (d)Abolished the National Board for Wild Life
Show answer
Correct: (b)Six schedules became four.
Q7

Which of the following species has a conservation breeding programme at Sam and Ramdevra in Rajasthan?

  1. (a)Lesser florican
  2. (b)Great Indian Bustard
  3. (c)Jerdon's courser
  4. (d)Forest owlet
Show answer
Correct: (b)~60 chicks bred by 2025.
Q8

The Gandhi Sagar Wildlife Sanctuary was in the news in 2025 as:

  1. (a)India's 58th Tiger Reserve
  2. (b)The second site for the reintroduction of the cheetah
  3. (c)A new Ramsar site
  4. (d)A dugong conservation reserve
Show answer
Correct: (b)Kuno was the first site (2022).
Q9

Consider the following statements about Biosphere Reserves in India: 1. They are a legal category under the Wild Life (Protection) Act, 1972. 2. Of the 18 reserves, 12 are part of UNESCO's World Network under the Man and Biosphere Programme. 3. Panna was the most recent Indian addition to the World Network.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Biosphere Reserve is not a WPA category.
Q10

The first population estimate of the Gangetic river dolphin under Project Dolphin, released in 2025, counted approximately:

  1. (a)1,200
  2. (b)3,300
  3. (c)6,300
  4. (d)12,000
Show answer
Correct: (c)6,327 in the Ganga–Brahmaputra system.
