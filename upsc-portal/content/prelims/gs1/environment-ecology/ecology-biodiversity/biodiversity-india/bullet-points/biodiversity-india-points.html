Biodiversity India — Bullet Points

Hotspot-term flash.

Hotspot flash

  • 4 hotspots → Himalaya · Indo-Burma · Western Ghats-Sri Lanka · Sundaland (Nicobar).
  • Megadiverse 17-18 club · endemism peaks: Ghats amphibians, Himalaya flora.
  • Invasives → Lantana, water hyacinth, Prosopis juliflora.

IUCN flash

  • EX → EW → CR → EN → VU → NT → LC (+DD) · threatened = CR+EN+VU.
  • GIB-vulture CR · tiger EN · snow leopard VU · criteria = decline-range-fragmentation.
  • Red List ≠ Red Data Book.

Law flash

  • BD Act 2002 → NBA (Chennai) + SBB + BMC · PBR at BMC level.
  • 2023 amendment → decriminalised, AYUSH fast-track, adjudication.
  • Nagoya 2010 = ABS · in-situ triad = park-sanctuary-biosphere · ex-situ = NBPGR-zoo-cryo.

Trap flash

  • Nicobar → Sundaland (not Indo-Burma) — hotspot trap.
  • NT ≠ threatened — category ladder ask.
  • NBA seat = Chennai (not Delhi).
  • FRA 2006 ≠ BD Act 2002 — act-pair (MFP vs ABS).
  • Keystone = functional; flagship = symbolic — role swap.
