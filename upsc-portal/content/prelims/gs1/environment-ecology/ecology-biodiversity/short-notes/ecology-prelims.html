Ecology & Biodiversity — Prelims Drill

Bio-foundations

  • Ecosystem functions: producers → consumers → decomposers; 10% energy-transfer law (Lindeman); productivity (GPP=NPP+respiration); food chains (grazing vs detritus) & webs.
  • Biomes quick: tundra · taiga (boreal conifer) · temperate deciduous · Mediterranean · grassland · savanna · desert · tropical rainforest; India spans tropical forest→montane→desert.

Conservation hotspots (global designation logic)

  • Criteria (Conservation International): ≥1,500 endemic vascular plants + ≥70% habitat loss. India's four: (1) The Himalayas (eastern), (2) Indo-Burma, (3) Western Ghats & Sri Lanka, (4) Sundaland (Nicobar group).

Protected-area numbers (use ranges at exam)

  • National Parks ~107 · Wildlife Sanctuaries ~570+ · Conservation Reserves ~100 · Community Reserves ~200+ · Tiger Reserves 57+ (newest: Ratapani & Guru Ghasidas–Tamor Pingla cohort 2024–25) · Elephant Reserves ~33 · Ramsar sites 80+ (2025 band) · Biosphere Reserves 18 (Nilgiri first 1986; latest Panna 2020; Khangchendzonga UNESCO-mixed 2016; Great Nicobar MAB-listed).
  • Tiger census 2022: 3,682 mean (band 3,167–3,925); first-elephant-synchronised count underway/2023-25 (~27–30k band); Snow Leopard ~718 (2024, first estimate); Gangetic dolphin ~6,300 (Dolphin count 2024 release band) — quote as approximations.

Species ↔ habitat pair-index

  • GIB → Desert NP (Raj) + Abdasa (Guj) — CR · Hangul → Dachigam (JK) · Sangai → Keibul Lamjao (Manipur; looks like "dancing deer") · Lion-tailed macaque → Silent Valley (Kerala) · Pygmy hog → Manas (Assam) · Jerdon's courser → Sri Lankamalleswara CWS (AP) · Red Panda → Singalila/Khangchendzonga (WB/Sikkim) · Nilgiri Tahr → Eravikulam (Kerala) · Ganges river dolphin → Vikramshila GS dolphin sanctuary (Bihar) · Dugong → Palk Bay — Gulf of Mannar (TN) · Olive Ridley turtle → Gahirmatha-Rushikulya (Odisha) · Leatherback → G Nicolbar (Great Nicobar) · Great Hornbill → Namdapha & W Ghats forests · Sarus crane → ETawah UP wetlands · Black-necked crane → Tso Kar/Changthang (Ladakh) · Bengal florican → Dibru-Saikhowa & Assam terai · Malabar civet → Kerala-WG rainforests (critically-endangered lore).

IUCN status vocabulary

LC → NT → VU (vulnerable) → EN → CR → EW (extinct in wild) → EX. CITES Appendix gradation: App I (commercial-trade prohibition) / App II (regulated) / App III (national request).

Biodiversity institutions

  • NBA (National Biodiversity Authority, 2002 Act; Chennai HQ) + SBBs + BMCs (local bodies); Access & Benefit Sharing (Nagoya echo); People's Biodiversity Registers (PBR).
  • Institutions: WII Dehradun · ZSI Kolkata · BSI Kolkata/Howrah · FSI Dehradun (ISFR biennial) · ICFRE Dehradun · WWF-India · BNHS Mumbai · CZA · GEAC (GM-crop regulator lineage) · IBCA (2023; big-cat global alliance HQ India).

National statuses/memoranda

  • National animal tiger · bird Indian peafowl · flower lotus · fruit mango · tree banyan · river Ganga (decl 2008 "National River") · aquatic animal Ganges dolphin (2009); national-heritage-animal elephant (2010 declaration).
  • Project Tiger 1973 (Corbett first) · Project Elephant 1992 · Project Cheetah 2022 (Kuno; SA-batches 2023) · Project Hangul · Project Rhino (re. Kaziranga line) · Recovery Programmes: 22 species incl GIB, vultures (BNHS captive-breeding — Diclofenac ban 2006 Vet use).

Vegetation shorthand (Champion & Seth flavour)

  • Tropical wet evergreen (Andaman + WG Kerala-Karnataka; rosewood/ebony) · semi-evergreen · moist deciduous (teak/sal) · dry deciduous · thorn (NW) · tidal (Sundarbans mangrove: sundari) · Himalayan belts: moist temperate (oak) → dry temperate → alpine.
  • Mangroves share: Sundarbans largest (WB) > Bhitarkanika (Odisha) > Krishna/ Godavari-Coringa deltas · Pichavaram (TN) · Baratang-Great Nicobar (A&N).

Traps

  • Keibul Lamjao sits ON Loktak — the world's only floating NP (phumdi mats).
  • Edge-case tiger-reserve membership shifts across notifications — verify against the latest NTCA list; examples often probed: Valmiki (Bihar), Dampa (Mizoram), Parambikulam (Kerala), Debrigarh (Odisha).
  • Khangchendzonga is mixed WHS + BR; Cold Desert BR = Kutch (designated 2008 — not the Himalayan one).
  • Namdapha = tiger+leopard+snow-leopard+clouded-leopard "four big cats in one park" tag.
