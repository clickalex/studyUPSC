Ecosystems — Structure and Function

Levels, energy-nutrient logic, succession, productivity, aquatic ecosystems. Concept-pair recall.

1. Structural levels

  • Organism → population → community → ecosystem (biotic + abiotic interacting) → biome (large climatic region) → biosphere. Ecotone = transition zone between ecosystems (mangrove-estuary, grassland-forest edge) with edge effect; ecological niche = functional role (habitat = address, niche = profession).
  • Components: producers (autotrophs), consumers (herbivore-carnivore-omnivore; trophic levels), decomposers (fungi-bacteria — mineralisers), abiotic (inorganic-organic-climatic).

2. Energy flow & nutrient cycling

  • Energy: one-way, ~10% transfer between trophic levels (Lindeman); food chain (grazing vs detritus) + food web stability; energy pyramids always upright (biomass-number pyramids can invert — parasitic-tree chains).
  • Nutrient cycles: gaseous (C, N, O — global pools in atmosphere) vs sedimentary (P, S — crustal pools); phosphorus has no significant gaseous phase; nitrogen fixation (biological: rhizobia-cyanobacteria; industrial: Haber).
  • Ecological succession: bare area → pioneer (lichens on rock) → seral stages → climax community; primary (new substrate) vs secondary (disturbed, soil intact); hydrarch vs xerarch directions.

3. Productivity & aquatic systems

  • GPP − respiration = NPP; NPP rank: coral reefs-tropical rainforest-estuary-mangrove top, open ocean-desert bottom (ocean low per-unit but vast); standing crop vs turnover.
  • Wetlands: Ramsar definition (1971; used by humans) — India ~90 Ramsar sites (2025 band, highest in Asia); functions: flood buffer, recharge, carbon sink; threats: eutrophication-invasion-reclamation.
  • Mangroves: halophytes, viviparous germination, pneumatophores; sundari (Sundarbans); cyclone-shield value. Corals: polyp + zooxanthellae symbiosis; bleaching = symbiont expulsion under heat stress; India: Gulf of Kutch, Gulf of Mannar, Lakshadweep, Andaman-Nicobar. Estuaries: brackish, high-nutrient nursery (hooghly-mahanadi).

MCQ hooks

  • 10% law = Lindeman; energy one-way, nutrients cycle — direction pair.
  • Pyramid of energy always upright; number/biomass can invert.
  • Gaseous cycles (C-N-O) vs sedimentary (P-S) — cycle-type pair.
  • Ecotone = transition; edge effect high diversity claim often asked.
  • Coral bleaching = zooxanthellae loss (not polyp death directly).
  • Mangrove adaptations: vivipary + pneumatophores — term pair.
  • NPP: estuary-reef high, open ocean low (per-unit-area trap).
