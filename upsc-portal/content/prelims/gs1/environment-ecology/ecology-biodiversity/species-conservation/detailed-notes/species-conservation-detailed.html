Species Conservation Programmes — Detailed Notes

Flagship projects, legal schedules, treaties. Project-year-agency recall.

1. Flagship projects

  • Project Tiger (1973; NTCA statutory 2006; 58 reserves 2025 band; M-STrIPES monitoring); Project Elephant (1992; MIKE + Gaja Yatra + corridor push); Project Snow Leopard (2009; GSLEP global); Project Lion (2020; Gir landscape + Barda expansion); Project Dolphin (2021; Gangetic + oceanic; Dolphin Day 5 Oct); Project Cheetah (2022; Kuno-Palpur translocation from Namibia-South Africa; Gandhi Sagar second site); vulture action plan (diclofenac ban 2006; multi-vulture centres); GIB breeding centre (Rajasthan, Jaisalmer Sam).
  • Results-recall band: tiger ~3,600+ (2022 AITE census), elephants ~30,000, lions ~670+ (2025 count band), rhino ~4,000 (Kaziranga core).

2. Legal instruments

  • WPA 1972: Schedules I-VI (I-II highest protection; V = vermin; VI = plants); 2022 amendment: schedule rationalisation aligned to CITES appendices; hunters-licence frame; sanctuary-park notification + settlement rights.
  • International: CITES (1975; appendices I-III; trade permits), CMS/Bonn (migratory species; India is a party), MIKE-ETIS (elephant monitoring), IBCA (International Big Cat Alliance, HQ India, 2023-24 treaty frame), Global Tiger Forum (New Delhi), GSLEP (snow leopard, Bishkek).
  • Captive-breeding-cum-reintroduction: gharial (1975 onward), pygmy hog (Assam), vultures (PIN), sangai (Manipur) — species-site pairs asked.

MCQ hooks

  • Tiger 1973-NTCA 2006; Elephant 1992-MIKE; Dolphin 2021; Lion 2020; Cheetah 2022 Kuno.
  • WPA: I-II top, V vermin, VI plants; 2022 = CITES alignment.
  • CITES = trade; CMS = migratory — treaty pair.
  • IBCA HQ = India; GTF = New Delhi.
  • Diclofenac → vulture crash (2006 ban) — chain ask.
  • Cheetah sites: Kuno first, Gandhi Sagar second.
  • GIB CR + Sam (Jaisalmer) centre — species-site pair.
