## Conventions quick-pair
- **Ramsar** (1971) — wetlands; Montreux Record = threatened register.
- **CITES** (Washington 1973) — flora/fauna trade; app I/II/III gradation; **CoP19 Panama 2022; CoP20 2025 Samarkand, Uzbekistan**.
- **CBD** (Rio 1992) — with **Nagoya ABS (2010)**, **Cartagena biosafety (2000)**, **Kunming-Montreal GBF (2022, 30×30)**.
- **Bonn CMS** (1979) — migratory species (India hosted CoP13 Gandhinagar 2020).
- **UNFCCC** (Rio 1992) + Kyoto + Paris.
- **UNCCD** Bonn 1994 — India COP14 (2019, New Delhi NCR) land degradation neutrality goal 2030.
- **Stockholm Convention** (POPs, 2001); **Rotterdam** PIC (1998); **Basel** (Waste 1989); **Minamata** (Mercury 2013 — Japan city).
- Montreal Protocol 1987 (+Kigali 2016 HFC amendment; India ratified Kigali 2021).
- VCLT not environmental.
