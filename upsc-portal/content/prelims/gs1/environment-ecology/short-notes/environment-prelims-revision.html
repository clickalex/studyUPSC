Environment & Ecology — Prelims Rapid Revision

Species/state/conservation pairing floods this area. Pair with Mains env set for narrative.

Conventions quick-pair

  • Ramsar (1971) — wetlands; Montreux Record = threatened register.
  • CITES (Washington 1973) — flora/fauna trade; app I/II/III gradation; CoP19 Panama 2022; CoP20 2025 Samarkand, Uzbekistan.
  • CBD (Rio 1992) — with Nagoya ABS (2010), Cartagena biosafety (2000), Kunming-Montreal GBF (2022, 30×30).
  • Bonn CMS (1979) — migratory species (India hosted CoP13 Gandhinagar 2020).
  • UNFCCC (Rio 1992) + Kyoto + Paris.
  • UNCCD Bonn 1994 — India COP14 (2019, New Delhi NCR) land degradation neutrality goal 2030.
  • Stockholm Convention (POPs, 2001); Rotterdam PIC (1998); Basel (Waste 1989); Minamata (Mercury 2013 — Japan city).
  • Montreal Protocol 1987 (+Kigali 2016 HFC amendment; India ratified Kigali 2021).
  • VCLT not environmental.

IUCN categories & Red List

LC → NT → VU → EN → CR → EW → EX (thermometer). India Red Data Book by BNHS/ZSI-BSI drafts.

  • Snow leopard ~718 (2024 estimate) — Lahaul-Spiti strongest density.

Vegetation-biome shorthand

  • Tropical evergreen (Malabar coast, Andamans) ↔ rosewood/ebony; aldrovanda? no.
  • Deciduous teak/sal trades pole positions.
  • Thorn forests (NW arid).
  • Montane (Shola-grassland mosaic in Ghats; insectivorous species).
  • Mangroves: Sundarbans largest; Bhitarkanika (Odisha), Pichavaram (TN), Baratang.

Pollution & waste rules

  • Air Act 1981 / Water Act 1974; EPA 1986 umbrella (post-Bhopal 1984).
  • NGT Act 2010; NCAP 2019 revised target 40% PM reduction by 2026; CAQM-GRAP NCR.
  • EPR expansions 2022 (plastics, e-waste, batteries); tyres+EUSD added 2023-24.
  • Plastic ban July 2022 (19 SUP items); PM SUP capex counters.

Climate set

  • NDC: net-zero 2070; 2030 500 GW non-fossil · 50% RE capacity · 45% EI cut · 1 bn tn CO2 avoidance.
  • PAT → CCTS 2023; 8 NAPCC missions + LiFE (2022).
  • IPCC AR6 synthesis (1.1°C reached; 1.5°C band risk by 2030s).
  • COP beat: 26 Glasgow (methane, phase-down) → 27 Sharm (L&D fund agreement) → 28 Dubai GST ("transition away") → 29 Baku ($300 bn NCQG; India dissent) → 30 Belém Nov 2025 (Brazil; TFFF agenda — verify final outcomes) → 31 in 2026 (Australia / Türkiye host race).
