Geography — Prelims PYQs

Map-pairing & pattern MCQs — answers original.

Q1. [PYQ-pattern] Between India and East Asia, the navigation-time and petroleum-shipment matter revolves around: (a) Red Sea (b) Mediterranean Sea (c) Malacca Strait (d) Bering Strait

Model answer: (c). Malacca is the choke point for India's east-bound energy & trade flows.

Q2. Which of these rivers does not flow into the Bay of Bengal? (a) Godavari (b) Mahanadi (c) Narmada (d) Kaveri

Model answer: (c). Narmada (west-flowing, joins Arabian Sea through Gulf of Khambhat estuary).

Q3. Consider pairs:

  1. Pampas — Argentina
  2. Prairies — Australia
  3. Steppes — Eurasia

Which are correct? (a) 1 and 3 only (b) 2 only (c) 1 and 2 only (d) 1, 2 and 3

Model answer: (a). Australia's grasslands are the Downs; pair 2 is the distractor.

Q4. Which tributary-side pairing is correct for the Ganga? (a) Ramganga — right bank (b) Kosi — right bank (c) Ghaghara — left bank (d) Son — left bank

Model answer: (c). Ghaghara and Ramganga/Kosi are left-bank; Son is right-bank (the "forgotten" right-bank test).

Q5. The laterite soils of India are typically found in: (a) The Deccan trap highlands (b) High-rainfall western ghats & Meghalaya zones (c) The alluvial fans of Punjab (d) The cold-arid zone of Ladakh

Model answer: (b). Laterite forms under high rainfall + leaching (Western Ghats scarps, parts of eastern hills, Kerala coast).

Q6. Western disturbances primarily bring: (a) Winter rain to northwestern India (b) Pre-monsoon norwesters to Bengal (c) Cyclones in the Arabian Sea (d) Summer hail in the Deccan

Model answer: (a). Mediterranean-origin extra-tropical systems yield NW winter rains (Dec–Feb), crucial for rabi wheat.

Q7. Chilika lake is: (a) A freshwater lake in Kerala (b) Asia's largest lagoon, Odisha, Ramsar-listed (c) A glacial lake in Uttarakhand (d) An artificial irrigation reservoir, Rajasthan

Model answer: (b). Brackish-water lagoon; first Indian Ramsar site (with Keoladeo, 1981).

Q8. Which of the following describes a cold desert in India? (a) Spiti valley (b) Great Rann (c) Marusthali (d) Bundelkhand

Model answer: (a). Spiti & Ladakh are cold deserts; Thar = hot desert.

Q9. The "Ring of Fire" refers to: (a) Mid-Atlantic ridge (b) Circum-Pacific tectonic belt (c) Alpine-Himalayan orogenic belt (d) East African rift valley

Model answer: (b). Concentrates most of the world's earthquakes & active volcanoes.

Q10. National Waterway-1 runs along: (a) Krishna, between Vijayawada & Kakinada (b) Ganga, between Prayagraj & Haldia (c) Brahmaputra, between Sadiya & Dhubri (d) Kerala backwaters, Kollam to Kozhikode

Model answer: (b). NW-2 is Sadiya-Dhubri (Brahmaputra); NW-3 is Kerala's Kollam-Kozhikode.
