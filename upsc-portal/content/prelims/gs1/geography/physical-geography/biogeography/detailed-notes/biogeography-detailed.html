Biogeography — Detailed Notes

Biomes, diversity metrics, island theory, soil-vegetation links. Light-read area with trick MCQs.

1. Biomes & vegetation belts

  • Whittaker's frame: temperature x precipitation → biome. Sequence pole-to-equator: tundra → taiga (boreal coniferous) → temperate deciduous-grassland → Mediterranean scrub → desert → tropical rainforest (max NPP) → savanna.
  • India forest types (Champion-Seth 1968, 16 major groups): tropical evergreen (Western Ghats-NE-Andaman), tropical deciduous (most extensive — moist then dry), tropical thorn (NW), montane (oak-conifer above), littoral-swamp (mangroves), alpine. Forest Survey of India (ISFR biennial): forest+tree cover ~25% of geographic area (verify latest ISFR figure).

2. Biodiversity metrics & hotspots

  • Metrics: alpha (within habitat), beta (between-habitat change), gamma (landscape total). Endemism: species confined to an area (India: high in Himalaya-Western Ghats-Andaman). India's 4 hotspots (of 36 global): Himalaya, Indo-Burma, Western Ghats-Sri Lanka, Sundaland (Nicobars).
  • Species roles: keystone (ecosystem-defining: tigers, sea otters), flagship (iconic: panda), umbrella, indicator; IUCN categories: EX-EW-CR-EN-VU-NT-LC-DD-NE; Red List updates annual.
  • Island biogeography (MacArthur-Wilson 1967): species richness rises with area, falls with isolation; immigration-extinction balance; SIBA-conservation design basis.

3. Soil-vegetation geography

  • Soil classification (ICAR 8-fold): alluvial (largest; khadar-new vs bangar-old), black/regur (cotton; Deccan basalt, moisture-retentive, self-ploughing), red (ferruginous, weathered crystalline), laterite (intense leaching; brick-tea-cashew), arid (calcified; kankar layers), saline-usara (alkaline; irrigation mismanagement), peaty-organic (Kerala), forest-mountain soils.
  • Soil-vegetation coupling: laterite ↔ degraded deciduous; black ↔ dry savanna-ish zones; alluvium ↔ agriculture belt; erosion (sheet-rill-gully-ravine — Chambal badlands) + conservation (contour bunding, watershed, agroforestry).

MCQ hooks

  • India's 4 hotspots; Andaman-Nicobar chain sits under the Sundaland hotspot.
  • Champion-Seth classification 1968; tropical deciduous = most extensive type.
  • Alpha-beta-gamma definitions verbatim.
  • Black soil = regur = Deccan basalt; laterite = leaching (not lava).
  • Khadar = newer alluvium (flood-refreshed) vs bangar older.
  • MacArthur-Wilson = island theory (area up, richness up).
