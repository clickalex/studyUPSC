# Biogeography: Soils & Natural Vegetation — Prelims Fact Card

> Prelims GS-I › Geography › Physical Geography. Soil-type traits (ICAR classification) and biome–climate matching.

## Soil formation & profile
- Factors (Jenny): parent material, climate, relief, organisms, time (**Cl-O-R-P-T**). Processes: weathering → humification → eluviation/illuviation, **laterisation** (hot-wet; silica leached, Fe/Al oxides stay), **podzolisation** (cool-wet coniferous; acidic, ash-grey), **calcification** (semi-arid; CaCO₃), **salinisation/alkalisation** (arid; capillary rise), **gleisation** (waterlogged). Profile: **O** (organic) → **A** (topsoil, humus; eluviation) → **E** → **B** (illuviation; subsoil) → **C** (regolith) → **R** (bedrock). Texture triangle (sand-silt-clay; loam best); pH (6.5–7.5 ideal); **soil health card** (12 parameters — NPK, S, Zn, Fe, Cu, Mn, B, pH, EC, OC); NBSS&LUP (Nagpur).

## Indian soils (ICAR 8 major)
| Soil | Area & regions | Traits | Crops |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Alluvial** (~43% area) | Indo-Gangetic-Brahmaputra plains, coastal deltas | **khadar** (new, fertile, floodplain) vs **bhangar** (old, *kankar* nodules); rich in potash & lime, poor N, P, humus; *bhabar*, *terai* belts | wheat, rice, sugarcane, jute |
| **Black/Regur** (~15%) | Deccan Trap — Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, Telangana, N Karnataka | basalt-derived; clayey (montmorillonite); **self-ploughing** (cracks), moisture-retentive; rich Ca, Mg, K, Fe, Al; poor N, P, humus | cotton, soybean, jowar, sugarcane |
| **Red & yellow** (~18%) | peninsular — TN, Karnataka, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, Jharkhand, Telangana | crystalline igneous/metamorphic; iron oxide (red), hydrated (yellow); porous, poor N-P-humus | millets, groundnut, pulses (with irrigation rice) |
| **Laterite** | Western Ghats (Karnataka, Kerala, Goa), Odisha, Meghalaya, Assam, MP; high rainfall + high temp | leached; Fe/Al rich; acidic; hardens on exposure — **bricks**; poor fertility; needs manure | tea, coffee, cashew, rubber, tapioca |
| **Arid/desert** | Rajasthan W of Aravalli, Gujarat, Haryana | sandy; saline; low humus; **kankar** layer restricts infiltration; high phosphate; low N | bajra, pulses with irrigation (IGNP) |
| **Saline/alkaline (usar, reh, kallar)** | Gujarat, Rajasthan, Punjab-Haryana (canal irrigation), coastal deltas, Sundarbans | Na, K, Mg salts; poor drainage; **gypsum** reclamation; ~6.7 Mha degraded | — |
| **Peaty/marshy** | Kerala (Kuttanad), Odisha coast, Sundarbans, Bihar | high organic (40%), black, acidic; below sea level — Kuttanad | rice |
| **Forest/mountain** | Himalaya, Ghats | acidic loamy on slopes; podzols in conifers; low humus higher up | tea, fruits, spices |
- Erosion: sheet, rill, **gully** (ravines — Chambal *badlands*), wind; **desertification** (~30% land degraded — ISRO Atlas 2016; Land Degradation Neutrality target 26 Mha by 2030; UNCCD COP14 Delhi 2019); conservation — contour ploughing/bunding, terracing, shelterbelts, strip cropping, afforestation, check dams, mulching, **zero tillage**, PM Krishi Sinchayee, Watershed (IWMP), Soil Health Card (2015), Neem-coated urea, PM-PRANAM (2023, alt fertilisers), Micro-irrigation Fund.

## World biomes & natural vegetation (climate → vegetation → soil → fauna)
| Biome | Climate | Vegetation & traits | Regions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Equatorial/tropical rainforest (selva)** | Af; 25–27 °C, >200 cm, no dry season | evergreen, layered canopy, epiphytes, lianas, buttresses, hardwoods (mahogany, ebony, rosewood), highest biodiversity, latosols | Amazon, Congo, SE Asia, W Ghats-NE India |
| Tropical monsoon | Am; wet–dry | deciduous teak, sal, sandalwood; shed leaves in dry season | India, SE Asia |
| **Savanna/tropical grassland** | Aw; distinct dry season, 50–150 cm | tall grasses (elephant grass), scattered acacia/baobab; big game; fires | **Llanos** (Venezuela), **Campos** (Brazil), E Africa, N Australia |
| Hot desert | BWh; <25 cm | xerophytes (cactus, date palm), succulents, salt-tolerant; oases | Sahara, Thar, Arabian, Atacama, Kalahari-Namib, Sonoran, Great Victoria |
| **Mediterranean** | Cs; dry summer, wet winter | sclerophyll — olive, cork oak, citrus, vines, maquis/chaparral | Med basin, California, C Chile, Cape, SW & S Australia |
| **Temperate grassland** | BSk/Dfa; continental interiors | short/tall grass; chernozem (black earth); wheat-cattle | **Prairies** (N America), **Steppes** (Eurasia), **Pampas** (Argentina), **Veld** (S Africa), **Downs** (Australia), **Canterbury** (NZ), Puszta (Hungary), Manchuria |
| Temperate deciduous | Cfb/Dfb | oak, beech, maple, elm; autumn colour; brown forest soil | W Europe, E USA, E China, Japan, NZ |
| **Taiga/boreal coniferous** | Dfc; long winters | spruce, fir, pine, larch; softwood (pulp); podzol; fur animals | Canada, Scandinavia, **Siberia** (largest) |
| **Tundra** | ET; <10 °C warmest month | mosses, lichens, dwarf shrubs; permafrost; reindeer, musk ox; polygonal ground | Arctic fringe; alpine analog |
| Montane | altitude zonation | Himalaya: tropical → sub-tropical pine → temperate oak/deodar/chir → sub-alpine fir/birch/rhododendron → alpine meadows (*bugyals*, *margs*) → snow | — |

## India's vegetation (Champion & Seth 1968 — 16 types; **ISFR 2023: forest cover 7,15,343 km² = 21.76%, tree cover 3.41%, total 25.17%**; MP largest area, Mizoram highest %; Lakshadweep ~90%; mangroves 4,992 km² — WB 42%)
- **Tropical evergreen** (>200 cm; W Ghats, NE, A&N; rosewood, ebony, mahogany, aini), semi-evergreen; **tropical moist deciduous** (100–200 cm; most widespread; **teak, sal**, shisham, sandalwood; Chhota Nagpur, Ghats foothills); **tropical dry deciduous** (70–100 cm; teak, tendu, palas, khair; UP, MP, peninsular interior); **thorn/scrub** (<70 cm; acacia, babul, khejri, ber, date; Rajasthan, Gujarat, Deccan rain shadow); **montane** (wet temperate — Nilgiri *sholas*; Himalayan — oak, chestnut, pine, deodar, silver fir, spruce, birch; alpine — juniper, rhododendron); **littoral/mangrove** (Sundarbans — *Heritiera fomes* (sundari), Rhizophora, Avicennia; Bhitarkanika, Pichavaram, Godavari-Krishna, Gulf of Kutch; pneumatophores, viviparity). Forest Survey of India (Dehradun; biennial ISFR); National Forest Policy 1988 (33% target); Van Mahotsav (July); Green India Mission; CAMPA; Nagar Van; Mangrove — **MISHTI 2023**; Great Indian Bustard grasslands; *sacred groves* (Meghalaya *law kyntang*, Kerala *kavu*, Rajasthan *oran*, Karnataka *devarakadu*).

## Traps
- Black soil is rich in **Ca, Mg, K, Fe** but poor in **N, P, humus** — same NPH deficiency as most Indian soils.
- Laterite forms under **alternating wet-dry tropical** conditions, and hardens like brick (Latin *later*).
- Khadar = new & fertile; bhangar = old & kankar-rich (**not** the reverse).
- Chernozem = temperate grasslands; latosol = rainforest; podzol = taiga.
- ISFR 2023 (released Dec 2024): forest+tree cover **25.17%**; forest cover alone 21.76%.
