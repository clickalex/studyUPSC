Physical Geography — Prelims Revision

Earth's interior & rocks

  • Layers: Crust (SIAL continental / SIMA oceanic) → Mantle (asthenosphere ductile zone ~80–200 km) → Core (NiFe — NIFE; outer liquid drives magnetosphere).
  • Seismic traps: P-waves pass solid+liquid; S-waves solid only → outer-core liquid proof; shadow zones beyond 103°/142°.
  • Rocks: Igneous (granite acidic, basalt basic; Deccan traps = flood basalt) → Sedimentary (sandstone/limestone/shale; fossils live here) → Metamorphic (gneiss from granite; marble from limestone; slate from shale; quartzite from sandstone).

Tectonics & landforms

  • Plates: 7 majors + minors; Indian plate = Indo-Australian fragment; boundaries: convergent (Himalayas = continent-continent), divergent (Mid-Atlantic ridge, East African Rift), transform (San Andreas).
  • Fold vs fault-block vs volcanic mountains: Himalayas (fold), Vosges/Black Forest (block/horst), Fuji (volcanic).
  • Earthquake basics: focus/hypocenter → epicenter; Richter logarithmic (×10 per unit amplitude; ~×31.6 energy); Mercalli measures intensity.
  • Volcano drill: Ring of Fire (Pacific) concentrates; Barren Island (India's only active volcano, Andaman Sea); dormant ≠ extinct (Teide/Etna active, Deccan extinct).

Atmosphere & climate

  • Layers (up): Troposphere (~8 km poles/18 km equator; weather), Stratosphere (ozone layer ~15–35 km), Mesosphere, Thermosphere (ionosphere sub-layer), Exosphere.
  • Pressure belts: Equatorial Low → Subtropical High (horse latitudes) → Subpolar Low → Polar High; winds = trades → Westerlies → polar easterlies; Coriolis (NH deflects right).
  • Local winds: Loo (NW India hot), Foehn (Alps warm dry), Chinook (Rockies eastern slope), Mistral (Rhône valley cold), Sirocco (Sahara→Mediterranean), Bora (Adriatic), Blizzard (polar).
  • Fronts & cyclones: tropical cyclones need >26.5°C SST, Coriolis assist (no-formation zone ±5° lat), low vertical shear; extra-tropical cyclones = frontal (temperate lanes); jet streams: subtropical westerly jet splits winter over Himalaya; Somali jet energises summer monsoon onset.
  • ENSO: El Niño = warm east Pacific, Peruvian anchovies crash, Indian monsoon weakens probability; La Niña = reverse; Southern Oscillation links; IOD (Indian Ocean Dipole) modulates monsoon — positive IOD can bail a weak-ENSO monsoon.
  • Koppen shorthand: Amw = tropical monsoon (Malabar); Aw savanna; BWhw hot desert (Thar); Cwg monsoon-with-dry-winter (Gangetic); D snow climates absent in India except Himalayan fringes (E = polar).

Oceans

  • Currents: cold ↔ warm pairs — Labrador (cold) meets Gulf Stream (warm) → fog & Grand Banks fishing; Kuroshio (warm) + Oyashio (cold) → Hokkaido fisheries; Peru/Humboldt (cold; upwelling; El Niño reverses); Agulhas (warm); Benguela (cold); Canaries (cold); Brazil (warm); West Australian (cold).
  • Salinity basics: mid-ocean subtropical highs max; Baltic lowest (freshwater inflow + low evap); Dead Sea exceptional.
  • Relief: continental shelf (fish+oil zones) → slope → rise → abyssal plains → trenches (Mariana deepest; Challenger Deep ~11 km); mid-ocean ridges = new crust.

Soils & vegetation (world frame)

  • Podsol/taiga conifers; chernozem (steppe black-earth, world's best grain soil); terra rossa (Mediterranean); laterite (tropical leached); peat (high-latitude bogs).
  • Grasslands ↔ regions: Pampas (Argentina), Prairies (N America), Steppes (Eurasia), Veld (S Africa), Downs (Australia), Canterbury (NZ).

Common traps

  • Coriolis: NH-right, SH-left; zero at equator → cyclones absent ±5°.
  • "Warm currents raise coastal rainfall" — generally true on eastern margins; cold currents → aridity (Atacama/Namib adjacent).
  • Day-nightline: equinoxes Mar 21/Sep 23; solstices Jun 21/Dec 22; Tropic of Cancer ~23.5°N passes through 8 Indian states (GJ-RJ-MP-CG-JH-WB-TR-MZ).
  • Kuroshio = Japan; Gulf Stream ≠ North Atlantic Drift (drift = continuation toward Europe).
  • Earth's perihelion Jan (~closest) vs aphelion Jul — popular inversion trap.
