Geomorphology: Earth, Landforms & Rocks — Prelims Fact Card

Prelims GS-I › Geography › Physical Geography. Concept-definition questions (which landform is formed by X?) plus earth-interior facts.

Earth's interior

  • Layers: crust (continental sial 30–70 km, oceanic sima 5–10 km; Conrad discontinuity between) → Mohorovičić (Moho) → mantle (to 2,900 km; asthenosphere 100–200 km partially molten; Repetti 700 km) → Gutenberg → outer core (liquid; no S-waves; nife) → Lehmann → inner core (solid; 5,150–6,371 km).
  • Evidence: seismic waves — P (longitudinal, all media, fastest), S (transverse, solids only; shadow zone 105°–145° for S entirely beyond 105°; P shadow 105°–145°), L/R surface waves (most destructive). Density 2.7 crust → 13 core; temperature ~5,500 °C core.
  • Earthquakes: focus/hypocentre & epicentre; scales — Richter (magnitude, log₁₀; energy ×31.6 per unit), Mercalli (intensity I–XII), Moment magnitude (Mw — now standard). India seismic zones II–V (V: Kashmir, HP, Uttarakhand, Bihar-Nepal, NE, Kutch, Andaman); Bureau of Indian Standards IS 1893. Tsunami: shallow-focus subduction quakes (2004 Sumatra 9.1).
  • Volcanoes: shield (Hawaii; basaltic), composite/strato (Fuji, Vesuvius; andesitic), caldera (Toba, Crater Lake), flood basalt (Deccan Traps; Siberian), mid-ocean ridge; India: Barren Island (only active; Andaman), Narcondam (dormant); Ring of Fire; VEI; lava — pahoehoe/aa; intrusive forms: batholith, laccolith, lopolith, phacolith, sill, dyke.

Plate tectonics & continental drift

  • Wegener 1912 (Pangaea, Panthalassa; Laurasia/Gondwana; Tethys; evidence: jigsaw fit, Glossopteris, tillites, placer gold Ghana–Brazil; forces: pole-fleeing & tidal — inadequate), Arthur Holmes (convection currents 1930s), Harry Hess sea-floor spreading 1961 (palaeomagnetic stripes — Vine & Matthews), Morgan/McKenzie plate tectonics 1967–68.
  • Seven majors (Pacific largest, Antarctic, N. American, S. American, Eurasian, African, Indo-Australian) + minors (Nazca, Cocos, Arabian, Philippine, Caribbean, Juan de Fuca, Scotia). Boundaries: divergent (mid-Atlantic ridge, East African Rift, Red Sea), convergent (ocean–continent Andes; ocean–ocean Japan/Marianas/Andaman arc; continent–continent Himalaya), transform (San Andreas). Hotspots (Hawaii, Réunion → Deccan). Indian plate moved ~15–20 cm/yr; collision ~50 Ma; still 5 cm/yr NE.

Rocks & minerals

  • Igneous (primary; intrusive/plutonic — granite, gabbro; extrusive — basalt, rhyolite, obsidian, pumice), Sedimentary (stratified; clastic — sandstone, shale, conglomerate; chemical — limestone, rock salt; organic — coal; fossils; 75% surface, 5% crust volume), Metamorphic (heat/pressure — contact/thermal & regional/dynamic; foliated: slate←shale, schist, gneiss←granite; non-foliated: marble←limestone, quartzite←sandstone, anthracite←coal). Rock cycle. Mohs scale (talc 1 → diamond 10). Bowen's reaction series.

Landforms by agent (define-by-feature bank)

  • Weathering (in situ: physical — frost, exfoliation/onion, salt; chemical — carbonation (karst), hydration, oxidation, solution; biological) vs mass movement (creep, solifluction, slump, landslide, debris flow) vs erosion (transport). Exogenic vs endogenic; geomorphic cycle (Davis: youth–maturity–old; Penck).
  • Fluvial: V-valley, gorge (hard rock; Indus), canyon (horizontal strata, arid; Grand Canyon), waterfalls (Jog, Angel), potholes, plunge pools, meanders, oxbow lakes, floodplains, natural levees, point bars, braided channels, river terraces, alluvial fans (piedmont), deltas (arcuate — Nile, Ganga; bird's-foot — Mississippi; cuspate — Tiber; estuarine — Narmada/Tapi), peneplain, monadnock/inselberg (residual), river capture/piracy, knick point, incised/entrenched meanders.
  • Glacial: cirque/corrie (armchair) → tarn lake; arête, horn (Matterhorn), U-shaped valley, hanging valley (waterfalls), fjord (drowned U-valley — Norway), roche moutonnée, crag & tail; depositional — moraines (lateral, medial, terminal, ground), drumlins (basket of eggs; blunt end up-ice), eskers (sinuous, sub-glacial streams), kames, kettle holes, outwash plains, erratics, till/boulder clay, varves.
  • Aeolian/arid: deflation hollows (Qattara), yardangs (parallel ridges), zeugen (table), mushroom/pedestal rocks, ventifacts, desert pavement/reg, inselbergs, pediments, playas (salt lakes; Sambhar), bajada, mesa → butte, wadi; dunes — barchan (crescent, horns downwind), seif/longitudinal, transverse, parabolic (vegetated; horns upwind), star; loess (wind-blown silt — China, Hwang Ho).
  • Karst (limestone; carbonation): sinkholes/dolines (solution, collapse), uvala, polje, swallow holes, lapies/karren, caves — stalactites (ceiling), stalagmites (floor), columns, blind valleys, cockpit karst, tower karst (Guilin); India — Borra (AP), Belum (AP), Meghalaya (Mawsmai, Krem Liat Prah longest), Kutumsar (Chhattisgarh), Robber's cave.
  • Coastal: cliffs, wave-cut platforms, sea caves → arches → stacks → stumps, blowholes/geos, spits (Chilika), bars, tombolo (connects island), lagoons (Chilika, Pulicat, Vembanad), beaches, barrier islands, cuspate foreland, raised beaches (emergent), rias, fjords, dalmatian (submergent); coral reefs — fringing, barrier (Great Barrier), atoll (Lakshadweep; Darwin subsidence theory); mangroves.
  • Groundwater: aquifer, aquiclude, artesian well, water table, springs, geysers (Old Faithful), hot springs (Manikaran, Tattapani).

Mountains & structural forms

  • Fold (young: Himalaya, Alps, Andes, Rockies — Alpine orogeny; old: Aravalli (world's oldest fold), Urals, Appalachians), block/horst (Vosges, Black Forest; Satpura?) & rift/graben (Rhine, Narmada-Tapi, East Africa, Baikal, Dead Sea), volcanic (Kilimanjaro, Fuji), residual/relict (Nilgiri, Parasnath). Fold terms — anticline, syncline, recumbent, overthrust/nappe; fault types — normal, reverse/thrust, strike-slip; plateau types — intermontane (Tibet, Bolivia), piedmont (Patagonia), continental (Deccan), lava (Columbia, Deccan), dissected (Chota Nagpur). Plains — structural, erosional (peneplain), depositional (alluvial, glacial, loess, lacustrine — Kashmir karewas).

Traps

  • Barchan horns point downwind; parabolic horns upwind.
  • Drumlin's blunt end faces the ice-source; stalactites hang down ("c" for ceiling).
  • Fjord = glacial + submergence; ria = drowned river valley.
  • S-waves don't cross the outer core — evidence it is liquid.
