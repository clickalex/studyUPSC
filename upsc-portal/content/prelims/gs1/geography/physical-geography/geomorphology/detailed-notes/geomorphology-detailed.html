Geomorphology — Detailed Notes

Interior, plate tectonics, earthquakes-volcanoes, and the four erosional agents. Statement-true/false territory.

1. Earth's interior

  • Layers: crust (continental ~35-70 km vs oceanic ~5-10 km; SIAL-SIMA terminology legacy), mantle (to ~2900 km; upper asthenosphere = partial-melt, plate lubricant), core (outer liquid, inner solid — Fe-Ni). Discontinuities: Conrad (crust-internal), Moho (crust-mantle), Repetti (mantle-internal), Gutenberg (mantle-core), Lehmann (inner core).
  • Evidence: direct (mining, drilling — deepest Kola ~12 km) vs indirect (seismic P-S wave shadow zones, meteorites density, isostasy — Airy vs Pratt).

2. Plate tectonics

  • Theory chain: continental drift (Wegener 1912: Pangaea-Panthalassa, jigsaw-fit, Glossopteris flora evidence) → sea-floor spreading (Hess 1961; palaeomagnetic stripes, youngest-at-ridge) → plate tectonics (1960s; 7 major plates: Pacific largest; Indian plate split from Gondwana, NE-drift ~5-6 cm/yr).
  • Boundaries: divergent (mid-Atlantic ridge, East African rift), convergent (ocean-continent: Andes; ocean-ocean: Japan arc; continent-continent: Himalaya), transform (San Andreas). Hotspots: Hawaii chain (intra-plate volcanism), Reunion-Deccan linkage (K-T flood basalt ~66 Ma).

3. Earthquakes & volcanoes

  • Earthquake terms: focus-hypocentre, epicentre, seismograph; Richter magnitude vs Mercalli intensity; waves P (fastest, all media) S (solids only) L/R (surface, most destructive). India seismic zones: II-V (zone V: Himalaya-NE-Rann-Kutch-Andaman).
  • Volcano types: shield (Mauna Loa), composite-strato (Fuji, Andes), cinder, caldera (Krakatoa), flood-basalt (Deccan); extrusive landforms (cone-crater-lava plateau) vs intrusive (batholith, laccolith, lopolith, phacolith, sill-dyke). Distribution: Pacific Ring of Fire (~2/3 of world volcanoes), mid-Atlantic, East African rift. Barren Island = India's only active volcano.

4. Erosional agents & landforms

  • Fluvial: youth (V-valleys, gorges, waterfalls) → maturity (meanders) → old (oxbow lakes, deltas — arcuate/bird-foot/estuarine); peneplain concept. Arid: deflation-hollows, mushroom rocks, yardangs-zeugen, inselberg-playa; loess (wind-blown, China). Glacial: cirque-horn-arete (erosion), moraine-drumlins-eskers (deposition), U-valleys, fjords. Coastal: cliffs-wave-cut platforms, beaches-spits-bars, lagoons; Karst (groundwater): sinkholes, caves-stalactites-stalagmites.
  • India anchors: Himalaya (Siwalik-Lesser-Great-Karakoram-Ladakh ranges; syntaxial bends), Peninsular block stability, Deccan Traps, Western vs Eastern Ghats asymmetry, coastal plains (Konkan-Malabar vs Utkal-Coromandel), islands (Andaman-Nicobar extension of Arakan-Yoma; Lakshadweep coral atolls).

MCQ hooks

  • Moho = crust-mantle; Gutenberg = mantle-core; shadow zones = liquid outer core proof.
  • Sea-floor spreading youngest-at-ridge; Hess; palaeomagnetism evidence.
  • Zone-V list = Himalayan arc (J&K, HP, Uttarakhand) + NE states + Kutch + Andaman-Nicobar; Bihar is zone IV.
  • Barren Island active; Narcondam dormant.
  • Esker = glacial-fluvial deposit ridge; loess = wind.
  • Himalaya = continent-continent convergence; Andes = ocean-continent.
