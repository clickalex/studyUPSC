Map Practice — Method & Drills (Prelims)

Map questions almost always reward position/sequence recall. Build a weekly 15-minute drill loop using blank outline maps (Sketchy/Exammaps books or blank printouts).

Method

  1. Triangle anchors: fix 3 memory points per region first (e.g., S Asia: Gwadar-Karachi West, Dhaka East, Assam's Sadiya NE) then fill middles by interpolation.
  2. Sequence runs: learn orders, not singles — e.g., Himalayan passes west→east; major ports north→south on each coast.
  3. Relation frames: Which desert is east of Andes? (Patagonian rain-shadow). What strait connects Bay of Bengal–S China Sea region? (Malacca). Learn via "through-X-one-reaches-Y" phrasing.
  4. News-anchored marking: every fortnight, plot 10 current-affairs locations (conflicts, projects, storms) on an outline map.

India lists to rehearse

  • Mountain passes W→E: Banihal (Pir Panjal tunnel corridor) → Zoji La → Burzil → Shipki La → Nathu La → Jelep La → Bomdi La → Sela/Tunga co corridor → Diphu.
  • West coast ports N→S: Deendayal (Kandla) → Mumbai (Nhava Sheva adjacent) → Mormugao → New Mangalore → Cochin → VOC Tuticorin (technically south-east).
  • East coast S→N: VOC Tuticorin → Chennai → Ennore → Krishnapatnam (non-major) → Visakhapatnam → Gangavaram → Kakinada → Paradeep → Dhamra → Haldia/Kolkata.
  • Tiger reserves — pick 8 anchor states: Uttarakhand (Corbett+Nandhaur), Rajasthan (Sariska, Ranthambore, Mukundra Hills), MP (Kanha, Bandhavgarh, Pench), Karnataka (Bandipur/Nagarhole), TN (Mudumalai/Anamalai), Uttarakhand, Assam (Kaziranga is NP not TR — careful), Sundarbans is NP+TR.
  • Biosphere reserves map: Nilgiri (tri-junction) · Nokrek · Simlipal · Sundarbans · Gulf of Mannar · Great Nicobar · Nanda Devi · Pachmarhi · Achanakmar-Amarkantak · Dibru-Saikhowa · Manas · Khangchendzonga · Agasthyamalai · Kutch (Cold Desert 2008) · Panna (2020).

World list

  • Deserts plot: Sahara, Arabian, Thar, Gobi, Kalahari, Namib, Atacama, Patagonian, Great Victoria, Simpson, Gibson, Great Sandy, Sonoran/Mojave.
  • Hotspot biogeography: India's 4 + Madagascar, Mesoamerica, Mediterranean basin, California Floristic Province, Atlantic Forest, Cape Floristic Region, Caucasus, Sundaland, Wallacea, Philippines.
  • IUCN emblem stories plot: Pygmy hog (Manas belt), Jerdon's courser (Sri Lankamalleswara), Hangul (Dachigam), Ganges dolphin (Vikramshila), Blackbuck (Velavadar-Rajkot region), Lion (Gir).
  • Chokepoint chain tracing: from Mumbai to Rotterdam marking Hormuz→Bab-el-Mandeb→Suez→Gibraltar; from Chennai to Yokohama marking Malacca→Taiwan.

Self-test format

  • Weekly: 10 blank-map locations + 5 match-the-region pairs.
  • Streak-tracking: score /10 — two consecutive perfect sheets before moving on.
  • Post-test conversion: convert misses into flashcards ("Kalahandi-Koraput = bauxite, Odisha").

Common map-question patterns in UPSC

  • "Which countries does the X flow through…" (multi-select — river passage orders).
  • "The strait between K island and L strait is…"
  • "The following ports in geologic sequence... "
  • News-tied: e.g., a cyclone's landfall district and its hemisphere wind sense.
