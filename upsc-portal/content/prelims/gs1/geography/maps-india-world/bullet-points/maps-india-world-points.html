Maps & Location Drills — Bullet Points

Locate-fast flash.

India flash

  • Tropic of Cancer → 8 states: GJ-RJ-MP-CG-JH-WB-TR-MZ · southern tip → Indira Point (Nicobar) · mainland south → Kanyakumari.
  • IST meridian → 82.5E Mirzapur · extremes → Indira Col (N), Kibithu (E), Guhar Moti (W).
  • Lakes → Chilika (OD), Pulicat (AP-TN), Vembanad (KL), Loktak (MN), Wular (J&K), Sambhar (RJ), Kolleru (AP).
  • City-rivers → Delhi-Yamuna, Ahmedabad-Sabarmati, Lucknow-Gomti, Surat-Tapi, Vijayawada-Krishna, Cuttack-Mahanadi, Srinagar-Jhelum, Guwahati-Brahmaputra.

World flash

  • Straits → Hormuz (gulf exit), Malacca (busiest), Bab-el-Mandeb (Red Sea), Bosphorus-Dardanelles (Black Sea), Gibraltar (Atlantic-Med), Palk (India-Lanka).
  • Lines → Equator: Ecuador-Kenya-Indonesia · IDL: zigzag 180 · Prime Meridian: Greenwich.
  • Regions → Sahel (Sahara-savanna belt), Horn of Africa, Maghreb, Caucasus, ASEAN spine, stans belt.

Projection flash

  • Mercator → direction-true, poles blown up · Gall-Peters → area-true, shape-stretched · Robinson → compromise · conic → mid-latitudes.
  • Scale → large = detail; small = coverage · topo → 1:50,000 SOI · close contours = steep.

Trap flash

  • Kanyakumari = mainland tip; Indira Point = all-India tip ( submerged risk post-2004).
  • IDL ≠ straight 180 line (deviates around Siberia-Aleutians-Fiji).
  • Chilika = lagoon (brackish), Wular = freshwater — salinity trap.
  • Large-scale ≠ large-area — inverse relation trap.
