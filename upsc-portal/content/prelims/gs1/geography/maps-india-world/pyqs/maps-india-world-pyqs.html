Maps and Location — PYQ-Style Practice

Map-frame questions from UPSC prelims patterns, rebuilt as drills. Answers at the end.

Set 1 — Location match (2016-2020 pattern)

  1. Q1. The 'Phumdis' (floating islands) are found in which lake? (a) Chilika (b) Loktak (c) Wular (d) Kolleru
  2. Q2. Which one of the following pairs is NOT correctly matched? (a) Sambhar — Rajasthan (b) Pulicat — Andhra Pradesh-Tamil Nadu (c) Vembanad — Karnataka (d) Chilika — Odisha
  3. Q3. Consider the rivers and cities: 1. Ahmedabad–Sabarmati 2. Lucknow–Gomti 3. Surat–Mahanadi 4. Cuttack–Tapi. Which pairs are correctly matched? (a) 1,2 (b) 2,3 (c) 1,4 (d) 3,4
  4. Q4. Indira Point, the southernmost point of India, is located in: (a) Lakshadweep (b) Great Nicobar (c) Minicoy (d) Car Nicobar
  5. Q5. Which of the following states does the Tropic of Cancer pass through? 1. Rajasthan 2. Chhattisgarh 3. Tripura 4. Odisha. Choose: (a) 1,2,3 (b) 1,2,4 (c) 2,3,4 (d) all

Set 2 — Straits, lines & projections

  1. Q6. The Strait of Hormuz connects the Persian Gulf with: (a) Red Sea (b) Gulf of Oman (c) Arabian Sea directly (d) Gulf of Aden
  2. Q7. The International Date Line deviates from 180° meridian mainly to: (a) avoid polar ice (b) keep some countries within one date (c) follow shipping lanes (d) align with IST
  3. Q8. A 'large-scale' map shows: (a) larger area with less detail (b) smaller area with more detail (c) whole world (d) only oceans
  4. Q9. The Gall-Peters projection is preferred because it: (a) preserves directions (b) preserves areas (c) removes all distortion (d) suits navigation
  5. Q10. The Bosphorus Strait connects the Black Sea with: (a) Aegean Sea directly (b) Sea of Marmara (c) Mediterranean directly (d) Caspian Sea

Answers

1-b (Loktak, Manipur — Keibul Lamjao floating national park) · 2-c (Vembanad = Kerala) · 3-a (Surat–Tapi, Cuttack–Mahanadi) · 4-b · 5-a (Odisha lies south of the Tropic) · 6-b · 7-b · 8-b · 9-b · 10-b.
