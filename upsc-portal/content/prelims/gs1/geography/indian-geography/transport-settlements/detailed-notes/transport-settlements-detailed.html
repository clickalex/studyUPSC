Transport, Ports and Settlements — Detailed Notes

Roads-rail-ports-waterways-air + settlement typology. Corridor-number anchors dominate.

1. Roads

  • Network: world's 2nd largest (~63 lakh km total incl. rural); NH ~1.46 lakh km (MoRTH), carry ~40% traffic; NH numbering grid (even E-W, odd N-S). NH-44 = longest (Srinagar-Kanyakumari); older NH-7 merged. Golden Quadrilateral (5846 km: Delhi-Mumbai-Chennai-Kolkata) + NS-EW corridors; Bharatmala Pariyojana (2017; 34,800 km phase-1; corridors-based); Gati Shakti (2021) multimodal GIS; expressway list: Mumbai-Nagpur Samruddhi, Delhi-Mumbai (underway), Dwarka, Yamuna, Purvanchal.
  • Institutions: NHAI (1995 act), NHIDCL (NE-borders), PMGSY (2000 rural roads, now 4.0), road-safety MVAA 2019.

2. Railways

  • First run Bombay (Bori Bunder)-Thane 16 Apr 1853; network ~68,000 route-km (4th largest); 18 operational zones; DFCs: Eastern (Ludhiana-Sonnagar) + Western (JNPT-Dadri) freight corridors — DFC corporation (2021); Vande Bharat semi-high-speed sets, Amrit Bharat trains-stations, station redev (Amrit Bharat Station Scheme), MMLPs, Kavach train-protection.
  • Heritage: Darjeeling-Nilgiri-Kalka mountain railways (UNESCO); Konkan railway (1998 ROB-line).

3. Ports, waterways, air

  • 12 major ports (Deendayal-Kandla, Mumbai, JNPT, Marmagao, New Mangalore, Cochin, VO Chidambaranar, Chennai, Kamarajar-Ennore, Paradip, Visakhapatnam, Kolkata-Haldia); JNPT = largest container port; Vizag = deepest; Vadhavan (new major port approved 2024); Sagarmala (2015 port-led prosperity), PM-MVT (2021 mega port vision).
  • National Waterways: NW-1 (Ganga: Haldia-Varanasi, 1620 km) — Inland Waterways Authority 1986 act; NW-2 (Brahmaputra); NW-3 (West Coast Canal Kollam-Kottapuram); 111+ notified NWs; Jalvahak cargo scheme era.
  • Aviation: UDAN-RCS regional scheme; 150+ operational airports; Air India-Tata consolidation (2022-24). Pipelines: Hajira-Bijaipur-Jagdishpur (HBJ gas) and others.

4. Settlement geography

  • Rural types: compact/nucleated (plains), linear (road-river-aligned), rectangular (planned, Chhattisgarh-MP?), circular (pond-centric UP?), star (crossroads), dispersed/fragmented (hills-forests-plantations). Urban census terms: statutory towns, census towns (4000+ pop, 75% male-non-farm, density 400), UA (urban agglomeration), outgrowths; 2011: 31.16% urban; megacities 50+; million-plus 53 (2011).
  • Urban systems: million-plus spine along Ganga plains + coasts; primate-city debates; smart-city typology; peri-urban sprawl; census-2027 urban frame upcoming.

MCQ hooks

  • 1853 Bombay-Thane first; Darjeeling Himalayan Railway = UNESCO 1999.
  • NH-44 longest; GQ = 4 metros 5846 km; Bharatmala = corridors approach.
  • 12 major ports; JNPT biggest container; NW-1 = Haldia-Varanasi; NW-3 = Kollam-Kottapuram.
  • Census town test: 3 conditions (pop 4000+, 75% male non-farm workers, density 400/km2).
  • Settlement shapes: linear along roads, circular around ponds, dispersed in hills.
