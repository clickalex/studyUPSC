# Soils, Irrigation & Agriculture Patterns of India — Prelims Fact Card

> Prelims GS-I › Geography › Indian Geography. Crop–condition–state matching, irrigation sources, cropping seasons; soil detail sits in the biogeography card.

## Land use & basics (2021–22, ~328 Mha reporting ~307)
- Net sown area ~140 Mha (~46%); gross cropped ~220 Mha; **cropping intensity ~155%**; forests ~72 Mha; fallow ~25 Mha; culturable waste ~12; barren ~17; net irrigated ~76 Mha (**~55% of net sown**); rainfed ~45%. Agriculture ~16–18% GVA, ~45% workforce. Landholdings avg **1.08 ha** (Agri Census 2015–16); 86% small & marginal.
- Cropping seasons: **Kharif** (Jun–Oct; rice, maize, jowar, bajra, cotton, jute, groundnut, soybean, tur, moong, urad, sugarcane planting), **Rabi** (Oct–Mar; wheat, barley, gram, mustard, peas, linseed, lentil/masoor), **Zaid** (Mar–Jun; watermelon, cucumber, fodder, moong). Kerala rice: *virippu/mundakan/punja*; Bengal *aus/aman/boro* (boro = winter irrigated, highest yield).

## Irrigation
- Sources (net irrigated area): **wells & tube-wells ~63%** (groundwater; Punjab 98% irrigated), canals ~24% (UP, Punjab, Haryana; Indira Gandhi/Rajasthan canal — Harike; Sirhind, Upper Bari Doab, Sarda Sahayak, Nagarjuna Sagar, KC canal, Buckingham (navigation), Ganga canal (Roorkee), Western/Eastern Yamuna, Bhakra, Tungabhadra, Narmada main canal (longest lined 458 km)), tanks ~2% (TN, AP, Karnataka — Kudimaramathu), others (lift, drip). Micro-irrigation ~15 Mha (Karnataka, AP, Gujarat, MH lead); **PMKSY** (2015 — AIBP, Har Khet Ko Pani, Per Drop More Crop, Watershed; 99 priority projects; **PMKSY-AIBP extended to 2026**), Micro Irrigation Fund (NABARD ₹5,000 cr), Mission Amrit Sarovar, **Atal Bhujal (7 states)**, Command Area Development, PM-KUSUM (solar pumps), **Jalyukt Shivar**, Mission Kakatiya (tank restoration Telangana), Ken–Betwa, Polavaram, Upper Bhadra (national project 2023), Sardar Sarovar (18 lakh ha), Shahpurkandi, **Renukaji**; groundwater — over-exploited 14% of assessment units (2023 CGWB), Punjab/Haryana/Rajasthan/Delhi crisis; water-use efficiency ~38%; Water-intensive crops in wrong regions (paddy Punjab, sugarcane Marathwada); crop diversification (Punjab DSR incentive; Haryana Mera Pani Meri Virasat).

## Traps
- **WB leads rice**, UP wheat *and* sugarcane, **Gujarat cotton**, Karnataka coffee (not Kerala), Kerala rubber & pepper & cardamom, Assam tea, **MP pulses & soybean**, Rajasthan mustard & bajra, Andhra chilli/aquaculture, Maharashtra onion/grapes/pomegranate/banana (?), J&K apple & saffron & walnut, Bihar litchi & makhana (GI 2022; Makhana Board 2025), Karnataka ragi & silk & areca.
- MSP is *announced* for 22 crops but *procured* mainly wheat & paddy; sugarcane is **FRP** (Centre) + **SAP** (states).
- Irrigation: wells > canals > tanks nationally; canals dominate in **Punjab? No — Punjab is tube-well 70%+; canals highest share in Odisha/J&K/UP-Haryana mix; tanks in TN/AP/Karnataka.
- Cropping intensity = GCA/NSA × 100 (~155%); Punjab ~190% highest; Rajasthan lower.
