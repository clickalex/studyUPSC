Indian Drainage and Rivers — Detailed Notes

Himalayan vs peninsular systems, interlinking, disputes, dams. River-atlas recall is the skill.

1. Two systems

  • Himalayan (perennial, snow-fed, antecedent): deep gorges, dendritic-braided reaches, large deltas.
  • Peninsular (mostly rain-fed, seasonal, graded): east-flowing giants (Mahanadi-Godavari-Krishna-Kaveri → Bay) + west-flowing rift rivers (Narmada-Tapi-Sabarmati-Mahi-Luni → Arabian Sea) + interior Yamuna tributaries (Chambal-Betwa-Ken-Son) + independent east-coast streams (Subarnarekha-Baitarani).

2. Indus & Ganga systems

  • Indus: rises near Mansarovar (Tibet), enters India in Ladakh; Indus Water Treaty 1960 (World Bank-brokered): India = Ravi-Beas-Sutlej (eastern rivers); Pakistan = Indus-Jhelum-Chenab (western). Jhelum source = Verinag; Sutlej enters via Shipki la.
  • Ganga: Bhagirathi (Gangotri) + Alaknanda → Devprayag confluence; Panch Prayag chain; right-bank Yamuna (largest tributary — Chambal-Sind-Betwa-Ken join it), left-bank Ghaghara-Gandak-Kosi (sorrow of Bihar) + Ramganga-Son (south bank, from Amarkantak). Delta: Bhagirathi-Hooghly distributary (Kolkata port); Sundarbans.
  • Brahmaputra: Tsangpo (Tibet) → Dihang (Arunachal; Siang-Dibang-Lohit) → Assam valley (Majuli island) → Jamuna (Bangladesh).

3. Peninsular detail

  • Godavari (largest peninsular; Trimbakeshwar-Nashik; tributaries Manjira-Pranhita-Indravati-Sabari; Polavaram project), Krishna (Mahabaleshwar; Bhima-Tungabhadra-Musi; Nagarjuna Sagar-Almatti), Kaveri (Talakaveri-Coorg; Bhavani-Amaravati-Hemavati; Mettur dam; delta rice bowl; Cauvery dispute — 2007 tribunal award, 2018 SC modification), Mahanadi (Sihawa; Hirakud long dam).
  • Narmada (Amarkantak; rift estuary; Sardar Sarovar-Indira Sagar; Jabalpur marble rocks-Dhuandhar falls), Tapi (Multai; Ukai; Surat estuary), Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni (Aravalli source; turns saline downstream), Periyar-Pamba (Kerala).

4. Management & disputes

  • Interlinking: Ken-Betwa first (Panna tiger-reserve section); disputes: Cauvery (TN-KA), Krishna (MH-KA-AP-TS), Mahadayi/Mandovi (KA-GO-MH), Ravi-Beas/SYL (Punjab-Haryana-Rajasthan), Vansadhara (AP-OD).
  • Frame: Art 262 — Parliament may exclude court jurisdiction; Inter-State River Water Disputes Act 1956 (tribunals; 2019 single-tribunal proposal); Namami Gange (2014), Jal Shakti ministry (2019), Atal Bhujal (2020). Flood-prone: Kosi-Bihar, Brahmaputra-Assam.

MCQ hooks

  • IWT 1960: eastern rivers (Ravi-Beas-Sutlej) = India; western (Indus-Jhelum-Chenab) = Pakistan.
  • Devprayag = Bhagirathi + Alaknanda; Kosi = sorrow of Bihar; Damodar = once sorrow of Bengal.
  • Polavaram = Godavari; Hirakud = Mahanadi; Mettur = Kaveri; Sardar Sarovar = Narmada; Tehri = Bhagirathi.
  • Rift-valley west-flowers: Narmada + Tapi.
  • Ken-Betwa = first interlink; Cauvery dates 2007/2018 pair.
  • Art 262 + ISRWD Act 1956 tribunal regime.
