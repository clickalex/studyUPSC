Minerals & Industries — Bullet Points

Location-pair flash.

Mineral flash

  • Best iron → hematite: Odisha-Jharkhand-Chhattisgarh (Bailadilla) + Bellary-Hospet (KA) · coal → Jharia-Bokaro-Raniganj-Korba-Talcher · lignite → Neyveli (TN).
  • Bauxite → Kalahandi-Koraput (OD) + Gujarat · manganese → OD-MP-KA · mica → Koderma (JH) + Nellore (AP).
  • Gold → Hutti (KA; Kolar closed 2001) · uranium → Jaduguda (JH) + Tummalapalle (AP) · thorium → monazite sands Kerala-TN · lithium 2023 → Reasi (J&K).
  • Belts → NE plateau richest · offshore → Mumbai High + KG basin.

Steel flash

  • TISCO Jamshedpur 1907 (first) · IISCO Burnpur · Bhilai = Soviet · Rourkela = West Germany · Durgapur = UK · Bokaro = USSR · Vizag-Salem modern.

Core-industry flash

  • 8 core = refinery (28%), electricity (19.9%), steel (17.9%), coal (10.3%), crude (8.98%), gas (6.88%), cement (5.37%), fertilisers (2.63%) — ~40% of IIP.

Hub flash

  • Textiles → Ahmedabad-Mumbai, Tiruppur knits, Surat synthetics · leather → Kanpur-Chennai-Agra · auto → Chennai-Pune-Gurgaon.
  • IT → Bengaluru-Hyderabad-Pune · pharma → Hyderabad-Baddi · electronics → Noida-Sriperumbudur · petro → Jamnagar (largest).
  • Policy → DMIC, PLI 14 sectors, Udyam MSME, Gati Shakti.

Trap flash

  • Collaboration swap: Rourkela = Germany, Durgapur = UK — often inverted.
  • Refinery products carry the biggest core weight (not electricity).
  • Monazite = thorium source (not uranium).
  • Kudremukh mining stopped (park), Bellary regulated — 'active mine' phrasing.
  • Coal India est. 1975 — nationalisation era 1971-73.
