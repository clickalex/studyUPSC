## Traps
- Largest coal producer state is now **Odisha** (overtook Jharkhand/CG 2023–24 by production; Jharkhand has largest **reserves**).
- Bailadila (CG) iron ore exports via **Vizag**; Kudremukh mining banned 2005; Goa mining ban 2018 (partial resumption via auctions 2022–24).
- Zawar-Agucha = **zinc**; Khetri = **copper**; Sukinda = **chromite**; Panna = **diamond**; Hutti = **gold**; Koderma = **mica**; Jaduguda = **uranium**; Tummalapalle = largest uranium **reserve**.
- **Digboi** (1889/1901) oldest refinery in Asia; Jamnagar largest; **Mumbai High** still largest producer; Barmer largest **onshore**.
- Nuclear share of *installed* capacity <2% but generation ~3%; 50% non-fossil **capacity** milestone (June 2025) ≠ generation share (~25–30%).
