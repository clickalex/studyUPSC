Indian Geography — Prelims Revision

Physiography stack (north → south)

  1. Himalayas (2,400 km arc): Trans (Karakoram/K2-Godwin Austen 8,611 m is POK side; Kanchenjunga 8,586 m India's highest on-map), Great (Nanda Devi 7,816 m highest fully-Indian), passes Zoji La (Srinagar–Leh), Shipki La (Sutlej entry), Nathu La (Sikkim–Tibet), Bomdi La (Arunachal); Lesser/Inner (Pir Panjal, Dhauladhar — hill stations: Shimla-Mussoorie-Nainital-Darjeeling); Shiwaliks outermost (duns: Dehradun/Kotli/Patli).
  2. Northern Plains: bhabar → terai → alluvial (bangar old / khadar new); deltaic east.
  3. Peninsular Plateau: two units — Central Highlands (Malwa/Bundelkhand/Baghelkhand + Aravalli residual, Guru Shikhar 1,722 m on Mt Abu; Chambal badlands; Bundelkhand ravines) & Deccan Plateau (tilt east → rivers flow east; Western Ghats continuous high wall (Anamudi 2,695 m tallest south; passes: Thal/Bhor/Palghat); Eastern Ghats broken (Mahendragiri)); Deccan trap basalt covers NW.
  4. Coastal Plains & Islands: West = Konkan (MH-Goa) + Karnataka + Malabar (backwaters; Kayals; Vembanad largest lake-state wetland) — estuaries; East = Northern Circars + Coromandel — deltas (fertile but cyclone-exposed); Andaman-Nicobar (volcanic arc north; Indira Point southernmost, under 2004 waves); Lakshadweep = coral atolls (Agatti airstrip; Minicoy separated by Nine Degree Channel, Kavaratti capital).

Rivers (which-bank drill)

  • Indus system: Jhelum-Chenab-Ravi-Beas-Sutlej (the five unite into the Panjnad before meeting the Indus); Upper tributaries Zanskar/Nubra/Shyok/Hunza/Suru; IWT 1960 — eastern rivers (Ravi-Beas-Sutlej) India full; western (Indus-Jhelum-Chenab) Pakistan major use; 2025 India kept IWT in abeyance post-Pahalgam — current-affairs anchor.
  • Ganga system: Bhagirathi (source Gangotri) + Alaknanda at Devprayag = Ganga; confluences: Vishnuprayag/Nandprayag/Karnaprayag/Rudraprayag/Devprayag (panch prayag); tributaries left-bank: Ramganga·Gomti·Ghaghara·Gandak·Baghmati·Kosi·Mahananda; right-bank: Yamuna (+Chambal·Betwa·Ken lineage) & Son.
  • Brahmaputra: enters at Sadiya trio — Lohit·Dibang·Dihang(Siang); left: Dihang, Burhi Dihing; right: Kameng·Manas·Subansiri; Majuli river island (eroding); U-turn at Namcha Barwa.
  • Peninsular flows: Godavari (Dakshin Ganga, origin Trimbakeshwar; tributaries Pranhita=biggest (Wainganga+Wardha confluence lineage), Indravati, Sabari); Krishna (Mahabaleshwar; Bhima — Sina; Tungabhadra; Musi via Hyderabad); Cauvery (Talakaveri; Bhavani·Hemavati·Amaravati·Kabini); west-flowers: Narmada (rift valley Narmada-Son; Amarkantak; falls: Dhuandhar Jabalpur-marble rocks), Tapi (Satpura-Son? no—Satpura southern edge; Purna/Waghur tributaries), Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni (inland salt drainage Rann), Sharavati (Jog falls).

Climate & monsoon

  • Monsoon 2 branches: Arabian Sea strikes Kerala coast ~June 1 first (Western Ghats orographic deluge); Bay of Bengal hits NE (Mawsynram/Cherrapunji) then turns NW along Himalaya trough. Retreating NE monsoon (Oct-Dec) → Tamil Nadu's main rain.
  • Western disturbances = winter rabi rain NW (Dec–Feb).
  • Loo/Norwesters (Kalbaisakhi Bengal-Assam pre-monsoon), Mango showers (S coastal pre-monsoon).
  • Koppen India quickloc: Thar BWhw; Malabar Amw; Gangetic Cwg; coasts Aw gradations; Ladakh BWk cold.

Crops ↔ hooks

  • Kharif: rice, cotton, sugarcane, jowar, bajra, maize, groundnut, pulses; Rabi: wheat, mustard, gram, sunflower; Zaid: watermelon/cucumber/fodder.
  • State tops: Rice — West Bengal; Wheat — UP; Sugarcane — UP & Maharashtra; Cotton — Gujarat,Maharashtra; Groundnut — Gujarat; Coffee — Karnataka (~70%); Tea — Assam (+Darjeeling GI); Rubber — Kerala (Tripura #2 now); Spices/cardamom — Kerala-Karnataka-TN (Idukki); Saffron — Kashmir (Pulwama GI); Jute — WB (Golden fibre); Apple — HP-J&K; Makhana — Bihar.

Minerals & energy (state-pair)

  • Coal: Jharkhand (Jharia-Bokaro), Odisha (Talcher), Chhattisgarh (Korba/Koriya), WB (Raniganj), MP/UP (Singrauli), Telangana (Singareni).
  • Iron: Odisha (Keonjhar, Sundargarh, Mayurbhanj), Chhattisgarh (Bailadila — Dantewada/Durg), Karnataka (Ballari), Goa.
  • Bauxite: Odisha (Kalahandi/Koraput), Jharkhand (Lohardaga), Chhattisgarh, Gujarat.
  • Mica: Koderma (Jharkhand) + Nellore (AP); Manganese: Odisha/MP; Copper: Rajasthan (Khetri) + MP (Malanjkhand only open-cast depth-style) + Jharkhand (Singhbhum).
  • Gold: Kolar closed (Karnataka), Hutti (Karnataka active), Ramagiri (AP).
  • Lithium finds (Reasi J&K + Northeast + Rajasthan exploration) — 2023–25 news cycle.
  • Uranium: Jharkhand (Jaduguda — first mine), Andhra (Tummalapalle).

National waterways & ports

  • NW-1 Prayagraj–Haldia (Ganga, announced 1986 era); NW-2 Sadiya–Dhubri (Brahmaputra); NW-3 Kollam–Kottappuram + Kozhikode links (Kerala); NW-4 Godavari-Krishna canal-linked; 111 declared NWs (2016 act).
  • Major ports (13): West — Deendayal (Kandla, tidal), Mumbai, JNPT (Nhava Sheva), Mormugao, New Mangalore, Cochin; East — V.O. Chidambaranar (Tuticorin), Chennai, Ennore (Kamarajar), Visakhapatnam, Paradip, Haldia/Kolkata; Kolkata = riverine port, Paradip = iron-ore.
  • Non-major biggies: Mundra (largest private), Krishnapatnam, Hazira.
