World Physical Geography — Practice MCQs

8 questions

8 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Which strait connects the Red Sea with the Gulf of Aden?

  1. (a)Hormuz
  2. (b)Bab-el-Mandeb
  3. (c)Tiran
  4. (d)Malacca
Show answer
Correct: (b)Yemen–Djibouti/Eritrea chokepoint; Houthi attacks.
Q2

Match the river with the sea/ocean it drains into: A. Volga — 1. Black Sea · B. Danube — 2. Caspian Sea · C. Rhine — 3. North Sea

  1. (a)A-2 B-1 C-3
  2. (b)A-1 B-2 C-3
  3. (c)A-3 B-1 C-2
  4. (d)A-2 B-3 C-1
Show answer
Correct: (a)Volga → Caspian; Danube → Black; Rhine → North Sea.
Q3

Consider Lake Baikal: 1. It is the deepest freshwater lake in the world. 2. It holds about 20% of the world's unfrozen surface freshwater. 3. It lies in a rift zone.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct; Siberia.
Q4

The Atacama desert's extreme aridity is primarily due to:

  1. (a)Its location on the east coast of South America
  2. (b)The cold Humboldt current and rain-shadow of the Andes
  3. (c)Its high latitude
  4. (d)The warm Brazil current
Show answer
Correct: (b)Cold upwelling + double rain shadow.
Q5

Which of the following countries does the Danube NOT flow through or border?

  1. (a)Austria
  2. (b)Hungary
  3. (c)Poland
  4. (d)Romania
Show answer
Correct: (c)Poland's rivers (Vistula, Oder) drain to the Baltic.
Q6

The Antarctic Treaty (1959): 1. Applies to the area south of 60° S latitude. 2. Bans mineral resource activity through the Madrid Protocol. 3. India became a consultative party in 1983.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q7

Which of the following is the southernmost tip of Africa where the Atlantic and Indian Oceans officially meet?

  1. (a)Cape of Good Hope
  2. (b)Cape Agulhas
  3. (c)Cape Point
  4. (d)Cape Verde
Show answer
Correct: (b)Agulhas; Good Hope is the SW tip.
Q8

The "lithium triangle" of South America comprises:

  1. (a)Brazil, Peru, Colombia
  2. (b)Bolivia, Chile, Argentina
  3. (c)Chile, Peru, Ecuador
  4. (d)Argentina, Uruguay, Paraguay
Show answer
Correct: (b)Salar de Uyuni, Atacama, Hombre Muerto salt flats.
