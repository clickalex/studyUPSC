World Geography — Prelims Revision

Extreme points drill

  • Highest: Everest (8,849 m, Nepal-China); K2 (8,611, Karakoram); Andean giant = Aconcagua (6,961 m, Argentina); Africa = Kilimanjaro (5,895); Europe = Elbrus (5,642, Caucasus — borderline-continent debate); Antarctica = Vinson (4,892); Australia = Kosciuszko; N.America = Denali (6,190); S.America = Aconcagua.
  • Deepest/largest waters: Mariana Trench (Challenger Deep ~10,935 m); largest lake enclosed by area = Caspian Sea; deepest + largest freshwater by volume = Baikal; largest freshwater by surface = Lake Superior; largest salt-water lake = Caspian (as "lake"), True-salt Great Salt Lake; largest river Amazon by discharge (~20% world river discharge), Nile by longest debate (Amazon-Nile contest revises).
  • Deserts: Sahara (largest hot) · Arabian · Gobi (cold, Mongolia/China) · Kalahari · Atacama (driest, Andean rain-shadow) · Patagonian (rain-shadow of Andes) · Great Victoria/Great Sandy/Gibson/Simpson (Australia) · Antarctica & Arctic = polar deserts.

Mountain & river pairs (world-map)

  • Rockies — N America · Great Dividing Range — Australia · Andes — S America · Alps — Europe · Atlas — NW Africa · Drakensberg — S Africa · Urals — Europe/Asia divide · Caucasus — Black–Caspian gap · Zagros — Iran.
  • Rivers↔cities/countries: Rhine — Rotterdam delta (Ruhr access); Danube — 10+ countries, Budapest/Belgrade; Volga — Europe's longest; Mississippi-Missouri — US interior; Mekong — SE Asia 6 countries; Yangtze — China longest (Three Gorges dam); Nile — Khartoum confluence (White+Blue); Amazon — Manaus midstream port; Paraná — Itaipu dam; Murray-Darling — Australia; Ob/Yenisei/Lena — Siberia north-flow.

Straits & canals (ships' choke points)

  • Malacca (Malaysia-Indonesia ↔ Indian Ocean core) · Sunda · Lombok (deep-water alternate) · Taiwan Strait · Bab-el-Mandeb (Red Sea mouth) · Hormuz (Persian Gulf oil jugular) · Bosporus-Dardanelles pair (Black Sea ↔ Med) · Gibraltar · Suez Canal (1869; Med–Red Sea) · Panama (1914; Pacific–Atlantic; expansion locks 2016) · Dover (Calais narrows) · Mozambique · Bering (Russia–USA Arctic diagonal) · Palk (India–Sri Lanka, shallow) · Torres (Australia–PNG).

Themes via region

  • Mediterranean climate: winter rain/summer drought; citrus-vines-olives belt (Med basin + California + Central Chile + SW Australia + Cape).
  • Equatorial rainforest gravitation: Amazon, Congo Basin, SE Asia (Kalimantan/Sumatra) — biodiversity peaks + REDD+ lines.
  • Monsoon zone: S & SE & E Asia; West-African monsoon; N-Australian monsoon.
  • Steppe-veldt-downs world-comparison already in physical sheet: reinforce with Prairies(NA), Pampas(Arg), Veld(SA), Steppes(Eurasia), Downs(Aus), Canterbury(NZ).
  • Export-economy pairs: Chile-Peru copper (Escondida biggest mine); DRC cobalt; Australia lithium/iron ore (Pilbara); Kazakhstan uranium; Gulf oil cluster; Argentina lithium triangle (Chile-Argentina-Bolivia); Rare-earths — China leadership + Myanmar overlap.

Climate-Koppen world quickloc

Af = Amazon basin; Aw = Brazilian Cerrado & Deccan savannas; BWh = Sahara/Atacama; Csa = Mediterranean; Cfb = W Europe; Dfb = Russian interior; ET/EF = tundra/ice caps.

UPSC-typical "sailor" questions

  • "A ship sailing from Chennai to San Francisco via the shortest oceanic route will pass near…" — reconstruct string: Indian Oc → Malacca → S China Sea → up the Pacific — trace each leg yourself from memory.
  • "Between which two countries is the 38th parallel…" — Korea (DMZ); 49th parallel = USA-Canada; Durand Line = Af-Pak; Radcliffe = 1947 partition line; McMahon = India(Tibet)–China east; Line of Control = Kashmir sector.

Map-point news cycle (2024–26)

  • Red Sea attacks → shipping via Cape of Good Hope re-routings (insurance spikes) — pair with Bab-el-Mandeb & Suez stakes.
  • Panama Canal drought drawdowns 2023–24 (water-hungry locks) → rail/dry-canal proposals.
  • Arctic routes (NSR) activity + India's Arctic Policy 2022 → Himadri station; Nagapattinam-to-Vladivostok corridor talk (Eastern Maritime Corridor).
  • IUU fishing shadow fleets in S Atlantic/Indian Oceans.
