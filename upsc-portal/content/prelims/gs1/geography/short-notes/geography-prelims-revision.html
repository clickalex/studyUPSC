Geography — Prelims Rapid Revision

Map-pairing and pattern questions dominate. Pair with Mains geography sets for depth.

Physiography of India (order from north)

  1. Himalayas: Trans (Karakoram-Ladakh-Zanskar-Kailash), Great (Himadri: passes Zoji La → Shipki La), Lesser (Pir Panjal-Dhauladhar-Mahabharat: hill stations sit here), Shiwaliks (duns between).
  2. Peninsular plateau: Deccan (trap), Central Highlands (Malwa/Bundelkhand/Chota Nagpur), NE extension (Meghalaya Plateau — one-time fractured eastward bit; Karbi-Anglong).
  3. Coastal plains: Western (Konkan+Karnataka+Malabar) vs Eastern (Utkal+Andhra+Coromandel) — estuaries vs deltas signature contrast.
  4. Islands: Andaman-Nicobar (volcanic line; Barren Island active volcano) vs Lakshadweep (coral).

Rivers (tributary-side drill)

  • Ganga: left-bank Ramganga/Gomti/Ghaghara/Gandak/Kosi; right-bank Yamuna (+ Chambal-Betwa-Ken lineage)/Son.
  • Indus: Jhelum-Chenab-Ravi-Beas-Sutlej (left-bank join pattern); Zanskar-Nubra upper, Shyok source.
  • Brahmaputra: left-bank Dibang/Lohit/Burhi Dihing; right-bank Subansiri/Kameng/Manas/Sankosh/Teesta.
  • Peninsular: Godavari (Pranhita biggest; Indravati/Sabari), Krishna (Bhima-Tungabhadra-Musi), Kaveri (Bhavani-Amaravati-Hemavati), Narmada (west-flowing, rift valley; Amarkantak source), Tapi (Purna-Waghur), Mahi, Sabarmati, Luni (inland drainage).

Climate & monsoon mechanics

  • Jet streams steer breaks; western disturbances (winter rain NW); El Niño-La Niña/ENSO + IOD coupling effects (2023 skew: El Niño + positive IOD).
  • Monsoon 2025 recap: June 109% · July 105% · Aug 105% LPA; season above normal (~105–107%).
  • Koppen-classification hits: Amw (Malabar), BWhw (Thar), Cwg (Gangetic), Dfc (upper Himalaya fringes).

Soils pairing

  • Alluvial: Bangar vs Khadar; Black/Regur (Deccan trap; cotton); Red-Yellow (granite/gneiss); Laterite (leaching; cashew/tea); Mountain; Arid; Saline; Peaty (Kerala backwaters).

Agriculture-ecology pairs

  • Kharif (Jun–Oct: rice, cotton, sugarcane, jowar), Rabi (Nov–Apr: wheat, mustard, gram), Zaid (summer: watermelon/cucumber).
  • Coffee: Karnataka ~70% (Kodagu+Chikmagalur); Tea: Assam + Nilgiri/Darjeeling; Rubber: Kerala + Tripura; Spices: Kerala/Karnataka; Groundnut: Gujarat/AP-Rajasthan belt; Saffron: Pulwama (Kashmir GI).

Mineral mapping

  • Coal: Jharia-Bokaro (Jharkhand), Raniganj (WB), Korba (Chhattisgarh), Singrauli (MP-UP), Talcher (Odisha).
  • Iron ore: Odisha (Keonjhar, Mayurbhanj), Chhattisgarh (Bailadila), Karnataka (Bellary-Ballari), Goa.
  • Bauxite: Odisha (Kalahandi-Koraput), Jharkhand (Lohardaga), Chhattisgarh, Gujarat.
  • Lithium finds: Reasi (J&K) exploration blocks (2023–25).

World geography quick-hits

  • Straits: Hormuz (oil choke), Malacca (Indochina), Bab-el-Mandeb, Bosphorus-Dardanelles, Taiwan, Lombok-Sunda.
  • Deserts: Thar (only hot India), Gobi (cold Asia), Atacama (driest), Sahara (largest hot).
  • Grasslands: Pampas (Argentina) · Prairies (USA/Canada) · Steppes (Eurasia) · Veld (SA) · Downs (Australia).
  • Volcano/earthquake tectonics: Ring of Fire circum-Pacific; Alpide belt (Alps-Himalaya-Indonesia); most earthquakes via convergent margins.

Map practice pairs

  • National waterways: NW-1 (Prayagraj-Haldia Ganga), NW-2 (Brahmaputra Sadiya-Dhubri), NW-3 (Kerala Kollam-Kozhikode).
  • Tiger reserves ↔ states (link to env set); biosphere reserves ↔ state pairs.
  • Ports: Paradeep (iron ore), Vishakhapatnam (bulk + LNG), Deendayal (Kandla), JNPT Nhava Sheva, Chennai-Ennore, Mundra (private), Mormugao, Haldia.
