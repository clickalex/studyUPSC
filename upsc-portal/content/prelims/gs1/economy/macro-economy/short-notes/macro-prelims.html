Macroeconomy — Prelims Drill

National-income algebra

  • GDP (market) = Σ GVA (basic) + product taxes − product subsidies; GNP = GDP + NFIA.
  • Depreciation shift: NNP = GNP − depreciation; national income = NNP at factor cost.
  • Base years: National accounts 2011–12; IIP 2011–12; WPI 2011–12 (L=100); CPI-C 2012; CPI-AL/RL 1986–87 (revision underway).
  • Real vs nominal: real uses constant prices (base yr); nominal current prices; GDP deflator = nominal/real × 100.
  • Growth maths: 6.5% real growth approx doubling in ~11 years (rule of 72).

Inflation set

  • Types: demand-pull / cost-push / built-in (wage-price spiral) / creeping-walking-galloping-hyper stagflation = stagnation + inflation.
  • Measures: CPI-C (MPC anchor: 4% ± 2% band to Mar 2031, extended 2025), CPI-IW (industrial workers → DA linkage; Labour Bureau), CPI-AL/RL (rural labour/agri labour), WPI (697-commodities; base 2011–12; no services).
  • PPI-India doesn't exist; gap = WPI-CPI wedge stories 2021–24.
  • Core ex-food-fuel; headline swings drive MPC votes in 2024–25 band.

Money & monetary policy

  • MPC: 6 members (3 RBI + 3 government-appointed); Governor tie-break vote; inflation target set by government in consultation with RBI.
  • LAF corridor: SDF (absorb no-collateral floor, introduced Apr 2022) → Repo (central policy rate) → MSF (repo+25bp ceiling historically; overnight emergency against SLR).
  • CRR (cash with RBI; no interest), SLR (liquid securities; LCR-relief overlap debates).
  • OMOs (buy/sell GS to inject/drain), FX swaps (buy-sell), term repos (TLTRO during stress).
  • Money measures: M0 (reserve money) · M1=narrow · M3 (broad) commonly quoted · M4 widest rarely used; money multiplier = M3/M0.

Banking anatomy

  • Nationalisation phases: 1969 (14 banks) + 1980 (6 more); consolidation to 12 PSBs (Apr 2020).
  • NPA pipe: SARFAESI 2002 enforcement of security interest; IBC-2016 (330-day outer timeline incl litigation per 2019 amendment + SC clarifications); NARCL (bad bank, PSB-led, 2021) + IDRCL; ARCs originally via SARFAESI.
  • DICGC: deposit insurance ₹5 lakh/depositor/bank (raised 2020 from ₹1 lakh).
  • PCA framework: PCR indicator sets (CRAR, NPA, RoA, leverage) triggers.
  • Payments-banks/PPI/UPI ecosystems; UPI volumes crossed ₹20-lakh-crore monthly value band (2024) & >1,400–1,500 crore txns/month 2025.

Fiscal ledger

  • Deficit defs: Revenue deficit = rev exp − rev receipts. Fiscal deficit = total exp − (rev receipts + non-debt capital receipts). Primary deficit = FD − interest payments. Effective revenue deficit (ERD) = RD − grants for capital-asset creation.
  • FRBM 2003 targets — deferred paths (4.5% FD/GDP by FY25-26 commitment; glide re-narrated 2024-25 budgets).
  • Debt-to-GDP: Centre ~57%·States ~28%·combined ~85% zone (FY24-25 estimates; 16th FC coins).
  • Fiscal Responsibility rules → FD trajectory; 16th FC (Panagariya chair) deciding 2026–31 devolution.

Populations/economy metric CA hooks

  • PLFS unemployment rate (usual status, 15+): 5.6% (2022-23) → ~3.2% (2023-24); FLFPR up to ~41.7% (2023-24 PLFS) / ~37% (periodic series caution).
  • MPI (NITI-Oxford OPHI): 24.85% (2015-16) → 14.96% (2019-21); UNDP 2024 figure ~16.4% (2019-21 band).
  • Extreme poverty $2.15/day (2017 PPP): below ~3% by recent World Bank estimates (2022-23) — debated in literature.
