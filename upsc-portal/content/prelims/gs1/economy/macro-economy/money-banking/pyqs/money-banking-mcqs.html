Money, Banking & Monetary Policy — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Broad money (M3) in India is defined as:

  1. (a)Currency with the public + demand deposits with banks
  2. (b)M1 + time deposits with banks
  3. (c)M1 + savings deposits with post offices
  4. (d)Reserve money + net foreign exchange assets of RBI
Show answer
Correct: (b)M1 = currency + demand deposits + other deposits with RBI.
Q2

Consider the Monetary Policy Committee: 1. The inflation target is fixed by the MPC every five years. 2. External members are appointed for four years and are not eligible for reappointment. 3. If inflation stays outside the tolerance band for three consecutive quarters, the RBI must report to the Central Government.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Target is notified by the Government in consultation with RBI.
Q3

In the Liquidity Adjustment Facility corridor since April 2022, the floor and ceiling are respectively:

  1. (a)Reverse repo rate and bank rate
  2. (b)Standing Deposit Facility rate and Marginal Standing Facility rate
  3. (c)Repo rate and MSF rate
  4. (d)SDF rate and repo rate
Show answer
Correct: (b)SDF replaced fixed reverse repo as floor.
Q4

Which of the following statements about currency issuance is correct?

  1. (a)One-rupee notes and all coins are issued by the Government of India
  2. (b)All currency notes including the one-rupee note are issued by the RBI
  3. (c)Coins are minted by the RBI under the Coinage Act
  4. (d)The RBI must hold gold equal to 40% of notes issued
Show answer
Correct: (a)RBI issues notes of ₹2 and above under minimum reserve system.
Q5

A loan account is classified as a non-performing asset when interest or principal remains overdue for more than:

  1. (a)30 days
  2. (b)60 days
  3. (c)90 days
  4. (d)180 days
Show answer
Correct: (c)SMA-2 covers 61–90 days.
Q6

Consider the Deposit Insurance and Credit Guarantee Corporation: 1. Deposits are insured up to ₹5 lakh per depositor per bank. 2. The insurance premium is paid by depositors. 3. It covers deposits in cooperative banks as well as commercial banks.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Premium is paid by banks.
Q7

Under Priority Sector Lending norms, the overall target for Small Finance Banks is:

  1. (a)40% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit
  2. (b)60% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit
  3. (c)75% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit
  4. (d)18% of Adjusted Net Bank Credit
Show answer
Correct: (c)RRBs also 75%; commercial banks 40%.
Q8

Which of the following is NOT a Domestic Systemically Important Bank in India?

  1. (a)State Bank of India
  2. (b)HDFC Bank
  3. (c)ICICI Bank
  4. (d)Punjab National Bank
Show answer
Correct: (d)Only SBI, HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank.
Q9

The "One State One RRB" consolidation in 2025 reduced the number of Regional Rural Banks to:

  1. (a)43
  2. (b)34
  3. (c)28
  4. (d)12
Show answer
Correct: (c)From 43 to 28.
Q10

Consider the Cash Reserve Ratio: 1. It is maintained as balances with the RBI. 2. The RBI Act prescribes a statutory floor of 3% and ceiling of 15%. 3. Banks earn no interest on CRR balances.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Floor/ceiling removed by the 2006 amendment.
