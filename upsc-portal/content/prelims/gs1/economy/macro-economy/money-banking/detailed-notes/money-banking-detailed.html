Money, Banking and RBI Policy — Detailed Notes

Money supply, RBI instruments, MPC frame, banking structure. Rate-name precision decides marks.

1. Money supply & creation

  • RBI measures: M1 = currency + demand deposits + other deposits with RBI; M2 = M1 + savings with Post Office; M3 = M1 + time deposits + government deposits with RBI (aggregate monetary resources); M4 = M3 + total Post Office deposits (ex-NSC). High-powered money (M0) = currency + banks' reserves with RBI.
  • Money multiplier = M3/M0; driven by reserve behaviour; seigniorage; demonetisation 2016 (86% value returned; 99.3% deposited); digital currency: e-rupee CBDC (pilots since 2022; retail + wholesale).

2. RBI instruments (LAF corridor era)

  • Repo rate = single policy rate (since 2021 revised framework docs); SDF (standing deposit facility) = floor (2022, replaced fixed reverse-repo role); MSF = ceiling (+25 bp). Bank rate = penal/long-term signal (aligned to MSF). CRR = 4% (no interest, bi-fortnight maintenance); SLR = 18% (statutory, includes G-sec holdings). OMOs (outright + switches), forex swaps, VRR/VRRR variable-rate auctions, SDF-MSF corridor ±25 bp.
  • MPC (RBI Act, 1934 — amended 2016): 6 members (3 RBI incl. Governor — casting vote; 3 external), inflation target 4% CPI ±2%, failure band (3 consecutive quarters outside band triggers report); bi-monthly cadence — six meetings a year since Oct 2022.
  • Transmission: base rate → MCLR (2016) → EBLR (2019 external-benchmark for retail/SME loans); repo-linked pricing.

3. Banking structure & regulation

  • Segments: PSBs (consolidation 2019-20: 10 mergers → 12 PSBs), private (incl. small finance banks, payments banks — Paytm Payments Bank RBI curbs 2024), RRBs ('One State One RRB' — 28 RRBs from May 2025), co-ops (dual control; 2020 amendment widened RBI power), NBFCs (scale-based regulation 2022), DFIs: NABARD (1982), SIDBI, NaBFID (2021 infrastructure DFI).
  • Regulatory actions: PCA framework, Prompt-corrective; deposit insurance DICGC ₹5 lakh (2021 amendment); Resolution: IBC 2016 (2021 amendment added NBFC route); cyber-fraud norms; UPI-credit lines.
  • Priority sector 40% ANBC (agri 18%, weaker sections 12% target raise, MSME 7.5%); financial inclusion: PMJDY, JAM; BC model; KCC animal-husbandry extension 2020.

MCQ hooks

  • Repo = policy rate; SDF = floor; MSF = ceiling (±25 bp corridor).
  • MPC = 6 (3+3), Governor casting vote; target 4%±2 CPI; bi-monthly.
  • CRR 4% unremunerated; SLR 18% remunerated; both RBI-set (current CRR 4%, SLR 18% with 40% ceiling).
  • M1 < M2 < M3 < M4 composition order; M0 = high-powered.
  • EBLR = external benchmark (repo-linked) since Oct 2019.
  • DICGC cover = ₹5 lakh; NBFC scale-based 2022; RRB 'one state one bank' 2025.
