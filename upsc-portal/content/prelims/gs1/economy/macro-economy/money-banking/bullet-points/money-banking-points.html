Money & Banking — Bullet Points

Rate-and-measure flash.

Money flash

  • M1 → currency + demand deposits + RBI other-deposits · M3 = broad money · M0 = high-powered (currency + reserves).
  • Multiplier = M3/M0 · CBDC e-rupee pilots 2022 (retail + wholesale) · demonetisation 2016.

Rate flash

  • Repo = policy rate · SDF = corridor floor (2022) · MSF = ceiling (+25bp) · bank rate = penal signal.
  • CRR 4% no-interest · SLR 18% · OMO-VRR/VRRR = liquidity tools.
  • Transmission chain → base rate → MCLR (2016) → EBLR (2019).

MPC flash

  • 6 members (3 RBI + 3 external) · Governor casting vote · target 4% CPI ±2% · bi-monthly · failure = 3 quarters outside band → report to Government.

Bank flash

  • PSB consolidation → 12 PSBs · payments banks = deposits-only no-credit · SFBs · NBFC scale-based 2022.
  • DFIs → NABARD 1982, SIDBI, NaBFID 2021 · DICGC ₹5 lakh · priority sector 40% (agri 18, MSME 7.5, weaker 12 target).
  • RRB 2025 → 'one state one bank' consolidation.

Trap flash

  • Reverse repo ≠ SDF (SDF replaced its floor role, rate cut 40bp in 2022) — instrument-name trap.
  • CRR earns no interest; SLR securities do — swap trap.
  • MPC target = CPI headline (not core, not WPI).
  • Payments banks can't lend — 'loans' option trap.
  • Bank rate ≠ repo rate (bank rate now aligned to MSF, penal use).
