Fiscal Policy and Budget — Detailed Notes

Receipts-expenditure-deficit arithmetic + FRBM + extra-budgetary resources. Deficit-identity questions repeat.

1. Budget architecture

  • Art 112 Annual Financial Statement (union budget; merged rail budget 2017; date advanced to 1 Feb 2017); Consolidated Fund (266(1)) vs Public Account (266(2)) vs Contingency Fund (267 — at President's disposal); demand-for-grants + appropriation + finance-bill cycle; guillotine; Vote-on-account; budget documents (AFS + three FRBM statements: macroeconomic framework, medium-term fiscal policy-cum-fiscal policy strategy, medium-term expenditure framework).
  • Receipts: revenue (tax: direct (corporate, income) + indirect (GST); non-tax: dividends, interest, spectrum) + capital (recoveries, disinvestment — now 'asset monetisation' CPSE pipeline + NMP). Expenditure: revenue vs capital; committed (interest > 40% of revenue receipts band); schemes (CSS bucketing 2016; 250+ flagship-umbrellas); plan-nonplan binary abolished 2017.

2. Deficit identities

  • Revenue deficit = revenue expenditure - revenue receipts; Fiscal deficit = total expenditure - (revenue receipts + non-debt capital receipts) = borrowing requirement; Primary deficit = FD - interest payments; effective PD (interest-net interest swap legacy); monetised deficit legacy term.
  • FRBM Act 2003 (amended 2018): FD-GDP target 3%; debt targets (Centre 40%, general-govt 60%); escape clause (0.5% deviation); COVID suspension → glide path: FY26 FD target 4.4% of GDP (announced FY25 budget); debt-GDP anchor shift (FY31 review idea).
  • State fiscals: 3% FD limit (borrowing tied); Article 293 consent; off-budget obs (2019-21 brought on-book: FCI subsidies, food-subsidy accounting change 2020-21).

3. Extra-budgetary & fiscal-federal bits

  • Extra-budgetary resources: PSU bond-borrowing for capex, guarantee stock, NSSF loans swap (ended), IEBR of CPSEs; National Monetisation Pipeline (2021, ₹6 lakh crore 2022-25); asset-monetisation vs disinvestment distinction; strategic-sector policy (2021: 4+5 sectors).
  • Tax-buoyancy, counter-cyclicality debates; fiscal-multiplier usage (PMGKY 2020, capex-loan-to-states 2020-25 = 50-year interest-free special window); UDAY legacy (power DISCOM debt 2015, now RDSS 2021).

MCQ hooks

  • FD = borrowings; PD = FD minus interest — the recurring formula pair.
  • Contingency fund = Art 267, President's disposal; Public account = no-vote trust (PF, small savings).
  • FRBM 2003, escape clause 0.5%, glide path FY26 = 4.4% FD.
  • Plan/non-plan ended 2017; rail budget merged 2017; 1 Feb date 2017.
  • NMP 2021 = monetisation (not sale); strategic sectors 2021 policy.
  • States: 3% FD + Art 293 borrowing consent.
