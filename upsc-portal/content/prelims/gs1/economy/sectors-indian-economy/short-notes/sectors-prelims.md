# Sectors of the Indian Economy — Prelims Drill

## Three-sector shares (GVA)
Services ~55% · Industry ~27% · Agriculture & allied ~18% — but employment share inverts: **agriculture still employs the largest slice (~45%)** (PLFS 2023-24 band).

## Agriculture pack
- Crops: staging (Kharif/Rabi/Zaid), MSP — **23 crops** (14 Kharif + 6 Rabi + 2 commercial + Toria/copra etc count variants); fixing formula **1.5 × A2+FL** (paid-out costs + family labour) per Budget-2018 line; announced by **CCEA on CACP recommendation**.
- **NFSA 2013**: priority-household entitlement 5 kg/person/month; Antyodaya 35 kg/family/month; coverage up to **75% rural / 50% urban**; central issue prices (rice ₹3, wheat ₹2, coarse ₹1/kg; **free grains via PMGKAY continuation through Dec 2023 → 5-year free run to 2028 announced**).
- Buffer norms + FCI economics; decentralised procurement states.
- Missions: PM-KISAN (₹6,000/yr) · PMFBY (2016; premium 2% kharif/1.5% rabi/5% commercial-horticultural; rest shared centre:state 50:50 default [90:10 NE/hilly]) · e-NAM · Soil Health Cards · PMKSY (per-drop-more-crop; Har Khet Ko Pani) · KUSUM (solar pumps; feeder solarisation) · MIS (Market Intervention Scheme) · Operation Greens (TOP → TOTAL 2018) · Agriculture Export Policy 2018 e commerce Agristack CA 2024-25 (registries + crop-sown data pilots).
- Irrigation stats: net-irrigated share ~50%+ of net sown (micro-irrigation penetration growing ~MP/Gujarat/Andhra leaders).
- Animal economy: **India world's largest milk producer** (~24% share, 230+ mn tonnes recent); NDDB Operation Flood heritage; Rashtriya Gokul Mission.
- Fisheries: **Blue Revolution**, PMMSY (2020), largest-2nd/3rd exporter profiles; Inland vs marine split inland-dominant now.
- Horticulture crossed foodgrains production bands late-2010s (horticulture ~355 mn tonnes recent standing > grains) — framing note.

## Industry pack
- **IIP** (2011–12 base): mining ~14.4%, manufacturing ~77.6%, electricity ~8%; use-based categories (primary/capital/intermediate/infrastructure/consumer durable-non-durable).
- **PMI (S&P Global)**: manufacturing & services trackers (>50 expansion) — record highs printed 2024-25 services runs.
- **CPSE taxonomy**: Maharatna (13–14), Navratna (~20+), Miniratna I/II — verify current counts at exam.
- Industrial Policy moments: 1956 (schedule A/B), 1977/1980 (focus small-scale), **1991** (delicensing sweep), DIPP→DPIIT avatar (2019), industrial corridors (DMIC anchor), IPRs.
- MSME definitions: 2006 MSMED Act → 2020 composite (investment+turnover micro ₹1/5 cr; small ₹10/50 cr; medium ₹50/250 cr) → **Budget 2025 uplift**: micro ₹2.5/10 cr, small ₹25/100 cr, medium ₹125/500 cr (effective FY26 notification).
- PLI: 14 key sectors (electronics-IT hardware bulk of disbursements, pharma APIs-KSMs, telecom gear, white goods ACC/AC/LED, solar PV, autos & ACC batteries, drones, textiles, food processing, speciality steel, medical devices); ₹1.97 lakh cr envelope announcement lineage.
- Steel: 2nd-largest producer (~140 MT; China ~bn-tonne scale); Coal India & SCCL etc; Commercial coal mining opened 2020; offshore mineral auctions regime 2023 amendments.

## Services pack
- Share GVA ~55%; big blocks: trade-hotels-transport, financial-real estate-professional, public admin.
- IT-BPM exports run above $200 bn (FY24 ~$254 bn including hardware cited by industry bodies — quote the safe floor as "software & services exports > $200 bn"); GCC (global capability centres) boom metrics (~1,600+ centres; Bengaluru/Hyderabad hyderabad clusters).
- Tourism: **Dekho Apna Desh**, Swadesh Darshan 2.0, PRASHAD; foreign-arrival numbers recovery to ~pre-2020 bands recently.
- Logistics: PM Gati Shakti layering; Logistics Policy 2022; LEADS state rankings (MoC).
- Railways: Vande Bharat rollout counts rising; Kavach anti-collision systems rollout (post-2023 Balasore accident impetus).

## Infra/energy flash
- Installed power capacity ~4.4–4.6 lakh MW band (2025) with non-fossil crossing **~48-50%** (target markers triangulate 2030 500 GW).
- Renewables: solar parks (Bhadla largest-claim), wind (TN/GJ), bio-energy, small-hydro buckets under MNRE; **Green Hydrogen Mission ₹19,744 cr** targets 5 MMT/yr by 2030; **Nuclear ~7-8 GWe**.
- Coal peak shares: coal ~55% of commercial primary energy mix still.
