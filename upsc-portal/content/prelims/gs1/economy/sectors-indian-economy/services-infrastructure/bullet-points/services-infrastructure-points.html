Services & Infrastructure — Bullet Points

Pipeline-anchor flash.

Services flash

  • ~55% GVA · IT-BPM ~8% GDP, $200bn exports · GCC 1,600+ · NASSCOM body.
  • Tourism → SD 2.0 + PRASHAD · medical travel · LPI 38th · LEADS domestic · 7.5% logistics target.

Pipeline flash

  • NIP 2019 ₹111L cr · NMP ₹6L cr · NIIF 2015 · Gati Shakti Oct 2021 · capex ~3.4% GDP.
  • DFC east+west · Bharatmala · UDAN · Vadhavan 2024 · NW-1 cargo.

Energy-urban flash

  • Surya Ghar 2024 (1 cr rooftops) · KUSUM pumps · RDSS 2021 · OSOWOG grid idea.
  • AMRUT 2.0 · SBM-U 2.0 · PM-eBus 10k · SVAMITVA cards · CCTS 2023 carbon market.

Trap flash

  • NIP (pipeline) ≠ NMP (monetisation) — numbers and acronyms swap.
  • LEADS = Indian survey; LPI = World Bank ranking.
  • Gati Shakti (2021 platform) ≠ NLP (2022 policy document).
  • UDAN = air connectivity, not highways.
  • SVAMITVA = rural property; SVANidhi = vendor credit — name trap.
