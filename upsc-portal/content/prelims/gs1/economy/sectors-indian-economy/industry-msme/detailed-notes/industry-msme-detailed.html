Industry and MSME — Detailed Notes

MSME definitions, schemes, industrial-policy frame. Definition-table precision is the exam skill.

1. MSME frame

  • Udyam registration (1 Jul 2020; self-declared online); composite definition (investment + turnover): Micro ≤₹1 cr / ≤₹5 cr; Small ≤₹10 cr / ≤₹50 cr; Medium ≤₹50 cr / ≤₹250 cr — 2025 budget: limits raised (investment 2.5x, turnover 2x); 45-day payment rule (MSME Samadhaan); Udyam Assist for informal units.
  • Credit: CGTMSE (collateral-free guarantee; ₹5 cr cover 2023), PMEGP, Mudra tiers, TReDS invoice-discounting platforms (RXIL-M1xchange-Invoicemart), ECLGS wound down.
  • Skill-quality: ZED certification, Lean clusters, tech centres; PM Vishwakarma (2023; 18 trades); SFURTI; Aatmanirbhar-era packages.

2. Industrial policy & manufacturing

  • Lineage: IPR 1948-56 (licence-quota) → 1980s easing → NIP 1991 (de-licensing, 51% auto-FDI) → Make in India (2014) → PLI (2020; 14 sectors) → NMP (2021) → Gati Shakti (2021) + National Logistics Policy (2022).
  • Institutions: DPIIT, 4 Labour Codes (Wages 2019 + IR-Social Security-OSH 2020; enacted, staged), e-Shram registry; World Bank B-READY (successor to EoDB); defence corridors (UP-TN); electronics PLI winners (Noida-Sriperumbudur; mobile exports #1 category).

MCQ hooks

  • MSME test = investment AND turnover (Micro 1/5, Small 10/50, Medium 50/250 cr).
  • Udyam 2020 self-declaration; 45-day payment + Samadhaan.
  • CGTMSE = guarantee; TReDS = invoice discounting; PMEGP = employment subsidy.
  • PLI 14 sectors; Make in India 2014; NIP 1991; NMP 2021.
  • Labour codes = 4 (1+3), staged implementation.
  • ZED = quality cert; Vishwakarma = 18 trades 2023.
