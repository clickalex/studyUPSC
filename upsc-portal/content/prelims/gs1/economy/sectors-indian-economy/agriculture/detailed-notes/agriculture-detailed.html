Agricultural Economy — Detailed Notes

Shares, market reforms, input stack, allied sectors. Number-bands + scheme-name recall (FY25 budget vintage).

1. Sector profile

  • ~18% of GVA but ~46% of employment (PLFS) — productivity-gap headline; average holding ~1.08 ha; ~86% small-marginal (Agriculture Census); foodgrain ~330+ mt band; buffer norms (rice-wheat monthly); agri-exports ~$50bn — rice #1, marine, spices, sugar cycles.
  • Cropping: rice-wheat dominance, pulses (MP-MH-RJ), oilseeds gap (edible-oil imports ~55-60%), sugarcane (FRP-SAP), cotton, horticulture value > foodgrain (~355 mt band).

2. Markets, prices, reforms

  • MSP: CACP recommends (22 crops + copra), Cabinet notifies; procurement FCI-NAFED-NCCF; PM-AASHA (2018: PSF + PDPS arms); Swaminathan C2 vs A2+50% debate; 2020 farm acts repealed Nov 2021.
  • Reform arc: APMC model acts (2003, 2018), eNAM (2016; 1,380+ mandis), FPO scheme (2020; 10,000); storage: 10-year grain-silo programme (2023); PMKSY cold chain.

3. Inputs, credit, allied

  • Fertiliser: NBS 2010, neem coating, nano-urea (2021), PM-PRANAM (2023); seeds: Bt-cotton only GM, genome-editing SDN-1/2 exempt (2022); credit: KCC saturation, interest subvention, NABARD RIDF; insurance: PMFBY 2.0 (2020-21 revisions); tech: AgriStack, Krishi-DSS (2024), Kisan e-Mitra AI, Namo Drone Didi (SHGs); natural-farming mission (2024).
  • Allied: dairy — world's largest producer (~24% global; Operation Flood 1970, Kurien-NDDB); fisheries — PMMSY (2020) + Matsya Kisan Samridhi (2024); poultry; beekeeping (NBHM); bamboo (MSDP).

MCQ hooks

  • Agri: ~18% GVA, ~46% jobs; holdings 1.08 ha; 86% marginal.
  • MSP = 22 + copra; CACP recommends → Cabinet notifies; PM-AASHA 2018.
  • eNAM 2016; FPO 2020; farm laws repealed 21 Nov 2021.
  • Nano-urea 2021; PM-PRANAM 2023; Krishi-DSS 2024; Drone Didi SHGs.
  • Dairy = world #1 (~24%); Operation Flood 1970; Kurien.
  • PMMSY 2020; horticulture value > foodgrain.
