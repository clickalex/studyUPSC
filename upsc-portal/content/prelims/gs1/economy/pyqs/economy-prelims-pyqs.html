Indian Economy — Prelims PYQs

Macro-patterned; answers original; cross-check live CA before exam.

Q1. Which correctly describes the components of revenue deficit? (a) Total expenditure minus total receipts (b) Revenue expenditure minus revenue receipts (c) Fiscal deficit minus interest payments (d) Capital expenditure minus capital receipts

Model answer: (b). Fiscal deficit uses total figures; (c) is primary deficit.

Q2. The MPC's inflation target band as of March 2031 framework is: (a) 2% ± 1% (b) 4% ± 2% (c) 5% ± 1% (d) 6% point-target

Model answer: (b). 4% ± 2% band, extended via 2025 government notification through FY30-31.

Q3. Which tool drains liquidity without explicit collateral transfer to the RBI? (a) Repo (b) Reverse repo (c) Standing Deposit Facility (SDF) (d) MSF

Model answer: (c). SDF (introduced April 2022) absorbs liquidity without collateral — floor of the LAF corridor.

Q4. [PYQ-pattern] The national-income accounting base year in India currently is: (a) 2004–05 (b) 2011–12 (c) 2017–18 series prevailing (d) 1993–94

Model answer: (b). Quick check, avoid the distractor "2017-18" revision debates — actual series remains 2011–12.

Q5. NARCL refers to: (a) National Asset Reconstruction Company — the government-backed bad bank (b) National Aadhaar Registry for Credit Lines (c) National Agricultural Recovery Cooperative Ltd (d) Naval Arsenal Research Command Lab

Model answer: (a). Incorporated 2021; aggregates large NPAs; IDRCL handles resolution.

Q6. After 2019–20 public-sector bank consolidation, the count of PSBs fell to: (a) 10 (b) 12 (c) 18 (d) 21

Model answer: (b). From 27 (2017) to 12 by April 2020 mergers.

Q7. LIBOR/ORR regime transitions in India have moved to: (a) MIBOR exclusively (b) T-bill benchmark (c) CRISIL policy rate (d) Modified MIFOR abolished / fallback to ARR-style references

Model answer: (a)–(d) trap: correct framing = Indian markets standardized MIBOR as the primary overnight benchmark post-LIBOR; MIFOR being wound down. For exam safety: pick (a).

Q8. The "divisible pool" of taxes shares between centre and states excludes: (a) Personal income tax (b) Cesses and surcharges (c) Corporation tax (d) Customs duty

Model answer: (b). Cesses/surcharges are centre-exclusive — a classic federalism friction point.

Q9. Which statement on India’s forex reserve record is correct as per 2024–25 data? (a) Reserves never crossed $600 bn (b) Reserves peaked near $704 bn (~Oct 2024) (c) Reserves are counted in SDR only (d) RBI does not hold gold within reserves

Model answer: (b). RBI holds gold within reserves; statements (c) and (d) are false.

Q10. Under IBC (2016, as amended), the corporate insolvency resolution process standard timeline is: (a) 90 days (b) 180 days extendable by 90 (c) 330 days absolute hard limit including litigation (d) No time limit

Model answer: (c). 180 + 90 extensible; Supreme Court/Amendment clarified outer limit 330 days including litigation (2019 amendment; 2020 Essar-related articulation).
