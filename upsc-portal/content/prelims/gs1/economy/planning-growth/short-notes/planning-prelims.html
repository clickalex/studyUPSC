Planning, Growth & Development — Prelims Drill

Planning lineage

  • Planning Commission (1950 executive resolution; PM chair; NDC approved plans) → NITI Aayog 2015 (resolution — "Think tank + cooperative federalism" mandate; Governing Council = PM + CMs + LGs; no fund-allocation power (that moved to Finance Commission & line ministries)).
  • Five-Year Plans history: 1st (1951-56, Harrod-Domar; target 2.1% achieved 3.6%); 2nd (Mahalanobis heavy-industry; 4.5% target; 4.3% achieved); 7th, 8th etc; 10th (8% target; 7.6/7.7 achieved); 11th (faster-inclusive; ~6.8/7.9 range achieved); 12th (2012-17; 8% target; ~6.5-7 achieved).
  • 1951 → green revolution (mid-60s, HYV wheat/rice), 1970s poverty-line discourse, 1991 crisis-reform pivot (BoP crisis; IMF conditionalities; LPG liberalisation-privatisation-globalisation), 1997 Asian crisis insulated, 2008 GFC stimulus/deficit surge, 2020 COVID V-shape.

Poverty lines — succession

  • Alagh committee 1979 (calorie 2400/2100 kcal rural/urban).
  • Lakdawala committee 1993 (state-specific CPIAL/CPIIW indexes).
  • Tendulkar committee 2009: MPCE linked — mixed recall period; poverty line ~₹33.3 urban / ₹27.2 rural (2011-12 prices; monthly).
  • Rangarajan committee 2014: higher lines ~₹47 urban / ₹32 rural (2011-12; daily) & ~₹1,407/₹972 monthly; poverty ratios 29.5% (2011-12) vs Tendulkar's 21.9%.
  • Post-2011: no official consumption-based update; NSO 2017-18 recall methodology quashed → MPI alternative; NITI MPI (2021): 24.85% (2015-16) → 14.96% (2019-21); UNDP 2024 ~16.4% (2019-21).
  • World Bank $2.15 (2017 PPP) extreme poverty — India under ~3% claimed band in 2022-23 estimates; IHD debates on comparability.

Employment measures

  • Sources: PLFS (NSO; annual since 2017-18), CMIE CPHS (private gauge), QES, EPFO/MUDRA payroll proxies (indicators not headcounts).
  • Concepts: UPS (usual status; includes principal+subsidiary = UPSS convention), CWS (current week), US (daily); LFPR, WPR, UR definitions earn marks.
  • Recent banding: UR (usual status 15+) 5.6% (2022-23) → 3.2% (2023-24); LFPR improving; FLFPR ~37–42% band (2023-24 PLFS frames).

Growth arithmetic anchors

  • Pre-1991 "Hindu rate" ~3.5%; post-1991 6%-there; 2004-08 ~8%+ golden run; FY21 COVID -5.8%; FY22 rebound ~9.7%; FY23 ~7%; FY24 ~8.2% (provisional revised); FY25 ~6.5%; FY26 ~6.4-6.8% band estimate (RBIsurveys); catalogued long-run aspiration 7-8%.
  • Infra capex push: ₹10–11.2 lakh crore capex budget lines (FY25/26); capex-GDP ~3.4% centre.
  • Population-economics: median age ~28 (2023); working-age bulge till ~2040-41 (UN band); dependency declines then rises.

Human development indices

  • HDI (UNDP, 189 countries): India mid-130s rank (2022 report: 134/193; 2023-24 report update around 130-136 — verify latest).
  • MPI: dimensions education/health/living-standard × 10 indicators × deprivations>33.33% convention.
  • GHI (Global Hunger Index): India ~105/127 (2024) — methodological disputes with official counters; hedged use.
  • GII (Gender Inequality), GGG (WEF Global Gender Gap: India ~127/146 (2023) band), GGI (Global Gender Gap, separate), EIU democracy, CPI Transparency (~93rd/180 in 2024, score 38) etc.

Distribution picture

  • Consumption-expenditure Gini (~35-ish HCES 2022-23 upscale debate vs older ~0.35); income Gini far higher (WID world estimates >0.5).
  • Oxfam inequality reports vs World Bank/NSS trends — present both with methodological caveats.
  • Rural-urban consumption ratio updates (HCES 2022-23 MPCE rural ₹3,773 urban ₹6,459 — first release Aug 2024).

Growth-with-jobs puzzle lines

  • Premature deindustrialisation discussions; construction's absorption role; services-productivity-without-jobs lines; gig-elastance; micro-enterprise quotients.
  • ST/SC/EWS/OBC-linked job data — PLFS caste cuts.

Traps

  • NITI Aayog = executive resolution body; NOT statutory/constitutional.
  • Finance Commission ≠ Planning-era aligned; FC constitutional (Art 280) — 16th FC: Arvind Panagariya chair (2023 constitution; report window 2025 → FY2026-31).
  • GHI ≠ government index — private (Welthungerhilfe/Concern); official counters dispute methodology.
  • MPI uses NFHS (three dimensions/10 indicators); headcount-ratio × intensity.
  • Percentile vs growth statistic conflation is the standard trap.
