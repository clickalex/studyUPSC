Planning, NITI Aayog & Growth Strategy — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Consider the following statements about NITI Aayog: 1. It was created by an Act of Parliament in 2015. 2. Its Governing Council comprises the Prime Minister, Chief Ministers and Lieutenant Governors/administrators of Union Territories. 3. It allocates plan funds to states.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 only
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Cabinet resolution, no fund allocation powers.
Q2

Match the Five-Year Plans with their features: 1. Second Plan — Mahalanobis heavy-industry strategy 2. Eighth Plan — launched after two years of annual plans and coincided with liberalisation 3. Twelfth Plan — theme "faster, more inclusive and sustainable growth" Which of the above are correctly matched?

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correctly matched.
Q3

The "plan holiday" in India's planning history refers to:

  1. (a)1978–80 when the Janata government adopted rolling plans
  2. (b)1966–69 when annual plans replaced a regular Five-Year Plan after wars and drought
  3. (c)1990–92 between the Seventh and Eighth Plans
  4. (d)2014–15 when the Planning Commission was abolished
Show answer
Correct: (b)Three annual plans 1966–69.
Q4

The Aspirational Blocks Programme launched in 2023 covers how many blocks?

  1. (a)112
  2. (b)250
  3. (c)500
  4. (d)750
Show answer
Correct: (c)500 blocks; 112 districts under ADP.
Q5

According to the World Bank's April 2025 update, the share of India's population living in extreme poverty (at $2.15 per day, 2017 PPP) in 2022–23 was about:

  1. (a)2.3%
  2. (b)5.3%
  3. (c)11.3%
  4. (d)16.2%
Show answer
Correct: (a)16.2% in 2011–12 to 2.3%; 5.3% at the new $3 line.
Q6

Consider the following statements about the Fiscal Responsibility and Budget Management framework: 1. The 2018 amendment set a general government debt target of 60% of GDP. 2. The Union Budget 2025–26 shifted the medium-term anchor to a Centre debt-to-GDP ratio of about 50% by March 2031. 3. The fiscal deficit target for 2025–26 was 4.4% of GDP.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q7

In the SDG India Index 2023–24 released by NITI Aayog, the top-ranked states were:

  1. (a)Kerala and Uttarakhand
  2. (b)Tamil Nadu and Goa
  3. (c)Himachal Pradesh and Sikkim
  4. (d)Kerala and Tamil Nadu
Show answer
Correct: (a)Both scored 79; Bihar last.
Q8

The Income-tax Act, 2025 which replaces the 1961 Act: 1. Comes into force from 1 April 2026. 2. Replaces the concepts of "previous year" and "assessment year" with a single "tax year". 3. Raises the highest income-tax slab rate to 40%.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Slab rates were not changed by the new Act.
Q9

The Sixteenth Finance Commission, whose report was submitted in November 2025, is chaired by:

  1. (a)N.K. Singh
  2. (b)Arvind Panagariya
  3. (c)Y.V. Reddy
  4. (d)Suman Bery
Show answer
Correct: (b)N.K. Singh chaired FC-15.
Q10

Which of the following bodies in independent India's planning framework were constituted by executive resolution rather than by statute? 1. Planning Commission 2. National Development Council 3. NITI Aayog 4. Finance Commission

  1. (a)1 and 3
  2. (b)1, 2 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (b)Finance Commission is constitutional (Art. 280).
