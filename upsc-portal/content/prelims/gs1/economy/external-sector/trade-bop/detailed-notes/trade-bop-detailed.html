External Trade and Balance of Payments — Detailed Notes

BoP structure, India's services-led profile, FTAs and export-promotion instruments.

1. BoP structure

  • Current account: goods (chronic BoT deficit), services (IT-BPM surplus), primary income (interest-dividends, negative), secondary income (remittances — world #1, ~$120bn band). Capital account: FDI (stable), FPI (volatile), ECBs, NRI deposits, trade credit. Balancing item = reserve changes.
  • CAD anchors: FY13 peak ~4.8% GDP (taper-tantrum) → FY21 sub-1% → FY24 ~0.7%; oil-gold sensitivity; rupee managed float; reserves ~$650-700bn band (top-4).

2. Trade profile

  • Merchandise exports: engineering, petroleum products, pharma, gems-jewellery, electronics (rising), agri (rice #1, marine, spices). Imports: crude (largest), gold, electronics, machinery, coal. Partners: US #1 destination, China #2 source (largest deficit), UAE-Gulf. FTP 2023: $2 trillion combined exports by 2030; districts-as-export-hubs.
  • Services: IT-BPM engine (~$200bn band); travel recovering; GATS modes 1-4 — India pushes Mode 1 (cross-border supply) + Mode 4 (movement of natural persons).

3. Policy instruments

  • FTP 2023 (continuous, no sunset); RoDTEP (2021; replaced MEIS; WTO-consistent duty remission); RoSCTL (apparel-made-ups); EPCG + Advance Authorisation; SEZ (2005 act) - EOU; DGTR (2018 merged trade-remedies authority); QCOs; LEADS logistics index; Gati Shakti.
  • FTAs: UAE CEPA (2022), Australia ECTA (2022; CECA talks), Japan-Singapore-ASEAN-Korea-Malaysia legacy, EFTA TEPA (2024: $100bn/15-yr investment commitment), India-UK CETA (2025), EU negotiations; RCEP exit (2019); IPEF partial join (supply-chain pillar).

MCQ hooks

  • CAD = goods + services + primary + secondary income (capital flows excluded).
  • Remittances = secondary income, world #1.
  • RoDTEP → replaced MEIS 2021 (WTO ruling context); DGTR = 2018.
  • FTP 2023 target: $2tn by 2030; no sunset clause.
  • EFTA TEPA 2024 ($100bn/15y); UK CETA 2025; RCEP exit 2019.
  • GATS: Mode 1 supply, Mode 3 presence, Mode 4 person-movement.
