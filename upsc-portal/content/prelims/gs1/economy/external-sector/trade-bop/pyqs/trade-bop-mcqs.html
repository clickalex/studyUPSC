Trade & Balance of Payments — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Which of the following are recorded in the current account of India's Balance of Payments? 1. Remittances from Indians working abroad 2. Foreign direct investment inflows 3. Software services exports 4. Interest paid on external commercial borrowings

  1. (a)1, 3 and 4
  2. (b)1 and 2
  3. (c)2, 3 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)FDI is in the financial/capital account.
Q2

Consider India's external sector in 2024–25: 1. The merchandise trade deficit was a record of about $287 billion. 2. The current account deficit was below 1% of GDP. 3. Services exports exceeded $380 billion.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct (CAD 0.6%).
Q3

In 2024–25, India's largest source of imports and the country with which India ran its largest bilateral trade deficit was:

  1. (a)United States
  2. (b)China
  3. (c)Russia
  4. (d)UAE
Show answer
Correct: (b)Imports $113.5 bn; deficit ~$99 bn.
Q4

Under the Liberalised Remittance Scheme, a resident individual may remit abroad up to:

  1. (a)$100,000 per financial year
  2. (b)$250,000 per financial year
  3. (c)$500,000 per financial year
  4. (d)$1 million per financial year
Show answer
Correct: (b)TCS applies above ₹10 lakh (2025).
Q5

Consider the India–EFTA Trade and Economic Partnership Agreement: 1. It came into force on 1 October 2025. 2. It is India's first trade agreement containing a binding investment commitment by the partner bloc. 3. EFTA includes Switzerland, Norway, Iceland and Liechtenstein.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q6

RoDTEP replaced the Merchandise Exports from India Scheme mainly because:

  1. (a)MEIS was found inconsistent with WTO subsidy rules
  2. (b)MEIS covered only services
  3. (c)RoDTEP offers higher direct cash subsidies
  4. (d)MEIS was limited to SEZ units
Show answer
Correct: (a)WTO panel ruled against MEIS in 2019.
Q7

Which of the following statements about India's foreign exchange reserves is/are correct? 1. They peaked above $700 billion in September 2024. 2. Gold constitutes roughly half of the reserves. 3. India holds the fourth largest reserves in the world.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Gold is ~12–13% of reserves.
Q8

The Foreign Exchange Management Act, 1999:

  1. (a)Treats forex violations as criminal offences
  2. (b)Replaced FERA and treats violations as civil offences
  3. (c)Is enforced by the Reserve Bank of India alone
  4. (d)Prohibits current account transactions without RBI permission
Show answer
Correct: (b)ED enforces FEMA.
Q9

India's single largest export destination and its largest overall two-way trading partner in 2024–25 was:

  1. (a)China
  2. (b)United Arab Emirates
  3. (c)United States
  4. (d)Netherlands
Show answer
Correct: (c)US exports $86.5 bn; two-way $131.8 bn.
Q10

Consider the additional US tariffs on Indian goods in August 2025: 1. A 25% "reciprocal" tariff and a further 25% penalty linked to Russian oil purchases took the total to 50%. 2. Pharmaceuticals and smartphones were exempted. 3. India responded by withdrawing from all bilateral trade talks with the US.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)BTA negotiations continued.
