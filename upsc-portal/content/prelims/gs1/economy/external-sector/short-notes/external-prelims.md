# External Sector — Prelims Drill

## BoP architecture
- Current Account = goods trade + services + primary income + secondary income (remittances big here).
- Capital/Financial Account = FDI + FPI + external loans (ECB/NRI deposits) + banking capital + SDR allocations.
- Errors & omissions line; overall balance → **change in reserves** (valuation adjustments non-flow).
- India's typical shape: **merchandise deficit** (~$25–30 bn/month recent band) − services surplus (+) + remittance surplus (+) → CAD narrow (~0.5–1.2% GDP recent bands).

## Forex-management tools
- Reserves composition: **foreign currency assets** (dominant) + gold + SDRs + reserve tranche in the IMF; peak ~**$704 bn (Sept–Oct 2024)**; import-cover ~11–12 months; **sovereign debt-servicing coverage** ratios strengthening.
- Rupee regime: managed float (no hard band); NEER/REER tracked on 40-currency basket; capital-account convertibility partial; full capital-account convertibility not adopted (Tarapore committee legacy tasks).
- RBI FX actions: spot interventions, dollar-rupee buy/sell swaps, LCR-tension smoothing, forward-book posture — LRS ($250k/yr remittance ceiling maintained).

## FDI rules pockets (verify-ceiling updates)
- Routes: automatic vs government approval (sensitive sectors).
- Defence: **74% auto; above via government route** (modernization condition text 2020 onwards).
- Insurance: **74% automatic (2021)**; pension 49% auto; multi-brand retail **51% government-route** with conditions; single-brand 100% (with sourcing relaxation cadence); print news 26% gov-route; digital news (upload/stream) **26% gov route (2019)**; space sector **2024 liberalisation**: 100% auto for components/systems/sub-systems — capped routes for satellites & launch vehicles (49%-auto satellites data levels; launch vehicle mfg 49% auto / beyond gov); insurance-for-rail... etc.
- Top sourcing jurisdictions: Singapore, Mauritius, UAE, Netherlands, US (CGN Surveillance 2020→Press Note 3: land-border countries need government approval).

## Trade agreements ledger
- India-UAE CEPA (2022) · India-Australia ECTA (2022) · **India-EFTA TEPA (signed Mar 2024; EFTA = Switzerland, Norway, Iceland, Liechtenstein; US$100 bn over 15 yrs investment pledge)** · India-UK CETA (signed Jul 2025) · ASEAN-India CEPA (goods 2010; review active AITIGA) · India-Korea CEPA 2010 · India-Japan CEPA 2011 · recent with Mauritius CECPA 2021.
- RCEP: India opted out (2019) — keep citing why (China imbalance, dairy/steel sensitivities).

## Trade stats pairs
- Top exports: petroleum products, gems & jewellery, textiles+RMG, pharma formulations, chemicals, engineering goods, rice (largest exporter band ~40% world share buffets), marine products (shrimp anchor).
- Top imports: crude oil & products, gold, electronics, machinery, coal & coke (imports essential), chemicals, plastics; vegetable-oil basket (Indonesia/Malaysia palm; Argentina/Brazil soya; sunflower Ucr/Rus).
- Major partners: US (largest goods partner 2023-24+), China (largest imports partner; deficit ~$85-100 bn), UAE.
- Trade-deficit-with-China concentration (electronics APIs, solar cells, APIs).

## Currency-internationalisation moves
- Rupee trade-settlement windows since Jul 2022 (RBI framework; SRVA vostro stack) — adoption slow.
- Asian Clearing Union conversations; rupee-Dirham/Dollar-Rupee on-exchange futures; LRS unchanged; GIFT City IFSC — NSE IX derivatives; GIFT IFSCA as single regulator (2020).

## Traps
- Current Account NOT same as Balance of Trade (BOT). BOT = goods only.
- Reserves peak figure — note "peaked ~$704 bn" and "hovering $650–700 bn in 2025".
- FPI vs FDI: portfolio (<10% intent) vs stakes-acquisitions; ADR/GDR distinct (foreign-listed shares).
- ECB = corporates' foreign borrowings; masala bonds (offshore rupee bonds) = IFC-issued kind since 2015 (HDFC/NTPC series).
- Press Note 3 (2020) — not tariff, it's the land-border FDI approval gate (anti-opportunistic takeover).
