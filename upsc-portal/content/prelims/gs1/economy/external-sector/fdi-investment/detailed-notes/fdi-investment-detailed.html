FDI, FPI and Investment Vehicles — Detailed Notes

Routes, sector caps, vehicles (InvIT-REIT-AIF), and the FDI-FPI distinction. Route-cap questions recur.

1. FDI routes & sector map

  • Automatic route (most sectors: manufacturing 100%); approval route (few: multi-brand retail with 30% local sourcing, print media 26%, banking-private 74%, insurance 74%, defence 74% automatic since 2020 with safeguards, space 100% certain activities 2024, telecom 100% automatic era); prohibited list: lottery-gambling-betting, chit funds, Nidhi, real-estate business (trading; construction-development allowed), tobacco manufacturing, agriculture/plantation (with exceptions), atomic energy, railway operations (services FDI 100% allowed, operations state monopoly).
  • Policy anchors: 2016+ liberalisation rounds; press-note 3 (2020): land-bordering-country approvals (China screening); DPPM sourcing rules; e-commerce FDI (inventory model banned, marketplace allowed).
  • Flows: FY21-24 gross inflows ~$70-85bn band; sources: Singapore > Mauritius > USA (Mauritius declining post-2017 treaty amendment; Singapore-Mauritius treaty tightening 2024); sectors: services, computer-software, trading, automobiles.

2. FDI vs FPI & vehicles

  • FDI = 10%+ stake with lasting interest-management participation (IMF BoP definition); FPI = listed securities without control (FPI framework 2014 merged FIIs-sub-accounts-QFIs; two-category simplification 2019).
  • InvITs (2014 SEBI regs; IRB-IndiGrid-NHAI InvIT), REITs (2014; Embassy-Mindspace), SM REITs (2024); AIF (2012 regs): Cat-I (VC, social-venture, infrastructure, SME), Cat-II (PE-debt), Cat-III (hedge/trading, leverage allowed); FVCI; startup-unicorn financing lines.
  • GIFT IFSC (Gujarat; IFSCA act 2019 unified regulator): offshore rupee products, GIFT-Nifty (2023 shift from SGX), aircraft-shipping leasing, FDI-agnostic regime.

MCQ hooks

  • FDI = 10%+ control test; FPI = no-control listed portfolio.
  • Prohibited list verbatim (lottery-chit-real-estate-trading-tobacco-atomic).
  • Defence = 74% automatic (2020); space liberalisation 2024; insurance-banking = 74%.
  • Press Note 3 (2020) = land-border screening.
  • AIF: Cat-III only leveraged-hedge; InvIT-REIT = trust structures.
  • IFSCA 2019 = unified IFSC regulator; GIFT-Nifty 2023.
  • Top sources: Singapore, Mauritius (post-treaty decline), USA.
