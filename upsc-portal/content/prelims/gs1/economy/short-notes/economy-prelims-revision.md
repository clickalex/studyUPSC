# Indian Economy — Prelims Rapid Revision

> Number-heavy recall; growth + inflation + fiscal ladder questions.

## Macro anchor set
- GDP base year **2011–12** (national accounts & IIP); WPI 2011–12; CPI combined 2012 base.
- GDP vs GNP: GDP = domestic territory; GNP adds NFIA (net factor income from abroad).
- GVA vs GDP: GDP = ΣGVA + net product taxes.
- Fiscal deficit = total expenditure − non-debt receipts; primary = fiscal − interest; revenue deficit = revenue exp − revenue receipts.

## Inflation mechanics
- CPI-C headline target band **4% ± 2%** (MPC, affirmed to **March 2031**); WPI tracks wholesale.
- Demand-pull vs cost-push vs built-in (wage-price); stagflation = stagnation + inflation together.
- Core inflation = CPI minus food & fuel (sticky services); headline volatility from food basket weight ~45%.

## Money & banking
- Repo: overnight; MSF: marginal standing facility at repo+; SDF: standing deposit facility (2022; corridor floor; equal to repo- often).
- CRR: cash reserve (RBI); SLR: liquid assets (government securities); NSFR/LCR Basel III.
- NPA taxonomy via SARFAESI 2002 → IBC 2016 (14→330 day timelines), ARCs/NARCL bad bank (2021), PCA framework for weak banks.
- 12 public-sector banks after consolidations (PNB-led mega mergers 2019-20).

## Budget arithmetic
- Deficits: fiscal (~5.1% FY26 trajectory target; 4.5% by FY25-26 committed path), revenue (declining), effective revenue deficit ~nil target.
- Tax ratios: gross tax-GDP ~11.5–12%; direct-vs-direct growing after 2021 (collections buoyancy ~1.5).
- FRBM Act 2003 (targets postponed multiple times); 15th FC window 2021–26 → 16th FC (Panagariya) 2026-31 deliberations.

## Trade & external
- Current account: goods + services + remittances; deficit financed by capital/financial account (FDI, FPI, ECB, NRI flows).
- Forex reserves: **$650–700 bn zone (2024–25 peak ~$704 bn)**; import cover ~11-12 months.
- Rupee managed-float; NEER/REER 40-currency basket; RBI intervention vocabulary: OMOS/FX swaps.
- Trade deficit ~$25-30 bn/mo recent band; services surplus owner.
