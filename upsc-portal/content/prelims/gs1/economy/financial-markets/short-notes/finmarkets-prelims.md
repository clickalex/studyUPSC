# Financial Markets & Inclusion — Prelims Drill

## Market regulator anatomy
- **SEBI** (1992 statutory; earlier 1988 non-stat): securities regulator; replaces separate Controller of Capital Issues.
- **IRDAI** (1999 → statutory by Act): insurance; pullback "Bima Trinity" 2025 era (Bima Sugam marketplace + Bima Vistaar composite + Bima Vaahak women-led distribution).
- **RBI** ( stat under RBI Act 1934; nationalised 1949): monetary authority + banking regulator + payment systems (PSS Act 2007) + Forex (FERA→FEMA 1999) + G-sec manager ( FRBNF story).
- **PFRDA** (pensions; NPS/APY regulator).
- **IFSCA** (2020; GIFT City unified regulator — banking+capital+insurance at IFSC).

## Market pipelining
- Exchanges: **NSE** (1992 set up; 1994 operations — demutualised electronic pioneer), **BSE** (oldest in Asia — 1875 as Native Share & Stock Brokers' Association), MCX (commodities), NCDEX (agri), MSE-I metropolitan revival attempts.
- Indices: Sensex-30 (1986 base 1978-79=100); Nifty-50 (1996 base 1995=1000); Bank Nifty; India VIX.
- Demat: CDSL (1999) + NSDL (1996) — depositories; demat accounts crossed 15+ crore band (2024-25).
- Investor-protection: SCORES portal; IPEF funds; ODR.
- Primary markets: book-building (IPOs); OFS preferential; QIP; rights issues; REIT/InVIT launches (2014 framework; Embassy REIT 2019 first listing case).

## Bond & debt
- G-sec (dated SLR stock), T-bills (91/182/364-day), SDL (state development loans), cash-management bills; retail-direct G-sec scheme (Nov 2021); **RBI floating-rate savings bonds (2020 scheme, NSC-linked reset)**.
- Corporate bond depth project: Tri-Party repo (TREPS), credit-enhancement funds (surety), LD-dealer framework reforms.
- **Green bonds**: sovereign green bond first issues Jan-2023 (₹16k crore in 2 tranches); framework 2022.
- India's inclusion **JP Morgan GBI-EM index** (2024 phased weight ~1% per month to 10% cap by Mar'25) + **Bloomberg EM local-currency inclusion (2025)** story (dates: announced 2023 start June 2024; verify) — foreign-flow step-function narrative.

## Inclusion & payments
- PMJDY (2014): >50 crore accounts by 2024-25; Rupay-debit stack; **zero-balance** design; OD facility.
- Payments stack: **UPI (2016) >1,400–1,500 crore txns/month (2025)**; **NACH, AePS, BBPS, FASTag, NETC, IMPS (24×7 since early), NEFT/RTGS 24×7**; Digital Rupee e-R (wholesale Nov-22 pilot, retail Dec-22 pilot) — CBDC story.
- ONDC (open commerce network pilot-scaling); **Account Aggregator** (NBFC-AA consent layer, DEPA stack).
- Insurance & pension micro: **PMJJBY** ₹2-lakh cover — premium ₹436/yr (2022 hike from ₹330; verify) · **PMSBY** accident cover ₹2 lakh — ₹20/yr · **APY** guaranteed ₹1–5k pension slabs at 60; **PMVVY** seniors pension (LIC channel mech).
- SHG stack: NRLM/NULM; Lakhpati Didi targets (crore-scale women SHGs).

## Financial-sector laws/history quick rows
- RBI Act 1934 · Banking Regulation Act 1949 · SBI 1955 · Nationalisations 1969/1980 · Narasimham I (1991) & II (1998) committees (PCA/NPA lines) · FEMA 1999 · SARFAESI 2002 · IBC 2016 · DICGC amendments 2021 (₹5 lakh cover quick payout ~90-day window) · FRDI bill withdrawn (bail-in anxiety 2018) → reliance on IBC+DICGC route.
