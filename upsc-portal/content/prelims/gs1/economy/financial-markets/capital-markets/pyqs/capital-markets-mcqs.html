Capital Markets & SEBI — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Which of the following instruments belong to the money market? 1. Treasury bills 2. Certificates of deposit 3. Corporate debentures 4. Commercial paper

  1. (a)1, 2 and 4
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2, 3 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)Debentures are capital-market (long-term) instruments.
Q2

Consider the following statements about SEBI: 1. It was established as a statutory body in 1988. 2. Appeals against its orders lie with the Securities Appellate Tribunal and then the Supreme Court. 3. It regulates commodity derivatives since the merger of the Forward Markets Commission.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)SEBI set up 1988, statutory only in 1992.
Q3

"T+1 settlement" in Indian stock exchanges means:

  1. (a)Trades are settled one hour after execution
  2. (b)Securities and funds change hands one working day after the trade date
  3. (c)Traders get one extra day to pay margins
  4. (d)Options expire one day after the index futures
Show answer
Correct: (b)India moved fully to T+1 in Jan 2023.
Q4

Which of the following are regulated primarily by SEBI? 1. Credit rating agencies 2. Mutual funds 3. Pension funds under NPS 4. Real Estate Investment Trusts

  1. (a)1, 2 and 4
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2, 3 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)NPS pension funds are under PFRDA.
Q5

Under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016: 1. The corporate insolvency resolution process must ordinarily be completed within 330 days including litigation. 2. A resolution plan needs approval by at least 66% of the committee of creditors by voting share. 3. Pre-packaged insolvency is available only to MSMEs.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q6

India's government bonds were added to the JP Morgan emerging-market bond index from June 2024. Which securities qualified for this inclusion?

  1. (a)All dated G-secs
  2. (b)Securities under the Fully Accessible Route
  3. (c)State development loans
  4. (d)Sovereign green bonds only
Show answer
Correct: (b)FAR securities carry no FPI limits.
Q7

Consider the following pairs: 1. MCX — agricultural commodity derivatives 2. NCDEX — bullion and energy derivatives 3. NSDL — first depository in India Which pairs are correctly matched?

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)3 only
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)MCX = metals/energy; NCDEX = agri.
Q8

The National Asset Reconstruction Company Ltd (bad bank) acquires stressed loans by paying banks:

  1. (a)Fully in cash
  2. (b)15% cash and 85% government-guaranteed security receipts
  3. (c)Equity shares of NARCL
  4. (d)50% cash and 50% bonds
Show answer
Correct: (b)Guarantee of ₹30,600 cr backs the SRs.
Q9

Which of the following was NOT a Union Budget 2024–25 change relating to capital markets?

  1. (a)Long-term capital gains on equity raised to 12.5%
  2. (b)Abolition of angel tax
  3. (c)Discontinuation of the Sovereign Gold Bond scheme
  4. (d)Short-term capital gains on equity raised to 20%
Show answer
Correct: (c)SGB discontinuation came in Budget 2025–26.
Q10

The "India VIX" index measures:

  1. (a)Foreign portfolio inflows into Indian equities
  2. (b)Expected near-term volatility derived from Nifty option prices
  3. (c)The premium of Indian bonds over US Treasuries
  4. (d)Retail participation in F&O
Show answer
Correct: (b)Volatility index modelled on CBOE VIX.
