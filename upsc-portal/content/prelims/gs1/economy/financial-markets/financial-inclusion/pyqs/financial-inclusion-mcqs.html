Financial Inclusion, Digital Payments & Insurance — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Consider the following statements about Payments Banks: 1. They cannot extend loans. 2. They can accept demand deposits up to ₹2 lakh per customer. 3. They can issue credit cards.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Payments banks may issue debit but not credit cards.
Q2

The Unified Payments Interface is operated by:

  1. (a)Reserve Bank of India directly
  2. (b)National Payments Corporation of India, a not-for-profit company owned by banks
  3. (c)Department of Financial Services
  4. (d)Indian Banks' Association
Show answer
Correct: (b)NPCI works under the PSS Act 2007.
Q3

Which of the following payment systems are owned and operated by the RBI itself? 1. RTGS 2. NEFT 3. IMPS 4. UPI

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)3 and 4
  3. (c)1, 2 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)IMPS and UPI are NPCI systems.
Q4

Under the Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, the maximum premium payable by farmers for kharif food crops is:

  1. (a)1.5% of sum insured
  2. (b)2% of sum insured
  3. (c)5% of sum insured
  4. (d)Nil — fully subsidised
Show answer
Correct: (b)Kharif 2%, rabi 1.5%, commercial/horticulture 5%.
Q5

Consider the following statements: 1. The Atal Pension Yojana is open to all citizens aged 18–40 including income-tax payers. 2. The Unified Pension Scheme assures 50% of the average basic pay of the last 12 months after 25 years of service. 3. The Social Security Code, 2020 brings gig and platform workers within social security coverage.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)Income-tax payers barred from APY since Oct 2022.
Q6

The "Digital Rupee" (e₹) differs from money in a UPI-linked bank account because:

  1. (a)It can only be used offline
  2. (b)It is a direct liability of the Reserve Bank of India rather than of a commercial bank
  3. (c)It earns a higher interest rate
  4. (d)It is issued by NPCI
Show answer
Correct: (b)CBDC is central bank money.
Q7

Which of the following was the first foreign country to license India's UPI technology stack to build its own instant payment system?

  1. (a)Singapore
  2. (b)France
  3. (c)Namibia
  4. (d)Sri Lanka
Show answer
Correct: (c)Namibia licensed the stack in 2024.
Q8

Under MUDRA, the "Tarun Plus" category introduced in 2024 covers loans:

  1. (a)Up to ₹50,000
  2. (b)Above ₹5 lakh up to ₹10 lakh
  3. (c)Above ₹10 lakh up to ₹20 lakh
  4. (d)Up to ₹1 crore
Show answer
Correct: (c)Announced in Budget 2024–25.
Q9

Consider the following pairs of schemes and their benefits: 1. PMJJBY — ₹2 lakh life cover for annual premium ₹436 2. PMSBY — ₹2 lakh accident cover for annual premium ₹20 3. PM-JAY — ₹5 lakh per family per year hospitalisation cover Which pairs are correctly matched?

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q10

The Reserve Bank's Financial Inclusion Index, launched in 2021, is:

  1. (a)A binary measure of whether a district has a bank branch
  2. (b)A composite of access, usage and quality sub-indices scored 0–100 without a base year
  3. (c)Computed only for rural areas
  4. (d)Published every month
Show answer
Correct: (b)Annual index; 67.0 for March 2025.
