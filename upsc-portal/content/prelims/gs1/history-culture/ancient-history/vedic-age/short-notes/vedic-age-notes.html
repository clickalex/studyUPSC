Vedic Age & Later Vedic Period — Prelims Fact Card

Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Ancient History. Text-classification and social-term questions dominate.

Sources & chronology

  • Early/Rig Vedic c. 1500–1000 BCE (Sapta Sindhu — Punjab, Afghanistan); Later Vedic c. 1000–600 BCE (Ganga-Yamuna doab; Kuru-Panchala); archaeology: Painted Grey Ware (PGW) = later Vedic; iron (shyama/krishna ayas) from ~1000 BCE (Atranjikhera).
  • Boghaz-Koi (Hittite–Mitanni) inscription 1400 BCE — Indra, Mitra, Varuna, Nasatya.

Rig Vedic society & polity

  • Tribal (jana), rajan not hereditary rigidly; assemblies sabha, samiti, vidatha, gana; purohita (Vasishtha, Vishvamitra), senani; bali voluntary; Dasarajna — Battle of Ten Kings (Sudas of Bharatas on the Parushni/Ravi).
  • Pastoral: cow = wealth (gavishti = war), gomat; barley (yava); horse chariot; rivers: Sindhu, Vitasta (Jhelum), Asikni (Chenab), Parushni (Ravi), Vipas (Beas), Shutudri (Sutlej), Sarasvati (most sacred), Drishadvati, Kubha (Kabul), Gomati (Gomal); Ganga once, Yamuna thrice.
  • Gods: Indra (250 hymns; Purandara), Agni (200), Varuna (rita), Soma, Surya, Savitri, Pushan, Ushas, Aditi (goddesses minor); no temples/idols.
  • Women: Apala, Ghosha, Lopamudra, Vishvavara — hymn composers; sabha attendance; no child marriage; widow remarriage/niyoga.
  • Varna: Purusha Sukta (10th mandala) — first mention of four varnas.

Later Vedic changes

  • Territorial kingdoms (rashtra; janapada), hereditary kingship, grand sacrifices rajasuya, vajapeya, ashvamedha; titles samrat, ekrat; ratnins (12 jewels); sabha-samiti decline, vidatha disappears; bali compulsory, bhaga, shulka.
  • Agriculture central (rice vrihi, wheat godhuma); varna rigid; gotra appears; four ashramas (Jabala Upanishad first); women's status falls (Aitareya — daughter a misery; but Gargi & Maitreyi in Brihadaranyaka); Shatapatha Brahmana — Videgha Mathava's eastward move; Prajapati, Vishnu, Rudra rise; Pushan becomes Shudra god.
  • Upanishads (108 traditional; 13 principal) — atman-brahman, karma, moksha; tat tvam asi (Chandogya), aham brahmasmi (Brihadaranyaka), Satyameva Jayate (Mundaka).

Traps

  • Gayatri is in the Rig (3rd mandala), addressed to Savitri; Nasadiya & Purusha Sukta in the 10th.
  • Atharva is outside the trayi; Shatapatha is a Yajur Brahmana; Chandogya is Sama.
  • Iron appears in the later Vedic age (PGW), not Rig Vedic (which had copper/bronze — ayas).
