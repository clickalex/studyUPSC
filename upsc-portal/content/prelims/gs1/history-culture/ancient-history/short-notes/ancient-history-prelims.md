# Ancient History — Prelims Revision

> Pairing-heavy card: sites↔findings, texts↔authors, rulers↔inscriptions. Complement `history-culture/art-culture` and mains `indian-heritage-culture`.

## Prehistory & IVC
- Palaeolithic → Mesolithic → Neolithic (Mehrgarh earliest agri-evidence ~7000 BCE); Chalcolithic.
- Bhimbetka (MP) rock shelters — UNESCO 2003.
- **IVC (Mature 2600–1900 BCE)**: Harappa (1921, Ravi; Daya Ram Sahni), Mohenjo-daro (1922, Indus; R D Banerji — Great Bath, Granary), Dholavira (Gujarat — water-harvesting, three-part city; UNESCO 2021); Lothal (dockyard), Kalibangan (ploughed field, fire altars), Chanhudaro (bead factory, no citadel), Banawali (barley hoard), Rakhigarhi (largest footprint claims), Sutkagendor (trading post), Surkotada (horse-bone debate).
- IVC craft: seals (humped bull iconic), steatite, bronze dancing girl (lost-wax), red sandstone torso (Harappa), script undeciphered (right-to-left ~375 signs).
- Decline theories: climate-river shifts (Saraswati/Ghaggar-Hakra drying), trade collapse — avoid Aryan-invasion framing (discredited).

## Vedic & Later Vedic
- Early Vedic (Rigvedic 1500–1000 BCE): Sapta-Sindhu, tribal pastoral, **Battle of Ten Kings (Dasarajna)** on Parushni (Ravi); Rigveda = oldest text; mandalas 2–7 core family books; Gayatri mantra = RV 3.62 (to Savitri).
- Later Vedic (1000–600 BCE): Brahmanas/Aranyakas/Upanishads; Painted GreyWare culture; Kuru-Panchala consolidation; satyakama/Varna crystallisation.
- 16 Mahajanapadas (Anguttara Nikaya list): capitals pairs (Magadha-Rajagriha; Kosala-Shravasti; Vatsa-Kaushambi; Avanti-Ujjain; Anga-Champa; Vajji-Vaishali republic; Kashi-Varanasi; Gandhara-Takshashila; Kamboja-Rajapura; Mall republics Kusinara/Pava).

## Buddhism & Jainism
- Buddha: **born Lumbini (563 BCE conventional), enlightenment Bodh Gaya (Mahabodhi), first sermon Sarnath (Dharmachakra Pravartana), mahaparinirvana Kushinagar**.
- Buddhist councils: **1st Rajgriha (Ajatashatru)**; **2nd Vaishali** (schism roots: Sthavira vs Mahasanghika); **3rd Pataliputra (Ashoka; Mogaliputta Tissa)**; **4th Kundalvana Kashmir (Kanishka; Sarvastivada codification: Vasumitra presided, Ashvaghosha)**.
- Right-bank texts: Tripitaka (Vinaya conduct; Sutta sermons; Abhidhamma doctrine) in Pali.
- Mahayana vs Hinayana/Theravada split; **Nalanda** (Kumaragupta I patronage; Hieun Tsang studied).
- Jainism: 24 tirthankaras — **Rishabhanatha (1st), Parshvanatha (23rd), Mahavira (24th, Vardhamana)**; councils: **1st Pataliputra (Sthulabhadra)**; **2nd Vallabhi (Devardhigani — final canon codification)**; schism → **Digambara vs Svetambara**; Chandragupta Maurya's Shravanabelagola migration (Bhadrabahu).

## Mauryas (322–185 BCE)
- Chandragupta (Chanakya link; deposed Dhana Nanda; **Seleucus treaty 305/303 BCE — 500 elephants, Megasthenes' *Indica***); Bindusara (Amitraghata; Antiochus contact); **Ashoka (Kalinga 261 BCE → dhamma; edicts found by James Prinsep decipherment 1837; Sarnath capital; saraient agent raj path)**.
- Ashokan edict anchors: Rock Edict XIII (Kalinga remorse); II & XIII-dhamma propagation; pillars (Sarnath, Vaishali single-lion, Lauriya Nandangarh); language Brahmi/Kharoshthi + Greek-Aramaic (Kandahar).
- Decline; **Pushyamitra Shunga (coup 185 BCE) → Shunga dynasty**.

## Post-Mauryan & Deccan
- Indo-Greeks (Menander/Milinda — *Milindapanho* dialogue with Nagasena; first gold coins in India lineage), Shakas (Rudradaman I — **Junagadh inscription 150 CE — first long Sanskrit inscription**; repaired Sudarshana lake), Kushanas (**Kanishka; Saka era start 78 CE**; Gandhara art), Satavahanas (**Gautamiputra Satakarni**; Nashik/Karle cave patronage), Chedis (**Kharavela — Hathigumpha inscription**), Sangam age (three Tamil kingdoms: **Chera (bow), Chola (tiger), Pandya (fish)**; Silappadikaram (Ilango Adigal), Manimekalai (Sattanar), Tirukkural (Thiruvalluvar)).
- Indianised SE-Asia missions; Amaravati stupa school.

## Guptas (320–550 CE)
- Chandragupta I (Gupta era 319–20); **Samudragupta (Prayag Prashasti by Harisena — "Napoleon of India" tag via V A Smith); Chandragupta II Vikramaditya (Chinese pilgrim Fa-Hien visits c. 399–414)**; Kumaragupta I (Nalanda); Skandagupta (repelled Hunas, Junagadh inscription sequel).
- Culture bloom: **Kalidasa (works under Chandragupta II's court lore): Abhijnana Shakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha**; Aryabhata (*Aryabhatiya* 499 CE), Varahamihira (*Brihat Samhita*), Sushruta/Charaka texts older roots.
- Art: Ajanta phases (Vakataka link — Harisena); Sarnath Buddha style; Dashavatara temple Deogarh.
- Huna invasions; post-Gupta fragmentation → **Harsha (606–647, Kannauj; Banabhatta's *Harshacharita* & Kadambari; Hieun Tsang 630s–644; Prayag assemblies)**.

## Coins shorthand
- Punch-marked (earliest, Mauryan mass issue); Indo-Greek first portrait gold coins; Kushana gold stardard (Vima Kadphises); Gupta dinaras (Samudragupta types: Ashvamedha, Archer); Satavahana lead/potin with bilingual legends.

## Prelims traps
- Hathigumpha = Kharavela (Chedi) — not Mauryan.
- Prayag Prashasti = Allahabad pillar = Samudragupta-Harisena.
- Saka era 78 CE = Kanishka; Gupta era 319–20 CE = Chandragupta I.
- Milindapanho = Menander; not Ashoka.
- Junagadh inscriptions: Rudradaman (150 CE, Sanskrit) vs Skandagupta (456 CE) vs Ashoka's rocks — three landlords on one boulder; sequence matters.
