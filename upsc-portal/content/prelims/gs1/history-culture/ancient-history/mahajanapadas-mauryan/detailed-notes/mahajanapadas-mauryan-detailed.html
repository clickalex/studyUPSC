Mahajanapadas and Mauryas — Detailed Notes

The making of the first empire: 16 states → Magadha → Nandas → Maurya → Ashoka's dhamma. UPSC loves ruler→measure and edict→place pairs.

1. The 16 Mahajanapadas (6th c. BCE)

  • Source: Anguttara Nikaya (Buddhist) + Bhagavati Sutra (Jain list varies). Monarchies (Magadha, Kosala, Kashi, Vatsa...) vs ganasanghas/republics (Vajji — Licchavis of Vaishali, Malla, Shakyas of Kapilavastu).
  • Why Magadha wins: iron ore (Jharkhand belts), fertile Ganga valley, elephants, riverine trade, ambitious rulers, capitals Rajagriha → Pataliputra (Udayin — confluence point).

3. Alexander (326 BCE)

  • Crossed Indus 326; Hydaspes (Jhelum) battle vs Porus (Puru) — impressed, restored him; army mutinied at Beas (Hyphasis); retreat via Makran; died 323 Babylon. Effects: trade routes, Greek colonies, Seleucus successor.

4. Mauryan Empire (c. 322–185 BCE)

  • Chandragupta (with Kautilya — Arthashastra): 305 Seleucus pact — marriage alliance, Arachosia/Kandahar ceded, Megasthenes ambassador (Indica). Jain tradition: became Bhadrabahu follower, died at Shravanabelagola.
  • Bindusara: 'Amitraghata'; Deccan up to Mysore (except Kalinga); Ajivika leanings.
  • Ashoka (268–232): Kalinga 261 (13th Rock Edict — remorse, dhamma-vijaya instead of bheri-ghosha); officials: dhamma mahamattas (14th year); tours; edicts in Prakrit (Brahmi script — most; Kharoshthi NW: Shahbazgarhi, Mansehra; bilingual Greek-Aramaic: Kandahar); 14 Major Rock Edicts (Kalsi, Girnar, Dhauli, Jaugada...), 7 Pillar Edicts (Delhi-Topra, Delhi-Meerut, Lauriya-Araraj...); Sarnath lion capital = national emblem; chakra = flag wheel.
  • Administration: saptanga (7 limbs), 18 tirthas (mantrin top), provinces at Ujjain, Taxila, Tosali, Suvarnagiri (+Pataliputra); army per Megasthenes (6,00,000 infantry, 30,000 cavalry, 9,000 elephants); revenue: bhaga = 1/6, sita lands, punch-marked silver panas (karshapana family); espionage (gudhapurusha), urban officials (nagaraka, 30-member? committees per Arthashastra).
  • Art: monolithic polished pillars, stupas (Sanchi core), Barabar caves (Lomas Rishi — first), Yaksha-Yakshini sculpture (Didarganj).
  • Decline: 185 Pushyamitra (Brahmanical reaction + economic-fiscal + succession readings).

MCQ hooks

  • 2nd Buddhist Council = Vaishali, Kalashoka's time (1st = Rajgriha, Ajatashatru).
  • Kharoshthi edicts = only Shahbazgarhi & Mansehra; rest Brahmi.
  • Bilingual (Greek-Aramaic) = Kandahar only.
  • Tosali = eastern province (Kalinga); Suvarnagiri = southern.
  • 'Devanam Piya' = beloved of gods (Ashoka's title; named only in Maski, Gujarra, Nettur, Udegolam — Minor REs).
  • Megasthenes = Indica (fragments via Strabo/Arrian); Deimachos (Bindusara, Antiochus I), Dionysius (Ashoka, sent to Ptolemy II of Egypt).
