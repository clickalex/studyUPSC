Prehistory: Stone Age & Indus Valley Civilisation — Prelims Fact Card

Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Ancient History. Site–feature matching (especially Harappan) recurs almost every year.

Stone Age ladder

  • Palaeolithic (up to ~10,000 BCE): Lower — Soan valley (pebble tools), Bhimbetka, Attirampakkam, Hunsgi; Middle — Nevasa, Bhimbetka (flakes); Upper — Bhimbetka paintings, Patne, bone tools at Kurnool caves; Homo erectus Hathnora (Narmada) skull.
  • Mesolithic (~10,000–6,000 BCE): microliths; Bagor (Rajasthan; earliest domestication evidence claim), Adamgarh (MP), Langhnaj (Gujarat), Sarai Nahar Rai & Mahadaha (UP; burials), Bhimbetka rock art.
  • Neolithic: Mehrgarh (Balochistan; c. 7000 BCE; wheat-barley, cattle; pre-Harappan), Burzahom (Kashmir; pit dwellings, dog burials), Gufkral, Chirand (Bihar; bone tools), Koldihwa (rice claim ~6000 BCE), Hallur, Maski, Piklihal, Sanganakallu, Utnur (ash mounds — South), Daojali Hading (Assam).
  • Chalcolithic cultures: Ahar-Banas (Rajasthan; copper, Gilund), Kayatha, Malwa (Navdatoli), Jorwe (Inamgaon, Daimabad — largest; bronze rhinoceros/chariot hoard), Savalda, OCP/Copper Hoards (Ganga-Yamuna; anthropomorphs), Ganeshwar (copper source for Harappans?).

Harappan/Indus (c. 2600–1900 BCE mature)

  • Phases: Early (3300–2600; Kot Diji, Amri, Sothi), Mature (2600–1900), Late (1900–1300; Cemetery H, Jhukar, Rangpur, Lustrous Red Ware).
  • Discovery: Harappa (Daya Ram Sahni 1921), Mohenjo-daro (R.D. Banerji 1922); John Marshall announced 1924; largest sites: Mohenjo-daro, Harappa, Ganweriwala, Rakhigarhi (largest in India), Dholavira.

  • Features: grid planning, baked bricks 1:2:4, drainage, standard weights (binary/decimal; 16 base), seals (steatite; unicorn commonest), undeciphered script — boustrophedon, no temples/palaces unambiguously, cotton earliest, copper-bronze (no iron), wheel-made pottery (red-black), trade with Mesopotamia (Meluhha; Dilmun = Bahrain, Magan = Oman), Mother goddess, Pashupati, peepal, bull worship, no horse (contested).
  • Decline theories: Aryan invasion (Wheeler; discredited), floods (Marshall, Dales), tectonic/river shift (Raikes; Ghaggar-Hakra drying), ecological (Fairservis), climate change/monsoon weakening.

Traps

  • Ploughed field = Kalibangan; dockyard = Lothal; Great Bath = Mohenjo-daro; citadel-less = Chanhudaro.
  • Harappans knew bronze, not iron; script is undeciphered; Rakhigarhi (not Dholavira) is the largest in India by area.
