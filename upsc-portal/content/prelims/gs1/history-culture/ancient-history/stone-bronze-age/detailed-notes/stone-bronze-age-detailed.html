Stone, Bronze & Iron Ages — Detailed Notes

MCQ core: match culture → tool type → site → region. Names and pairings below are the tested facts.

1. Palaeolithic (Old Stone Age) — c. 2.6 mya to c. 12,000 BCE

  • Lower: handaxes, cleavers, choppers. Sites: Attirampakkam (Tamil Nadu — Acheulian dates pushed back to ~1.5 million years, among the oldest in India), Sohan valley (Pakistan), Bhimbetka (MP), Hunsgi (Karnataka).
  • Middle: flakes, scrapers, borers (Nevasa, Belan valley).
  • Upper: blades, burins, bone tools begin (Bhimbetka paintings begin late phase — hunting, dancing scenes).

2. Mesolithic (c. 10,000–6,000 BCE)

  • Tiny microliths (composite tools); first rock paintings peak (Bhimbetka).
  • Early domestication evidence: Bagor (Rajasthan), Adamgarh (MP) — sheep/goat; Langhnaj (Gujarat).

4. Chalcolithic (c. 2800–700 BCE) — copper + stone

  • Cultures: Ahar-Banas (Rajasthan — white-painted black-and-red), Kayatha & Malwa (MP), Jorwe (Maharashtra — Daimabad 'bronze' finds, Inamgaon), Svalda; eastern: Chirand, Pandu Rajar Dhibi.
  • Traits: painted pottery (black-on-red), tiny villages, no bronze tools in bulk — copper scarce; continued stone use.

5. Iron Age & Megalithic (c. 1500–500 BCE south)

  • Megalith burials: South India (Tamil Nadu–Kerala–AP) + Vidarbha (Maharashtra); types: dolmens, cists, menhirs; iron in graves (black-and-red ware).
  • Significance: second urbanisation groundwork — iron plough → surplus → janapadas.

MCQ hooks

  • Mehrgarh = pre-Harappan farming (NOT Harappan town).
  • Burzahom = pit-dwelling (not megalith).
  • Microlith = Mesolithic (not Palaeolithic).
  • Chalcolithic = 'copper-stone'; first to paint pottery in India's villages.
  • Bhimbetka = continuous Palaeolithic→historical rock art (UNESCO).
  • Attirampakkam = Acheulian; Levallois finds (~3.85 lakh BP) rewrote Indian Middle Palaeolithic dates.
