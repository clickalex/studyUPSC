# Post-Mauryan Age & Gupta Empire (c. 185 BCE–550 CE) — Prelims Fact Card

> Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Ancient History. Dynasty–ruler–inscription/coin–art school matching, plus Gupta "golden age" facts.

## Post-Mauryan dynasties
- **Shungas** (185–73 BCE; Pushyamitra — ashvamedha; Patanjali *Mahabhashya*; **Bharhut** stupa railings, Sanchi gateways began; Heliodorus pillar Besnagar 113 BCE — Garuda pillar, Bhagavata; Agnimitra — Kalidasa's *Malavikagnimitram*) → Kanvas (Vasudeva 73 BCE).
- **Satavahanas** (Deccan; Simuka; Pratishthana/Paithan & Amaravati; **Gautamiputra Satakarni** — Nasik prashasti by Gautami Balashri; destroyed Shakas (Nahapana coins restruck); Vasishthiputra Pulumavi; Yajna Sri Satakarni — ship coins; **lead coins**; Prakrit; matronymics; land grants to Brahmanas (earliest — Naneghat, Naganika); Karle, Bhaja, Kanheri chaitya; Amaravati school, Nagarjunakonda; *Gathasaptasati* (Hala); *Brihatkatha* (Gunadhya, Paishachi)).
- **Indo-Greeks** (Demetrius, **Menander/Milinda** — Sakala/Sialkot; Nagasena; first gold coins in India; portrait coins; Gandhara art influence; Heliodorus).
- **Shakas** (Maues; Western Kshatrapas — **Rudradaman I** — **Junagadh inscription 150 CE**, first long Sanskrit inscription; Sudarshana lake repair (built by Pushyagupta under Chandragupta Maurya, conduits by Tushaspa under Ashoka); Vikram Samvat 58 BCE (Vikramaditya of Ujjain legend); Shaka Samvat 78 CE = **Kanishka**).
- **Parthians/Pahlavas** (Gondophernes; St Thomas tradition 1st c. CE).
- **Kushanas** (Yuezhi; Kujula Kadphises; Vima Kadphises — gold coins, Shiva; **Kanishka 78 CE** — Purushapura/Peshawar; Mathura second capital; fourth Buddhist council; Ashvaghosha, Vasumitra, Charaka (*Charaka Samhita*), Nagarjuna; Mathura & Gandhara schools; headless statue at Mat; Rabatak inscription; Silk Road; Roman trade — *Periplus of the Erythraean Sea*, Arikamedu; largest number of copper coins).
- **Kharavela** of Kalinga (Chedi/Mahameghavahana; **Hathigumpha inscription**, Udayagiri; Jain; 1st c. BCE).
- Sangam South: Cheras (Vanji; Senguttuvan — Pattini cult), Cholas (Uraiyur/Puhar; **Karikala** — Kallanai/Grand Anicut, Venni), Pandyas (Madurai; Sangam patrons; Nedunjeliyan); *Tolkappiyam*, *Ettutogai*, *Pattuppattu*, *Silappadikaram* (Ilango Adigal), *Manimekalai* (Sattanar); *tinai* landscapes; *Muvendar*; *vellalar*; Yavana trade at Muziris.

## Guptas (c. 320–550 CE)
- Sri Gupta → Ghatotkacha → **Chandragupta I** (320 CE; *Maharajadhiraja*; Licchavi marriage — Kumaradevi coins) → **Samudragupta** (c. 335–75; **Allahabad/Prayag prashasti** by Harishena on Ashokan pillar — 12 Dakshinapatha kings defeated & restored, 9 Aryavarta uprooted; "Napoleon of India" (V.A. Smith); *kaviraja*, veena coins; ashvamedha; Meghavarman of Sri Lanka — Bodh Gaya monastery) → **Chandragupta II Vikramaditya** (c. 375–415; Shaka conquest — Gujarat/Malwa (Ujjain second capital); **Mehrauli iron pillar** ("Chandra"); Fa-hien; Navratnas — Kalidasa, Amarasimha, Varahamihira(?), Dhanvantari; Udayagiri caves (Vidisha) — Varaha) → **Kumaragupta I** (Nalanda founded; Huna threat; Mahendraditya) → **Skandagupta** (Huna repulse; Junagadh inscription — Sudarshana repaired again; Bhitari pillar) → decline (Hunas — Toramana, Mihirakula; feudatories; Yashodharman — Mandasor 532).
- Administration: *bhukti* (uparika) → *vishaya* (vishayapati; *adhikarana* board — nagarasreshthi, sarthavaha, prathama-kulika, prathama-kayastha) → *vithi* → *grama*; **land grants (agrahara, brahmadeya) → feudalism debate**; taxes *udranga, uparikara, bhaga, bhoga, vishti* (forced labour); *dinara* (gold), *rupaka* (silver), fewer copper coins; guilds (*shreni*; Mandasor silk-weavers); *Kumaramatyas*, *sandhivigrahika*.
- Culture: Sanskrit peak — Kalidasa (*Abhijnanashakuntalam, Meghaduta, Raghuvamsha, Kumarasambhava, Ritusamhara, Malavikagnimitram, Vikramorvashiyam*), Shudraka *Mrichchhakatika*, Vishakhadatta *Mudrarakshasa & Devichandraguptam*, Amarasimha *Amarakosha*, Vishnu Sharma *Panchatantra*; Puranas finalised; **Aryabhata** (*Aryabhatiya* 499; zero-place value, π, rotation, eclipses), Varahamihira (*Brihat Samhita, Pancha-siddhantika*), Brahmagupta (628 — later), Vagbhata; Nalanda; Dashavatara temple Deogarh, Bhitargaon brick temple, Sanchi Temple 17, Ajanta caves 16, 17, 19 (Vakataka Harishena), Bagh caves, Sarnath Buddha (sandstone), Sultanganj copper Buddha; Vishnu-Bhagavata cult; iron pillar rustless; *Nagara* style origins; *shikhara* appears.
- Fa-hien (399–414): peaceful, Chandalas outside towns, no capital punishment, Pataliputra hospital.

## Traps
- Junagadh inscription = **Rudradaman** (Sanskrit, 150 CE) *and* also Skandagupta's record — same rock, both mention Sudarshana lake.
- Allahabad prashasti = **Harishena** (Samudragupta), on Ashoka's pillar; Mehrauli pillar = Chandragupta II.
- Nalanda was founded by **Kumaragupta I**, patronised later by Harsha and the Palas.
- Shaka era 78 CE = Kanishka's accession (used in India's national calendar).
