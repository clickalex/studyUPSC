Ancient History — Prelims PYQs

Q1. The Prayag Prashasti (Allahabad Pillar inscription) eulogises: (a) Chandragupta I (b) Samudragupta (c) Ashoka (d) Harsha

Model answer: (b). Composed by court poet Harisena in Sanskrit — the classic match question.

Q2. Milindapanho records a dialogue between: (a) Ashoka & Upagupta (b) Menander & Nagasena (c) Kanishka & Vasumitra (d) Harsha & Hieun Tsang

Model answer: (b). Indo-Greek king Menander (Milinda) with monk Nagasena.

Q3. [PYQ-pattern] Which of the following are associated with jainism?

  1. Rishabhanatha — 1st tirthankara 2. Parshvanatha — 22nd

(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both (d) Neither

Model answer: (a). Parshvanatha was the 23rd tirthankara; Mahavira 24th. Classic "off-by-one" trap.

Q4. The Junagadh rock carries inscriptions of: (a) Ashoka only (b) Rudradaman I & Skandagupta only (c) Ashoka, Rudradaman I & Skandagupta (d) Kanishka & Samudragupta

Model answer: (c). All three on the same boulder — 2nd c. BCE Ashokan edicts, Rudradaman's 150 CE Sanskrit prashasti (Sudarshana lake), Skandagupta's 456 CE record.

Q5. The Saka era starting ~78 CE is conventionally tied to: (a) Chandragupta Maurya (b) Kanishka (c) Chandragupta I of Guptas (d) Vikramaditya VI of Karnataka

Model answer: (b). Don't confuse with Gupta era 319-20 CE (Chandragupta I) or Vikrama era 57 BCE.

Q6. Silappadikaram and Manimekalai are: (a) Sanskrit court epics under Guptas (b) Prakrit Jaina cantos from Karnataka (c) Tamil Sangam epics — llango Adigal and Sattanar respectively (d) Buddhist plays in Pali

Model answer: (c). Silappadikaram's author ilango Adigal (Jain); Manimekalai authored by Sattanar (Buddhist).

Q7. Ajanta paintings predominantly depict: (a) Jain narrative cycles (b) Buddhist Jataka tales & bodhisattva imagery (c) Rajput court scenes (d) Bhakti saints

Model answer: (b). Ajanta = Buddhist cave monastery complex (2nd c. BCE–6th c. CE phases); Ellora = Buddhist-Hindu-Jain mix.

Q8. Which doesn't belong to the fourth Buddhist council (Kundalvana, Kashmir) narrative? (a) Kanishka patronage (b) Vasumitra presiding; Ashvaghosha associated (c) Sarvastivada canon codified (d) Mogaliputta Tissa presided

Model answer: (d). Mogaliputta Tissa presided over the third council (Pataliputra, Ashoka).

Q9. Punch-marked coins in ancient India are most associated with: (a) Indo-Greeks (b) Mauryan empire (c) Kushanas (d) Guptas

Model answer: (b). Indo-Greeks pioneered die-struck portrait coins; Kushanas spread gold; Guptas' dinaras typify gold standards later.

Q10. Hieun Tsang's Indian journey overlaps with: (a) Chandragupta II (b) Harshavardhana (c) Kumaragupta I (d) Skandagupta

Model answer: (b). Fa-Hien belonged to Chandragupta II's era (c. 399–414); Hieun Tsang to Harsha (630s–644) — the staple FK-pairing confusion trap.
