## Legislation ladder
- Sati Regulation XVII **1829** · Widow Remarriage Act **1856** (Vidyasagar; Act XV) · Native Marriage (Civil/Brahmo) Act **1872** (min 14/18; Keshab) · Age of Consent Act **1891** (12 yrs; Malabari; Tilak opposed) · Sarda/Child Marriage Restraint Act **1929** (14/18; Harbilas Sarda) · Caste Disabilities Removal Act 1850.
- Female infanticide: Bengal Regulation XXI 1795, Act VIII 1870.

## Traps
- Rammohan's title "Raja" was given by Mughal Akbar II; he died at Bristol 1833.
- Arya Samaj's founding city is **Bombay** (1875), not Lahore.
- Prarthana Samaj was founded by *Atmaram Pandurang*; Ranade *joined* it.
- Vivekananda founded the Mission in 1897, the Belur Math also in 1897–98; Ramakrishna died 1886.
