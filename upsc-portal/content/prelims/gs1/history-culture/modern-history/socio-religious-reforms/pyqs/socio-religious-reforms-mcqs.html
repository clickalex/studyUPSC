Socio-Religious Reforms — Practice MCQs

8 questions

8 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Match: A. Satyashodhak Samaj — 1. Dadoba Pandurang · B. Paramahansa Mandali — 2. Jyotiba Phule · C. Prarthana Samaj — 3. Atmaram Pandurang

  1. (a)A-2 B-1 C-3
  2. (b)A-1 B-2 C-3
  3. (c)A-2 B-3 C-1
  4. (d)A-3 B-1 C-2
Show answer
Correct: (a)Phule 1873; Dadoba 1849; Atmaram 1867.
Q2

The Sadharan Brahmo Samaj (1878) was formed after a split caused by:

  1. (a)Debendranath's rejection of Vedic infallibility
  2. (b)Keshab Chandra Sen marrying his minor daughter to the Cooch Behar prince
  3. (c)Rammohan Roy's death
  4. (d)The Age of Consent Act
Show answer
Correct: (b)The 1878 Cooch Behar marriage violated the 1872 Act Keshab had championed.
Q3

Which Act fixed the minimum age of marriage at 14 for girls and 18 for boys?

  1. (a)Native Marriage Act 1872
  2. (b)Age of Consent Act 1891
  3. (c)Sarda Act 1929
  4. (d)Special Marriage Act 1954
Show answer
Correct: (c)Sarda Act; 1891 fixed 12 for consummation; 1872 Act was 14/18 only for Brahmo civil marriages.
Q4

Consider: 1. Arya Samaj was founded at Lahore in 1875. 2. The DAV movement and the Gurukul Kangri represented rival wings within Arya Samaj.

  1. (a)1 only
  2. (b)2 only
  3. (c)Both
  4. (d)Neither
Show answer
Correct: (b)Founded at Bombay 1875, HQ shifted to Lahore 1877; DAV (Hansraj) vs Gurukul (Shraddhanand).
Q5

Who founded the Deoband school (Dar-ul-Ulum) in 1866?

  1. (a)Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
  2. (b)Muhammad Qasim Nanautavi and Rashid Ahmad Gangohi
  3. (c)Maulana Abul Kalam Azad
  4. (d)Shibli Numani
Show answer
Correct: (b)Deoband later backed Congress via Jamiat-ul-Ulama-i-Hind.
Q6

The Vaikom Satyagraha (1924–25) demanded:

  1. (a)Abolition of the devadasi system
  2. (b)Entry of avarnas on roads around a temple
  3. (c)Right to temple entry in Travancore temples
  4. (d)Abolition of child marriage
Show answer
Correct: (b)Road access around Vaikom temple; temple entry proclamation came in 1936.
Q7

Behramji Malabari is associated with:

  1. (a)Age of Consent Act, 1891
  2. (b)Widow Remarriage Act, 1856
  3. (c)Sati Regulation, 1829
  4. (d)Child Marriage Restraint Act, 1929
Show answer
Correct: (a)Malabari's campaign after the Rukhmabai/Phulmoni cases.
Q8

Which reformer's slogan was "One caste, one religion, one God for mankind"?

  1. (a)E.V. Ramaswamy
  2. (b)Ayyankali
  3. (c)Sree Narayana Guru
  4. (d)Vaikunda Swami
Show answer
Correct: (c)SNDP Yogam 1903; Aruvippuram consecration 1888.
