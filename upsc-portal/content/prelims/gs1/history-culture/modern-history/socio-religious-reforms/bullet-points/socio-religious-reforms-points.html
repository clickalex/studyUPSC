Socio-Religious Reforms — Bullet Points

Reformer → org → year drills.

Pairs flash

  • Brahmo Sabha 1828 → Rammohan · Tattvabodhini 1839 → Debendranath · 1866 split → Keshab Sen · Young Bengal → Derozio.
  • Sati 1829 → Bentinck + Rammohan · Widow Act XV 1856 → Vidyasagar · Age of Consent 1891 → Phulmoni-Rukhmabai cases.
  • Prarthana Samaj 1867 → Atmaram Pandurang · Ranade → IN Conference 1883 + widow push · Servants of India 1905 → Gokhale.
  • Arya Samaj 1875 → Dayananda (Satyarth Prakash, Shuddhi, DAV 1886) · Theosophy Adyar 1882 → Olcott-Besant · BHU seed → Besant's Central Hindu College.
  • Chicago 1893 + RK Mission 1897 → Vivekananda · 'Daridra Narayana' service creed.
  • Satyashodhak 1873 + Gulamgiri → Jyotirao Phule · girls' school 1848 → Savitribai.
  • Aruvippuram 1888 + SNDP 1903 → Narayana Guru · Self-Respect 1925 → Periyar · Villuvandi yatra → Ayyankali · Mahad 1927 → Ambedkar.
  • MAO 1875 + Tahzeeb-ul-Akhlaq → Sir Syed · Deoband 1866 → Nanautavi · Singh Sabha 1873 Amritsar · Rahnumai Mazdayasnan 1851 → Parsi reform.
  • Sarda Act 1929 → 14/18 marriage ages · Special Marriage 1872 → Brahmo lineage · Devadasi ban → Madras 1947.
  • Newspapers: Sambad Kaumudi → Rammohan · Kesari (already used) · Tahzeeb → Sir Syed · Mooknayak → Ambedkar (1920).

Trap flash

  • Arya Samaj founded 1875 Bombay (Lahore shift later) — year same as Theosophy's NY origin.
  • RK Mission = 1897 (not 1893 — that's Chicago address).
  • Periyar: Vaikom (1924-25) hero BEFORE Self-Respect launch 1925.
  • Widow Remarriage Act 1856 — first legal widow remarriage (not Sati which is 1829).
  • Deoband ≠ Aligarh: opposite responses (traditionalist vs modernist).
