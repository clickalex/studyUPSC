Modern History — Prelims PYQs

Pairs with short-notes/socio-religious-reform-quick.md in this folder. Pattern-faithful MCQs.

Q1. The correct chronological order is: (a) Regulating Act → Charter Act 1833 → Pitts India Act → Act of 1858 (b) Pitts India Act → Regulating Act → Charter Act 1833 → Act of 1858 (c) Regulating Act → Pitts India Act → Charter Act 1833 → Act of 1858 (d) Charter Act 1833 → Regulating Act → Pitts India Act → Act of 1858

Model answer: (c). 1773 Regulating → 1784 Pitt's → 1833 Charter → 1858 Crown takeover.

Q2. Permanent Settlement (1793) was implemented by: (a) Cornwallis (b) Wellesley (c) Hastings (d) Bentinck

Model answer: (a). Bengal-Bihar-Orissa zamindari freeze; contrast with Ryotwari (Munro/Read) & Mahalwari.

Q3. [PYQ-pattern] The Ilbert Bill controversy (1883) concerned: (a) European judges over Indian subjects (b) Indian judges over European subjects (c) Press censorship (d) Vernacular language policy

Model answer: (b). Ripon's measure to let Indian judges try Europeans; Europeans resisted, frayed compromise.

Q4. Which was not a Swadeshi-era institution? (a) Bengal National College (1906, Aurobindo principal) (b) Tata Iron & Steel Co. (1907) (c) Forest Research Institute Dehradun (1906) (d) None — all are associated with the period

Model answer: (d). All three grew up amid the swadeshi/service-institution push.

Q5. Which session adopted Poorna Swaraj? (a) Calcutta 1920 (b) Lahore 1929 (c) Karachi 1931 (d) Faizpur 1936

Model answer: (b). Lahore 1929 (Nehru president); Jan 26 1930 first Independence Day; Karachi 1931 adopted Fundamental Rights/Economic Policy resolution (Sardar Patel president).

Q6. The Macdonald Award (1932) yielded Poona Pact between: (a) Gandhi and Irwin (b) Gandhi and Ambedkar (c) Nehru and Jinnah (d) Patel and Rajendra Prasad

Model answer: (b). Communal-award→Poona Pact retained joint electorates with reserved seats for depressed classes (147 seats legacy figure).

Q7. [PYQ] Who founded the Forward Bloc (1939)? (a) Jawaharlal Nehru (b) Subhas Chandra Bose (c) C Rajagopalachari (d) Jayaprakash Narayan

Model answer: (b). Post-Tripuri resignation from Congress president post.

Q8. Simon Commission (1928) faced boycott because: (a) It recommended partition (b) It had no Indian member (c) It sought dissolution of princely states (d) It opposed Dominion status demands

Model answer: (b). "All-white" commission — the protest trigger; Lala Lajpat Rai died after Lahore lathi-charge.
