East India Company and Expansion — Detailed Notes

Traders → rulers (1600–1858). Anchors: battles, Governor-Generals + reforms, land systems, subsidiary diplomacy.

1. Entry & early trade

  • EIC chartered 1600 (Elizabeth I); first factory Machilipatnam 1611; Surat factory after the Battle of Swally 1612 (vs Portuguese); Jahangir farman via Thomas Roe 1615-18; 1717 Farrukhsiyar farman = 'magna carta' duty-free Bengal trade (dastaks abused); Bombay ceded 1668 (Catherine dowry → Company lease).
  • Rivals: Portuguese (decline post-Swally), Dutch (Batavia focus), Danes, French Compagnie 1664; Dupleix — Carnatic wars 1746-63 (3 wars): Madras 1746, Trichinopoly-Severndroog arc, Wandiwash 1760 (Eyre Coote vs Lally) → Pondicherry ceded militarily-ended.

2. Conquest arc

  • Plassey 1757 (Clive; Siraj-ud-Daula; Mir Jafar conspiracy; Jagat Seth bankers; Omichund bribe plot), Buxar 1764 (Munro; Mir Qasim + Shuja-ud-Daula + Shah Alam II), Diwani 1765 (Treaty of Allahabad) → dual government (Clive) → Warren Hastings ends it 1772; Regulating Act 1773 + Pitt's India Act 1784 (Board of Control dual frame).
  • Mysore: 4 wars (Haidar Ali; Tipu 'Tiger'; Seringapatam 1799 death); Marathas: 3 wars (1775-82 Salbai; 1803-05 Assaye; 1817-18 final); Sikh wars 1845-46 (Sobraon), 1848-49 (Chilianwala, Multan, Gujarat; Dalhousie annexes Punjab 1849 — Koh-i-noor to Queen); Burma wars 1824-26, 1852, 1885 (later); Nepal 1814-16 (Sugauli); Sind 1843 (Napier 'Peccavi' legend).
  • Diplomatic frame: subsidiary alliance (Wellesley 1798+) — Hyderabad, Awadh, Tanjore, Peshwa (Bassein 1802); Doctrine of Lapse (Dalhousie 1848-56) — Satara 1848, Jaitpur-Sambalpur, Jhansi 1853, Nagpur 1854; Awadh 'misgovernment' annexation 1856.

4. Land revenue systems

  • Permanent Settlement 1793 (Cornwallis-John Shore; Bengal-Bihar-Odisha; zamindari; sunset law) — fixed demand, de-facto property; Ryotwari (Munro-Read; Madras-Bombay) — direct ryot assessment; Mahalwari (1822+; Holt Mackenzie, amended 1833 Bird; North-West, Punjab parts, Awadh later taluqdari) — village-mahal joint.

MCQ hooks

  • Order: Plassey 1757 → Buxar 1764 → Diwani 1765 (triple asked).
  • Wandiwash 1760 = French Indian defeat (Carnatic war III).
  • Swally 1612 = naval vs Portuguese → Surat factory.
  • Seringapatam 1799 = Tipu falls (war IV, Wellesley era).
  • PS 1793 (Cornwallis) vs Ryotwari (Munro) vs Mahalwari (Mackenzie-Bird) trio.
  • Farrukhsiyar farman 1717 = dastak duty-free abuse → Bengal friction.
  • Sati 1829 (Bentinck-Rammohan), Lapse = Dalhousie (Jhansi 1853, Nagpur 1854, Awadh 1856).
