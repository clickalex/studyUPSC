EIC & Expansion — Bullet Points

Match-drills.

Pairs flash

  • 1600 charter → Elizabeth I · Machilipatnam 1611 first factory · Swally 1612 → Surat · Roe → Jahangir farman.
  • 1717 farman → Farrukhsiyar (dastak abuse) · Bombay 1668 → Catherine dowry lease.
  • Carnatic wars 1746-63 · Wandiwash 1760 → Eyre Coote vs Lally (Dupleix era).
  • Plassey 1757 → Clive-Siraj-Mir Jafar · Buxar 1764 → Munro vs triumvirate · Diwani 1765 → Allahabad treaty.
  • Regulating Act 1773 → Warren Hastings · Pitt's Act 1784 → Board of Control · impeachment → Burke, acquitted 1795.
  • Mysore 4 wars → Haidar-Tipu · Seringapatam 1799 · Subsidiary alliance → Wellesley (Hyderabad, Bassein 1802).
  • Lapse → Dalhousie: Satara 1848, Jhansi 1853, Nagpur 1854 · Awadh 1856 'misrule'.
  • Punjab 1849 → Dalhousie (2 Sikh wars; Chilianwala-Gujarat) · Sind 1843 → Napier · Sugauli 1816 → Nepal.
  • Sati 1829 → Bentinck (+Rammohan) · English 1835 → Macaulay minute · Asiatic Society 1784 → William Jones (Hastings era).
  • 1853-57 infra: rail Bombay-Thana 1853, telegraph 1851, post 1854 half-anna, 3 universities 1857 → Dalhousie cluster.

Trap flash

  • Dual government = Clive (1765-72), ended by Warren Hastings — don't swap.
  • Permanent Settlement = zamindari (Bengal); Ryotwari = direct peasant (Madras-Bombay); Mahalwari = village joint (NW).
  • Carnatic wars = Anglo-French; Mysore wars = Anglo-Mysore (4, not 3).
  • Bassein 1802 = Peshwa treaty (war II trigger) — not Goa Bassein confusion.
  • Koh-i-noor → taken 1849 Punjab annexation (Dalhousie), displayed 1851.
