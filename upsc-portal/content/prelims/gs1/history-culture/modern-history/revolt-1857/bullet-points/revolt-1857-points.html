Revolt of 1857 — Bullet Points

Match-drills.

Centres flash

  • Delhi → Bahadur Shah II + Bakht Khan · Kanpur → Nana Saheb + Tantia Tope · Lucknow → Begum Hazrat Mahal · Jhansi → Rani Lakshmibai · Bareilly → Khan Bahadur Khan · Jagdishpur → Kunwar Singh · Faizabad → Maulvi Ahmadullah Shah.
  • Spark → Barrackpore, Mangal Pandey, 34th NI (29 Mar 1857) · start → Meerut 10 May 1857 · Delhi fell Sept 1857 (Nicholson).

Cause flash

  • Cartridge → Enfield grease rumour · Enlistment Act 1856 → overseas clause · Religious Disabilities Act 1850 → convert inheritance.
  • Lapse victims → Satara, Jaitpur, Sambalpur, Jhansi, Nagpur · Awadh 1856 → 'misgovernment'.
  • Historians → Savarkar 'FWI' (1909), S.N. Sen / Majumdar nuances, British 'sepoy mutiny'.

Aftermath flash

  • GoI Act 1858 → Company ends, Secy of State + 15-member Council, Viceroy = Canning.
  • Proclamation 1 Nov 1858 → Allahabad, 'Magna Carta' tag.
  • Army → European-heavy ratio, artillery reservation, caste-mix engineering.
  • States → paramountcy + protection (end of expansion).
  • Zafar → Rangoon exile (1862 death).

Trap flash

  • Tantia Tope executed April 1859 (not 1858); Lakshmibai died June 1858 Gwalior.
  • Nana Saheb's proclaimed identity = Peshwa successor (Baji Rao II adopted son).
  • Residency siege = Lucknow (Henry Lawrence), NOT Kanpur (Wheeler entrenchment).
  • First Viceroy = Canning 1858 (was GG 1856-58 too).
  • Jhalkari Bai = Jhansi woman-soldier lore, not a separate centre leader.
