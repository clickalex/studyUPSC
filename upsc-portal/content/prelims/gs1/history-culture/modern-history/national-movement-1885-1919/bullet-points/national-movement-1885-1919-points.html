National Movement 1885–1919 — Bullet Points

Match-drills.

Pairs flash

  • INC 1885 Bombay → Hume + Bonnerjee (72) · safety-valve debate → Bipan Chandra refutation.
  • Drain theory → Naoroji · Poverty & Un-British Rule → same · Economic History of India → R.C. Dutt.
  • East India Association 1866 (London) → Naoroji · Indian Association 1876 → S.N. Banerjee.
  • Partition 16 Oct 1905 → Curzon · Swadeshi resolution 7 Aug → Calcutta · Bengal National College principal → Aurobindo.
  • Swadeshi Steam → V.O.C. Pillai · Swadesh Bandhab → Ashwini Dutta · Bharat Mata painting → Abanindranath.
  • Lal-Bal-Pal trio · Kesari+Maratha → Tilak · Bande Mataram → Pal · Ganapati 1893/Shivaji 1895 → Tilak.
  • Surat split 1907 (Rash Behari Ghosh) · reunion 1916 Lucknow (with League pact).
  • Muzaffarpur 1908 → Khudiram-Prafulla Chaki · Alipore case → Aurobindo acquitted · Ghadar 1913 → Har Dayal · Komagata Maru 1914.
  • League Dacca 1906 → Salimullah · Morley-Minto 1909 → separate electorates · partition annulled + capital shift → 1911 durbar.
  • Home Rule 1916 → Tilak (Poona) + Besant (Madras) · Montagu declaration → 20 Aug 1917 · Jallianwala 13 Apr 1919 → Dyer; Udham Singh revenge 1940.

Trap flash

  • Naoroji president years: 1886, 1893, 1906 (Calcutta under Swadeshi mood).
  • Separate electorates arrive 1909 (Morley-Minto), NOT 1919 (which adds Sikhs etc.).
  • Champaran = indigo tinkathia; Kheda = tax relief; Ahmedabad = mill hunger strike (1918) — sequence often shuffled.
  • Bande Mataram newspaper ≠ song origin (Anandamath, Bankim).
  • Rowlatt satyagraha date 6 April 1919 hartal; Jallianwala 13 April (Baisakhi day).
