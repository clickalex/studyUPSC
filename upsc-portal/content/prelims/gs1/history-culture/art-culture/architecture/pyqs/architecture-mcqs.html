Architecture — Practice MCQs

8 questions

8 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Which of the following is a feature of the Nagara style rather than Dravida?

  1. (a)Enclosure walls with gopurams
  2. (b)Curvilinear shikhara topped by an amalaka
  3. (c)Storeyed pyramidal vimana
  4. (d)A temple tank within the complex
Show answer
Correct: (b)The other three are Dravida markers.
Q2

Consider the Hoysala temples: 1. Built mainly of soapstone. 2. Have stellate (star-shaped) plans. 3. Belur and Halebidu were inscribed as UNESCO sites in 2023.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All three correct.
Q3

Match: A. Rekha deul — 1. Khajuraho · B. Panchayatana on high jagati — 2. Odisha · C. Trefoil arches — 3. Kashmir

  1. (a)A-2 B-1 C-3
  2. (b)A-1 B-2 C-3
  3. (c)A-3 B-1 C-2
  4. (d)A-2 B-3 C-1
Show answer
Correct: (a)Odisha deul types; Chandela jagati; Kashmir Martand.
Q4

At Ellora, the Kailasa temple is cave number:

  1. (a)10
  2. (b)12
  3. (c)16
  4. (d)29
Show answer
Correct: (c)Cave 16, Rashtrakuta Krishna I.
Q5

The "double dome" technique in India appears first in:

  1. (a)Alai Darwaza
  2. (b)Sikandar Lodi's tomb
  3. (c)Humayun's tomb
  4. (d)Taj Mahal
Show answer
Correct: (b)Lodi Gardens, early 16th c.; perfected in Humayun's tomb.
Q6

Which site was inscribed on the UNESCO World Heritage list in 2024?

  1. (a)Santiniketan
  2. (b)Moidams of the Ahom dynasty
  3. (c)Dholavira
  4. (d)Ramappa temple
Show answer
Correct: (b)Charaideo Moidams, Assam — 43rd Indian site.
Q7

Consider the Pallava monuments at Mahabalipuram: 1. The Pancha Rathas are monolithic rock-cut structures. 2. The Shore temple is a structural temple. 3. They were commissioned by Rajaraja Chola.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Pallavas — Narasimhavarman I & II; not Chola.
Q8

The Vijay Stambh at Chittorgarh was erected by:

  1. (a)Rana Sanga
  2. (b)Rana Kumbha
  3. (c)Rana Pratap
  4. (d)Rana Hammir
Show answer
Correct: (b)1448, after victory over Mahmud Khalji of Malwa.
