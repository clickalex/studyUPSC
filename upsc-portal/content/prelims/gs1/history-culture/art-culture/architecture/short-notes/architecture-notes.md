# Architecture: Temple Styles, Forts & Monuments — Prelims Fact Card

> Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Art & Culture. Style-identification by feature is the exam's approach; learn the vocabulary first.

## Temple styles (Shilpa texts: *Nagara* north, *Dravida* south, *Vesara* Deccan)
| Feature | Nagara | Dravida | Vesara |
|---|---|---|---|
| Superstructure | *shikhara* curvilinear (rekha-prasada); *amalaka + kalasha* on top | *vimana* pyramidal storeys (*talas*); *stupi/shikhara* octagonal cap | hybrid; shorter vimana |
| Enclosure | usually none | high walls, **gopuram** gateways, tank | — |
| Plan | *panchayatana*; *garbhagriha, antarala, mandapa, ardha/maha-mandapa* | *garbhagriha, antarala, mandapa*, *Nandi mandapa*, *dvarapalas* | stellate plans (Hoysala) |
| Sub-schools | **Odisha/Kalinga** (*deul*, *jagamohana*, *rekha/pidha/khakhara deul*; Lingaraja, Konark, Jagannath, Mukteshwar), **Khajuraho/Chandela** (Kandariya Mahadeva; sandstone; erotic panels; high *jagati*), **Solanki/Maru-Gurjara** (Modhera Sun; Dilwara — marble), *latina, phamsana, valabhi* shikhara types | Pallava (rock-cut Mahabalipuram *rathas*, Shore temple; structural Kanchi Kailasanatha), Chola (Brihadishvara 1010 — 66 m vimana, monolithic 80-ton *shikhara*), Pandya (gopuram emphasis), Vijayanagara (*kalyana mandapa*, *raya gopuram*, Vitthala), Nayaka (Madurai Meenakshi — *prakaras*, 1000-pillar hall) | Chalukya (Badami rock-cut; Aihole — Durga (apsidal), Ladkhan; Pattadakal — Virupaksha (Dravida), Papanatha (Nagara)), Rashtrakuta (Kailasa Ellora), **Hoysala** (Belur Chennakeshava, Halebidu Hoysaleshwara, Somnathpur; soapstone; stellate; UNESCO 2023) |

- Others: **Kashmir** (Martand Sun — Lalitaditya; trefoil arches, pyramidal roofs), Bengal (*chala* & *ratna* terracotta — Bishnupur), Kerala (sloping roofs, circular *sreekovil*), Himachal (pagoda — Hidimba).
- Cave & stupa: Barabar (Maurya) → Bhaja, Karle, Kanheri (*chaitya* with wooden ribs; *vihara*) → Ajanta (1–29; 9 & 10 Satavahana chaityas; 16, 17, 19 Vakataka; murals), Ellora (34: 1–12 Buddhist, 13–29 Hindu, 30–34 Jain; Kailasa cave 16), Elephanta (Trimurti), Udayagiri-Khandagiri (Jain, Kharavela), Bagh, Badami. Stupa parts: *anda, harmika, yashti, chhatra, medhi, vedika, torana, pradakshina patha*; Sanchi (Ashoka; Shunga railing; Satavahana gateways), Bharhut, Amaravati, Dhamek (Sarnath, Gupta), Piprahwa, Kesariya (largest), Kanaganahalli.

## Indo-Islamic (arch, dome, minaret, *pietra dura*, *jali*, *charbagh*)
- Sultanate: Qutb complex (Aibak-Iltutmish; Alai Darwaza 1311), Tughlaq batter (Tughlaqabad, Firoz Shah Kotla), Lodi octagonal tombs & double dome (Lodi Gardens, Sikandar Lodi).
- Provincial: Bengal (Adina mosque Pandua; *bangla* roof), Jaunpur (Atala — propylon screens), Gujarat (Jama Masjid Ahmedabad; Sidi Saiyyed *jali*; Sarkhej), Malwa (Mandu — Jahaz Mahal, Hindola Mahal), Bijapur (Gol Gumbaz — 2nd largest dome; Ibrahim Rauza), Golconda (Charminar 1591), Bidar (Gawan madrasa).
- Mughal: Humayun's tomb (charbagh prototype), Fatehpur Sikri (red sandstone; Buland Darwaza; Salim Chishti tomb marble), Agra fort, Akbar's tomb Sikandra, Itimad-ud-Daula (*pietra dura*), Taj Mahal (Ustad Ahmad Lahauri; 1632–53), Red Fort & Jama Masjid Delhi, Badshahi Lahore, Bibi ka Maqbara (1660s).
- Sikh: Harmandir Sahib 1589–1604 (Guru Arjan; gold Ranjit Singh 1830). Rajput: Chittor (Kirti Stambh Jain 12th c., **Vijay Stambh** 1448 Rana Kumbha), Kumbhalgarh (36-km wall), Amber, Mehrangarh, Jaisalmer, Hawa Mahal 1799 (Pratap Singh; Lal Chand Ustad), Jantar Mantar (Jai Singh II; 5 — Delhi, Jaipur (UNESCO), Ujjain, Varanasi, Mathura lost). Stepwells: Rani ki Vav (11th c., UNESCO 2014), Chand Baori (Abhaneri, 9th c.), Adalaj (1499).

## Colonial
- Indo-Saracenic (Chepauk, Victoria Memorial (Emerson), Gateway of India (Wittet, 1924), Madras High Court, Mysore Palace (Irwin)), Gothic revival (CST Mumbai — Stevens, UNESCO 2004; Bombay University), neoclassical (Raj Bhavan Kolkata, Town Hall Mumbai), **New Delhi** (Lutyens — Rashtrapati Bhavan (Sanchi-style dome); Baker — Secretariats, Parliament House 1927), Art Deco Marine Drive (UNESCO 2018), Portuguese Goa churches (Bom Jesus — Xavier relics; Se Cathedral), French Pondicherry.

## UNESCO recent (India total **43** as of 2024)
- 2021 Dholavira & Ramappa (Kakatiya Rudreshwara) · 2023 Santiniketan & Hoysala ensembles · 2024 **Moidams (Charaideo, Ahom)** · Tentative list items keep appearing in news (Maratha Military Landscapes — inscribed 2025).

## Traps
- *Gopuram* dominance grows **later** (Pandya/Nayaka), not in early Pallava temples.
- Konark is a **Sun** temple of the **Eastern Gangas** (Narasimhadeva I) — not Chola.
- Gol Gumbaz is **Bijapur** (Adil Shahi), Charminar **Golconda** (Qutb Shahi).
- Hoysala temples use **soapstone (chloritic schist)**; Khajuraho **sandstone**; Dilwara **marble**.
