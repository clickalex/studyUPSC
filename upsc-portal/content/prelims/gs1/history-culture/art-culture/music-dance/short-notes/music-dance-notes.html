Classical music

  • Hindustani: dhrupad (Gwalior; Tansen, Dagar; alap-jod-jhala, pakhawaj; Man Singh Tomar), khayal (Sadarang/Niyamat Khan, Muhammad Shah Rangila), thumri (Lucknow; Wajid Ali Shah), tappa (Punjab camel-drivers; Shori Miyan), tarana (Khusrau attribution), ghazal, qawwali (Khusrau); gharanas — Gwalior (oldest), Agra, Kirana (Abdul Karim Khan; Bhimsen Joshi), Jaipur-Atrauli (Alladiya; Kishori Amonkar), Patiala (Bade Ghulam Ali), Rampur-Sahaswan, Mewati (Jasraj), Benares (thumri; Girija Devi); raga (thaat 10 — Bhatkhande; vadi/samvadi; time theory), tala (teental 16, jhaptal 10, ektal 12, rupak 7, dadra 6, keherwa 8).
  • Carnatic: Trinity — Tyagaraja (Tiruvaiyaru; Telugu; Pancharatna kritis), Muthuswami Dikshitar (Sanskrit; Navagraha), Syama Sastri (Kamakshi; swarajati); Purandara Dasa (Pitamaha; Mayamalavagowla); Melakarta 72 (Venkatamakhin Chaturdandi Prakashika), kriti, varnam, ragam-tanam-pallavi, tillana, javali, padam; Swathi Thirunal; M.S. Subbulakshmi (Bharat Ratna 1998), Semmangudi, Balamuralikrishna; instruments veena, mridangam, ghatam, kanjira, nadaswaram, tavil, morsing.
  • Instruments (Natya Shastra classes): tata (strings — sitar, sarod, veena, santoor, sarangi, violin), sushira (wind — bansuri, shehnai, nadaswaram), avanaddha (percussion — tabla, pakhawaj, mridangam, dhol), ghana (idiophones — manjira, ghatam, jal tarang). Exponents: Bismillah Khan (shehnai), Ravi Shankar (sitar), Ali Akbar Khan/Amjad Ali (sarod), Hariprasad Chaurasia (bansuri), Shivkumar Sharma (santoor), Zakir Hussain (tabla), Ram Narayan (sarangi), L. Subramaniam (violin), Vikku Vinayakram (ghatam), Rudra veena (Asad Ali Khan).
  • Folk/semi-classical tags in news: Baul (Bengal; UNESCO 2008), Sankirtana Manipur (UNESCO 2013), Ramman (Uttarakhand; 2009), Mudiyettu (Kerala; 2010), Kalbelia (Rajasthan; 2010), Kutiyattam (Kerala; 2008; oldest Sanskrit theatre), Ramlila (2008), Vedic chanting (2008), Nowruz, Yoga (2016), Kumbh Mela (2017), Durga Puja Kolkata (2021), Garba (2023) — India has 15 ICH elements.

Theatre & folk drama

  • Classical Sanskrit theatre: Bhasa (Svapnavasavadattam), Kalidasa, Shudraka, Bhavabhuti (Uttararamacharita), Vishakhadatta; Kutiyattam (Kerala, temple koothambalam).
  • Folk: Yakshagana (Karnataka), Bhavai (Gujarat), Tamasha (Maharashtra; lavani), Nautanki (UP), Swang (Haryana), Jatra (Bengal), Ankiya Naat/Bhaona (Assam), Therukoothu (TN), Bhand Pather (Kashmir), Maach (MP), Dashavatar (Konkan), Krishnattam (Guruvayur), Mudiyettu/Theyyam/Padayani (Kerala ritual), Burrakatha/Veedhi Natakam (AP), Powada (Maratha ballads), Alha (Bundelkhand), Pandavani (Chhattisgarh; Teejan Bai), Khyal (Rajasthan), Gambhira (Bengal), Karyala (HP), Bidesia (Bihar; Bhikhari Thakur).
  • Puppetry: string — Kathputli (Raj), Kundhei (Odisha), Gombeyatta (Karnataka), Bommalattam (TN); shadow — Tholu Bommalata (AP), Togalu Gombeyatta (Karnataka), Ravanachhaya (Odisha), Tholpavakoothu (Kerala), Chamadyache Bahulya (Maharashtra); rod — Putul Nautch (Bengal), Yampuri (Bihar); glove — Pavakoothu (Kerala), Kundhei Nach (Odisha).

Traps

  • Sattriya recognised 2000; Manipuri dancers wear no ankle bells; Kathakali is male-dominated; Mohiniyattam female solo.
  • Purandara Dasa = "Pitamaha" of Carnatic; Bhatkhande = Hindustani thaat system; Vishnu Digambar Paluskar — Gandharva Mahavidyalaya 1901.
  • Baul (2008) and Kalbelia (2010) are ICH; Chhau 2010; Garba 2023 — India has 15.
