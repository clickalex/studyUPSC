# Art & Culture — Prelims PYQs

**Q1.** Which of these classical dances draws from Sankardeva's Vaishnavite monasteries?
(a) Manipuri (b) Sattriya (c) Odissi (d) Kathak

**Model answer:** (b). Classical since 2000; ankia naats + borgeet core.

**Q2.** The "Bani Thani" painting belongs to which school?
(a) Bundi (b) Kishangarh (c) Kangra (d) Kangra-Basohli hybrids

**Model answer:** (b). Nihal Chand's iconic curvilinear-portrait style, Kishangarh court (Rajasthan).

**Q3.** Regarding manuscripts/scripts:
1. Kharoshthi was written right-to-left.
2. Brahmi was used in most Ashokan edicts.
(a) 1 only (b) 2 only (c) Both (d) Neither

**Model answer:** (c). Kharoshthi right-to-left (Gandhara region) vs Brahmi left-to-right — standard dual trap.

**Q4.** UNESCO intangible heritage entry for **Garba** corresponds to which year?
(a) 2017 (b) 2021 (c) 2023 (d) 2024

**Model answer:** (c). Sequencing anchors: Kumbh 2017, Durga Puja 2021, Garba 2023, Diwali 2024.

**Q5.** With reference to Yoga's UNESCO inscriptions:
(a) Listed 2014 (b) Listed 2016 (c) Listed via a multinational dossier (d) Listed by Nepal

**Model answer:** (b). Yoga inscribed Dec 2016; multinational-year techniques (Nowruz) distractor.

**Q6.** Hoysala temples inscribed in UNESCO's World Heritage List (2023) are:
(a) Belur, Halebidu & Somanathapura (b) Belur & Halebidu & Pattadakal
(c) Aihole & Pattadakal & Badami (d) Velloor & Talakadu

**Model answer:** (a). Triptic "Sacred Ensembles of the Hoysalas"; Pattadakal is earlier Chalukya UNESCO site.

**Q7.** Which GI-tag narrative is correct?
(a) Bengal's rasagola GI and Odisha's rasagola GI are the same registration
(b) Darjeeling tea was India's first GI (registered 2004-05)
(c) Makhana has no GI
(d) Basmati GI includes all states automatically

**Model answer:** (b). Bengal & Odisha hold **separate** rasmalai/rasagola-related GIs (2017 & 2019 nuance); Makhana GI registered 2022 (Mithila Makhana); Basmati coverage is state-specified, not automatic-all.

**Q8.** Kutiyattam is:
(a) A masked martial art from Kerala
(b) A Sanskrit theatre form of Kerala — first Indian UNESCO intangible tag of its cycle (2008)
(c) A Manipuri mime form
(d) A Sufi music genre

**Model answer:** (b). Keep distinct from Chhau (martial dance) and Kathakali.

**Q9.** Which pair matches an instrument with its maestro correctly?
(a) Zakir Hussain — santoor (b) Shivkumar Sharma — tabla
(c) Bismillah Khan — shehnai (d) Hari Prasad Chaurasia — sitar

**Model answer:** (c). (a) santoor is Shivkumar Sharma; (b) Zakir Hussain tabla; (d) Hari Prasad = bansuri.

**Q10.** The Ramman festival in UNESCO's list hails from:
(a) Bundelkhand (b) Garhwal (Uttarakhand) (c) Kangra (d) Malwa

**Model answer:** (b). Masked village ceremony of the Saloor-Dungra belt; inscribed 2009.
