## Traps
- Onam is **Kerala**'s harvest (Chingam, Aug–Sep); Pongal is TN's (Thai, Jan).
- Hornbill = Nagaland Dec 1–10; Sangai = Manipur (Nov); Ziro = Arunachal; Losar = Buddhist new year.
- Kumbh's four sites, three rivers (Godavari at Nashik, Shipra at Ujjain, Ganga at Haridwar, Sangam at Prayagraj).
