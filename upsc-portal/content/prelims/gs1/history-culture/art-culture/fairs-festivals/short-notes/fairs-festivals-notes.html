Harvest & seasonal festivals (state map)

  • Makar Sankranti cluster (Jan 14–15): Pongal (TN; Bhogi, Thai/Surya, Mattu, Kaanum), Lohri (Punjab, day before), Bihu — Magh/Bhogali (Assam; also Rongali/Bohag Apr, Kati/Kongali Oct), Uttarayan kites (Gujarat), Makaravilakku (Sabarimala), Poush Sankranti (Bengal).
  • Spring/new year (Apr): Baisakhi (Punjab; Khalsa 1699), Vishu (Kerala), Puthandu (TN), Poila Boishakh (Bengal), Bohag Bihu, Ugadi (AP/Karnataka), Gudi Padwa (Maharashtra), Cheti Chand (Sindhi), Navreh (Kashmir), Sajibu Cheiraoba (Manipur).
  • Monsoon/autumn: Onam (Kerala; Mahabali; Vallamkali; Pookalam; Thiruvathira), Hornbill (Nagaland; Dec 1–10; Kisama), Nuakhai (Odisha), Wangala (Garo; 100 drums), Ka Pomblang Nongkrem (Khasi), Behdienkhlam (Jaintia), Chapchar Kut (Mizoram; March), Losar (Ladakh/Tibetan new year), Hemis (Ladakh; Padmasambhava; masked cham), Saga Dawa (Sikkim), Losoong/Namsoong (Sikkim), Moatsu (Ao Nagas; May), Sekrenyi (Angami; Feb), Myoko (Apatani), Dree (Apatani; July), Solung (Adi), Nyokum (Nyishi), Torgya (Monpa; Tawang), Bathukamma (Telangana; floral), Bonalu (Telangana), Chhath (Bihar; Sun; Kartik), Teej (Rajasthan; Hariyali, Kajari, Hartalika), Gangaur (Rajasthan), Mewar festival, Karma (Jharkhand/Chhattisgarh; Karam tree), Sarhul (Oraon; sal), Sohrai (Santhal; cattle), Madai (Bastar), Bastar Dussehra (75 days; Danteshwari), Mysuru Dasara (Wodeyar; Jamboo Savari), Kullu Dussehra (Raghunath), Rath Yatra (Puri; Ashadha), Ganesh Chaturthi (Tilak 1893 public), Durga Puja (UNESCO 2021), Thrissur Pooram (Vadakkunnathan; April–May), Attukal Pongala (largest women gathering), Nehru Trophy boat race (Punnamada, Alappuzha; Aug), Ambubachi (Kamakhya; June), Kharchi Puja (Tripura), Garia (Tripura), Kang/Rath Yatra (Manipur), Lai Haraoba (Manipur), Yaoshang (Manipur Holi), Shigmo (Goa), Sao Joao (Goa; June), Bonderam (Divar).
  • Islamic/other: Eid-ul-Fitr (Shawwal 1), Eid-ul-Adha (Dhul Hijjah 10), Muharram (Ashura), Milad-un-Nabi; Parsi Navroz (Jamshedi/Shahenshahi Aug), Pateti; Buddha Purnima (Vaishakha); Mahavir Jayanti (Chaitra); Paryushana (Jain); Gurpurabs; Christmas; Losar; Hanukkah (Cochin Jews).

Fairs (melas)

  • Kumbh (UNESCO 2017): Prayagraj (Ganga-Yamuna-Sarasvati; Magh; Maha Kumbh 2025 144-yr), Haridwar, Ujjain (Simhastha; Shipra), Nashik-Trimbakeshwar (Godavari); Ardh Kumbh 6 yrs, Purna 12; Magh mela annual.
  • Pushkar (Rajasthan; Kartik Purnima; camels), Sonepur (Bihar; Kartik; Asia's largest cattle), Hemis (Ladakh), Surajkund crafts (Faridabad; Feb), Tarnetar (Gujarat; Saurashtra; embroidered umbrellas), Bateshwar (UP), Gangasagar (WB; Makar Sankranti), Baneshwar (Raj; tribal), Chandrabhaga (Odisha; Konark), Kolayat, Nagaur, Mahamaham (Kumbakonam; 12 yrs), Ambubachi, Jonbeel (Assam; barter; Tiwa), Shamlaji, Nauchandi (Meerut), Bhagoria (MP Bhils; haat before Holi), Malinithan, Kila Raipur rural olympics (Punjab), Hampi Utsav (Vijaya), Khajuraho, Konark, Sangai (Manipur; Nov), Ziro (Arunachal music), Rann Utsav (Kutch; Nov–Feb), Nagaland Hornbill, Losar, Saga Dawa, Tawang, Dwijing (Bodoland), Brahmaputra Pushkaram.

Heritage institutions & tags

  • UNESCO — WHS 43 (2024); ICH 15; Creative Cities (Jaipur crafts 2015, Varanasi music 2015, Chennai music 2017, Mumbai film 2019, Hyderabad gastronomy 2019, Srinagar crafts 2021, Gwalior music 2023, Kozhikode literature 2023); Memory of World (Rigveda, Gilgit manuscripts, Abhinavagupta, Tarikh-e-Khandan-e-Timuriyah; 2024 added Ramcharitmanas, Panchatantra, Sahṛdayāloka-Locana); MAB reserves 12 (latest Panna 2020); Global Geoparks — none yet (Lonar, Deccan Traps, etc. in tentative).

  • Institutions: Sangeet Natak Akademi (1953), Lalit Kala (1954), Sahitya (1954), NSD (1959), IGNCA (1987), ASI (1861; Cunningham; AMASR Act 1958; 3,697 monuments; 100-m prohibited/200-m regulated), NMMA, Anthropological Survey, Zonal Cultural Centres (7), INTACH (1984), National Mission on Manuscripts (2003), Kala Sanskriti Vikas Yojana, Adopt a Heritage 2.0 (Monument Mitras), PRASHAD (pilgrimage), Swadesh Darshan 2.0, Dekho Apna Desh, Project Mausam, Scheme for Safeguarding ICH, National Culture Fund (1996), Global Engagement Scheme, Vibrant Villages; awards — SNA Awards, Tagore Ratna, Padma; National Tribal Dance festival (Raipur), Rashtriya Sanskriti Mahotsav.
  • Classical languages: Tamil 2004, Sanskrit 2005, Telugu & Kannada 2008, Malayalam 2013, Odia 2014, Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali (Oct 2024) = 11.

Traps

  • Onam is Kerala's harvest (Chingam, Aug–Sep); Pongal is TN's (Thai, Jan).
  • Hornbill = Nagaland Dec 1–10; Sangai = Manipur (Nov); Ziro = Arunachal; Losar = Buddhist new year.
  • Kumbh's four sites, three rivers (Godavari at Nashik, Shipra at Ujjain, Ganga at Haridwar, Sangam at Prayagraj).
