Fairs & Festivals — Bullet Points

State-festival match drills.

Pairs flash

  • Shipra → Ujjain Kumbh · Godavari → Nashik · sangam → Prayagraj · Ganga → Haridwar.
  • Hornbill → Nagaland (Dec 1) · Chapchar Kut → Mizoram · Moatsu → Ao Naga · Wangala → Garo.
  • Onam-Vallam Kali → Kerala · Thrissur Pooram → Sakthan Thampuran · Theyyam season → Kerala north.
  • Chhath → Bihar (Surya Shashthi) · Nuakhai → Odisha · Sarhul → Jharkhand · Bihula → Bengal-Bihari folk.
  • Pushkar → camel + Brahma temple · Sonepur → cattle/elephants · Surajkund → crafts · Bateshwar → cattle (UP).
  • Hemis → Padmasambhava mask (Ladakh) · Losar → Tibetan new year · Ambubachi → Kamakhya tantric.
  • Uttarayan kites → Gujarat · Gudi Padwa → Maharashtra · Ugadi → Karnataka-AP · Poush Sankranti → Bengal.
  • Kumbh ICH → 2017 · Durga Puja → 2021 · Garba → 2023 · Yoga → 2016.
  • Ramlila 2005 · Ramman (Uttarakhand) 2009 · Mudiyettu + Chhau + Kalbelia 2010 · Sankirtana (Manipur) 2013 · Thatheras (craft) 2014.

Trap flash

  • Bihu has 3 versions (Bohag/Kati/Magh) — Rongali = Bohag only.
  • Hornbill is state-cultural, not religious.
  • Thatheras = metal-craft (Jandiala Guru), not a dance.
  • Gangasagar = Makar Sankranti (Jan), not Kartik.
  • Rath Yatra chariots: 3 — don't add Garuda as separate chariot.
