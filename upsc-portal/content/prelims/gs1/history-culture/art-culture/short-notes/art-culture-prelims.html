Paintings ↔ themes

  • Ajanta — Jataka panels; Padmapani/Vajrapani bodhisattvas; tempera
  • Bagh (MP cave frescoes), Badami ceiling, Sittanavasal (Jain TN)
  • Mughal: Humayun (Mir Sayyid Ali + Abd-us-Samad brought Persian masters; Hamzanama), Akbar (ateliert scale, Akbarnama/Tutinama illustrated), Jahangir (naturalism peak: Ustad Mansur), Shah Jahan (gilded formal portraits), Aurangzeb (decline)
  • Rajput: Mewar bold-reds epics; Bundi hunts; Kishangarh (Bani Thani — Nihal Chand); Kangra Krishna-leela lyric poetry style; Basohli bold; Guler lyrical
  • Deccan: Bijapur lush gold-loving; Golconda elongated elegance; Ahmadnagar refined
  • Pahari vs Rajasthani distinction standard
  • Company painting (19c): natural-history + trade ethnography
  • Bengal School wash vs Progressives (1947: Souza, Husain, Raza, Ara, Bakre, Gade)

Music pairs

  • Carnatic Trinity: Tyagaraja / Muthuswami Dikshitar / Shyama Sastri
  • Hindustani gharana pradurge: Gwalior (oldest), Kirana (Abdul Karim Khan — notes fluidity), Jaipur-Atrauli (complex layakari), Mewati, Agra
  • Instrument pairs: Annapurna Devi — surbahar; Ravi Shankar — sitar; Hari Prasad Chaurasia — bansuri; Ustad Bismillah Khan — shehnai; Zakir Hussain — tabla; Amjad Ali Khan — sarod; Shivkumar Sharma — santoor; L Subramaniam — violin; MS Subbulakshmi — vocal.

Languages / literature

  • Classical = 11 total: Tamil (2004), Sanskrit (2005), Telugu & Kannada (2008), Malayalam (2013), Odia (2014); 2024: Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese, Bengali.
  • 8th Schedule = 22 languages (14 + Sindhi '67 + Konkani/Manipuri/Nepali '92 + Bodo/Dogri/Maithili/Santhali '03).
  • Scripts: Brahmi (Ashokan, left-to-right) → families; Kharoshthi (right-to-left, Gandhara); Sharda (Kashmir), Grantha→Malayalam; Tirhuta/Maithili; Modi (Maratha docs).
  • Epics → regional tellings: Kamban's Ramavataram (Tamil), Tulsidas Ramcharitmanas (Awadhi), Krittibas (Bengali), Ezhuthachan Adhyatma Ramayanam (Malayalam).

GI firsts & icons

  • First GI (2004-05): Darjeeling Tea; earliest crafts then included Chanderi Saree, Pochampally Ikat, Kancheepuram Silk, Mysore Silk (silk cluster wave).
  • Modern icons: Makhana (Bihar GI 2022), Mithila Makhana pace-setter; Basmati GI dynamics (Madhya Pradesh addition legal tussles); Nanjangud rasabale banana revival; Odisha Rasagola GI (separate from Bengal's GI) — frequent setter traps.

Trap lines

  • Sattriya classical status = 2000 (not 2015).
  • Chhau UNESCO year = 2010; Kumbh = 2017; Durga Puja = 2021; Ramleela = 2008.
  • Kutiyattam = Sanskrit theatre Kerala (2008), NOT Kathakali.
  • Ramman = Garhwal Uttarakhand religious festival — not theatre.
