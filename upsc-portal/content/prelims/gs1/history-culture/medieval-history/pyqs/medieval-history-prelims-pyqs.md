# Medieval History — Prelims PYQs

**Q1.** [PYQ-pattern] The token-currency experiment is associated with:
(a) Alauddin Khilji (b) Muhammad bin Tughlaq (c) Sher Shah Suri (d) Balban

**Model answer:** (b). ~1330 bronze/brass token tanka; parallel trap: market control is Khilji's.

**Q2.** Which ordered pair of foreign travellers–rulers is correct?
(a) Ibn Battuta — Akbar
(b) Marco Polo — Pandyan ruler Maravarman Kulasekhara era (Madurai courts)
(c) Nicolo Conti — Krishnadevaraya court
(d) Sir Thomas Roe — Jahangir

**Model answer:** (b) and (d) are correct — trap format: if forced one, (d) is the canonical pair (1615, Mughal court), while (c) is wrong (Conti came ~1420, Devaraya II era).

**Q3.** Battle of Talikota/Rakkasa-Tangadi (1565) led to:
(a) End of Delhi Sultanate (b) Collapse of Vijayanagara power (c) First Maratha expansion (d) Mughal accession

**Model answer:** (b). Deccan sultanate confederacy defeated Rama Raya; Hampi sacked.

**Q4.** Chauth and Sardeshmukhi under Maratha revenue practice correspond to:
(a) 25% land revenue and 10% additional levy (over/near Mughal subject territories)
(b) 10% and 25% respectively
(c) Customs duties
(d) War indemnity and religious tax

**Model answer:** (a). Chauth = 1/4 of standard chauth; sardeshmukhi = additional 10%.

**Q5.** Guru Nanak's contemporary Mughal ruler was:
(a) Humayun (b) Babur (c) Akbar (d) Jahangir

**Model answer:** (b). Guru Nanak (1469–1539) witnessed Babur's invasions; *Babarvani* hymns capture it. Classical trap: Akbar/Jahangir both post-date.

**Q6.** Which Mughal emperor is associated with transferring two Ashokan pillars to Delhi?
(a) Akbar (b) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (c) Jahangir (d) Shah Jahan

**Model answer:** (b). Tophra & Meerut pillars — pre-Mughal logistics feat, one of the great prelims traps (Firoz Shah = Sultanate, not Mughal — read stems carefully).

**Q7.** The Ashtadiggajas were the court poets of:
(a) Krishnadevaraya (b) Akbar (c) Harsha (d) Samudragupta

**Model answer:** (a). Bijapur's Ibrahim Adil Shah II lore distracts; Akbar had the Navaratnas.

**Q8.** The city **Nagalapur (Hospet)** was founded by Krishnadevaraya to commemorate:
(a) Krishnadevaraya's mother (b) Akbar's victory (c) Shivaji's coronation (d) Harsha's alliance

**Model answer:** (a). Krishnadevaraya built it in honour of his mother Nagala Devi.

**Q9.** *Majma-ul-Bahrain* (confluence of two oceans) was authored by:
(a) Abul Fazl (b) Dara Shukoh (c) Amir Khusrau (d) Nizamuddin Auliya

**Model answer:** (b). Dara's Vedantic-Sufi synthesis work; he also translated Upanishads (*Sirr-i-Akbar*).

**Q10.** Fatawa Alamgiri was a compilation under:
(a) Akbar (b) Aurangzeb (c) Firoz Shah Tughlaq (d) Sher Shah Suri

**Model answer:** (b). Hanafi fiqh digest compiled under Aurangzeb's patronage.
