Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526) — Prelims Fact Card

Prelims GS-I › History & Culture › Medieval History. Ruler–reform–monument matching plus a few economic-administration terms.

Dynasties & rulers (five, in order)

DynastyYearsRulers & hooks
Mamluk / Slave1206–90Aibak (Qutb Minar begun, Quwwat-ul-Islam, Adhai-din-ka-Jhonpra; died playing chaugan 1210) · Iltutmish (real founder; capital Lahore→Delhi; silver tanka & copper jital; Iqta system; Chahalgani/Turkan-i-Chahalgani (40); mansab of Caliph 1229; completed Qutb Minar) · Raziya 1236–40 (first woman ruler; Yakut) · Balban (1266–87; Zil-i-Ilahi, sijda & paibos, Persian Nauroz; blood-and-iron; crushed Chahalgani; Diwan-i-Arz military dept)
Khalji1290–1320Jalaluddin · Alauddin (1296–1316) — market control (Diwan-i-Riyasat, Shahna-i-Mandi), first standing army with dagh (branding) & chehra (descriptive roll), land revenue 50% (kharaj), ghari & charai taxes, Mongol repulse, Deccan campaigns (Malik Kafur; Devagiri, Warangal, Dwarasamudra, Madurai), Chittor 1303 (Padmini legend), Siri fort, Alai Darwaza 1311, Hauz Khas; Sikandar-i-Sani; Amir Khusrau
Tughlaq1320–1414Ghiyasuddin (Tughlaqabad; canals) · Muhammad bin Tughlaq (1325–51) — capital to Daulatabad 1327, token copper/brass currency 1329–30, Doab tax hike, Qarachil & Khurasan plans, Diwan-i-Kohi (agriculture), Ibn Battuta (qazi of Delhi, Rihla) · Firoz Shah (1351–88) — jizya on Brahmins, canals (Yamuna–Hisar), Diwan-i-Khairat, Diwan-i-Bandagan (slaves), 300 towns (Firozabad, Jaunpur, Hisar, Fatehabad), moved two Ashokan pillars (Topra, Meerut), Futuhat-i-Firozshahi, abolished 24 taxes (kept 4: kharaj, khams, jizya, zakat + haqq-i-sharb irrigation) · Timur's invasion 1398 (Nasiruddin Mahmud)
Sayyid1414–51Khizr Khan (Timur's deputy; Rayat-i-Ala) · Mubarak (Yahya Sirhindi's Tarikh-i-Mubarakshahi)
Lodi1451–1526Bahlul (first Afghan dynasty) · Sikandar (1489–1517) — founded Agra 1504, Gaz-i-Sikandari measurement, Gulrukhi poetry, destroyed temples yet Persian–Sanskrit translations (Farhang-i-Sikandari) · Ibrahim — Panipat 21 April 1526

Administration vocabulary

  • Wazir (Diwan-i-Wizarat, finance) · Ariz-i-Mumalik (Diwan-i-Arz) · Diwan-i-Insha (correspondence) · Diwan-i-Risalat (religious/foreign) · Sadr-us-Sudur · Qazi-ul-Quzat · Barid (intelligence) · Muqti/Wali (iqta holder) · Khut, Muqaddam, Chaudhari (rural intermediaries) · Amir-i-Dad.
  • Land types: khalisa, iqta, inam/waqf/milk.

Culture & architecture

  • Indo-Islamic firsts: arch & dome (true arch: Balban's tomb), Alai Darwaza first true dome with scientific arch; Tughlaq batter/sloping walls; Lodi octagonal tombs & double dome (Sikandar Lodi's tomb, Lodi Gardens).
  • Literature: Amir Khusrau (Tughlaqnama, Khazain-ul-Futuh, Nuh Sipihr; "Tuti-i-Hind"; qawwali, tabla/sitar attributions), Ziauddin Barani (Tarikh-i-Firozshahi, Fatawa-i-Jahandari), Minhaj-us-Siraj (Tabaqat-i-Nasiri), Isami (Futuh-us-Salatin), Shams Siraj Afif.
  • Sufi silsilas: Chishti (Moinuddin Ajmer, Bakhtiyar Kaki, Farid Ganj-i-Shakar, Nizamuddin Auliya, Nasiruddin Chiragh-i-Dehli), Suhrawardi (Bahauddin Zakariya Multan; accepted state service), Firdausi (Sharfuddin Yahya Maneri), Qadiri, Naqshbandi (Baqi Billah, Sirhindi — later).

Traps

  • Iltutmish, not Aibak, is the "real founder" and issued the pure Arabic-legend tanka.
  • MbT's token currency was copper/brass, on a silver-tanka standard — failed by forgery.
  • Firoz Tughlaq's jizya on Brahmins was new; Hindus in general already paid it.
