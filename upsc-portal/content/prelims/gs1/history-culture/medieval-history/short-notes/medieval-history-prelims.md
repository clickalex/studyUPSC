# Medieval History — Prelims Revision

> Sultanate–Mughal–Maratha rail; Bhakti/Sufi pairs are the perennial supply.

## Delhi Sultanate (1206–1526)

### Slave/Mamluk (1206–1290)
- Qutb-ud-din Aibak (Lahori start, *Lakh Baksh* charitable reputation; Qutub Minar begins; died 1210 chaugan accident).
- **Iltutmish (1211–36)**: consolidator — Iqta system formalised, Chalisa/Chahalgani (Forty), Silver Tanka + Copper Jital coinage, completed Qutb Minar; **Raziya Sultan (1236–40)** — first woman ruler of Delhi; Balban (1266–87) — Divine Right theory (Zil-e-Ilahi), Sijda & Paibos protocol, crushed Mewatis, spy system (barids).

### Khilji (1290–1320)
- **Alauddin (1296–1316)**: market control (3 markets: food, cloth, horses+slaves+ghee/oil — Mint + Shahana-i-Mandi supervision), first major Mongol-repeller (Kili 1299 etc.), Deccan expeditions via Malik Kafur (Devagiri/Warangal/Dwarasamudra/Madurai), Alai Darwaza + Siri fort, Raja-Koh revenue system (measurement), Amir Khusrau at court; **Qutb-ud-din Mubarak** abolishes controls partially.

### Tughlaq (1320–1414)
- Ghiyasuddin (Tughlaqabad).
- **Muhammad bin Tughlaq (1325–51)**: the "projects list" — **token currency (1330 tanka of bronze/brass), Daulatabad capital transfer (1327), Khurasan/Karajal-Kumaon expedition**, tax hikes (Gangetic doab), rebellion map (Bengal lost 1338+); Ibn Battuta's *Rehla/Rihla* records it; founded Diwan-i-Kohi (agriculture dept).
- **Firoz Shah Tughlaq (1351–88)**: canals (Yamuna projects; "canal ruler"), Diwan-i-Khairat (welfare), Diwan-i-Bandagan (slaves dept), towns (Hisar, Firozabad, Firoz Shah Kotla with Ashokan pillar transport feat); orthodox turn, Jaziya on Brahmins included.
- Timur invasion 1398 ruins Delhi → Sayyids (1414–51) → **Lodis (1451–1526 — first Afghan dynasty: Bahlul, Sikandar (capital shift to Agra 1504), Ibrahim — defeated at First Panipat 1526 by Babur)**.

## Vijayanagara & Deccan
- Vijayanagara founded **1336 by Harihara & Bukka (Sangama dynasty)** on Tungabhadra; foreign travelers dazzle: Nicolo Conti (c. 1420), Abdur Razzaq (1443), Domingo Paes (c. 1520), Fernao Nuniz.
- **Krishnadevaraya (Tuluva, 1509–29)**: Amuktamalyada author, Ashtadiggajas court, Battle of Raichur 1520; Nagalapur (Hospet) for mother; Tuluva admin reforms.
- Fall: **Battle of Talikota/Rakkasa-Tangadi 1565 — Deccan sultanate confederacy vs Rama Raya**.
- Bahmani (1347, Gulbarga→Bidar by Ahmad Shah Wali) → fragments into **Berar, Bidar, Ahmadnagar, Golconda (Charminar 1591, Hyderabad 1591 by Quli Qutb Shah), Bijapur (Gol Gumbaz 1656 — huge dome, whispering gallery)**.

## Bhakti & Sufi
- South roots: **Alvars (Vaishnava) + Nayanars (Shaiva)**; philosopher-saints line — **Ramanuja (Vishishtadvaita), Madhvacharya (Dvaita), Nimbarka, Vallabhacharya**.
- North wave: **Ramananda → disciples network (Kabir, Raidas the cobbler, Dhanna, Sena)**; **Kabir** (doha couplets, sectless monotheism; *Bijak*); **Guru Nanak (1469–1539)** — Sikh foundation; **Chaitanya Mahaprabhu** (Gaudiya Vaishnavism, Bengal); **Tulsidas** (Ramcharitmanas, Akbar period), **Surdas** (Sursagar, Krishna bhakti), **Mirabai** (Mewar Krishna bhakti), **Namdev, Chokhamela, Eknath, Tukaram** (Varkari, Maharashtra); **Ramprasad (Shakta, Bengal)**; **Lal Ded (Kashmiri Shaiva)**; **Sankardeva (Assam; Sattriya; *ekasarana* dharma)**.
- Sufi silsilas: **Chishti** (India: Moinuddin Chishti Ajmer; Nizamuddin Auliya Delhi; Amir Khusrau bridge), **Suhrawardi** (Bahauddin Zakariya Multan; state-service friendly), **Naqshbandi** (orthodox; Aurangzeb-era; Sirhindi's shariat emphasis), **Qadiri** (liberal adaptation; Dara Shukoh links).
- Synthesis: Dara Shukoh (*Majma-ul-Bahrain*, Upanishads translation *Sirr-i-Akbar*); Guru Granth compiled 1604 (Arjan Dev).

## Mughals (1526–1857)
- Babur: **Panipat-I (1526, artillery), Khanwa (1527 vs Rana Sanga), Chanderi (1528), Ghaghra (1529)**; *Tuzuk-i-Baburi/Baburnama* (Turki).
- Humayun: Chausa loss (1539), Kannauj (1540) → **Sur Empire: Sher Shah Suri (1540–45)** — Grand Trunk Road legacy (Sadak-e-Azam), Rupiya currency, land measurement (zabt), Purana Qila + Rohtas + Sher Shah's tomb Sasaram; Islam Shah till 1553; Humayun restored 1555.
- **Akbar (1556–1605)**: Bairam Khan regency → Second Panipat (1556) vs Hemu; Mansabdari (1562 abolish pilgrimage tax; 1564 abolish Jaziya); 1575 Ibadat Khana; 1579-80 Mazhar/infallibility controversy; jahgir classification (paibaqi etc); architecture Fatehpur Sikri (Buland Darwaza, Salim Chishti dargah); Tansen & Navaratnas; Ain-i-Akbari by Abul Fazl (Akbarnama incl.); Jesuit missions.
- **Jahangir (1605–27)**: Nur Jahan junta; Chains of justice court lore; Sir Thomas Roe (1615); painting golden phase (Mansur animal-nature studies; Ustad Mansur); Guru Arjan Dev execution (1606) — Sikh-Mughal rupture seeds.
- **Shah Jahan (1628–58)**: peacetime peak — Taj Mahal (1632–53, Ustad Ahmad Lahauri chief architect attribution), Red Fort (1638), Jama Masjid Delhi (1656), Peacock Throne; Deccan annexations (Ahmadnagar formal 1636), Aurangzeb viceroy years; **war of succession 1657–59** (Dara killed 1659).
- **Aurangzeb (1658–1707)**: Alamgir; re-imposed Jaziya (1679); Deccan obsession (Bijapur 1686, Golconda 1687 vs Qutb Shahis; Maratha wars from 1680s — Shaista Khan, Shivaji escape 1666), Muazzam→Shah Alam arcs; Moti Masjid Red Fort; Fatawa Alamgiri compilation; temple decrees (Kashi/Vrindavan episodes) — contested historiography; **Nadir Shah 1739 post-Mughal blow (Peacock Throne to Persia)**; Battle of Buxar 1764 already-sunk succession; titular Bahadur Shah Zafar II exiled 1857.

## Marathas & regional
- **Shivaji (1630–80)**: Javali (Afzal Khan 1659 episode), Shaista Khan raid (1663), Surat sack (1664), **Treaty of Purandar (1665 with Jai Singh)**, Agra escape (1666), **Coronation 1674 at Raigad**; **Ashtapradhan council** (Peshwa, Amatya, Sachiv etc); **Chauth (25%)** & **Sardeshmukhi (10%)**; naval forts (Sindhudurg).
- Sambhaji (1680–89, executed); Rajaram; **Tarabai regency**; Peshwa era: **Balaji Vishwanath → Baji Rao I ("Hindu pad padshahi" expansion) → Balaji Baji Rao (Nanasaheb; Third Panipat 1761 vs Ahmad Shah Abdali — Sadashivrao Bhau); Madhavrao I revival → Bajirao II (Bassien 1802 → Second Anglo-Maratha war) → 1818 Peshwaship abolished (Bithoor pension to Bajirao II)**.

## Travellers ledger
- **Ibn Battuta (1333+, Muhammad Tughlaq court)**; Nicolo Conti; Abdur Razzaq; Domingo Paes; Nuniz (fireworks/muskets at Vijayanagara); Bernier + Tavernier (Aurangzeb/Shah Jahan courts — Bernier's *Travels in the Mogul Empire*, Tavernier 6 journeys, diamond trade); Marco Polo (13th c. Pandyan south); Al-Beruni (11th c., with Mahmud Ghaznavid entourage — *Kitab-ul-Hind*); Sir Thomas Roe; William Hawkins; Ralph Fitch.

## Prelims traps
- Iqta vs Jagir distinction (iqta = revenue assignees for military; mansabdari jagir overlaps but Mughal-specific).
- First Panipat = Babur vs **Ibrahim Lodi** (1526); Second = Akbar/Bairam vs **Hemu** (1556); Third = Maratha vs **Abdali** (1761).
- Khilji market control ≠ revenue reform: separate axes.
- Shaykh Bahai/Shaikh Salim Chishti architecture ≈ Fatehpur Sikri; Buland Darwaza = 1575 victory over Gujarat commemoration.
