Vijayanagara, Bahmani & Marathas — Bullet Points

Pair-drills.

Pairs flash

  • Harihara-Bukka 1336 → Vijayanagara (Sangama) · Alauddin Hasan 1347 → Bahmani (Gulbarga).
  • Raichur 1520 + Amuktamalyada + Ashtadiggajas → Krishnadevaraya · Conti-Abdur Razzaq → Devaraya I-II visits.
  • Talikota 1565 → Rama Raya death, Hampi sack · Aravidu = last dynasty.
  • Mahmud Gawan → Bahmani wazir, executed 1481 · 5 sultanates → Nizam-Adil-Qutb-Imad-Barid.
  • Kitab-i-Nauras + Jagadguru → Ibrahim Adil Shah II · Hyderabad 1591 → Quli Qutb Shah.
  • Pratapgad 1659 → Afzal Khan · Lal Mahal 1663 → Shaista Khan · Purandar 1665 → Jai Singh · Agra escape 1666.
  • Coronation 1674 Raigad → Chatrapati Shivaji · ashtapradhan 8 · chauth-sardeshmukhi.
  • Sambhaji executed 1689 · Jinji → Rajaram · Tarabai regency · Shahu 1707 return.
  • Peshwa line → Balaji Vishwanath → Baji Rao I → Balaji Baji Rao (Panipat-3 1761) → Madhavrao → Baji Rao II.
  • Salbai 1782 (war I) · Assaye 1803 (II) · Bassein 1802 → war II trigger · 1818 end (III).

Trap flash

  • Talikota year 1565 (not 1564); battle site = Rakkasa-Tangadi village.
  • Nayankara ≠ iqta: military-noble land in Vijayanagara, revenue-assignment in Sultanate.
  • Chauth = 1/4; sardeshmukhi = 1/10 additional — often swapped.
  • Panipat-3 = Abdali vs Marathas (Sadashivrao Bhau, Vishwasrao); Mughal Shah Alam absent on field.
  • Kanhoji Angre = navy (Sarkhel), pre-Peshwa era.
