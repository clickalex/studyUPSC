Science & Tech — Prelims Rapid Revision

Space mission-stack questions repeat; pair with Mains S&T set for institutional depth.

Space ladder (must-know dates)

  • Aryabhata 1975 (first sat, Soviet launcher)
  • SLV-3 1980 (Rohini)
  • ASLV
  • PSLV 1993
  • GSLV (Russian cryo-debptive then indigenous CE-7.5)
  • GSLV Mk III/LVM3 2017.
  • Chandrayaan-1 2008 (MIP + M3 water)
  • MOM/Mangalyaan 2013
  • Chandrayaan-2 2019 (orbiter alive; Vikram hard landing)
  • Chandrayaan-3 2023 (23 Aug soft landing, Shiv Shakti Point)
  • Aditya-L1 Sep 2023
  • XPoSat Jan 2024
  • SpaDeX Dec 2024 (docking Jan 2025)
  • NISAR Jul 2025.
  • Gaganyaan: TV-D1 (Oct 2023) → Vyomanauts named (Feb 2024) → Bharatiya Antariksh Station target ~2035.
  • Space policy: IN-SPACe (2020) + Indian Space Policy 2023 + FDI liberal 2024 (auto 100% components; capped routes upstream).

Launch pads & centres

  • Sriharikota (SHAR) launch
  • Thumba sounding rockets
  • VSSC Thiruvananthapuram (vehicles)
  • URSC Bengaluru (satellites)
  • SAC Ahmedabad (payloads)
  • LPSC (liquid propulsion; Thiruvananthapuram/Valiamala+Bangalore)
  • NRSC Hyderabad (remote sensing)
  • MCF Hassan/Bhopal
  • IPOs: NSIL commercial (New Delhi)
  • IN-SPACe HQ Ahmedabad
  • Kulasekarapattinam TN second spaceport construction (SSLV-focused).

NavIC vs GPS

  • NavIC 7 satellites (4 GSO + 3 GEO) with L5/S bands (L1 civil added); coverage India + 1,500 km.
  • GPS 24+ MEO
  • GLONASS (Russia)
  • Galileo (EU)
  • BeiDou (China) global.

Missiles gestalt

  • IGMDP (1983): Prithvi
  • Agni
  • Akash
  • Trishul
  • Nag.
  • Agni-V MIRV — Mission Divyastra Mar 2024
  • Akash exports (Armenia Dec 2023)
  • BrahMos supersonic anti-ship/land (Russian JV)
  • Pinaka MLRS
  • QRSAM/VSHORADS projects
  • Project Kusha LRSAM (sanctioned 2022).
  • ASAT test: Mission Shakti March 2019 (interceptor hit LEO ~300 km microsat).

Nuclear & particle

  • 3-stage programme
  • PFBR 500 MWe Kalpakkam
  • ITER partner
  • SST tokamaks Gandhinagar.
  • BARC (Mumbai), IGCAR (Kalpakkam), NPCIL fleet; SHANTI-style private-opening bill Dec 2025 — verify final provisions; fusion NPCIL conceptual + Indian SMR (220 MWe) Budget 2025 nudge.

IT & AI

  • IndiaAI Mission ₹10,371 cr (Mar 2024) — compute (GPU pool at ~38k), AIKosh datasets, apps in agri/health/logistics, skilling, startup funds, safe-AI → AI Safety Institute (2025).
  • Supercomputing NSM: Airawat (C-DAC), Param Siddhi-AI (top-100 class); chips Shakti (IIT-M RISC-V), Vega (C-DAC 64-bit?), DIR-V.
  • DPI set: UPI (international links: SG, UAE, FR, Mauritius...), ONDC, Bhashini (translation), MOSIP (Philippines ID), DPDP Act 2023.

Biotech

  • DBT + BIRAC (~6k+ bio-startups)
  • Genome India (10k genomes; wave-1 2024–25)
  • One Health Mission 2024
  • BioE3 Policy 2024 (bio-manufacturing incl smart proteins/marine biotech)
  • GEMCOVAC (first indigenous mRNA).
  • NBM — National Beekeeping; NBT (National Biomanufacturing?) skip.

Defence-dual-use quick hits

  • INS Vikrant commissioned Sep 2022; INS Arighaat SSBN Aug 2024; P-75(I) subs factory (MDL-L&T+TKMS Spain legacy debate) — verify at exam.
  • Tejas LCA Mk1A deliver-steps (F404 engine deliveries accelerating 2024–25); AMCA Phase-1 ₹15,000 cr sanction (2025); CATS warrior loyal-wingman prototypes.
  • DURGA directed-energy weapon family; hypersonic HSTDV demo tests.

Awards / factoids on rotation

  • National Science Day Feb 28 (CV Raman); National Space Day Aug 23 (Chandrayaan-3 landing).
  • Bharat Ratna in science only (C V Raman 1954·Subrahmanyan not · Kalam 1997); Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize lineage.
