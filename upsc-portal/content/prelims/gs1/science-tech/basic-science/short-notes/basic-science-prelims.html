Basic Science — Prelims Drill

Physics quicks

  • Laws/statements pairing: Newton's 1st-inertia (Galileo attribution) · 2nd F=ma · 3rd action-reaction · Archimedes (buoyancy) · Bernoulli (lift principle misuse caution) · Pascal (hydraulic press) · Ohm V=IR · Faraday (induction) · Lenz (direction) · Kepler (planetary) · Titius-Bode· Doppler (ambulance pitch) · Steffan-Boltzman (radiation) · Planck-Einstein photon E=hν · photoelectric (Einstein Nobel 1921).
  • Instrument-purpose: pyrometer (high temperatures), sphygmomanometer (BP), altimeter, anemometer (wind speed), Fathometer (ocean depth echo), odometer, tachometer (rpm), hydrometer (relative density of liquids; NOT hygrometer — humidity), lactometer (milk), audiometer, galvanometer vs ammeter vs voltmeter vs potentiometer.
  • Units: SI base 7 (m, kg, s, A, K, mol, cd); derived staples (N=kg·m/s², J, W, Pa, Hz, C, V, Ω, S, F, T, Wb, H); knots (nautical miles/hr), mach ratios.

Chemistry quicks

  • Common-name ↔ formula: washing soda Na2CO3·10H2O · baking soda NaHCO3 · bleaching powder CaOCl2 · plaster of Paris CaSO4·½H2O · gypsum CaSO4·2H2O · quicklime CaO · slaked lime Ca(OH)2 · limestone/marble CaCO3 · dry ice solid CO2 · laughing gas N2O · tear gas chloropicrin CCl3NO2 · green vitriol FeSO4·7H2O · blue vitriol CuSO4·5H2O · white vitriol ZnSO4·7H2O · hypo Na2S2O3·5H2O · marsh gas CH4 · caustic soda NaOH · caustic potash KOH · potash alum K2SO4·Al2(SO4)3·24H2O.
  • Acid-base: pH<7 → acid (strongest common acids H2SO4/HCl/HNO3), bases NaOH/KOH; indicators: litmus, phenolphthalein (pink in base), methyl orange, turmeric.
  • Alloys: brass (Cu-Zn) · bronze (Cu-Sn) · stainless steel (Fe-Cr-Ni) · solder (Pb-Sn) · duralumin (Al-Cu-Mg-Mn aircraft) · german silver (Cu-Zn-Ni — NO silver) · amalgam (Hg+metal) · bell metal (Cu-Sn high tin) · pewter, gunmetal; 18-carat gold = 75% purity.
  • Polymers: PVC (vinyl chloride), PE/HDPE/LDPE, PP, PET, teflon (PTFE), bakelite (first synthetic — phenol+formaldehyde), nylon (polyamide), polyester, natural rubber (isoprene cis-poly), vulcanisation (Charles Goodyear), neoprene, Buna-S/N (styrene/nitrile copolymers).
  • Air/fuel chemistry: CNG = methane-dominant · LPG = propane-butane mix · water-gas (CO+H2) · producer gas (CO+N2) · biogas (CH4+CO2) — syngas line.

Biology quicks

  • Cell organelles: mitochondria (powerhouse; own DNA; ATP) · ribosomes (protein synthesis) · lysosomes (suicidal bags — hydrolytics) · Golgi (packaging) · chloroplasts (photosynthesis; own DNA) · ER rough/smooth division.
  • Tissues: xylem (water UP) & phloem (food both-ways) functional distinction.
  • Vitamins deficiency pairs: A-xerophthalmia/night blindness · D-rickets(osteomalacia adults) · E-neuromuscular · K-clotting · C-scurvy · B1-beriberi · B2-ariboflavinosis · B3 (niacin)-pellagra (3D: dermatitis diarrhoea dementia) · B6-anaemia · B7-biotin-dermatitis · B9-folate-neural tube defects · B12-pernicious anaemia (Vegan-gap trope).
  • Hormones pick: insulin (pancreatic β-cells) · glucagon (α-cells) · thyroxine (metabolism) · GH (pituitary-somatotropin) · ADH (water) · oxytocin ("love") · aldosterone · melatonin (sleep) · adrenaline/emergency.
  • Disease transmission pairs: malaria — female Anopheles (Plasmodium) · dengue+chikungunya+Zika — Aedes aegypti · Japanese Encephalitis — Culex · sleeping sickness — tsetse · plague — rat flea (Xenopsylla) · kala-azar — sandfly.
  • System anchors: blood groups (ABO Landsteiner; Rh factor; universal donor O−; universal recipient AB+) · cardiac output arithmetic · nephron filtration.
  • Immunity: active/passive/vaccine class: BCG (TB) · DPT (diphtheria-pertussis-tetanus) · OPV/IPV polio · MMR · rotavirus vaccine · JE vaccine · HPV (Cervavac — Serum Institute India-made costed) · Covaxin (inactivated; Bharat Biotech) · Covishield (ChAdOx1 AstraZeneca/Oxford SII) · GEMCOVAC-19 (India's first indigenous mRNA, Gennova) · Sputnik-V in-market.

Everyday-modern quicks

  • Refrigerant F-gases → Kigali phase-down (HFC-134a etc) · LCD vs LED vs OLED distinction · lasers (stimulated emission coherence) · MRI (strong magnetic resonance; not X-ray) · CT (X-ray computed) · ultrasound (sonar analogy; >20 kHz) · pagers/5G waves — non-ionising RF · RADAR/Radio detection · LIDAR (laser-based mapping) · SONAR (sound under-water; blue-whale interference lore).
  • Nano basics: 2024-materials; 1 nm = 10^-9 m — classification by dimension.
