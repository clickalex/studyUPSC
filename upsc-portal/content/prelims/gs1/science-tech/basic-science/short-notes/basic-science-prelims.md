## Chemistry quicks

- Tissues: xylem (water UP) & phloem (food both-ways) functional distinction.
- Vitamins deficiency pairs: **A-xerophthalmia/night blindness · D-rickets(osteomalacia adults) · E-neuromuscular · K-clotting · C-scurvy · B1-beriberi · B2-ariboflavinosis · B3 (niacin)-pellagra (3D: dermatitis diarrhoea dementia) · B6-anaemia · B7-biotin-dermatitis · B9-folate-neural tube defects · B12-pernicious anaemia (Vegan-gap trope)**.
