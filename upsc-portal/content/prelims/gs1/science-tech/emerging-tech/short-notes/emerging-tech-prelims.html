Emerging Tech — Prelims Drill

Missions & money anchors (2023–26 frame)

  • IndiaAI Mission (₹10,371 cr; Mar 2024): compute pillar (GPU pooling — tender outcome toward ~38,000 GPUs provisioning programmes), AIKosh datasets, skilling (Data & AI Labs), application development (agri/health/logistics), startup financing, Safe & Trustworthy AI → India's AI Safety Institute (announced 2025); frontier-models homespun (AI4Bharat IndicTrans2; BharatGen IIT-B consortium 2024; Krutrim & Sarvam private builds; Hanooman family).
  • National Quantum Mission (₹6,003 cr; Apr 2023): T-Hubs at IISc + IIT Madras/Kanpur/Delhi triads; verticals: computing (superconducting, photonic, ion-trap, neutral-atom), communication (QKD free-space + fibre; network build with BSNL + army pilots), sensing/metrology, materials & devices; announced target horizon ~50–100-qubit class machines by mid-decade (verify current milestone claims).
  • National Supercomputing Mission (₹4,500 cr lineage): Rudra family (PARAM Rudra etc), AIRAWAT C-DAC (~14 PFLOPS peak, AI-rated rank-band); chips native: Shakti (IIT-M RISC-V), Vega (C-DAC); DIR-V processor family (IIT-M).
  • Semiconductor mission (ISM, ₹76,000 cr; 2021→): fab windows (Tata-PSMC Dholera 28nm-ish-class fab; Micron ATMP Sanand; CG Power-Renesas JV OSAT; Kaynes Sanand unit); DLI design-linked incentive; modernisation of SCL Mohali to 180nm-capable trajectory.
  • National Research Foundation (ANRF Act 2023; ₹50,000 cr 5-yr) — research governance over SERB inheritance.
  • Space-heavy: revisit science-tech (prelims) short-notes and mains GS-3 set.
  • Green Hydrogen (NM-GH ₹19,744 cr) — 5 MMT/yr by 2030; SECI tenders cadence; shipping/mobility pilots; Green Hydrogen Standard notifies ≤2 kgCO2e/kgH2.
  • BioE3 Policy (2024) — bio-manufacturing clusters: smart proteins, functional foods, marine biotech, climate-resilient agri, carbon-capture bio-routes; Genome India (10,000 samples wave-1 2024–25; 20 biobanks); One Health Mission; Precision medicine.

Traps

  • ANRF ≠ NQM ≠ ISM (rupees/year-allocation cards rotate).
  • GEMCOVAC ≠ Covaxin ≠ Covishield ≠ ZyCoV-D.
  • QKD (secure key distribution networks) ≠ PQC (math-hardened classical migration).
  • SCL Mohali is semi-conductor lab fab-modernization story, not a commercial greenfield fab.
