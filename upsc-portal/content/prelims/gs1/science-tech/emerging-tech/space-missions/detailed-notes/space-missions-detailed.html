Space Missions and ISRO — Detailed Notes

Mission-year-outcome table + upcoming pipeline. Chandrayaan-3 and Aditya-L1 dominate recent asks.

1. Legacy missions

  • Aryabhata (1975; USSR-launched); SLV-3 (1980; Rohini; Kalam); INSAT-IRS operational eras; PSLV first flight 1993; PSLV-C37 (2017): 104 satellites world record; LVM3 = heavy-lift (Chandrayaan-3, OneWeb, Gaganyaan).
  • Lunar arc: Chandrayaan-1 (2008; Moon Impact Probe; M3 instrument = water-molecule signatures) → Chandrayaan-2 (2019; orbiter science continues; Vikram lander lost) → Chandrayaan-3 (launched 14 Jul 2023; landed 23 Aug 2023 near lunar south pole; Vikram-Pragyan; LIBS detected sulphur; 4th nation to soft-land, 1st at south pole); National Space Day = 23 August.
  • Planetary-scientific: MOM/Mangalyaan (2013; first-attempt Mars orbit; retired 2022); Astrosat (2015); Aditya-L1 (Sep 2023 launch; halo orbit around L1 from Jan 2024; VELC coronagraph flagship); XPoSat (2024 X-ray polarimetry).

2. Current & upcoming

  • Gaganyaan: uncrewed abort/test flights first, crewed LEO target (~2027 band); Indian astronaut on Axiom-4 ISS mission (2025) — historic tie-in; SpaDeX (Dec 2024-Jan 2025): in-space docking demonstrated — 4th nation; NISAR (NASA-ISRO SAR, 2025); approved: Chandrayaan-4 sample-return, Venus orbiter, Bhartiya Antariksh Station (~2035 vision); launchport-2 Kulasekarapattinam (TN).
  • Ecosystem: IN-SPACe (2020; promotion-authorisation), NSIL (commercial arm, 2019), space FDI liberalisation (2024), private launches: Skyroot Vikram-S (2022 first private rocket), Agnikul Agnibaan (2024 3D-printed semi-cryo), Pixxel hyperspectral constellation; NavIC NVS-series refresh.

MCQ hooks

  • National Space Day = 23 Aug (Chandrayaan-3 landing).
  • Ch-1 water (M3) → Ch-2 orbiter → Ch-3 south pole + sulphur.
  • MOM = first-attempt Mars (2013-14); retired 2022.
  • Aditya-L1 = L1 halo (1.5M km); VELC payload; arrived Jan 2024.
  • 104 satellites = PSLV-C37 2017; LVM3 = Ch-3 launcher.
  • SpaDeX = docking (Jan 2025; 4th nation).
  • IN-SPACe = promoter-authoriser; NSIL = commercial; Skyroot = first private launch 2022.
