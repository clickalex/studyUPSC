Biotech and Health Technology — Detailed Notes

Bio-missions, gene-therapy frontier, health-programme anchors, pharma regulation.

1. Biotech missions & economy

  • Bio-E3 policy (2024; biomanufacturing, biofoundries, Economy-Environment-Employment framing); bio-economy ~$130bn (2024) with $300bn-2030 target; BIRAC (2012, DBT PPP arm); Genome India (~10,000 genomes; IBDC data deposit); Biotech-KISAN; 25+ biotech parks.
  • Frontier: Casgevy (2023, UK-US) = world's first CRISPR therapy (sickle cell); India's first CRISPR sickle-cell trial approved; NexCAR19 = indigenous CAR-T (IIT-Bombay-Tata); iNCOVACC = world's first nasal COVID vaccine; Gennova mRNA platform; dengue vaccine phase-3.

2. Health programmes & digital health

  • Ayushman Bharat: PM-JAY (₹5L/family) + Ayushman Arogya Mandirs (1.6L+); ABDM (2021: ABHA IDs, HPR-HFR, eSanjeevani teleconsults — world's largest govt telemedicine); Ayushman Vay Vandana (2024; 70+ universal); NTEP = TB-elimination 2025 target; Anemia Mukt Bharat; RBSK.
  • Immunisation: U-WIN (2024 immunisation portal); Mission Indradhanush; HPV-vaccination budget push (2025 cervical-cancer); NPCDCS-NCDEG; One-Health mission (2025); PM-ATMANAD diagnostics legacy.

3. Pharma, devices, regulation

  • Generics world-leader ('pharmacy of the world'); PLI for APIs + medical devices; 4 device parks; Jan Aushadhi (PMBJP) stores; NLEM 2022 (NPPA-enforced price caps).
  • Regulation: CDSCO-DCGI; New Drugs & Clinical Trials Rules 2019; NIB Bengaluru; PvPI pharmacovigilance; device classes A-D; NAP-AMR (2017); e-pharmacy debates.

MCQ hooks

  • Bio-E3 = 2024 policy; bio-economy $130bn → $300bn (2030).
  • Casgevy = first CRISPR therapy (2023, abroad); NexCAR19 = India's CAR-T.
  • iNCOVACC = nasal-first (Bharat Biotech); ZyCoV-D = needle-free (Zydus).
  • ABDM = ABHA + eSanjeevani; Vay Vandana = 70+ (2024).
  • NTEP = 2025 TB target; U-WIN = 2024 portal; Indradhanush immunisation.
  • NLEM 2022 (NPPA enforces); CTR 2019; NAP-AMR 2017.
