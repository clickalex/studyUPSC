AI, IT, Semiconductors & Quantum — Practice MCQs

10 questions

10 original prelims-pattern questions; key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

Consider the following statements about the IndiaAI Mission: 1. It was approved by the Union Cabinet in March 2024 with an outlay of over ₹10,000 crore. 2. One of its pillars is the creation of a shared compute facility of graphics processing units. 3. It is implemented by the Department of Science and Technology. Which are correct?

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Implemented by MeitY through IndiaAI (Digital India Corporation).
Q2

The Nobel Prize in Physics 2024 was awarded for:

  1. (a)The discovery of attosecond pulses of light
  2. (b)Foundational discoveries enabling machine learning with artificial neural networks
  3. (c)Experiments with entangled photons
  4. (d)Macroscopic quantum tunnelling in electric circuits
Show answer
Correct: (b)Hopfield and Hinton; (d) is the 2025 prize.
Q3

Consider the following statements about the Telecommunications Act, 2023: 1. It repeals the Indian Telegraph Act, 1885. 2. It provides for assignment of spectrum for satellite communication through administrative allocation rather than auction. 3. It abolishes the Telecom Regulatory Authority of India.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)TRAI continues.
Q4

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023: 1. A 'child' is any person below 18 years of age. 2. Appeals against orders of the Data Protection Board lie to the Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal. 3. All personal data of Indians must mandatorily be stored within India.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)No blanket localisation; cross-border transfer blacklist approach.
Q5

Which one of the following is India's first commercial semiconductor fabrication (front-end) plant approved under the India Semiconductor Mission?

  1. (a)Micron, Sanand
  2. (b)Tata Electronics–PSMC, Dholera
  3. (c)Kaynes Semicon, Sanand
  4. (d)HCL–Foxconn, Jewar
Show answer
Correct: (b)Others are ATMP/OSAT units.
Q6

Consider the following statements about the National Quantum Mission: 1. It has four Thematic Hubs, each hosted by a different institution. 2. The hub for quantum computing is at IISc Bengaluru. 3. It aims to develop quantum computers of 50 to 1,000 physical qubits within eight years.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)IISc computing, IIT-M communication, IIT-B sensing, IIT-D materials.
Q7

'Shor's algorithm' is often mentioned in the context of:

  1. (a)Training large language models more efficiently
  2. (b)Factoring large integers on a quantum computer, threatening RSA encryption
  3. (c)Compressing video for 5G streaming
  4. (d)Detecting deepfakes
Show answer
Correct: (b)Basis of the post-quantum cryptography push.
Q8

Which of the following are functions of the Indian Computer Emergency Response Team (CERT-In)? 1. It mandates reporting of cyber incidents within six hours. 2. It functions under the Ministry of Home Affairs. 3. It is the national nodal agency for responding to computer security incidents.

  1. (a)1 and 3
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 2
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)CERT-In is under MeitY.
Q9

Consider the following statements: 1. The EU Artificial Intelligence Act follows a risk-based approach. 2. India enacted a standalone Artificial Intelligence Act in 2025. 3. The Global Partnership on Artificial Intelligence was chaired by India in 2024.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (b)India issued governance guidelines (Nov 2025), not a separate AI law.
Q10

'Bhashini' is:

  1. (a)An indigenous 4G core developed by C-DOT
  2. (b)India's AI-led language translation platform under the National Language Translation Mission
  3. (c)A quantum key distribution network
  4. (d)A supercomputer under the National Supercomputing Mission
Show answer
Correct: (b)MeitY platform covering 22 scheduled languages.
