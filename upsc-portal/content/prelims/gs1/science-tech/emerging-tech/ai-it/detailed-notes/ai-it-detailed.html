AI, IT and Digital Public Infrastructure — Detailed Notes

AI policy stack + India's DPI exports + cyber-law frame. Mission-name-number recall.

1. AI policy & missions

  • NITI National Strategy for AI (2018; #AIforAll) → IndiaAI Mission (Mar 2024; ₹10,372 cr; components: GPU compute, innovation centre, AIKosh datasets, application development, startup financing, future-skills); Bhashini (2022; AI translation, 22 scheduled languages); responsible-AI advisories (2024 advisory reworked 2025 under DPDP-rules process); IndiaAI safety-institute line (2025).
  • Concepts: ML-DL-LLM chain (transformers, attention), generative diffusion models, AGI-narrow distinction, deepfakes, algorithmic bias; global: GPAI (India founding member 2020), AI-safety summits (Bletchley 2023 → Seoul 2024 → Paris 2025).

2. Digital Public Infrastructure

  • India Stack: Aadhaar (identity) + UPI (payments) + DigiLocker-eSign (documents) + DEPA (consent-based data sharing) + ONDC (open commerce) + UHI (health); G20 New Delhi 2023: Global DPI Repository + Social Impact Fund; UPI internationalisation (Singapore-UAE-France-Sri Lanka-Mauritius); CoWIN open-sourced; MOSIP = IIIT-B open-source identity platform used abroad (separate from Aadhaar).
  • Platforms: GeM, UMANG, e-Courts-3, BharatNet, PM-GatiShakti GIS; RISC-V software push, DESIGN-in-India semiconductors.

3. Cyber law & security

  • IT Act 2000 (2008 amendment); Sec 66A struck — Shreya Singhal 2015; DPDP Act 2023 (Data Protection Board; consent framework; penalties to ₹250 cr); CERT-In 2022 directions (6-hour incident reporting); NCIIPC critical-infrastructure; I4C portal + 1930 helpline; Grievance Appellate Committees (2022); terms: zero-trust, ransomware, phishing-vishing.

MCQ hooks

  • IndiaAI Mission = Mar 2024, ₹10,372 cr (not the 2018 strategy).
  • Bhashini = 2022 translation; GPAI = 2020 founding.
  • DPI stack = Aadhaar-UPI-DigiLocker (+DEPA consent layer); ONDC = open commerce network.
  • G20 2023 = DPI Repository + Social Impact Fund.
  • MOSIP = IIIT-B open-source (not Aadhaar export).
  • DPDP 2023: Board + ₹250 cr cap; 66A struck 2015; CERT-In 6-hour rule.
