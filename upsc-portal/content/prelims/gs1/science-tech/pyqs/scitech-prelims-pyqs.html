Science & Tech — Prelims PYQs

Q1. SpaDeX's primary purpose (Dec 2024/Jan 2025) was to demonstrate: (a) Solar-sail propulsion (b) Autonomous rendezvous & docking (c) Cryogenic deep-space staging (d) Reusable first-stage recovery

Model answer: (b). Key capability for Bharatiya Antariksh Station plans.

Q2. Aditya-L1 observes primarily at — (a) Earth's L2 (b) Sun-Earth L1 halo orbit (c) Lunar orbit (d) GEO

Model answer: (b). 1.5 million km sunward; PELKO coronagraph & UVIT etc.

Q3. The CE-20 is a: (a) Semi-cryogenic booster (b) Cryogenic upper-stage engine of LVM3 (c) Solid strap-on (d) Hypergolic thruster

Model answer: (b). India's most powerful cryogenic engine; human-rating variants drive Gaganyaan.

Q4. MIRV technology was validated by India via: (a) Agni-I "Shourya" (b) Agni-V "Mission Divyastra" (c) Prithvi-II (d) K-4 SLBM

Model answer: (b). Tested 11 March 2024.

Q5. [PYQ-pattern] India's ASAT test Mission Shakti (2019) used: (a) A modified Prithvi interceptor, ~300 km target altitude (b) Agni-III booster to GEO (c) Laser DEW (d) Kinetic grid-parity interceptor

Model answer: (a). Low-debris profile emphasized by GoI post-test.

Q6. NISAR is a joint mission with: (a) ESA (b) JAXA (c) NASA (d) Roscosmos

Model answer: (c). Dual-frequency L+S SAR; launched July 2025 via GSLV-F16.

Q7. AIRAWAT is: (a) A quantum network (b) A C-DAC AI supercomputer (~14 PFLOPS peak) (c) A naval early-warning aircraft (d) A solid booster variant

Model answer: (b). NSM flagship; top-100 class ranking band.

Q8. The National Quantum Mission's approved outlay + year: (a) ₹6,003 cr, 2023 (b) ₹10,000 cr, 2024 (c) ₹50,000 cr, 2023 (d) ₹2,000 cr, 2024

Model answer: (a). IndiaAI = ₹10,371 cr (2024); ANRF = ₹50,000 cr/5yr (2023). Numbers drift — memorise as triad.

Q9. Genome India project targets: (a) 100 genomes pilot (b) 10,000 genomes wave-1 (c) 1 million individuals (d) only tribal populations

Model answer: (b). Wave-1 completed reporting through 2024–25.

Q10. The Vyomanaut cadre announcement (Feb 2024) named: (a) 2 IAF pilots (b) 4 IAF Group Captains/Wg Commanders (c) 10 test pilots (d) 1 civilian astronaut-nominee

Model answer: (b). Four IAF officers for Gaganyaan crewed flights.
