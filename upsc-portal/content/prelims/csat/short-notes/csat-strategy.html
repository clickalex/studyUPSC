CSAT — Strategy Frame

CSAT = qualifying only; cutoff 33% (~66/200) but aspirants repeatedly fail it. This sheet codifies the survival discipline.

Paper surface (memorise before you touch GS1)

  • 80 questions · 200 marks · 2 hrs; marking: 2.5 mark per correct; negative 0.833 per wrong; unattempted = 0.
  • Mix share (recent): Reading comprehension ~35–40% · logical-analytical reasoning ~30–35% · basic numeracy ~25–30%.
  • Qualifying standard is brutal in absolute numbers: bad RC-block collapse + guesses pulls under 66.

Section-wise survival PlayBook

RC (Reading Comprehension)

  • Read the question stem first when options are inference-laden (last 50% efficiency gain).
  • Inference-vs-fact decoys: distract "according to passage" phrases; answer only with passage-grounded statements (not prior knowledge).
  • Titles/summary questions: map the paragraph ratio — the final answer must cover both sides of a balanced passage.
  • Tone track: cynical/hopeful/analytical/critical passages — mark the author's "but/although" pivot lines; they often settle tone-Qs.

Reasoning

  • Direction/distance → sketch-only answer, then verify.
  • Blood relations: draw-tree; avoid "exactly-two" traps without gender confirmation.
  • Series: split-double-series default (A13C15E17 pattern).
  • Puzzles (seating/table dates): create the grid skeleton first; fill negatives toward the end.
  • Syllogisms: draw two Venn variants when any "some-not" appears.

Numeracy

  • Standard bands: percentages/ratios/averages/lcm-hcf/time-work/time-distance/probability/basic mensuration/moduler clock-calendar questions.
  • Ratio band: scale-ups of 7-9-11 style problems; first verify integer consistency before solving algebraically.
  • Prime/factor tricks: 37×3=111; 101-prime translations; squares to 30 memorised.
  • Units conversions: kmph↔mps (×5/18); acres↔m² equivalents rarely demanded; focus SI.

Time & attempt policy

  • Three passes: Pass 1 = certain-line solves only (~30 Q in 40 min) → Pass 2 = medium effort (~25 Q in 40 min) → Pass 3 = select candidates; never random-punt more than 5.
  • Leave 8–10 min OMR buffer; bubble as you go.
  • Quota psychology: score ~90+ then stop on unknowns — negative spiral risk grows disproportionately.

Literacy of failure modes

  • Over-reading stems (double negatives skew pronouns).
  • Guessing rather than rereading passage.
  • Miscounting "at least" vs "at most" in reasoning.
  • Numerical slips in carrying (normalise with rough column method).

Mock economy

  • 4 PYQ papers (2018–2023 papers available online) + 4 sectional test sets; log the error categories (RC-inference vs Fact traps; reasoning pattern; numeric slips).
  • Week-1-to-exam plan: 2 mocks/week starting 8 weeks out; 1 mock/week from 2 weeks out; day-before = no new pitfalls.

Quick drills

  1. 42 is what % of 168? (25%)
  2. Ratio 3:4:5 amounts if A=36 → total = 144
  3. LCM(18,24,30) = 360
  4. 15% of 240 → 36
  5. Speed 72 km/h → 20 m/s
  6. If Monday 1 Jan 2024 → leap year; same-date 2025 = Wednesday (leap step 2)
  7. Two wheels: 3 kmph walk, 12 kmph cycle, 30 km distance, 3.75 hrs by cycle.
  8. Statement: "Some cats are dogs." Test: "No dog is cat" cannot follow; "All cats are dogs" cannot follow.
