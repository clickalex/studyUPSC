Comprehension — PYQ-Style Practice

10 questions

Short-passage drills in CSAT pattern. Pick the best answer; key at the end.

Set 1

Q1

'Urban flooding is less about rainfall and more about concrete.' The passage implies urban flooding is chiefly caused by:

  1. (a)excess rainfall
  2. (b)paved surfaces reducing absorption
  3. (c)poor weather forecasting
  4. (d)river mismanagement
Show answer
Correct: (b)(concrete ⇒ less absorption)
Q2

'While electric vehicles cut tailpipe emissions, battery sourcing raises new concerns.' Author's tone is:

  1. (a)dismissive of EVs
  2. (b)enthusiastic advocacy
  3. (c)balanced-cautious
  4. (d)neutral reporting with no view
Show answer
Correct: (c)(concession + concern = balanced caution)
Q3

'Most farmers in the survey adopted the technique after neighbours' visible success.' Inference:

  1. (a)government subsidies drove adoption
  2. (b)peer demonstration influenced adoption
  3. (c)all farmers adopted
  4. (d)technique failed
Show answer
Correct: (b)(visible neighbour success = peer effect)
Q4

'The committee neither rejected nor endorsed the proposal; it sought more data.' Committee's stance:

  1. (a)rejection
  2. (b)endorsement
  3. (c)agnostic-pending-evidence
  4. (d)indifference
Show answer
Correct: (c)(data awaited)
Q5

'Only two of the five promises were fulfilled; the rest remain aspirational.' Which statement follows?

  1. (a)no promise was fulfilled
  2. (b)three promises are unfulfilled
  3. (c)promises were illegal
  4. (d)all promises were fulfilled
Show answer
Correct: (b)(5−2 = 3 unfulfilled)

Set 2

Q6

'Critics call the dam a lifeline; displaced communities call it a loss.' The passage presents:

  1. (a)pro-dam position
  2. (b)anti-dam position
  3. (c)two contesting perspectives
  4. (d)government position
Show answer
Correct: (c)(two-sided framing)
Q7

'Sleep deprivation impairs judgement more than hunger, studies suggest.' Inference:

  1. (a)hunger has no effect
  2. (b)judgement is affected more by lack of sleep than by lack of food
  3. (c)sleep is irrelevant
  4. (d)studies are wrong
Show answer
Correct: (b)(comparative impairment)
Q8

'The scheme succeeded in districts with active panchayats; elsewhere, gains were marginal.' Passage suggests success depended on:

  1. (a)scheme design
  2. (b)local institutional engagement
  3. (c)central funding
  4. (d)weather
Show answer
Correct: (b)(active panchayats = local institutions)
Q9

'Reform, however gradual, outperforms revolution, the author argues, because institutions learn slowly.' Author prefers:

  1. (a)abrupt overhaul
  2. (b)incremental reform
  3. (c)no change
  4. (d)external imposition
Show answer
Correct: (b)(gradual reform argument)
Q10

'Digital literacy is necessary but not sufficient for financial inclusion.' Statement analysis:

  1. (a)digital literacy guarantees inclusion
  2. (b)inclusion needs digital literacy plus more
  3. (c)digital literacy is irrelevant
  4. (d)inclusion is impossible
Show answer
Correct: (b)(necessary ≠ sufficient).

Answers

1-b (concrete ⇒ less absorption) · 2-c (concession + concern = balanced caution) · 3-b (visible neighbour success = peer effect) · 4-c (data awaited) · 5-b (5−2 = 3 unfulfilled) · 6-c (two-sided framing) · 7-b (comparative impairment) · 8-b (active panchayats = local institutions) · 9-b (gradual reform argument) · 10-b (necessary ≠ sufficient).

Reattempt after a week; mark every trap type you fell for (reversal-extent-new-fact).
