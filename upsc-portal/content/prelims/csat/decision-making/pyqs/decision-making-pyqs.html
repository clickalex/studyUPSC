Decision-Making — PYQ-Style Practice

10 questions

Scenario drills in CSAT pattern. Choose the most appropriate administrative response; key at the end.

Set 1

Q1

A flood warning arrives at night; the hostel warden is absent. You should:

  1. (a)wait for the warden
  2. (b)begin evacuation and inform authorities meanwhile
  3. (c)sleep and report in the morning
  4. (d)only send a message
Show answer
Correct: (b)(act now + inform)
Q2

A vendor offers a festival gift to clear a pending bill faster. You:

  1. (a)accept, it is customary
  2. (b)refuse and process the bill on merit per rules
  3. (c)refuse and blacklist him
  4. (d)ask for a bigger gift
Show answer
Correct: (b)(merit + propriety)
Q3

Two teammates clash publicly daily. As leader you:

  1. (a)ignore, it will settle
  2. (b)hear both separately, then mediate with clear norms
  3. (c)transfer both
  4. (d)take sides
Show answer
Correct: (b)(hear-meditate-norms)
Q4

Your senior orders an improper verbal approval on a tender. You:

  1. (a)comply silently
  2. (b)seek written clarification citing rules and record your objection
  3. (c)leak to media
  4. (d)refuse and walk out
Show answer
Correct: (b)(written clarification + record)
Q5

Villagers block a road over a water shortage. Best first response:

  1. (a)police lathi-charge
  2. (b)meet the group, commit to a time-bound supply plan, restore traffic
  3. (c)ignore till they disperse
  4. (d)file FIR on all
Show answer
Correct: (b)(engage + time-bound plan)

Set 2

Q6

A colleague claims your project's credit in a review. You:

  1. (a)confront him loudly
  2. (b)present your documented contributions factually to the forum
  3. (c)sabotage his work
  4. (d)resign
Show answer
Correct: (b)(documented factual assertion)
Q7

Mid-year, funds run out for a midday meal programme. You:

  1. (a)stop meals till next budget
  2. (b)use contingency/reallocation per rules and flag to higher authority
  3. (c)divert from a stadium project quietly
  4. (d)borrow personally
Show answer
Correct: (b)(lawful reallocation + flag)
Q8

An RTI reply may expose a colleague's error. You:

  1. (a)delay the reply
  2. (b)provide the information as law requires and let accountability follow
  3. (c)alter records
  4. (d)deny holding records
Show answer
Correct: (b)(RTI compliance)
Q9

A subordinate is consistently late but talented. You:

  1. (a)ignore the punctuality issue
  2. (b)counsel, set expectations, monitor, act per rules if no change
  3. (c)dismiss immediately
  4. (d)public shaming
Show answer
Correct: (b)(counsel-monitor-act ladder)
Q10

During a riot-mobilisation rumour on social media, you:

  1. (a)share it for awareness
  2. (b)issue an immediate verified clarification and monitor hotspots
  3. (c)ban the internet permanently
  4. (d)do nothing till it trends
Show answer
Correct: (b)(verified clarification + monitoring).

Answers

  • 1-b (act now + inform)
  • 2-b (merit + propriety)
  • 3-b (hear-meditate-norms)
  • 4-b (written clarification + record)
  • 5-b (engage + time-bound plan)
  • 6-b (documented factual assertion)
  • 7-b (lawful reallocation + flag)
  • 8-b (RTI compliance)
  • 9-b (counsel-monitor-act ladder)
  • 10-b (verified clarification + monitoring).

Pattern to internalise: lawful + immediate + communicated + recorded beats all extremes.
