# CSAT Numeracy — Drills & Shortcuts

## Core formula rail (memorise+practise)
- **%↔fraction**: 1/8=12.5% · 1/6≈16.67% · 1/7≈14.28% · 1/9≈11.11% · 1/11≈9.09%.
- **CI vs SI** spread over 2 yrs = P×(r/100)².
- **Speed**: km/h × 5/18 = m/s; average speed (equal distances) = 2ab/(a+b).
- **Work**: combined = LCM method; efficiency ratios invert time.
- **Trains**: crossing pole = own length; crossing bridge = length+bridge; relative speeds — opposite add, same subtract.
- **Boats**: downstream = b+w; upstream = b−w; still-water speed = (d+u)/2.
- **Averages**: inclusion/replacement adjust via total-difference method.
- **Ratios**: a:b and b:c → a:b:c by b-matching.
- **Profit/loss**: SP = CP×(1±p); successive discounts = 1−Π(1−dᵢ); MP vs discount vs profit triangles.
- **Divisibility**: 3/9 digit-sum · 4 last two digits · 8 last three · 11 alternate-sum · 7 split-3-digit trick.
- **HCF/LCM**: product = HCF×LCM (two numbers); prime factors via division chains.

## Drill Set A (one-minuteers)
1. 15% of 240 → **36** · 2. 3/8 of 160 → **60** · 3. √1764 → **42** · 4. 12% of ₹2,500 → **300** · 5. If 40% of x = 96 → **x = 240**.

## Drill Set B (two-minuteers)
1. Two numbers ratio 3:5; sum 64 → **24 & 40**.
2. Simple interest doubles money in 8 yrs → **12.5% p.a.**
3. Pipe A 4h, Pipe B 6h, together → **2.4 h** (LCM=12; 3+2=5 units/hr; 12/5).
4. Train 180 m at 54 km/h crosses pole in → 54×5/18=15 m/s; **12 s**.
5. CP ₹500; 20% loss → **SP ₹400**. CP unknown, SP ₹450 at 10% loss → **CP ₹500**.
6. Avg of 7 numbers = 50; remove 68 & 85 → total drop 153; new total 350−153=197 over 5 → **39.4**.
7. Successive 20% & 10% discounts → net **28%** (1−0.8×0.9).
8. A does 1/3 work in 5 days → full in 15 d; B in 10 → combined 6 days.
9. LCM(15,20,24) = **120**; HCF: gcd(15,20) = 5 and gcd(5,24) = 1 → **HCF = 1**. Working: 15=3·5 · 20=2²·5 · 24=2³·3 → LCM = 2³·3·5 = 120.
10. Population: 2,000 grows 10%/yr → after 2 yrs **2,420**.

## Drill Set C (DI-lite table)
Table: (Units produced: Mon 120, Tue 150, Wed 180, Thu 130, Fri 210; defective %: 5, 4, 6, 5, 5)
- Total produced **790**; total defective = 6+6+10.8+6.5+10.5=**39.8**; % defective of total ≈ **5.04%**.
- Best quality day (lowest % defective): **Tuesday (4%)**.
- Conversions that punish: "defective in thousands" interpretations — read units line twice.

## Shape & mensuration (CSAT-level only)
- Rectangle/square/triangle area · circle πr² & circumference · cylinder (2πr(h+r) TSA; πr²h volume) · cone (1/3 πr²h) · sphere (4πr²; 4/3 πr²h) · cuboid diagonals √(l²+b²+h²).
- Path-around problems (garden frames), wire-to-circle conversions, tiled-floor counts.

## Probability & counting (light)
- Basics P = favourable/total; coins/dice/cards deck facts (52 cards; 4 suits; 12 face cards; 26 red).
- Combinatorics: nCr selections of committee questions; "at least one" = 1 − P(none).

## Number system traps
- "Between 100 and 200, numbers divisible by 7": count via floor division (200−100 bracket careful endpoints inclusivity).
- Remainder cycles: 3^n mod 4 alternates 3,1,3,1…
- "Five-digit numbers ending in 6 divisible by 6?" — test both even + digit-sum.

## Time-wasters caught early
- Multi-step ratio back-solves → verify by plugging options instead.
- Percentage-of-percentage chains ≥3 steps → convert to fractions before rounding.
- Mensuration decimals — carry π=22/7 until last multiplication for clean integers.
