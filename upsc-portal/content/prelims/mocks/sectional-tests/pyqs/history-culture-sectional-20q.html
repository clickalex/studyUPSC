Sectional Test — History & Culture (20 Questions · 25 Minutes)

20 questions

+2 / −0.66 marking. Covers ancient, medieval, modern history and art & culture in the proportion UPSC has used since 2020. Key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

The Bhimbetka rock shelters, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, are located in:

  1. (a)Chhattisgarh
  2. (b)Madhya Pradesh
  3. (c)Maharashtra
  4. (d)Odisha
Show answer
Correct: (b)Raisen district, MP.
Q2

Which Harappan site has yielded evidence of a stadium-like structure and a 'signboard' of ten large script signs?

  1. (a)Mohenjo-daro
  2. (b)Harappa
  3. (c)Dholavira
  4. (d)Banawali
Show answer
Correct: (c)Dholavira, Kutch.
Q3

The Vedic text that deals with rituals and is attached to the Rig Veda is:

  1. (a)Aitareya Brahmana
  2. (b)Shatapatha Brahmana
  3. (c)Gopatha Brahmana
  4. (d)Taittiriya Brahmana
Show answer
Correct: (a)Aitareya and Kaushitaki are Rig Vedic Brahmanas.
Q4

Consider the following statements about Jainism: 1. Mahavira was the 24th Tirthankara. 2. The first Jain Council was held at Vallabhi. 3. The Digambara–Svetambara split is traditionally linked to the migration led by Bhadrabahu to Karnataka.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (c)First council was at Pataliputra; second at Vallabhi.
Q5

'Sabha' and 'Samiti' in the Rig Vedic period were:

  1. (a)Categories of priests
  2. (b)Tribal assemblies
  3. (c)Types of sacrifices
  4. (d)Land-revenue units
Show answer
Correct: (b)Tribal assemblies.
Q6

Consider the following pairs: 1. Hathigumpha inscription — Kharavela 2. Junagadh rock inscription — Rudradaman I 3. Aihole inscription — Pulakeshin II 4. Mehrauli iron pillar inscription — Samudragupta How many pairs are correctly matched?

  1. (a)One
  2. (b)Two
  3. (c)Three
  4. (d)Four
Show answer
Correct: (c)Mehrauli pillar refers to 'Chandra' (Chandragupta II).
Q7

The Chola local self-government is best documented in the Uttaramerur inscriptions of:

  1. (a)Rajaraja I
  2. (b)Parantaka I
  3. (c)Rajendra I
  4. (d)Kulottunga I
Show answer
Correct: (b)Parantaka I (10th century).
Q8

Consider the following statements about the Delhi Sultanate: 1. Iltutmish introduced the silver tanka and copper jital. 2. Alauddin Khalji's market regulations were recorded by Ziauddin Barani. 3. Muhammad bin Tughlaq shifted the capital to Daulatabad and issued token currency.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q9

The Vijayanagara system under which military chiefs were granted territory in return for maintaining troops was called:

  1. (a)Iqta
  2. (b)Nayankara
  3. (c)Jagir
  4. (d)Ayagar
Show answer
Correct: (b)Nayankara system.
Q10

Akbar's 'Din-i-Ilahi' was propounded in:

  1. (a)1575
  2. (b)1579
  3. (c)1582
  4. (d)1605
Show answer
Correct: (c)1582 (Ibadat Khana 1575; Mazhar 1579).
Q11

Consider the following pairs of painting schools and their patrons: 1. Kishangarh — Raja Sawant Singh 2. Kangra — Sansar Chand 3. Bundi — Rao Chattar Sal Which pairs are correctly matched?

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q12

The Battle of Buxar (1764) was fought between the English and the combined forces of:

  1. (a)Siraj-ud-Daulah, Shuja-ud-Daulah and Shah Alam II
  2. (b)Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daulah and Shah Alam II
  3. (c)Mir Jafar, the Nizam and the Marathas
  4. (d)Tipu Sultan, the Nizam and the Marathas
Show answer
Correct: (b)Mir Qasim, Shuja-ud-Daulah, Shah Alam II.
Q13

The Permanent Settlement of 1793 was introduced in: 1. Bengal 2. Bihar 3. Orissa 4. Madras Presidency (parts)

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)1, 2 and 3
  3. (c)1, 2 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (d)Also extended to northern districts of Madras and Varanasi.
Q14

Consider the following statements about the Revolt of 1857: 1. Kunwar Singh led the revolt in Arrah. 2. Khan Bahadur Khan led it in Bareilly. 3. Maulvi Ahmadullah Shah led it in Faizabad.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q15

Arrange chronologically: 1. Lucknow Pact 2. Home Rule League of Tilak 3. Champaran Satyagraha 4. Rowlatt Act

  1. (a)2-1-3-4
  2. (b)1-2-3-4
  3. (c)2-3-1-4
  4. (d)1-3-2-4
Show answer
Correct: (a)Tilak's Home Rule League Apr 1916 → Lucknow Pact Dec 1916 → Champaran 1917 → Rowlatt 1919.
Q16

The 'Communal Award' of 1932 was announced by:

  1. (a)Lord Irwin
  2. (b)Lord Willingdon
  3. (c)Ramsay MacDonald
  4. (d)Stanley Baldwin
Show answer
Correct: (c)British PM Ramsay MacDonald.
Q17

Consider the following statements about the Cabinet Mission Plan (1946): 1. It rejected the demand for Pakistan. 2. It proposed grouping of provinces into three sections. 3. It provided for a Constituent Assembly elected directly by adult franchise.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)Constituent Assembly was elected indirectly by provincial assemblies.
Q18

Which of the following is NOT one of the classical languages of India?

  1. (a)Odia
  2. (b)Marathi
  3. (c)Konkani
  4. (d)Assamese
Show answer
Correct: (c)Konkani is a Scheduled but not a classical language.
Q19

'Thang-Ta' is a martial art from:

  1. (a)Kerala
  2. (b)Manipur
  3. (c)Punjab
  4. (d)Nagaland
Show answer
Correct: (b)Manipur.
Q20

Consider the following statements about Buddhist architecture: 1. The Great Stupa at Sanchi was originally built by Ashoka. 2. Chaityas are prayer halls and viharas are monasteries. 3. The Karla caves near Pune house one of the largest rock-cut chaityas in India.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
