Sectional Test — Environment & Ecology (20 Questions · 25 Minutes)

20 questions

+2 / −0.66 marking. Ecology concepts, biodiversity, climate negotiations, pollution law and institutions. Key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

In an ecological pyramid, which of the following is always upright?

  1. (a)Pyramid of numbers
  2. (b)Pyramid of biomass
  3. (c)Pyramid of energy
  4. (d)None of the above
Show answer
Correct: (c)Energy flow is unidirectional with loss at each level.
Q2

'Ecotone' refers to:

  1. (a)A zone of transition between two ecosystems
  2. (b)The functional role of a species
  3. (c)A layer of vegetation in a forest
  4. (d)The total genetic variation in a population
Show answer
Correct: (a)Transition zone with edge effect.
Q3

Which of the following are 'keystone species' examples commonly cited? 1. Sea otter 2. Elephant in savanna 3. Fig trees in tropical forests

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All are classic examples.
Q4

Consider the following statements about mangroves: 1. Sundarbans has the largest mangrove cover in India. 2. Gujarat has the second-largest mangrove cover among States. 3. Pneumatophores are breathing roots found in mangroves.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q5

Which of the following are biodiversity hotspots that include Indian territory? 1. Himalaya 2. Indo-Burma 3. Western Ghats–Sri Lanka 4. Sundaland

  1. (a)1, 2 and 3
  2. (b)1 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (d)Sundaland includes the Nicobar Islands.
Q6

Consider the following statements about tiger conservation: 1. India's tiger population as per the 2022 estimate is about 3,682. 2. Madhya Pradesh has the highest number of tigers. 3. The National Tiger Conservation Authority was constituted under the Wild Life (Protection) Amendment Act, 2006.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct (MP 785).
Q7

Which one of the following Ramsar sites is located in Bihar?

  1. (a)Sultanpur
  2. (b)Kanwar Taal
  3. (c)Renuka
  4. (d)Nanda Lake
Show answer
Correct: (b)Kanwar (Kabartal), Begusarai.
Q8

Consider the following statements about the Convention on International Trade in Endangered Species (CITES): 1. Appendix I species are prohibited from commercial international trade. 2. India ratified CITES in 1976. 3. CITES CoP19 (2022) was held in Panama.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q9

The 'Loss and Damage Fund' was operationalised at:

  1. (a)COP26 Glasgow
  2. (b)COP27 Sharm el-Sheikh
  3. (c)COP28 Dubai
  4. (d)COP29 Baku
Show answer
Correct: (c)Agreed at COP27, operationalised at COP28.
Q10

Consider the following statements about India's updated Nationally Determined Contribution (2022): 1. Reduce emissions intensity of GDP by 45% by 2030 from 2005 levels. 2. Achieve 50% cumulative installed electric power capacity from non-fossil sources by 2030. 3. Create an additional carbon sink of 2.5–3 billion tonnes of CO₂ equivalent.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q11

'Mission LiFE' (Lifestyle for Environment) was launched by India at:

  1. (a)COP26 Glasgow (2021)
  2. (b)Kevadia, Gujarat (2022)
  3. (c)COP27 Sharm el-Sheikh (2022)
  4. (d)G20 New Delhi (2023)
Show answer
Correct: (b)Launched with UN Secretary-General at the Statue of Unity, 20 October 2022.
Q12

Which of the following pollutants is NOT among the 12 notified under the National Ambient Air Quality Standards, 2009? (a) Benzo(a)pyrene (b) Ammonia (c) Carbon dioxide (d) Nickel

Show answer
Correct: (c)CO₂ is not an NAAQS pollutant.
Q13

Consider the following statements about the Plastic Waste Management Rules: 1. Single-use plastic items of low utility and high littering potential were banned from 1 July 2022. 2. Extended Producer Responsibility guidelines for plastic packaging were notified in 2022. 3. The minimum thickness of plastic carry bags is 120 microns.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct (120 microns from 31 December 2022).
Q14

'Eutrophication' is primarily caused by:

  1. (a)Heavy metals
  2. (b)Excess nitrates and phosphates
  3. (c)Acid rain
  4. (d)Thermal discharge
Show answer
Correct: (b)Nutrient enrichment.
Q15

Consider the following statements about the Compensatory Afforestation Fund: 1. CAMPA was set up following Supreme Court directions in the Godavarman case. 2. The CAF Act, 2016 allocates 90% of funds to the States and 10% to the Centre. 3. The Net Present Value is payable by the user agency for diversion of forest land.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q16

Under the Van (Sanrakshan Evam Samvardhan) Adhiniyam, 2023: 1. Forest land within 100 km of international borders may be used for strategic linear projects without forest clearance. 2. Zoos, safaris and eco-tourism facilities in forest areas are exempted from the definition of non-forest purpose. 3. In 2024 the Supreme Court directed that the 'dictionary meaning' of forest under Godavarman continue to apply until States complete identification of forest land.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct (SC interim order, February 2024).
Q17

Which of the following is/are correct about the Green Credit Programme? 1. It was notified under the Environment (Protection) Act, 1986 in 2023. 2. The Indian Council of Forestry Research and Education is its administrator.

  1. (a)1 only
  2. (b)2 only
  3. (c)Both 1 and 2
  4. (d)Neither 1 nor 2
Show answer
Correct: (c)Both correct.
Q18

Consider the following statements about the Great Indian Bustard: 1. It is found mainly in Rajasthan and Gujarat. 2. In 2024 the Supreme Court recognised a right against the adverse effects of climate change under Articles 14 and 21 while dealing with power lines in its habitat. 3. It is listed in Schedule I of the Wild Life (Protection) Act.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct (M.K. Ranjitsinh, 2024).
Q19

The 'Bonn Challenge' relates to:

  1. (a)Restoration of degraded and deforested land
  2. (b)Phasing out of coal
  3. (c)Plastic pollution treaty
  4. (d)Ocean acidification
Show answer
Correct: (a)350 million ha by 2030.
Q20

Which of the following organisations publishes the 'Living Planet Report'?

  1. (a)IUCN
  2. (b)WWF
  3. (c)UNEP
  4. (d)IPBES
Show answer
Correct: (b)WWF, biennial.
