Sectional Test — Polity & Governance (20 Questions · 25 Minutes)

20 questions

+2 / −0.66 marking. Constitution, institutions, recent amendments and landmark judgments. Key with one-line reasoning at the end.

Q1

The idea of a Constituent Assembly for India was first put forward by:

  1. (a)M.N. Roy in 1934
  2. (b)Jawaharlal Nehru in 1938
  3. (c)The Cabinet Mission in 1946
  4. (d)The Cripps Mission in 1942
Show answer
Correct: (a)M.N. Roy, 1934.
Q2

Which of the following provisions came into force on 26 November 1949 itself? 1. Citizenship 2. Elections 3. Provisional Parliament 4. Fundamental Rights

  1. (a)1, 2 and 3
  2. (b)2 and 4
  3. (c)1 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)Fundamental Rights commenced on 26 January 1950.
Q3

Consider the following statements about Article 21A: 1. It was inserted by the 86th Constitutional Amendment Act, 2002. 2. It covers children aged 6 to 14 years. 3. A corresponding Fundamental Duty was added under Article 51A(k).

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q4

Under which Article can Parliament restrict the Fundamental Rights of members of the armed forces?

  1. (a)Article 32
  2. (b)Article 33
  3. (c)Article 34
  4. (d)Article 35
Show answer
Correct: (b)Article 33.
Q5

Consider the following statements about the Vice-President of India: 1. He is elected by an electoral college consisting of members of both Houses of Parliament, including nominated members. 2. He can be removed by a resolution of the Rajya Sabha passed by an effective majority and agreed to by the Lok Sabha. 3. The Constitution prescribes no grounds for his removal.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q6

A Money Bill: 1. Can be introduced only in the Lok Sabha on the recommendation of the President. 2. Must be returned by the Rajya Sabha within 14 days with or without recommendations. 3. Requires the Speaker's certificate, which is final.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q7

Consider the following statements about the Rajya Sabha: 1. It can authorise Parliament to legislate on a State List subject under Article 249. 2. It can authorise the creation of new All-India Services under Article 312. 3. It alone can initiate the removal of the Vice-President.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q8

Which one of the following writs is issued to a lower court or tribunal to transfer a case or quash an order?

  1. (a)Mandamus
  2. (b)Prohibition
  3. (c)Certiorari
  4. (d)Quo Warranto
Show answer
Correct: (c)Certiorari is corrective; prohibition is preventive.
Q9

Consider the following statements about the Supreme Court's collegium: 1. It consists of the CJI and the four senior-most judges for appointments to the Supreme Court. 2. The NJAC Act was struck down in 2015 as violating the basic structure. 3. The Memorandum of Procedure has statutory status.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)The MoP has no statutory status.
Q10

The President's Rule under Article 356 can be extended beyond one year only if: 1. A National Emergency is in operation in the whole of India or in the State. 2. The Election Commission certifies that elections cannot be held.

  1. (a)1 only
  2. (b)2 only
  3. (c)Both 1 and 2
  4. (d)Neither 1 nor 2
Show answer
Correct: (c)Both conditions (44th Amendment).
Q11

Consider the following statements about the Inter-State Council: 1. It is established under Article 263 by the President. 2. It is a permanent constitutional body. 3. Its Standing Committee is chaired by the Union Home Minister.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (c)It is not permanent; it was set up in 1990 on Sarkaria recommendations.
Q12

Which of the following are appointed by the President but can be removed only in the manner of a Supreme Court judge? 1. Comptroller and Auditor General 2. Chief Election Commissioner 3. Chairman of the UPSC 4. Attorney General

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)1, 2 and 3
  3. (c)2 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)UPSC chairman is removed by the President after an SC inquiry; AG holds office at pleasure.
Q13

Under the Chief Election Commissioner and Other Election Commissioners (Appointment, Conditions of Service and Term of Office) Act, 2023, the selection committee consists of:

  1. (a)PM, CJI and Leader of Opposition
  2. (b)PM, a Union Cabinet Minister and Leader of Opposition
  3. (c)PM, Speaker and CJI
  4. (d)President, PM and CJI
Show answer
Correct: (b)CJI replaced by a Cabinet Minister nominated by the PM.
Q14

Consider the following statements about the 73rd Amendment: 1. Panchayats at the intermediate level are optional for States with population below 20 lakh. 2. The State Finance Commission is constituted every five years. 3. Reservation for women is one-third of seats, including chairperson posts.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q15

The Waqf (Amendment) Act, 2025 provides for: 1. Inclusion of non-Muslim members in Waqf Boards 2. Abolition of 'waqf by user' prospectively 3. Applying the Limitation Act to waqf property claims

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct.
Q16

Consider the following statements about the Right to Information Act, 2005: 1. Section 8(1)(j) exempts personal information unless larger public interest justifies disclosure. 2. The DPDP Act, 2023 amended Section 8(1)(j) to remove the public-interest override. 3. Political parties have been declared public authorities by the Supreme Court.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (a)SC has not declared parties public authorities (CIC did in 2013).
Q17

Which Supreme Court judgment struck down the Electoral Bond Scheme?

  1. (a)Anoop Baranwal v Union of India (2023)
  2. (b)Association for Democratic Reforms v Union of India (2024)
  3. (c)Sita Soren v Union of India (2024)
  4. (d)Supriyo v Union of India (2023)
Show answer
Correct: (b)ADR v UoI, 15 February 2024.
Q18

Consider the following statements about the Governor: 1. The Constitution prescribes no time limit for the Governor to decide on a Bill under Article 200. 2. In State of Tamil Nadu v Governor (April 2025) the Supreme Court invoked Article 142 to deem ten Bills assented. 3. The Governor enjoys immunity from criminal proceedings under Article 361 during his term.

  1. (a)1 and 2
  2. (b)2 and 3
  3. (c)1 and 3
  4. (d)1, 2 and 3
Show answer
Correct: (d)All correct (the Nov 2025 advisory opinion later held courts cannot fix timelines).
Q19

'Special majority under Article 368 plus ratification by half the State legislatures' is required to amend: 1. Election of the President 2. Representation of States in Parliament 3. Fundamental Rights 4. Any of the Lists in the Seventh Schedule

  1. (a)1, 2 and 4
  2. (b)1, 2 and 3
  3. (c)2, 3 and 4
  4. (d)1, 2, 3 and 4
Show answer
Correct: (a)Fundamental Rights need only a special majority.
Q20

The 'Sub-classification of Scheduled Castes' was upheld by a seven-judge bench in 2024 in:

  1. (a)E.V. Chinnaiah v State of AP
  2. (b)State of Punjab v Davinder Singh
  3. (c)Indra Sawhney v Union of India
  4. (d)Jarnail Singh v Lachhmi Narain Gupta
Show answer
Correct: (b)Davinder Singh (2024) overruled Chinnaiah (2004).
