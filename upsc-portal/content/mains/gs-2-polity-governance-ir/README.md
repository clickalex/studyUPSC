# General Studies II — Governance, Polity, Social Justice & IR

Four topic folders, each carrying the standard 5-section set
(`detailed-notes/` · `short-notes/` · `bullet-points/` · `diagrams/` · `pyqs/`):

- `constitution-polity/` — Constitution: sources, FR/DPSP/FD, amendments, federal structure
- `governance-administration/` — Parliament, executive, judiciary; bodies & accountability
- `social-justice/` — health, education, welfare architecture, vulnerable sections
- `international-relations/` — India's foreign policy, groupings, neighbourhood, global governance

After adding/editing files: `node cli/generate.mjs` from `upsc-portal/` re-indexes them.
