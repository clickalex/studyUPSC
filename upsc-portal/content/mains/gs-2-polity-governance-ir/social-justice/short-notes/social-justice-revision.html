Social Justice & Welfare — Short Revision Notes

One-liner

India's welfare state is constitutionally grounded (Preamble, FRs 14–16, 21, 21A, DPSP 38–47) and delivered through a JAM-enabled, rights-instruments-and-missions architecture — whose rural-employment spine shifted from MGNREGA (100-day entitlement) to VB-G RAM G (125-day allocation-based guarantee) from 1 July 2026.

Constitutional spine

  • Art 14–16 equality & reservations · Art 17 untouchability · Art 21 / 21A life & elementary education.
  • DPSP 38–47 welfare state instructions; Art 46 SC/ST/OBC interests.

Vulnerable-section statute kit (memorise)

PCR Act 1976 · PoA Act 1989 · FRA 2006 (community forest rights) · PESA 1996 · RPwD 2016 (21 categories, 4% jobs) · JJ Act 2015 · POCSO 2012 · Transgender Act 2019 · Maintenance of Parents Act 2007 · NCBC = Art 338B (102nd, 2018) · State-list for OBCs restored (105th, 2021).

Judgments to cite

  • Davinder Singh (2024) — SC sub-classification within SC reservation allowed (empirically).
  • Puttaswamy (2018) — Aadhaar OK for subsidies, not for exclusion.
  • ADR (2024) — Electoral bonds struck down.
  • Maratha (2021) → 105th Amendment — States' OBC-list powers.

The evaluation template for any scheme answer

  1. Constitutional/DPSP anchor
  2. Design (entitlement vs mission; whom it covers)
  3. Delivery mechanics (registries, DBT, panchayats)
  4. Data (NFHS-5, PLFS, SDG index)
  5. Critique (exclusion errors, fiscal federalism, capacity)
  6. Reforms (portability, local data, convergence)
  7. Conclusion tying to "leave no one behind" (SDG)
