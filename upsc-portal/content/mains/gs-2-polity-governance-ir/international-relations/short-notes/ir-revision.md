# International Relations — Short Revision Notes

## One-liner
India's foreign policy moved from **Nehruvian non-alignment (Panchsheel 1954 → NAM)** to **multi-alignment with strategic autonomy** — engaging QUAD, SCO, BRICS and the West simultaneously while keeping the Neighbourhood First (2014–) and Indo-Pacific (SAGAR 2015 → MAHASAGAR 2025) frames at the centre.

## Doctrines (memorise with years)
**Panchsheel (1954)** · **NAM (Belgrade 1961)** (Bandung precursor 1955) · **Gujral Doctrine (1996–97)** · **Look East 1992 → Act East 2014** · **Neighbourhood First 2014** · **SAGAR 2015** · **Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative 2019** · **MAHASAGAR 2025** · **Vasudhaiva Kutumbakam (G20 motto 2023)**.

## Great-power snapshot
- **USA** — civil nuclear deal 2005–08 (NSG waiver 2008); foundational pacts **LEMOA (2016), COMCASA (2018), BECA (2020)**; **iCET 2023**; tariff clash of 2025 → trade negotiations continue.
- **Russia** — 1971 Treaty; annual summits since 2000; S-400; post-2022 **top discounted-crude supplier**.
- **China** — LAC 3,488 km; 1962 war; five border pacts (1993/96/2005/12/13); **Galwan 2020** → **October 2024 patrolling pact** → leaders met at **Kazan (Oct 2024)** and **Tianjin SCO sidelines (2025)**; trade deficit ~$85–100 bn; Press Note 3 (2020) FDI scrutiny.
- **Pakistan** — 1947/65/71/Kargil 99; **Indus Waters Treaty (1960) in abeyance since 23 Apr 2025** (after **Pahalgam, 22 Apr 2025**); **Operation Sindoor, 7–10 May 2025**, DGMO ceasefire.

## Neighbourhood anchors
**Nepal** — 1950 Treaty, open border. **Bhutan** — 1949 Treaty (2007 update). **Bangladesh** — Land Boundary Agreement 2015; post-Aug-2024 transition watch. **Sri Lanka** — $4 bn 2022 crisis support. **Maldives** — Operation Cactus 1988 legacy; development ballast. **Myanmar** — trilateral highway, Kaladan. **Afghanistan** — Chabahar route (10-year contract May 2024).

## Groupings map
- **SAARC (1985)** — frozen since 2016.
- **BIMSTEC (1997)** — the working South-Asian platform (Bangkok summit 2025).
- **QUAD** — revived 2017, leader-level 2021 (India, US, Japan, Australia).
- **I2U2** (2022) — India, Israel, UAE, US.
- **SCO** — India member 2017; 2025 Tianjin.
- **BRICS** — expanded 2024 (Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE; Saudi invited); **India chairs in 2026**.
- **G20** — India presidency 2023 (New Delhi Declaration; **African Union** admitted); hosts: Brazil 2024, South Africa 2025, US 2026.
- **IMEC (2023 G20)**; **ISA (2015, Gurugram HQ)**; **CDRI (2019)**; **GBA (2023)**.

## UN & reform
- 8 UNSC non-permanent terms (latest 2021–22); **G4** (India-Brazil-Germany-Japan); supports the **Ezulwini Consensus**; top peacekeeping contributor.

## Diaspora
~35 million — the world's largest; **$120 bn+ remittances**; **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas (9 Jan)**; **OCI since 2005**.

## Essay/Mantra one-liners
- **"Strategic autonomy"** ≠ isolation; it is *issue-based partners without alliance obligations*.
- **"India cannot choose its geography"** — neighbours are destiny; diplomacy must be continuous.
- **Balancing act 2024–26**: Quad and BRICS, Washington trade friction and Moscow oil, Pahalgam-set track with Islamabad.
