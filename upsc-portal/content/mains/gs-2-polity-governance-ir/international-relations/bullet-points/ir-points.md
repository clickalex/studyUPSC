## Bullet facts (prelims gold)

- **Panchsheel** — signed **29 April 1954** (India–China, Tibet trade).
- **NAM** — institutionalised **Belgrade 1961**; Bandung Conference **1955** the precursor.
- **Gujral Doctrine** — 1996–97; non-reciprocal generosity toward smaller neighbours.
- **Look East 1992 → Act East 2014** (Myanmar, ASEAN, Indo-Pacific).
- **Neighbourhood First (2014)**; **SAGAR (2015)**; **Indo-Pacific Oceans Initiative (2019)**; **MAHASAGAR (2025)**.
- **Indo-US nuclear deal** — 2005–08; **NSG waiver September 2008**.
- Foundational defence pacts — **LEMOA (2016), COMCASA (2018), BECA (2020)**.
- **iCET (2023)** — critical & emerging technologies; **India–US 2+2 ministerial dialogue**.
- India–Russia: **1971 Treaty**, **annual summit mechanism (2000–)**, **S-400 deliveries**, **post-2022 top crude supplier**.
- **LAC = 3,488 km** (western, middle, eastern sectors); **Galwan clash — 15 June 2020**; **Kazan thaw — October 2024** (patrolling arrangement).
- **Tianjin SCO (Aug–Sep 2025)** — the first PM-level China visit in 7 years' frame.
- **Indus Waters Treaty (1960)** — placed **in abeyance 23 April 2025** after **Pahalgam (22 April 2025)**; **Kishenganga/Ratle** long-standing arbitration parallels.
- **Operation Sindoor — 7–10 May 2025** — terror-infrastructure strikes; **DGMO ceasefire 10 May**.
- **Article 370 & 35A** — special status reorganisation **August 2019**.
- **LAND BOUNDARY AGREEMENT 2015** (India–Bangladesh) — the enclave swap.
- **Sri Lanka: $4 bn (2022)** — emergency aid during the economic crisis.
- **Chabahar 10-year contract — May 2024**; INSTC connection.
- **SAARC** — last summit Kathmandu 2014; Islamabad 2016 postponed ever since.
- **BIMSTEC (1997)** — Bangkok 2025 summit agenda: connectivity + maritime.
- **QUAD revived 2017; leader summits from 2021**; IPMDA, critical-tech, cyber working groups.
- **I2U2 — July 2022**; **IMEC announced at G20 Delhi — September 2023**.
- **G20 presidency (2023)** — **African Union** permanent member; **New Delhi Declaration** by consensus; **Global Biofuels Alliance**; **DPI model**.
- **BRICS expansion 2024** — Egypt, Ethiopia, Iran, UAE join (Saudi invited); **India chairs 2026**.
- **SCO** — member since **2017**; **Tianjin 2025**.
- **UNSC bid** — **G4** + **L.69**; India supports the **Ezulwini Consensus**.
- **ISA (2015, Gurugram HQ)**; **CDRI (2019)**; **Mission LiFE (2021)**; **Net-zero 2070**; **500 GW non-fossil by 2030**.
- **WTO** — public-stockholding peace-clause politics; TRIPS waiver (with South Africa, 2020–22); fisheries-subsidies deal (2022, caveats).
- **Diaspora ~35 million; remittances ~$120 bn+**; **Pravasi Bharatiya Divas — 9 January**; **OCI 2005**.
