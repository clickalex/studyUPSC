# Psychology (Optional) — Orientation & Starter Framework

## Why candidates pick it
- GS-4 overlap in EI/attitude; tight syllabus; scoring history experiences steady median performance; useful for candidates with inclination toward human-behavior science.

## Syllabus map
- **Paper-I (Foundations)**: scope-methods (experimental, correlational, field/biological bases — nervous-system/brain localisation + sensors-perception modules incl. signal-detection theory; learning theories (classical/operant/observational — Bandura social-learning; conditioning applications); memory models (multi-store → working-memory Baddeley; forgetting curves-Ebbinghaus); thinking-problem solving & intelligence (psychometrics — Spearman/Thurstone/Cattell-Horn-fluid-crystallised, Gardner multiple-intelligences, Sternberg triarchic, emotional-intelligence stream); motivation & emotion (Maslow, intrinsic-extrinsic debates, cognitive-appraisal emotion-theories — James-Lange/Cannon-Bard/Schachter-Singer comparison tables); personality theories (trait Big-Five — Costa-McCrae; psychodynamic Freud/Erikson stages; humanistic Rogers/Maslow); psychological measurement essentials (reliability-validity norms), research-methods basics + statistics intro (central-tendency/variability/correlation/hypothesis-inference norm-schematics).
- **Paper-II (Applications)**: psychological well-being & mental health (DSM-lite awareness, anxiety-mood disorder descriptions, community-psych wave); work-organisation psych (leadership styles, motivation-at-work models — Herzberg/Vroom/Porter-Lawler), consumer psychology + media-psych (persuasion-discipline bond with CSAT-adjacent knowledge); education (exceptional children guidance, learning-disability classification, career guidance); application to disadvantaged groups; social psychology in Paper-II wrap: attitudes-stereotypes-prejudice-intergroup dynamics (contact-hypothesis Tremblay), environment-behaviour interface, terrorism/violence-psych roots.

## Framework skeleton
- Pattern answer: theory card (name + mechanism) → study evidence line (seminal experiment anchors: Milgram, Zimbardo-critiques, Loftus memory-malleability, Harlow attachment-isolation, Bandura Bobo doll, Ainsworth strange-situation, Asch conformity, Stroop, Kohlberg-Heinz dilemma) → Indian application + measurement note.
- Ethics of research block: informed consent + deception debriefing + orgaitho-Institute-Review-Boards (IRB) narrative.

## Standard spine books
- Ciccarelli & White (comprehensive foundations), Robert Baron (main-line text), NCERT XI-XII combo for scaffolding; Smarak Swain optional-targeted digests; Morgan-King older sets trusted for experiments summaries.

## Overlap
- GS-4 ethics (EI explicitly), essay material (human-science evidence), anthropological-lexical cohabitation.

## PYQ pattern notes
- Frequent: intelligence theory-comparison grids; motivation theory applications to governance-administration(!); prejudice-intervention drills; leadership models in organisations; research-ethics probes recur.

## Caution
- Don't over-statisticalise answers; examiners reward *concept-to-application* tightness over formula showers.
- Maintain a 40-experiment rapid-recall deck — flexible deployment wins answer-texture comparisons.
