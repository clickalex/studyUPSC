Psychology — Syllabus Map & Strategy

Paper 1: the science core (foundations, cognition, personality, social psychology). Paper 2: applications (well-being, disorders, organisational, educational, community). Compact syllabus, experiment-friendly examples, and a direct GS-4 bridge — but the marking rewards textbook precision plus Indian case application.

Paper 1 — Unit Map

BlockUnitsAnchors
Foundations1.x–4.xIntroduction & history (structuralism → behaviourism → cognitive turn); research methods (experiment, correlation, observation, ethics); biological bases (neurons, brain areas, endocrine links); sensation-attention-perception (thresholds, Gestalt laws, attention bottleneck models)
Cognition5.x–7.xLearning (classical/operant, observational — Bandura); memory (STM/LTM, Baddeley's working model, forgetting theories, mnemonics); thinking-problem-solving (heuristics — Kahneman lineage, creativity tests)

Social & applied edge11.x–12.xAttitudes (formation/change, cognitive dissonance); social cognition (schemas, attribution errors); groups (conformity — Asch, obedience — Milgram, leadership styles); language & communication; computer applications in psychology

Paper 2 — Unit Map (Psychology in Action)

  • Well-being: stress (Selye, Lazarus appraisal), coping, health-psychology (lifestyle interventions), positive psychology (Seligman's PERMA).
  • Disorders & therapy: anxiety, mood, schizophrenia spectrum, personality disorders — descriptive features + causes (diathesis-stress); therapies: CBT, behavioural, humanistic, psychodynamic, mindfulness-based; rehabilitation & community mental health (NMHS).
  • Work & organisational: personnel selection, training evaluation, motivation at work (Herzberg applied), leadership, organisational culture, ergonomics, consumer behaviour.
  • Educational: learning-style debates, giftedness & remedial teaching, inclusive education, teacher effectiveness.
  • Community & environment: community psychology (deinstitutionalisation), environmental stressors (noise, crowding), pro-environment behaviour design.
  • Applied frontier: sports psychology, military selection, psychology of terrorism & deradicalisation, media influence.

Booklist (lean and sufficient)

  • Core: Morgan & King (Introduction to Psychology) or Ciccarelli; Baron for social psychology; standard coaching compilations for structure.
  • Paper 2: applied-psychology compilations + NCERT-Class-XII Psychology for the Indian applied voice; NMHS/DOSH? — rather: NMHS (National Mental Health Survey) findings for data.
  • Data/current: NCRB suicide data, NIMHANS survey figures, workplace-survey headlines — refreshed quarterly.

Answer Strategy

  • Definition → theory/model → one classic experiment (named: Pavlov, Skinner, Milgram, Schachter-Singer) → Indian application → brief critique.
  • Experiments are evidence: describe in two lines (design + finding) — examiners credit empirical texture.
  • Paper 2 always lands on an Indian programme (DISHA, Manodarpan, workplace EAPs, NMHS districts) — theory without programme looks imported.
  • Diagrams: model boxes (memory pipeline, stress-appraisal flow, therapy ladders) — one per answer.

Scoring Profile and Fit

  • Best for: aspirants with any science/medicine/HR affinity; GS-4 (EI, attitude) synergy; essay empathy-psychology prompts.
  • Watch-outs: strict marking on loose claims — hallucinated studies hurt; Paper 2's applied breadth needs programme literacy.

Twelve-Week First Pass

  • Wk 1-3 foundations + perception. Wk 4-5 learning-memory-thinking. Wk 6-7 motivation-emotion-personality-intelligence. Wk 8 social + attitudes. Wk 9-10 Paper 2 well-being + disorders/therapies. Wk 11 work-education-community. Wk 12 PYQ mapping + experiment bank final.
