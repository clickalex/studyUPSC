Scoring levers

  • Thinker application cards: each canon thinker ↔ one current scheme/court line (Weber ↔ e-governance; Barnard ↔ acceptance-zone in transfer policy).
  • Reform ledger: police · district · appraisal · service-delivery — 4 cards, ARC-referenced.
  • Budget-cycle and accountability-web diagrams (drawn weekly).
  • Debate comfort: NPM-vs-NPS values clash; dichotomy's resurrection claim.

Common mistakes

  • Thinker biographies instead of applied claims.
  • Paper 2 without constitutional hooks (Art. numbers absent).
  • Ignoring Minnowbrook lineage in "new trends" questions.
  • Case-free welfare answers (no scheme/court/data anchor).

Overlap check

  • GS-2 governance: strong · GS-4: strong (accountability, values) · GS-3 (finance bits): mild · Essay (governance themes): moderate · Interview: experiential credibility.
  • Cost: low parallel load; but polarised scoring — craft (thinker-density, case-density) decides.

Twin questions before committing

  • Will I sustain a daily governance-currents ledger?
  • Can I write 12 thinker application cards and use them under time?
