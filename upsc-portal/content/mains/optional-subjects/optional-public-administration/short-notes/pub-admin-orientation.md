# Public Administration (Optional) — Orientation & Starter Framework

## Why candidates pick it
- GS-2 governance heartland sync + GS-4 ethics resonance; small-to-medium ecosystem (coaching footprint lighter than sociology/thinner than 2010s peak but sufficient).

## Syllabus map
- **Paper-I**: administrative theory lineage (Classical/Wilson-Taylor-Fayol-Gulick, Human Relations/ Mayo-McGregor, Behavioural/Simon-Barnard, Systems/Open-system; Public-Choice (Buchanan), NPM (self-institution logic), Governance-buzz (network governance), post-NPM (Neo-Weberian state)); theories/meanings on organisation, bureaucracy (Weber-type); accountability & control (executive-legislative-judicial triads + citizen-centricry right-to-information); administrative behaviour (decision making: Herbert Simon's bounded rationality; communication, morale, motivation (Maslow/Herzberg/McGregor), leadership (LIKERT-style systems)); public organisations & public enterprises dynamics; development administration (Weidner) → development dynamics; personal administration systems (recruitment, training, promotion, performance-appraisal, employer-employee relations (record Gentleman-agreements), Civil-service neutrality/activism/anonymity debates + 2nd-ARC ethos calibrations); public policy (models of policy-making: Lindblom incrementalism, Dror optimal-model etc; implementation sciences); financial administration: budget concepts (PPBS lineage, new performance-budget; fiscal federalism machinery in India — Finance Commission-centre-state-divisible-pool debates), parliamentary control (PAC/Estimates/COPU trilogy + CAG).
- **Paper-II (Indian administration)**: historical framework (Kautilya Arthashastra admin; Mughal admin lineages, British civil-service creation story, constitutional-civil-service anchor Art 308–323 protections/ART-311 Dismisal/due-process + officer accountability structures); Union executive (President-PM-CoM-CabSec machinery), parliament-judiciary gates, central secretariat vs field administration blur; Ministries-departments-Cabinet-committees-trinity, PMO-lore growth patterns, NITI Aayog bypass discussion (post-PC era reforms), State admin/Wild-West zones (Governor-CM dynamics, Secretariat-Directorate vitiations; district-administration emblematic DM narrative), municipal/local-side 73rd/74th implementation realities incl. FFC state-finance-commission; public-sector-undertakings (CPSE classification Maharatna-Navratna-Miniratna schools + recent strategic-disinvestment movement: Air India sale etc); civil services reforms arc & ARC reports (1st ARC + 2nd ARC digest) — specific 2nd ARC report names (Ethics in Governance; Citizen-Centric Administration; Personnel-administration report) — memorium rundowns; disaster management + welfare administration segments; social-sector governance armoury.

## Framework skeleton
- Every answer: theorist attribution (1) → Indian administrative example (1) → reform instrument name (1) → critique/deviation line (1).
- High-yield anchor kits: Simon (bounded rationality), Weber (authority types), Taylor (scientific management), Barnard (informal orgs), Follett (constructive conflict), Dror/Lindblom on policy models, Weidner on development admin, Riggs (sala model ecology-admin) — classic recycled favourites for 10/15 markers.

## Standard spine books
- Mohit Bhattacharya (theory), Fadia & Fadia (comprehensive mug), Nicholas Henry (public-administration-public-affairs), IGNOU MPA blocks, Prasad & Prasad (Thinkers), ARC digest compilations (2nd ARC summaries — core for Paper-II ammunition), Laxmikanth for governance flesh depth patches.
