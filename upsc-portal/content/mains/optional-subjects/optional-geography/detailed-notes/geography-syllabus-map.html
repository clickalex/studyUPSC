Geography — Syllabus Map & Strategy

Paper 1: physical + human geography and the model bank. Paper 2: India end-to-end plus planning and contemporary issues. The diagram-and-map craft decides bands — and the GS-1/Prelims overlap is the strongest of all optionals.

Paper 1 — Unit Map

BlockUnitsAnchors
Physical (Geomorphology)1.xEarth interior, isostasy (Airy/Pratt), continental drift → plate tectonics, earthquakes/volcanoes, mountain building, fluvial/arid/glacial/karst/coastal landforms, applied geomorphology
Climatology & oceanography2.x–3.xAtmosphere composition/structure, insolation & heat budget, winds, jet streams, air masses/fronts/cyclones, monsoon mechanism lineage (Halley → Hadley → ITCZ dynamics), ocean relief, temperature/salinity, currents (El Niño/La Niña), marine resources, coral bleaching, Law of the Sea basics
Bio & environmental4.xSoil profile & classification, biomes (Whittaker), ecosystems services, degradation, conservation frames
Human geography5.x–9.xPerspectives (environmental determinism → possibilism → neodeterminism); population (Malthus–Boserup, demographic transition, migration laws); economic activities (agriculture — von Thünen, Whittlesey; industry — Weber, Losch; services); settlements (Christaller, Zipf); regional planning concepts; models bank

Paper 2 — Unit Map (India)

  • Physical setting: structure-relief lineage, drainage evolution, climate (monsoon system, El Niño coupling), natural vegetation, soils.
  • Society & settlement: population distribution/density/growth, migration (internal flows, remittance belts), linguistic/religious geography, settlement hierarchies, rural-urban fringe, census towns.
  • Economy: agriculture (cropping regions, green-revolution geography, irrigation, watershed), minerals-energy belts, industry location (iron-steel, cotton, IT corridors), transport & trade (corridors, ports, inland waterways).
  • Planning & politics: regional development (planning regions, multi-level planning), asymmetric federations (NE council model, tribal areas), reorganisation line, boundary-disputes geography, contemporary issues — floods/droughts, urban sprawl, environmental conflicts (Narmada-type), smart-cities.

Booklist (lean and sufficient)

  • Physical: Savindra Singh (geomorphology) + Leong (GC Leong) for climates/biomes; classnotes-level oceanography.
  • Human/models: standard "Models in Geography" compilation + Majid Husain's human geography.
  • India: Majid Husain's India volume + NCERT XI India-Physical/Societypersonal, Census handbook, Economic Survey geography boxes; one school atlas (Oxford) + Survey of India outline maps for practice.

Answer Strategy

  • Map first: India/world sketches in every applicable answer (10-second outline, 5-7 labels).
  • The physical formula: process → landform/consequence → example → diagram; theory formula: geographer → model → assumptions → Indian verification/critique.
  • Paper 2 answers need administrative anchoring: scheme/plan name + region (e.g., watershed — Neeru-Meeru; corridor — Delhi-Mumbai Expressway).
  • Contemporary issues block = GS-paper honey pot: climate adaptation, urban heat, river-linking debates.

Scoring Profile and Fit

  • Best for: engineers/science grads comfortable with systems thinking; aspirants strong at drawing; those wanting Prelims-GS1 synergy.
  • Watch-outs: the largest syllabus among optionals — volume discipline and model-bank memorisation decide; oceanography/climatology depth surprises newcomers.

Twelve-Week First Pass

  • Wk 1-3 geomorphology + climatology. Wk 4 oceanography + bio/env. Wk 5-6 human geography + models bank. Wk 7-9 India physical-society-economy. Wk 10 planning + contemporary issues. Wk 11 map-work intensive (10 maps/week). Wk 12 PYQ mapping + sectional.
