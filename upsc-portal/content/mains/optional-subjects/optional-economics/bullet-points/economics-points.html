Scoring levers

  • Graph shelf: indifference+Slutsky, IS-LM shifts, MF triangle (impossible trinity), tariff welfare triangles, Laffer, Solow diagram with golden rule.
  • Derivation six-pack: Slutsky decomposition · multiplier with imports · money multiplier · Balassa line? — keep: HO theorem sketch · golden-rule condition · debt-dynamics identity.
  • Survey mapping: chapter → answer skeleton (growth chapter → 15-marker on potential output).
  • Debate bank: inflation-band adequacy · FRBM realism · GST-architecture fixes · fiscal-monetary dominance — positions + data each.

Common mistakes

  • Essay-style Paper-2 answers without theory lens or latest Survey/RBI figure.
  • Unlabelled axes — economics diagrams without units score zero spirit.
  • Neglecting numericals in micro/macro 10-markers.
  • Stale data (using old GST/A groups? — keep: old fiscal-deficit figures).

Overlap check

  • GS-3 economy: strong · Prelims economy: strong · Interview (policy credibility): strong · Essay (development themes): moderate.
  • Cost: Paper 1 runs parallel to GS — plan a full half-year for it; recent marking is exacting.

Twin questions before committing

  • Am I comfortable with graphs and light algebra weekly?
  • Will I refresh Survey/RBI data every cycle without fail?
