Scoring levers

  • Debate dossier: feudalism, 18th-century, deindustrialisation, Partition causes — one card each (claims ×3 schools + your verdict).
  • P1 map pack: 12 maps (Harappan sites, inscription find-spots, temple schools, trading ports).
  • Precise micro-quotes: inscriptions, Abul Fazl, Gandhi's court statements.
  • Society-economy-religion frames inside political chronology.

Common mistakes

  • Narrative answers without any historiographical voice.
  • Ignoring early-medieval south (Chola questions recur).
  • World history treated as newspaper history — revolutions need causes-phases-outcomes frames.
  • Map practice postponed to March.

Overlap check

  • GS-1 (modern + world + culture): strong · Prelims history: strong · Essay (ideas movements): moderate · Interview (constitutional lineage): mild.
  • Cost: heavy parallel volume — but same books serve GS if chosen early.

Twin questions before committing

  • Can I revise 4 subject-blocks monthly without dropping?
  • Am I comfortable arguing debates, not memorising "facts only"?
