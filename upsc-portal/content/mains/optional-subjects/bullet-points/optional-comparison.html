Underrated heuristics

  • Percentile > raw marks. Test-series percentiles beat absolute-score "scores well" folklore.
  • Peer pool matters for literature subjects — strong local study circles compound.
  • Dual-prep overlap traps — e.g., choosing History "because GS-1 has history" underestimates optional depth by an order of magnitude.
  • Medium of answer = optional medium for literature optionals: ensure your exam-Indian-language mechanics are match-fit, not just conversational.
  • Changing backdrop: verify the year's notification; optional-subject success tables vary meaningfully across years.
