Scoring levers

  • Comparison tables: pramana counts across schools; satkarya debates; God-proof pairs with named objections.
  • One-line objections mastered: Gettier to knowledge, Hume to design, Mackie to theodicy, Moore to idealism.
  • Paper-2 current hooks: environmental jurisprudence, capital-punishment reform debate, AI-personhood edge.
  • Five-membered Nyaya inference drawn as diagram in logic answers.

Common mistakes

  • Devotional or dismissive tone in religion answers — reconstruct fairly.
  • Quotes without argument (positions recited, never defended).
  • Ignoring logic block — regular 10-15 markers.
  • Advaita answers without presenting rival schools' objections.

Overlap check

  • GS-4: strong (thinkers, ethical theories) · Essay: strong (philosophical prompts) · Prelims: nil · Interview: reflective depth.
  • Cost: nil GS-crowding, but the pool has high-scoring veterans — reconstruction quality decides.

Twin questions before committing

  • Do I enjoy reconstructing arguments I disagree with?
  • Can I hold Sanskrit terminology + plain-English clarity together?
