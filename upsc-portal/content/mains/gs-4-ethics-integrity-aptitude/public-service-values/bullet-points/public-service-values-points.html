Public / Civil Service Values — Bullet Points

60-second revision card.

Government vs private cheat row

  • Duty: public interest vs shareholder value (stakeholder theory contests).
  • Accountability: legislature/RTI/audit vs board/regulator/market.
  • Bridge: CSR S.135 Companies Act 2013; both face conflicts & whistle-blowing.

Law vs conscience ladder

  • 1 Law/rules (certainty, equality) → 2 Advice through hierarchy → 3 Constitutional morality → 4 Conscience as alarm → recorded dissent / whistle / resignation.
  • Conscience must never mean private sabotage or selective leaks.

Moral judgment quickies

  • Kohlberg: pre-conventional (fear/reward) → conventional (rules/peers) → post-conventional (principles).
  • Knowing–doing gap: bridged by courage + protection + peer support.
  • Tests: legality · universalisability · harm · publicity (front-page) · reversibility.

Strengthening ethics — five Rs

  • Recruit (integrity probing) · Train (case-based) · Reward (honesty; fair postings) · Restructure (Lokpal, charters, social audit) · Role-model (tone at the top).

Golden phrase bank

  • "Values sustained by design outlast values sustained by virtue."
  • "The file defends the officer; conscience guides the file."
  • "Serve the Constitution; the government of the day is your client, not your conscience."

Pitfall flags

  • Don't romanticise resignation as the first step — exhaust the institutional ladder.
  • Don't oppose law and conscience caricaturally; they usually co-move.
