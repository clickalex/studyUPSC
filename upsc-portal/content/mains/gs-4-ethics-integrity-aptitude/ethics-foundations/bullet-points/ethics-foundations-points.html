Pitfall flags

  • Do NOT invent Gandhi quotes; use only verified lines.
  • Dilute Mills ("only harm principle present" is misleading — context matters).
  • Rawls is about arrangement not desert.
  • Arendt's banality ≠ justifying cruelty — frame as institutional corrupters.
  • Kohlberg stages: 6 (pre 2, conventional 2, post 2) not 3; shorthand only in answers.
