## Thinkers — one-liner citation pack
- **Socrates**: "unexamined life is not worth living" → conscience-over-consensus.
- **Plato**: philosopher-king; justice = harmony of parts.
- **Aristotle**: virtue as habituated mean; *phronesis* = practical wisdom (administrative judgment).
- **Kant**: categorical imperative; humanity as end, not means → objectivity anchor.
- **Bentham/Mill**: greatest happiness; Mill's qualitative upgrade; harm principle → utilitarian public-policy lens.
- **Rawls**: veil of ignorance → difference principle → redistribution ethics.
- **Arendt**: banality of evil (Eichmann) — bureaucratic routinisation risk.
- **Kohlberg**: 3 moral-development levels (pre → conventional → post).
- **Gilligan**: ethics of care — corrective to male-bias justice-only frameworks.
- **Thoreau**: civil disobedience → democratic resistance tradition.
- **Kautilya**: Arthashastra realism → *rajrishi* discipline ideal.
- **Buddha**: middle path; dharma as duty-correctness.
- **Mahavira**: anekantavada (ethical pluralism); ahimsa rigor.
- **Confucius**: junzi (exemplary person) via ren + li.
- **Gandhi**: satyagraha; ends-means inseparability; trusteeship; talisman (poorest-face test).
- **Ambedkar**: liberty-equality-fraternity; constitutional morality.
- **Vivekananda**: "service of man is service of God" — prakrati Vedanta in action.
- **Tagore**: universal humanism against narrow nationalism.
- **Aurobindo**: integral evolution; **J Krishnamurti**: choiceless awareness — education ethics line.

## Conscience × Accountability × Work culture
- Conscience: inner voice; "crisis of conscience" = moral collision with orders.
- Accountability: hierarchical · legal · professional · political · social; RTI 2005 as transparency spine; social audits (MGNREGA).
- Work culture: punctuality · discipline · innovation · quality (customer focus) · transparency.
- Codes: **Conduct rules enforceable** vs **ethics code aspirational**; AIS Rules 1968 · CCS Rules 1964.
- **Nolan 7 (UK 1994)**: selflessness · integrity · objectivity · accountability · openness · honesty · leadership. → mandatory Mains citation.
- Probity instruments: citizen charters (2nd ARC), Lokpal & Lokayuktas Act 2013 (functioning post-2023), CVC/CBI/ED independence thread (Vineet Narain line).

## Quick argument scaffolds (for any ethics question)
1. Define the concept precisely → 2. Name the thinker or framework behind it → 3. Give public-administration example → 4. Identify tension (integrity vs empathy, accountability vs efficiency) → 5. Conclude with equilibrium + one measure (training norm, code revision, grievance ergonomics).
