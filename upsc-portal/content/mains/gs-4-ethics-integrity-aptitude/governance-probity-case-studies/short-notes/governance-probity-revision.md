# Governance, Probity & Case Studies — Revision Notes

> Quick-scan: what each tool is, where it bites, what the the counterweight is.

## Probity instrument grid
| Instrument | What it is | Where it bites | Watch-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| PC Act 1988 (+2018 Amdt) | corruption offences; s17A prior approval; bribe-giver now offence | service counts, procurement | investigation chill if approvals impede |
| PMLA 2002 | money-laundering tail to predicate offences | asset trail enforcement | bail-strictness & proportionality debates |
| CVC (statutory 2003) | supervisory integrity commission | tender & service cases | advisory limits (can't prosecute directly) |
| CBI (DSPE) | central criminal investigator; Vineet Narain fixed tenure | PC offences off central servants | "caged parrot" politics narrative |
| Lokpal & Lokayuktas Act 2013 | anti-corruption ombudsman for PM-CM-officials | complaints since bench 2023-24 | offices under-utilised so far |
| RTI 2005 (+2019 tenure/salary amends) | right to information; ICs at centre/states | proactive Sec 4 disclosures | autonomy concerns; backlog |
| Social audit (MGNREGA lineage) | community verification of spends | GPS-photo + panchayat sabha | capacity gaps in newer schemes |
| Citizen charters · Sevottam | service standards + redress | RTO, passport, panchayat services | compensation clauses often non-justiciable |
| GeM (2016 onward) | online procurement marketplace | govt tenders | bid-rigging patterns in commodity categories |

## GS-4 case archetype table (memorise)
1. **Illegal orders** → written-order ask → escalate → document → dissent-file → last-resort exit.
2. **Family interest** → recuse + disclose + independent committee + file-note.
3. **Service triage** (medical resources) → publish ex-ante criteria → real-time dashboard → appeal channel → equity-audit post-event.
4. **Clearance vs rights** (environment) → verify FRA consent + cumulative assessment + litigation-risk record → process-integrity decision.
5. **Whistleblower** → hotline + protected disclosure + interim-freeze + audit-engagement; no premature media leak.
6. **Grievance flooding** → triage queues, oldest-first discipline, zero-backlog days, SLA scorecards.
