COI & rules

  • Disclosures in procurement, gift/foreign-hospitality norms, post-retirement one-year cooling for relevant joins, private-trade bans, political-neutrality expectations → Nolan-7 plus local flavour: 2nd ARC's conflict-of-interest contract clauses.

Corruption metrics (bracket-check at exam)

  • TI CPI 2024: India ~93rd/180, score 38.
  • DBT cumulative savings claim: ₹2.7+ lakh crore (2014–2023).
  • WGI control-of-corruption percentile mid-band improvements.

Service-quality anchors

  • CPGRAMS: 30+ lakh grievances/yr run-rate; Sevottam certification stacks.
  • MP Lok Sewa Guarantee Act (first legal guarantees; many states followed — ODisha, Karnataka Sakala, Bihar etc.).
  • Faceless assessment (IT) as corruption-reduction vignette.

Fund-integrity mechanisms

  • GFR 2017 (rules) + GeM platform + CPPP e-tendering.
  • Integrity Pacts + Independent External Monitors (IEM).
  • CAG role recall: constitutional; PAC thread; "watchdog vs bloodhound" restatement.
  • DBT stack: Aadhaar-PMJDY-Mobile trinity; cumulative savings figure as quality anchor.

Contemporary anchors (2024–26)

  • RTI 20 years (Oct 2025) — autonomy drift critique.
  • Electoral Bonds struck down (Feb 2024) — funding-transparency imperative persists.
  • ED conviction math debate (deepen process-record).
  • CEC Appointments Act 2023 vs Anoop Baranwal — separation-of-power conversation.

GS-4 case archetype table (memorise)

  1. Illegal orders → written-order ask → escalate → document → dissent-file → last-resort exit.
  2. Family interest → recuse + disclose + independent committee + file-note.
  3. Service triage (medical resources) → publish ex-ante criteria → real-time dashboard → appeal channel → equity-audit post-event.
  4. Clearance vs rights (environment) → verify FRA consent + cumulative assessment + litigation-risk record → process-integrity decision.
  5. Whistleblower → hotline + protected disclosure + interim-freeze + audit-engagement; no premature media leak.
  6. Grievance flooding → triage queues, oldest-first discipline, zero-backlog days, SLA scorecards.

Persuasive one-liners

  • "Probity is not the absence of shortcuts; it is the presence of auditability."
  • "Charters without compensation clauses are etiquette lists, not rights."
  • "Transparency must be understandable, or it becomes atmospheric noise." (RTI Section 4 framing)
