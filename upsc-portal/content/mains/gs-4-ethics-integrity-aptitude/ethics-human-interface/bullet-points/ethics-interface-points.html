Ethics and Human Interface — Bullet Points

60-second revision card.

ASCII mindmap

ETHICS & HUMAN INTERFACE
├── Essence ......... how humans ought to act; ≥ law, ≥ custom
├── Dimensions ...... meta · normative (consq/deont/virtue) · applied
├── Determinants .... individual · situational · systemic
├── Consequences .... micro (character) → meso (institution)
│                    → macro (State legitimacy)
└── Private vs Public ... two grammars; recusal where they collide

Core distinctions

  • Law = minimum & enforceable; Ethics = optimum & self-binding.
  • Consequentialism judges outcomes; deontology judges maxims; virtue ethics judges character.
  • Milgram lesson: bad situations out-produce bad people — design situations.
  • Constitutional morality (Ambedkar) anchors Indian public ethics above social morality.

Determinants cheat row

  • Individual: conscience, upbringing, self-interest.
  • Situational: peers, incentives, hierarchy, ambiguity.
  • Systemic: law, audits, norms, traditions.
  • Engineering line: e-auctions, GPS-PDS, conflict registers — make integrity easy.

Private vs public conflicts

  • Nepotism, favoritism, conflict of interest = private grammar invading public file.
  • Remedies: declaration → recusal → conduct rules → audit trail.
  • Punch line: "Affection is honoured by keeping it out of the file."

Golden phrase bank

  • "Ethics is law's elder sibling — it begins where the rule-book ends."
  • "Good people in bad designs produce bad outcomes."
  • "The public remembers one unfair order longer than a hundred fair ones."

Pitfall flags

  • Don't define ethics as religion; it includes the critique of custom.
  • Never present the dimensions as rivals only — applied ethics uses all three lenses.
  • Always add the design implication, not just the definition.
