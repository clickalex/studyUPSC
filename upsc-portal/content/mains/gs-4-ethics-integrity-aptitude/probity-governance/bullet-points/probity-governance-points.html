Probity in Governance — Bullet Points

60-second revision card.

Precision points (years and functions)

  • RTI 2005 — citizen's right to information; PIO timelines (30 days / 48h life-liberty).
  • Lokpal 2013 — apex ombudsman; Lokayuktas in states.
  • CVC statutory 2003 — central vigilance oversight.
  • Whistle Blowers Protection 2014 — disclosure channels (full operationalisation pending).
  • SEVOTTAM 2009 — charter + grievance + capability audit standard.
  • Rule 9 CCS (Pension) — post-retirement acceptance norms / cool-off doctrine.

Code of conduct vs code of ethics

  • Conduct = floor: dos/don'ts, gifts, conflicts, political activity limits.
  • Ethics = ceiling: values that guide where rules are silent.
  • Nolan 7: selflessness, integrity, objectivity, accountability, openness, honesty, leadership.

Funds and work culture

  • March-rush spending and idle balances = probity failure of planning.
  • Service quality = charter standards + grievance closure + capability (SEVOTTAM trio).
  • Social audit (MGNREGA §17) = citizen's eye institutionalised.

Golden phrase bank

  • "Probity is integrity with an audit trail."
  • "Sunlight is the best disinfectant; design is the best architecture."
  • "Corruption is monopoly plus discretion minus accountability."

Pitfall flags

  • Don't cite wrong years (RTI 2005, not 2004; Lokpal 2013, not 2011).
  • Don't treat e-governance as sufficient — pair technology with accountability.
