GS Paper IV — Practice Bullet Points

Compressed examiner-psychology for GS-4.

ASCII mindmap

GS-4 PRACTICE MACHINE
├── THEORY ....... define → dimensions → nuance → officer line (7-8 min)
├── CASE ......... annotate 3 min → 7-step memo → safeguards 2 min
├── ARMOURY ...... rule-situation pairs · officer one-liners · concept hooks
├── VOICE ........ first person, lawful floor, humane tone, recorded steps
└── LOOP ......... 2 theory + 1 case weekly · sectional Fri · rewrite Sun

Rewards

  • Visible structure — headings examiners can tick.
  • Decisions with sequence and documentation, not sermons.
  • The safeguards band — where most answers end early and lose marks.
  • Honest engagement with "wrong but understandable" options.

Leaks

  • Ethical vocabulary dumped without application.
  • Cases solved with lectures to society instead of personal action.
  • Theory answers without a single administrative example.
  • Overlong theory answers starving case time.

Drills

  • Case-annotation sprint: mark actors/verbs/constraints on 3 cases in 9 minutes.
  • Definition blitz: 20 GS-4 terms, one-line each, weekly.
  • Safeguard sentence bank: 15 institutional endings, memorised.

Golden phrase bank

  • "GS-4 grades your decision, not your devotion."
  • "Lawful, humane, recorded — the three-word answer design."
