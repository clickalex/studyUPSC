GS Paper IV — Practice Quick Card

One-page drill card for theory + case genres.

Clock

  • Theory: 7-8 min each · Case: 16-18 min (3 min annotating, 2 min reserved for safeguards).

Theory shape (150-200 words)

  • Definition → 2-3 dimensions with micro-examples → boundary nuance → officer-relevance line.

Case shape (7 headings)

  • Facts (+verify) → Dilemma → Stakeholders → Options ≥3 → Evaluation (legality·public interest·proportionality·precedent·conscience) → Decision+steps → Safeguards.

Example armoury — refill

  • Rules↔situations (conduct rules, RTI, manuals) · officer one-liners (Seshan, Sreedharan) · concept hooks (talisman, difference principle, Kautilyan audit).

Rubrics

  • Theory /10: definition 2 · depth 4 · examples 2 · close 2. Case /20: structure 4 · honesty 4 · tests 4 · decision-detail 5 · safeguards 3.

Reminders

  • First-person decisions. Law first, compassion on top, everything recorded.
  • No heroics as first moves — file → superior → institution → (last) exit.
