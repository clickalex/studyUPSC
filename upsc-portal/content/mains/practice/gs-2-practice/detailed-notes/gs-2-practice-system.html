GS Paper II — Practice System

Governance, constitution, polity, social justice and international relations — converting the pyqs/ question bank into marks.

1. What GS-2 Demands

GS-2 is the judgment paper: it asks you to take positions on constitutional design ("critically examine"), institutional performance ("assess"), and diplomacy ("discuss the significance"). It rewards authority-citation (Articles, cases, committees, Acts, indices) more than any other paper — every answer should carry at least one named anchor.

2. The Authority Bank (build before practising)

  • Constitutional: Articles (14, 21, 32, 226, 243, 356, 368…), landmark cases (Kesavananda, Maneka, Puttaswamy, SR Bommai, Vishaka).
  • Institutional: CAG, ECI, UPSC, Finance Commission, NHRC, NCW — one line each on mandate + one recent controversy.
  • Committees/Commissions: Punchhi, Sarkaria, ARC (2nd), Rajamannar — for federalism and governance answers.
  • Acts/policies: RTI, RPA 1951, DPDP 2023, NFSA, MGNREGA — with year.
  • Indices/data: World Governance Indicators, press-freedom index, NFHS-5, ASER — for social-justice answers.
  • IR anchors: neighbourhood-first doctrine lines, BIMSTEC/SCO/QUAD mandates, UN reform positions, treaty names.

3. Answer Anatomy for GS-2

  • Intro (2-3 lines): constitutional/statutory context — "Article X provides…" or case citation.
  • Body: mandate → performance (both records) → issues (structural, political) → comparisons (committees, best practices, other democracies).
  • Conclusion: reform direction with a committee recommendation or a balanced way-forward. GS-2 conclusions may propose — this is the proposing paper.

4. Judgment Phrases That Signal Maturity

  • "The provision is constitutionally sound; its implementation suffers from…" (separates design from delivery).
  • "Three objections are usually raised; the first and third survive scrutiny…"
  • "The committee's remedy addresses the symptom; the deeper leak is…"

5. The Weekly Cycle

  • Mon: polity/constitution (2 answers) · Tue: governance & social justice (2) · Wed: IR (2) · Thu: comparison-of-democracies + RPA/election answers (2) · Fri: full GS-2 sectional (10 Q) · Sat: evaluation + authority-bank update · Sun: rewrite two weakest.

6. Rubric /10

CriterionMarks

7. Pointers

  • IR answers: pair bilateral substance with structural context (region, doctrine, economics); avoid news-recap tone.
  • Social justice: institutional mechanism + beneficiary outcome + one datum, every time.
  • Never moralise where you can analyse; never analyse where the question says "suggest."
