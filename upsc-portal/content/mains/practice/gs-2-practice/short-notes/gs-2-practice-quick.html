GS Paper II — Practice Quick Card

One-page drill card for the judgment paper.

Timing

  • Same clock as GS-1: 7/11 minutes; but GS-2 outlines are 20 seconds longer — always draft the conclusion line first.

Anatomy

  • Intro: Article/case/Act context → Body: mandate → performance (both) → issues (structural) → comparison → Conclusion: sourced reform proposal.

Authority deposit — withdraw daily

  • Articles: 14 · 21 · 32 · 226 · 243 · 356 · 368 · Cases: Kesavananda · Maneka · Puttaswamy · Bommai · Vishaka.
  • Committees: Punchhi · Sarkaria · 2nd ARC · Indices: NFHS-5 · ASER · WGI · IR: BIMSTEC · QUAD · SCO mandates.

Rubric /10

  • Position 2 · Authority 3 · Balance 3 · Reform 2.

Judgment signals

  • "Design is sound; delivery leaks." · "Objection one survives, objection three does not." · "The committee treats the symptom; the leak is…"

Weekly rhythm

  • M polity · T governance/SJ · W IR · Th democracies+elections · F sectional · Sa evaluate+update bank · Su rewrite two.

Paper-specific reminders

  • IR: substance + structure (doctrine/region/economics), never a news summary.
  • Every answer needs one named anchor minimum — no-anchor answers drift to generic.
