GS Paper III — Practice Bullet Points

Compressed examiner-psychology for GS-3.

ASCII mindmap

GS-3 SOLUTION MACHINE
├── FRAME ........ magnitude datum ("about X%")
├── ANALYSE ...... structural causes; distribution of impacts
├── PRESCRIBE .... agent + incentive + constraint + sequence
├── DRAW ......... flow/graph/ring — labelled, value-adding
└── LINK ......... national mission / SDG, without slogans

Rewards

  • Mechanism answers ("PMO-to-panchayat: who does what, funded how") over principle lists.
  • Honest approximation of data; wrong precision is penalised.
  • Diagrams that compress argument, not decoration.
  • Distributional eye — who gains, who pays, who is exposed.

Leaks

  • Scheme-name showers without mechanism.
  • Security answers with tactical gossip instead of strategy.
  • Sci-tech answers that never explain the underlying science.
  • Environment answers without the finance/justice dimension.

Practice drills

  • Mechanism drill: take any headline; write agent/incentive/constraint in 5 lines.
  • Diagram drill: 6 pre-rehearsed shapes drawn in 60 seconds each, weekly.
  • Data drill: recite the data magazine against the clock, Sunday.

Golden phrase bank

  • "GS-3 buys mechanisms, not wishes."
  • "A labelled arrow is worth a paragraph of prose."
