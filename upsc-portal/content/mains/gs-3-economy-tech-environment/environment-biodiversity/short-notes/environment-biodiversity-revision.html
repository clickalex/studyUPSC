Environment & Biodiversity — Revision Notes

Numbers-first card; species+convention pairings are prelims gold.

Legal spine

  • EPA 1986 (umbrella) · WPA 1972 · F(Conservation)A 1980 + 2023 Amdt · Water Act 1974 (CPCB) · Air Act 1981 · Biodiversity Act 2002 (+ 2023 Amdt) · NGT 2010.
  • Constitutional: Art.21 (MC Mehta line) · 48A · 51A(g).

Protected areas

  • 57+ Tiger Reserves (latest: Veerangana Durgavati, MP 2023) — Project Tiger 1973.
  • Tiger count 2022: mean 3,682 (band 3,925).
  • Project Elephant 1992; elephant census (synchronised) rolling 2023–25 → ~27–30k estimate band.
  • Cheetah: Namibia batch Sep 2022 + SA batch 2023 → Kuno NP.
  • Ramsar sites 80+ (2025 band) — count rises around 2 February each year; use the latest notified figure.
  • Snow leopard first national estimate ~718 (2024).
  • IBCA (International Big Cat Alliance) operationalised 2023→HQ 2024.

Pollution programmes

  • NCAP 2019 → 40% PM reduction by 2026.
  • EPR fires across plastics (2022), e-waste (2022), batteries, tyres; single-use plastics ban July 2022 (19 items).
  • CAQM (Commission for Air Quality Management, 2021) runs Delhi-NCR GRAP.
  • Namami Gange 2015 → NMCG authority status 2016.
  • Great Nicobar project = environment-vs-strategy flashpoint (transhipment + 130 km2 city).

Climate stack

  • India NDC: net-zero 2070; 2030 markers: 500 GW non-fossil · 50% RE capacity share · 45% EI cut · 1 bn tn CO2.
  • Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (2023); PAT legacy.
  • 8 NAPCC missions + LiFE (2022).
  • HAPs 23+ states; Mission Mausam (₹2,000 cr) for forecasting.

Global beats

  • Paris 2015 (NDCs, ratchet, GST cycles).
  • COP26 Glasgow (methane, coal "phase-down") · COP27 Sharm 2022 (Loss & Damage Fund) · COP28 Dubai 2023 (first GST: "transition away" language) · COP29 Baku 2024 ($300 bn NCQG vs $1.3 tn Global South ask; India dissented) · COP30 Belém Nov 2025 (avoid fixed outcome notes; Brazil + TFFF agenda) · COP31 host contests (2026, Australia/Türkiye tussle).
  • K-M GBF (2022): 30×30; DSI debate → Cali Fund 2024.
  • IPBES assessments; WWF Living Planet 2024: −73% (1970–2020).

Landmark judgments

  • MC Mehta series; Godavarman mandamus (forests).
  • M K Ranjitsinh (2024) — right against climate change (Arts 14/21) — beautiful GS-3/GS-4 bridge answer opener.
  • Aravalli: SC 2024 unified definition push (FRI criteria debate).

Prelims quick pairs

  • IBCA ↔ big cats 7 | Kuno ↔ cheetah | Chilika ↔ Asia's largest lagoon Ramsar #1 | Keibul Lamjao ↔ Loktak floating park.
  • GBF ↔ Montreal 2022 (not a COP for climate).
  • Ramsar 1971 Iran city; Sites added Feb 2 annually.
  • Montreux Record = threatened Ramsar sites list (Keoladeo 1990s narrative).
