Science & Technology — Revision Notes

Mission-wise cheat sheet; recent-years missions weighted highest.

Missions at a glance

  • Chandrayaan-3 (2023): soft-landed 23 Aug 2023 south-polar region (Shiv Shakti Point); propulsion module return tech demonstrated 2024.
  • Aditya-L1 (2023): first Indian solar observatory at L1 halo orbit.
  • Gaganyaan: TV-D1 (Oct 2023) pad abort+crew escape success; crewed flight pipeline after G1/G2.
  • SpaDeX (Dec 2024): docking tech demo — Jan 2025 docking & undocking (autonomous docking milestone for Bharatiya Antariksh Station, target ~2035).
  • NISAR (launched July 2025): ISRO-NASA dual-frequency SAR for disaster/agri/climate mapping.
  • National missions: Quantum (2023, ₹6,003 cr) · IndiaAI (2024, ₹10,371 cr) · ANRF (2023, ₹50,000 cr/5yr) · Semiconductor ISM (₹76,000 cr) · Green Hydrogen (₹19,744 cr).

Launch-pad quick facts

  • PSLV: 4-stage (solid-liquid-solid-liquid), workhorse; SSLV: 3+ liquid-trim, on-demand; LVM3: heaviest (CE-20 cryo + C25).
  • Private: Skyroot Vikram-S (2022 first private rocket), Agnikul Agnibaan SorTeD (2024 3D-printed semi-cryo engine).
  • Space FDI 2024: auto route up to 100% components/systems; capped routes for launchers, satellites.
  • IN-SPACe: regulator/facilitator; NSIL commercial arm; Indian Space Policy 2023.

NavIC vs GPS

  • NavIC 7 sats (GEO + GSO), L5/S bands, added L1 civil; mandatory chip enablement in selected devices (since ~2020) — advantages: regional accuracy + civilian-restricted split. GPS = 24+ global MEO.

Nuclear stack

  • 3-stage (Bhabha): PHWR (natural U) → FBR (PFBR Kalpakkam 500 MWe in commissioning runway) → thorium AHWR-300 design.
  • 2025 Budget opened fleet-mode PHWR + Bharat SMR idea (220 MWe) + private entry paved by SHANTI-style bill Dec 2025 (provisions evolving — check latest before the exam).
  • Fusion: ITER partner; IPR SST-1/2 tokamaks.

IT/AI quick grid

  • IndiaAI Mission pillars: compute (GPU pooled), datasets (AIKosh), skilling, application solutions, startup funding, safe & trustworthy AI → AI Safety Institute (2025).
  • Models: AI4Bharat IndicTrans2; BharatGen (IIT-B 2024); private: Krutrim, Sarvam.
  • DPI exports: UPI links (SG/UAE/FR), MOSIP (Philippines ID), Bhashini language AI stack; DPDP Act 2023 as privacy frame.

Quantum facts

  • NQM 2023: 4 verticals (compute/comm/sensing/materials), T-Hubs at IISc + IITs; QKD trials with army/BSNL; QNu Labs deployments.

Biotech

  • Genome India (10k genomes wave-1 reported 2024–25); One Health Mission 2024; BioE3 policy 2024 (smart proteins, marine biotech, climate-agriculture); BIRAC startup ramp (~6k+).

Defence S&T

  • Mission Divyastra Mar 2024 = MIRVed Agni-V (5,000+ km, multiple warheads).
  • Akash exports (Armenia); QRSAM; Project Kusha LR-SAM.
  • Tejas Mk1A deliveries (F404 engines); AMCA Phase-1 funds (₹15,000 cr 2025); CATS warrior loyal wingman.
  • Navy: INS Vikrant (2022), INS Arighaat SSBN (Aug 2024), P-75(I) AIP subs.
  • Mission Shakti (March 2019) ASAT test at ~300 km LEO.
  • Hypersonics: HSTDV 2020; DURGA directed-energy family.

Repeatable framers for Mains

  • Tech sovereignty framing: cryo engine story (embargo → indigenous CE-7.5/CE-20) → chip design (Shakti/Vega) → chip mfg (ISM Tata Micron Kaynes plants 2024+).
  • DPI = India's "digital soft power" (UPI + ONDC + Bhashini + MOSIP).
  • Dual-use ethics line: MIRV + ASAT + AI → needs responsible-innovation frame (GS-IV bridge).
