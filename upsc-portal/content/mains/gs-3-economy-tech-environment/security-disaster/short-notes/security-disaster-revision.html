Security & Disaster — Revision Notes

Grid-style recall; combine one "architecture" line + one "trend" line + one "case" line per answer.

Architecture spine

  • Public Order + Police = STATE subjects (List II); Union keeps armed-forces-aid (Entry 2A), central forces, IB/NIA/NATGRID/NCORD.
  • CAPFs: BSF · CRPF · CISF · ITBP · SSB · Assam Rifles (dual control).
  • Prakash Singh 2006 — 7 directives; least-implemented = separation of investigation; Model Police Act 2015 draft outstanding.
  • NIA Act 2008 (+2019 amendment).

Theatres in one line each

  • LWE: Naxalbari 1967; contraction claim: from ~200 districts to ~38; govt deadline "liberate by 31 Mar 2026"; Bastar ops 2024–25 neutralised several senior commanders (verify names against latest MHA briefings); strategy = security + development + connectivity (5G/fibre in Dandakaranya).
  • Northeast: accords beat — Bodo 2020 · Bru-Reang 2020 · Karbi 2021 · ULFA pro-talks 2023; Manipur 2023 → President's Rule Feb 2025 (Meitei-Kuki) — Myanmar civil-war spillover + FMR suspension Dec 2024.
  • Kashmir: Art 370 dilution (Aug 2019) → UT + delimitation 2022 → Assembly elections (2024 NC government) → Pahalgam (Apr 2025) → Op Sindoor (May 2025) → doctrine: terror attacks = "acts of war" + IWT abeyance.
  • Narco: Golden Crescent sea route → Gujarat coast interceptions (2024–25 meth mega-busts); Golden Triangle via Myanmar-Manipur; NCORD + MANAS + JK/Delhi nexus studies.
  • Organised: drone drops (BSF anti-UAS procurement), crypto-hawala channels, wildlife corridors (Siliguri).

Border grid

  • Pakistan (3,323 km) BSF — drones, infiltration, post-Sindoor posture.
  • China (3,488 km) ITBP+Army — buffer zones post-Galwan; Oct 2024 patrolling resumption; Vibrant Villages II (2025).
  • Nepal (1,751 km) SSB — open border misuse; territorial Kalapani.
  • Bangladesh (4,096 km) BSF — post-Hasina tensions (Aug 2024); fencing-gaps; smuggling.
  • Myanmar (1,643 km) Assam Rifles — FMR suspension Dec 2024; fencing push + ethnic resistance.
  • Coastal: NC3I/Navy; radar chain Ph-I+II; ICG expansion; Sagar Kavach.
  • Infra: BRO record capex; Atal Tunnel (2020) · Sela Tunnel (2024) · Shinku La runway-…

Cyber & new domains

  • CERT-In (MeitY) · NCIIPC (critical info infra) · I4C (MHA) · NCS Coordinator (PMO).
  • DPDP 2023 half-architecture; cyber fraud reporting via I4C; advisories on AI-synthetic frauds / deepfakes (2023–25).

Disaster management

  • DM Act 2005 → NDMA(PM)/SDMA(CM)/DDMA(DM) + NDRF 16 Bns + SDRF rules.
  • NDMP 2016 — Sendai-aligned; 4 priorities: understand risk · governance · resilience investment · build back better.
  • 15th FC: ~₹1.6 lakh crore window 2021–26 for disaster funds.
  • Hazard cases: Chamoli 2021 · Joshimath 2023 · Sikkim GLOF Oct 2023 · Wayanad landslide Jul 2024 · Chennai floods (2015/2023) · Biparjoy/Mocha/Remal cyclones.
  • Tools: Mission Mausam (₹2k cr, 2024); Doppler grid ~100%; Aapda Mitra; IRS trainings; parametric insurance pilots.

Reusable quotes/stats

  • "The next civil war in India will be over water" — attributed to multiple; cite cautiously.
  • INSAS/BA/defence-psu quips — avoid; use verified stats only.
  • FATF India on "regular follow-up" (2024 ME).
