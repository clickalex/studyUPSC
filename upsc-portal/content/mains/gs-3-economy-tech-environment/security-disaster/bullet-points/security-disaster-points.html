Answer-builders (three-line formulae)

  • LWE: "Contraction claim + residual Bastar core + convergence package = current strategy."
  • Kashmir: "Constitutional reset ('19) → electoral return ('24) → doctrinal escalation ('25)."
  • NE: "Accord math (Bodo+Karbi+ULFA) minus Manipur ethnic conflict = incomplete peace."
  • Border: per-border = force × primary threat × tech-fix (CIBMS/smart fencing/VVP).
  • DM: "Framework capable on paper (DM Act+NDMP+Sendai) — deficit is last-mile execution (DDMA quality + hazard-specific early warning)."

Numbers to seed

  • 57+ Tiger reserves; 16 NDRF battalions; 3,323/3,488/4,096/1,643/1,751/699 border lengths.
  • ₹1.6 lakh crore FC-15 disaster window (2021–26).
  • GoM on police reform report (2024 draft) — highlighted in exam answers as pending-modernisation evidence.
  • Wayanad landslide ~300 deaths (Jul 2024); Sikkim GLOF +100 (Oct 2023).
  • BSF anti-drone units purchased post-2021 surge.

Counter-points for "critically examine"

  • LWE reduction vs surrenders inflation debate.
  • Statehood demand and Op Sindoor arms-length optics.
  • Coastal radar coverage vs small unflagged vessel blind spots.
  • HAPs present but heat-relief fiscal support absent (labour protection deficit).
  • FMR suspension: national security choice vs cross-border ethnic ties (Mizoram objection).
