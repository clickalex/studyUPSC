# Indian Economy — Short Revision Notes

## One-liner
India's economy — ~**4th-largest ($4.2 tn)** — moved from the **planning era (1947–91)** through the **1991 LPG reforms** into today's **mission-mode formalisation** (GST, PLI, DPI/UPI), with an inflation-targeting RBI alongside a capex-led fiscal strategy.

## Policy eras (one line each)
| Era | Signal |
|---|---|
| 1947–67 | Planning Commission + public sector (IPR 1956, Mahalanobis model) |
| 1967–91 | Bank nationalisation (1969/80), Green Revolution, MRTP licence raj |
| **1991** | **LPG reforms — delicensing, BoP-crisis-triggered** |
| 2003–2014 | FRBM (2003), VAT (2005), UIDAI (2009), MGNREGA (2005) |
| 2014– | Demonetisation (2016), IBC (2016), **GST (2017)**, PLI (2020), Gati Shakti (2021), **GST 2.0 (Sep 2025)** |

## Macro numbers to quote
- GDP ~**$4.2 tn** (crossed Japan, ~2025; next: Germany).
- Growth: **FY24 ~8%+; FY25 ~6.5%; FY26 ~6.5% band** projected.
- Structure (GVA): services ~55% · industry ~27–28% · **agriculture ~17% but 46% jobs**.
- Forex reserves **$650–700 bn** band; CAD modest ≤1.5% of GDP; rupee high-80s/USD late-2025 amid tariffs.
- External debt ~19% of GDP (comfortable).

## Monetary & banking
- **RBI (1934)**; **MPC since 2016** — target **4% ± 2% CPI, retained to 2031**.
- Corridor: **SDF (floor) | repo | MSF (ceiling)**.
- Banking: 1969 + 1980 nationalisations → **2020 mergers → 12 PSBs**; **IBC (2016)**; NARCL bad-bank (2021); PCA.
- **UPI** — world's largest real-time payments (1,500+ crore transactions/month; linked internationally).

## Fiscal
- **FRBM (2003)**; debt-GDP anchor post-2024; **FY26 FD budgeted ~4.4%**, capex ~3% of GDP.
- **GST: 1 Jul 2017 (101st Amdt) → GST 2.0 (22 Sep 2025: 5% + 18% + 40% demerit)**.
- **16th Finance Commission (Panagariya) covers 2026–31** devolution.

## Manufacturing & infra
- **PLI — 14 sectors (2020–21)**; semicon fabs (Dholera, Sanand OSATs).
- **Gati Shakti (2021)** + **National Logistics Policy (2022)** + Bharatmala.
- **National Green Hydrogen Mission (2023)**; renewables ~220+ GW.

## Inclusion
- **PM Jan Dhan (2014) — 56 cr+ accounts**; PM-MUDRA; e-Shram (29 cr+).
- NITI MPI: **24.85% → 14.96%** (2015-16 → 2019-21).
