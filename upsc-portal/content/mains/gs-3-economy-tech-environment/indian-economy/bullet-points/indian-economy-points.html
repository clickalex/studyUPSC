Bullet facts (prelims gold)

  • Planning Commission — set up 25 March 1950 (Cabinet resolution); PM = ex-officio chairman; D. R. Gadgil framed early plan frameworks.
  • NITI Aayog — 1 January 2015; PM chair, VC + CEO-led; Governing Council = PM + CMs + LGs.
  • 1991 reforms — PM P. V. Narasimha Rao + Finance Minister Manmohan Singh; BoP reserves crisis (2–3 weeks of imports); gold pledged (Bank of England) July 1991.
  • LPG tenets — de-licensing (most industries), trade liberalisation, FDI norms, PSU disinvestment began.
  • FERA 1973 → FEMA 1999.
  • SEBI — statutory in 1992.
  • TRAI — 1997; Competition Act 2002 (CCI functional 2009).
  • FRBM Act 2003 — fiscal-deficit & revenue-deficit targets; NK Singh (2017) review.
  • Budget presented 1 February since 2017 (Railway Budget merged 2017 & no separate plan/non-plan after FY18).
  • Monetary Policy Committee (2016) — 6 members; CPI 4% ± 2% target — framework retained to 2031.
  • Liquidity corridor — SDF (floor) · Repo · MSF (ceiling); LAF + OMOs + VRR/VRRR fine-tuning.
  • Bank nationalisation — 19 July 1969 (14 banks), 1980 (6 more); 2020 mergers → 12 PSBs; SBI merger with associates 2017.
  • IBC 2016 + NARCL (bad bank, 2021-22) → NCLT resolutions.
  • UPI — NPCI (2016 launch on a 2012 institution); Rs-trillion-scaled monthly; largest real-time payment system by volume.
  • Jan Dhan (2014) — 56 cr+ accounts; Rupay (2012).
  • GST — Article 279A Council; rates: 0/5/12/18/28% → GST 2.0 (Sep 2025) main slabs 5% & 18% + 40% demerit; benefits to consumption (festival-window timing).
  • GDP vs GVA — GDP = GVA + taxes − subsidies.
  • Per-capita ND ~$2.6–2.9k (mid-2020s nominal) — the middle-income-trap debate.
  • Trade: merchandise ~$430–450 bn, services ~$380 bn (FY25 scale); exports total ~$820 bn; remittances ~$120 bn.
  • PLI — 14 sectors; electronics assembled in India moved onto the export tables.
  • Semiconductor Mission: Dholera fab (Tata-PSMC), Sanand (Micron ATMP, CG OSAT) — 2023-24 announcements.
  • National Logistics Policy (2022) — target logistics cost 8% of GDP.
  • NARCL & IBC outcome: GNPA of banks fell to multi-year lows in mid-2020s.
  • e-Shram (2021) — 29 cr+ unorganised workers; four Labour Codes (2019–20) pending full implementation (phased rollout continuing).
  • CBDC e-Rupee — retail pilot December 2022.
  • IFSCA (2020) — unified regulator for GIFT City.
