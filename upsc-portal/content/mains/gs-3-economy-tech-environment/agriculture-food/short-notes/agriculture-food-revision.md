# Agriculture & Food Security — Short Revision Notes

## One-liner
Agriculture feeds 1.45 billion from farms averaging **~1.1 ha (86% small/marginal)** — its policy spine is **MSP + procurement + NFSA**, and its water spine is **groundwater (≈89% of irrigation) under stress**.

## Anchors (numbers to quote in any answer)
- GVA share ~**16–17%**; employment share ~**46%**.
- Average holding **~1.08 ha**; small & marginal ≈ **86%**.
- Largest exporter of **rice**; biggest producer of **milk** (~230+ mt).
- Import dependence: **edible oils ~55–60%**, pulses significant.
- Buffer godowns (FCI) vs buffer norms — monthly points (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct).

## MSP & procurement
- Recommended by **CACP**; approved by Cabinet.
- **23 crops**; formula since 2018-19: **MSP ≥ 1.5 × (A2 + FL)** (farmers demand the **C2** base and a statutory guarantee).
- Procurement concentrated: **Punjab/Haryana/West UP** (rice-wheat); PM-AASHA for other crops (PSS, PSF, PDPS).

## Farm laws circa 2020
3 Acts (trade-&-commerce outside APMC / contract farming / Essential Commodities stock limits) → year-long protest → **Repeal Act (November 2021)** → afterlife: e-NAM (1,360+ mandis), FPO-10,000 scheme (2020), Agri-Infrastructure Fund (₹1 lakh cr).

## Water & soil
- **PMKSY (2015)** — Har Khet Ko Pani + Per Drop More Crop (micro-irrigation).
- **Atal Bhujal Yojana (2020)** — community groundwater management (7 states).
- **Ken–Betwa river link** — foundation laid Dec 2024; Bundelkhand.
- **Free power ↔ groundwater decline** debate (Punjab).
- **Nano-urea / nano-DAP** — fertilizer reform push (₹1 lakh cr scale subsidy).

## Insurance & credit
- **PMFBY (2016)**: 2% kharif / 1.5% rabi / 5% horticulture premiums.
- **KCC (1998)** — crop/animal credit at concessional rates; saturation drives for fisheries & dairy.

## Allied sectors
- **Operation Flood (1970-96 — Kurien/NDDB)** → world's largest milk producer.
- **Operation Greens (2018)** — TOP → TOTAL (price stabilisation).
- **PMFME (2020)** food-processing micro-enterprises.
- **PMMSY (2020)** fisheries; **Rashtriya Gokul** breeds.
- **National Mission on Edible Oils–Oil Palm (2021)** (the ecology critique).
- **IYM 2023** millets push — millets inside MSP.

## Food security spine
- **NFSA 2013** — 75%/50% rural/urban; 5 kg/person/month at ₹3/2/1; Antyodaya 35 kg/family.
- **PMGKAY** — free NFSA grains from Jan 2023 (announced 5 years from 2024).
- **One Nation One Ration Card** — portability.
- **WTO Bali peace clause (2013)** — the public-stockholding shield; permanent solution still open.

## 2025–26 hooks
- **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana** (100 districts' convergence).
- **Ethanol: E10 (Jun 2022) → E20 target scaling (2025-26)**.
- **VB-G RAM G's 60-day agri pause** — the employment-linkage interlock.
