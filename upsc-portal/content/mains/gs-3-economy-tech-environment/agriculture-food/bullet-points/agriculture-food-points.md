## Bullet facts (prelims gold)

- **CACP recommends; Cabinet (CCEA) decides** — MSP announced each kharif/rabi season.
- **Formula: MSP ≥ 1.5 × (A2+FL)** — 2018-19 — *A2 = paid-out costs; FL = imputed family labour*; farmers demand the **C2** (comprehensive cost) base and a law.
- **23 crops**: 14 kharif + 6 rabi + copra + toria + jute (raw).
- **Procurement geography**: Punjab & Haryana supply the lion's share of FCI rice/wheat.
- **PM-AASHA (2018)** — PSS (procurement) + PSF (price-stabilisation buffer) + PDPS (piloted price-deficiency payments).
- **Green Revolution (1966-)** — HYV wheat/rice; **Punjab–Haryana–western UP** heartland; costs: groundwater decline, soil health, monoculture.
- **Operation Flood (1970–96)** — **Verghese Kurien**; NDDB+Anand (Amul) model.
- **Operation Greens (2018)** — TOP (Tomato/Onion/Potato) → TOTAL.
- **e-NAM (2016)** — online mandi network (1,360+ integrated by mid-2020s).
- **APMC reform** — the 2020 farm laws tried bypass; repealed Nov 2021.
- **Agri Infrastructure Fund (₹1 lakh crore, 2020)** — FPO/PACS-level storage, sorting.
- **FPO-10,000 scheme (2020)**.
- **PMKSY (2015)** — "Har Khet Ko Pani" + micro-irrigation "Per Drop More Crop".
- **Atal Bhujal Yojana (2020)** — community groundwater management (Punjab, Haryana, Rajasthan, Gujarat, MP, UP, Karnataka).
- **Ken–Betwa Project** — first river-link flagship; foundation **December 2024**.
- **PMFBY (2016)** — premium **2% kharif, 1.5% rabi, 5% horticulture**; satellite-based claim pilots.
- **KCC (1998)** — concessional agri-credit incl. animal husbandry & fisheries extension.
- **PM-KISAN (Feb 2019)** — **₹6,000/year/DBT** to ~11 crore farmer households.
- **Fertilizer** — urea imports + subsidy ~₹1 lakh cr scale; **Nano-urea (2021)** and nano-DAP; nutrient-imbalance debate (N:P:K skew).
- **NFSA (2013)** — **75% rural / 50% urban**; **5 kg/person/month** at ₹3/2/1; **Antyodaya 35 kg/family/month**; State Food Commissions.
- **PMGKAY & free NFSA grains** — from **January 2023** free for 5 years (announced).
- **ONORC (2019-)** — One Nation One Ration Card portability.
- **FCI** — est. **1965**; procurement + buffer + logistics; Shanta Kumar Committee (2015) reforms pending.
- **WTO** — **Bali 2013 peace clause** protects public-stockholding programmes pending permanent solution.
- **Millets** — **IYM 2023**; included in MSP basket (ragi/jowar/bajra).
- **Ethanol** — **E10 achieved (June 2022)**; E20 rollout target 2025-26; 2G bio-refinery (Panipat).
- **Oil palm mission (2021)** — NE & Andaman expansion; ecological critique.
- **PMMSY (2020)** — fisheries; India — #1 milk, #2 aquaculture producer.
- **Lumpy skin disease** — 2023–25 cattle outbreak — biosecurity example.
- **PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (budget 2025-26)** — 100 districts' scheme convergence.
