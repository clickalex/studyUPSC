Agriculture & Food Security: MSP, Irrigation, Marketing, Subsidies — Detailed Notes

Syllabus slot: GS 3 (Mains) · Agriculture & Food Security — MSP, irrigation, marketing, subsidies Also relevant for: Prelims GS Paper I — Economy & Geography; Essay (farmer welfare themes) Currency check: Updated to September 2026 (includes VB-G RAM G rural-livelihoods context & 2025–26 agricultural cycle).

1. Why agriculture matters for UPSC answers (anchors to cite)

  • Share of GVA: ~16–17% (FY25) — but employs ~46% of the workforce (PLFS) → the central structural mismatch: low productivity per worker.
  • Land fragmentation: average operational holding ~1.08 ha (Agriculture Census 2015-16); ~86% of holdings are small/marginal (<2 ha).
  • Population fed: ~1.45 billion with net foodgrains availability assured through the PDS + NFSA safety net.
  • Export footprint: among the world's largest exporters of rice (largest), spices, marine products, cotton, sugar; also imports pulses & edible oils heavily (the oil-import bill ~1.5 lakh crore scale).
  • Cropping seasons: Kharif (Jun–Oct, rain-fed: rice, cotton, pulses, oilseeds, sugarcane) · Rabi (Oct–Mar, irrigated: wheat, mustard, gram) · Zaid (summer: melons, fodder).
  • Staple stocks: FCI buffer norms vs actual — a healthy margin is the food-security pillar.

2. The Green Revolution & its afterlife

  • 1966–67: HYV wheat & rice imports (Mexico/IRRI lines) + irrigation + fertilizer + procurement support → Punjab–Haryana–western UP became the "bread basket"; Varanasi-to-Punjab yields multiplied.
  • Consequences: self-sufficiency in cereals by the 1970s–80s; regional & crop bias (rice-wheat); soil salinisation & waterlogging; groundwater decline (Punjab's water table fell alarmingly — the paddy-wheat cycle in a dry region); monoculture pest cycles.
  • "Second Green Revolution" themes: eastern India (Assam, Bihar, eastern UP, Odisha — the "green revolution east"), millets (International Year of Millets 2023 — India's UN initiative), natural/organic farming pilots, pulses & oilseeds missions, precision agriculture, horticulture already exceeding foodgrains in value.

3. MSP & procurement — the core exam topic

Mechanism

  • CACP (Commission for Agricultural Costs & Prices) recommends; Cabinet (CCEA) approves MSP announced each season (kharif/rabi).
  • 23 crops covered: 14 kharif (incl. paddy, jowar, bajra, maize, ragi, cotton, groundnut, soybean, sunflower, sesameum, nigerseed, moong, urad, tur/arhar + toria & de-husked coconut) and 6 rabi (wheat, barley, gram, masur, rapeseed-mustard, safflower) + copra + jute (raw).
  • Formula (since 2018-19): MSP ≥ 1.5 × (A2 + FL) (all paid-out costs + imputed family labour). The farmers' demand is the C2 formula (comprehensive cost incl. rent & interest) and statutory MSP.
  • Procurement: FCI + state agencies procure paddy/wheat in Punjab, Haryana, western UP, Chhattisgarh, Odisha, MP and now expanded regions; other crops' MSP is effectively "assurance without procurement" in most states (PM-AASHA umbrella: PSS (Price Support Scheme), PSF (Price Stabilisation Fund), PDPS (Price Deficiency Payment, piloted)).

Debate (Mains-frame)

  • For MSP-statute: income security, price certainty, hedging market volatility.
  • Against: fiscally heavy, distorting crop choice (rice in the dry belt), WTO disputes (public-stockholding), crowding out private storage/marketing innovations, and the fiscal arithmetic of universal procurement (~2 lakh crore+ subsidy load).

4. The 2020 farm laws episode (Mains essay fodder)

  • Three ordinances → Acts (Sept 2020): Farmers' Produce Trade & Commerce (Promotions & Facilitation) Act (bypass APMC mandis), Farmers (Empowerment & Protection) Agreement on Price Assurance & Farm Services Act (contract farming), Essential Commodities (Amendment) Act (deregulate stock limits on cereals/pulses except emergencies).
  • Agitation (Nov 2020 – Dec 2021), Supreme Court stay (Jan 2021; committee), repealed via Parliament in Nov 2021 (Farm Laws Repeal Act, 2021).
  • Afterlife debates: APMC reform continues at state level; e-NAM (2016) expansion; committee on MSP legalisation formed (2022) — announced but the statute demand stays open; ONDC/Agri e-commerce pilots.

5. Marketing & infrastructure

  • APMCs — ~7,000 regulated mandis; MSP procurement concentrated there; reform via model APLMC Act, private mandis, Farmer Producer Organisations (FPO 10,000 scheme (2020) — collectivised market power), e-NAM integrating mandis (1,360+ by mid-2020s).
  • Agriculture Infrastructure Fund (₹1 lakh crore, 2020) — credit for warehouses, sorting, cold chains at FPO/PACS level.
  • Operation Greens (2018) — TOP to TOTAL (tomato-onion-potato + all fruits/vegetables) — stabilising horticulture prices; PMFME (2020) — formalising micro food-processing; PMKSY food-processing wings; Mega Food Parks history.
  • Storage history: from 1960s P.L.480 / FCI to modern silos & steel silo concessions (Hub-and-Spoke model, PPP).

6. Irrigation & water

  • 96% of irrigation is groundwater (minor irrigation); canal share smaller but iconic (Indira Gandhi Canal oldest-legacy debate; Upper Ganga system).
  • PMKSY (2015) — "Har Khet Ko Pani" + watershed (IWMP) + "Per Drop More Crop" micro-irrigation (drip/sprinkler coverage ~90 lakh+ ha by mid-2020s).
  • Groundwater stress: India is the world's largest groundwater user; Punjab & Haryana are in the red (CGWB categorisation — "over-exploited" blocks); Atal Bhujal Yojana (2020) community-managed aquifer programme in 7 states; the energy-groundwater nexus (free power ↔ pumping).
  • Ken–Betwa river link project (foundation Dec 2024) — first National ILR project after the 2003 task force era; assures paddy/water to Bundelkhand.

8. Crop insurance & risk

  • PMFBY (Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana, 2016) — premiums 2% kharif/1.5% rabi/5% horticulture; claims via satellite & remote-sensing pilots; legacy issues (claim-payment delays; some states' opt-outs over fiscal burden).
  • Weather-based schemes (WBCIS) continued in pockets; LIVE-stock insurance expansion (NB: new 2025 umbrella on livestock risk).

9. Livestock, fisheries, horticulture, allieds

  • Dairy: Operation Flood (1970-1996 — Verghese Kurien; Anand model; NDDB) made India the world's largest milk producer (~230+ million tonnes scale by mid-2020s); Rashtriya Gokul Mission, breed improvements; the 2023–25 lumpy skin disease outbreak as the biosecurity wake-up call.
  • Fisheries: blue-economy push — PMMSY (2020); inland aquaculture in Andhra–WB; marine catch stagnation & the transition to mariculture; India's 2nd-largest aquaculture producer.
  • Horticulture: production > foodgrains (by value) for years; National Horticulture Mission (2006); APEDA export chain; GI tagging of special produce (Darjeeling tea 2004 — India's first GI); saffron from J&K revival.
  • Pulses & oilseeds: NFSM (food-security mission 2007-08) OAOP (oilseeds); import-dependence in oils (~55–60%) drives National Mission on Edible Oils–Oil Palm (2021) (the controversial NE/Andaman oil-palm push — ecology critique).
  • Millets: IYM 2023; millet MSP inclusion; Ragi/Jowar/Bajra revival in consumer basket.

10. Food security's legal & administrative spine

  • NFSA 2013 — entitlement to subsidised grains: 75% rural / 50% urban; 5 kg/person/month at ₹3/2/1; Antyodaya 35 kg/family; free grains under NFSA from Jan 2023 (PMGKAY layer merged; announced 5 years from Jan 2024).
  • One Nation One Ration Card (2019–) — migrant-portability milestone.
  • FCI — procurement, buffer-stocks, logistics; legacy issues: carrying-cost, storage losses, and the Shanta Kumar committee's (2015) roadmap for privatised storage & cash transfers pilot.
  • WTO angle: India's public stockholding at MSP — the 2013 Bali "peace clause" (temporarily shields breaches of the de-minimis caps) pending a permanent solution — the Indian G33 stand at MC13/MC14-era meetings.

11. Recent developments (2024–26 checklist)

  • PM Dhan-Dhaanya Krishi Yojana (announced 2025-26 budget) — convergence of 36 schemes in 100 low-productivity districts.
  • Mission for cotton productivity (2025-26) and pulses-atmanirbharta (Tur/Urad/Masoor full-self-sufficiency mission).
  • PM-JANMAN agri-linkages + VB-G RAM G — mentions agriculture's wage-support interlock (125-day guarantee, 60-day agri pause).
  • Agricultural export policy — grain-export management cycles (wheat export ban 2022; rice export curbs 2023 → gradual easing 2024-25, record rice exports resuming; sugar-quota cycles).
  • Ethanol Blending Programme — E10 achieved June 2022; E20 accreditation (target 2025-26) being rolled out; 2G bio-refineries (Panipat).

12. Quick PYQ anchors (Prelims)

  • FCI est. 1965; Buffer norms monthly (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct reference points).

  • NFSA 2013 coverage numbers (75% rural / 50% urban; 5 kg; ₹3/2/1).

14. The MSP Economics Debate: C2, Statute, Diversification, and WTO

Section 3 states the MSP mechanism (CACP → CCEA, 23 crops, 1.5×(A2+FL) since 2018–19, PM-AASHA's PSS–PSF–PDPS); this section argues the economics — because the MSP question is GS-3's most-recurring agriculture 15-marker, and it is graded on the debate's full staging. The cost-formula fight (A2+FL versus C2): CACP's A2+FL (paid-out costs plus imputed family labour — the 1.5× promise kept since 2018–19) against the farmers' C2 demand (comprehensive cost — A2+FL plus rental value of owned land plus interest on owned capital — the Swaminathan-Commission (2006) recommendation: \"50 per cent above C2\") — the gap quantified (C2 exceeding A2+FL by 30–50 per cent for most crops — the fiscally-impossible arithmetic honestly stated: C2-plus-50-per-cent for all 23 crops would multiply the food-subsidy several-fold). The statute fight (legal-guarantee demand — MSP as a floor price with private purchases below it punishable — the 2020–21 agitation's core demand, the 2022 MSP committee's brief, the demand still open per §4): for (income security, bargaining power for the 86-per-cent small-marginal (§1), private-trade discipline); against (the enforcement impossibility — 7,000 mandis plus countless farm-gates policed how?; the fiscal arithmetic — universal procurement at ~₹2-lakh-crore-plus; the market-chilling — private buyers withdrawing where the floor binds above market — the price-floor textbook-effect; the crop-distortion — the floor's crops (rice–wheat) expanding, the unfloored (millets–pulses–oilseeds) shrinking — the diversification-killer).

The diversification–ecology nexus (§2's afterlife deepened): MSP-plus-procurement-plus-free-power prices rice–wheat above their social value in Punjab–Haryana (paddy in a semi-arid zone — 4,000+ litres per kg — the water table's red blocks — §6's CGWB red; stubble's October smoke — the procurement-calendar's pollution; the N:P:K skew — urea-priced nitrogen flooding — §7's nutrient imbalance) — so the MSP debate is the ecology debate: reform proposals staged (price-deficiency-payments — PDPS piloted: pay the gap, don't procure the grain — the market-undistorted transfer; crop-neutral income support — PM-KISAN's ₹6,000 scaled-versus-targeted debate; procurement-diversification — millets–pulses–oilseeds procured seriously — the IYM-2023-to-procurement pipeline; the Gulati-line — §13's balanced-answers source: phase from price-policy to income-policy, invest the savings in R&D–irrigation–markets). The WTO angle (§10 — public stockholding, Bali-2013 peace clause, de-minimis breach-shield, G33 at MC13/MC14): India's defence (food-security box — NFSA's 81-crore coverage as the peace clause's moral base — the reference-price-1986 absurdity: breach computed against 1986–88 prices, inflation manufacturing \"violation\") against the Cairns–US pressure (stockholding-as-trade-distortion alleged) — the permanent-solution pending, the peace clause holding — the answer's international paragraph. The MSP synthesis: formula-fiscally-bound, statute-unenforceable-as-framed, ecology-demanding-diversification, WTO-defensible-on-food-security — the candidate's verdict sequenced (first PDPS-plus-diversified-procurement, then income-policy transition, always ecology-priced).

15. Water, Subsidies, Risk: The Input-Subsidy–Groundwater Nexus Unpacked

Sections 6–8 map irrigation, subsidies and insurance; this section unpacks the nexus connecting them — because agriculture's input economy is one system (power → pumping → paddy → stubble → subsidy), and answers that trace the chain outscore answers that list its links. The energy–groundwater spiral (§6's nexus named): free-or-nominal farm power (Punjab, Tamil Nadu, others — the political equilibrium: no party prices power and survives) → zero-marginal-cost pumping → water tables in the red (CGWB's \"over-exploited\" blocks across Punjab–Haryana–Rajasthan–Tamil-Nadu — India the world's largest groundwater user) → deeper bores, failed wells, the small-farmer squeezed first (86 per cent small-marginal, §1 — the borewell lottery the poor lose) → paddy locked in (MSP-plus-procurement-plus-free-water — §14's nexus) → stubble burned (the combine-plus-calendar compulsion — the window between paddy-harvest and wheat-sowing measured in days — Pusa-decomposer plus ex-situ economics plus the SMG-ban ambiguity — the CAQM–GRAP winter ritual treating the symptom). Reform proposals staged as a ladder (metered-supply-with-DBT-for-power — Punjab's seasonal pilots: pay the farmer, price the electron — the subsidy-targeted-not-abolished; crop-diversification incentives — the paddy-to-maize–pulses–oilseeds price-signal plus procurement-assurance; Atal Bhujal (2020 — 7 States — community aquifer-management: panchayat-level water budgets, the demand-side experiment); PMKSY's Per-Drop-More-Crop (drip–sprinkler ~90-lakh-ha — the efficiency wedge — evaluated: adoption skewed to horticulture-and-large-farmers, the cereal-last-mile pending); Ken–Betwa (foundation December 2024 — the ILR flagship — Bundelkhand's drought-proofing versus Panna-tiger–Ken-ecology costs — the link-debate's Indian prototype).

The subsidy ledger (§7's table argued): fertiliser (~₹1-lakh-crore-plus, gas-price-variable — the urea-pricing pathology: nitrogen-near-free, P-and-K-market-priced → N:P:K skew → soil-fatigue plus nitrous-oxide — the Nano-urea–Nano-DAP push as the efficiency bet, DBT-sales-side since 2018 as the leak-plug — the reform line: nutrient-based-subsidy extended to urea, soil-health-card linked); food (~₹2-lakh-crore — NFSA's 81-crore free-grains-from-January-2023, PMGKAY-merged, 5-years-from-January-2024 — the world's largest entitlement — evaluated by leakage-curves (DBT–Aadhaar–ONORC-plugged) and nutrition-gaps (calories secured, protein–micronutrient lagging — the POSHAN–millet bridge)); power (State-level, the spiral's fuel — above); irrigation (project-linked, cost-recovery-thin — the canal-maintenance deficit — the reform: volumetric-pricing pilots, WUA-empowerment). PM-KISAN (2019 — ₹6,000/year, ~11-crore households post-e-KYC — the income-support precedent: decoupled (crop-neutral — the diversification-friendly transfer), debated (adequacy — ₹500/month against input-costs; targeting — land-record-excluded tenants-and-women; the scale-up demand — the income-policy future glimpsed). Risk instruments (§8 — PMFBY 2016: 2-per-cent-kharif/1.5-per-cent-rabi/5-per-cent-horticulture premiums, satellite-assessment pilots, the legacy pathologies — claim-delays, State opt-outs over premium-share — the 2023-revamp (YES-TECH, WINDS weather-grid — technology-audited claims) as the repair; WBCIS pockets; the 2025 livestock-risk umbrella — the allieds-insured). The nexus synthesis: price the electron, diversify the procurement, target the subsidy, insure the risk technologically — the input economy reformed as one system, because it fails as one system.

16. Marketing, Allieds, Food Security: From Mandi to Plate

Sections 5, 9 and 10 map marketing, allieds and the NFSA spine; this section follows the farm-to-plate chain — because the chain's margins (who eats the price-spread) are the farmer-welfare question, and the examination asks it relentlessly. Marketing (§5's stack evaluated): APMCs (~7,000 mandis — the regulated floor: price-discovery-plus-levies, the reform arc — model-APLMC Acts, private mandis, the Bihar-2006-repeal natural experiment (markets didn't collapse, didn't transform either — the honest verdict), the 2020-laws' rise-and-repeal (§4 — bypass-APMC + contract-farming + EC-deregulation — the November-2020-to-December-2021 agitation, SC-stay-January-2021, Repeal-Act-November-2021 — the political-economy lesson: reform-without-consultation fails even where economics favours it); e-NAM (2016 — 1,360+ mandis — assaying-plus-online-bidding — evaluated: inter-mandi trade thin, assaying-uneven, the logistics-unreformed — the platform ahead of the plumbing); FPOs (10,000-scheme-2020 — collectivised bargaining — the Amul-memory applied to crops: aggregation-plus-processing-plus-branding — evaluated: working-capital-starved, professionally-thin — the handholding-decade needed); AIF (₹1-lakh-crore-2020 — warehouses–cold-chains–sorting at FPO–PACS level — the post-harvest-loss war: 15–20 per cent fruits-vegetables wastage as the prize); Operation Greens (2018 — TOP-to-TOTAL — the horticulture-price-stabilisation bet); PMFME–PMKSY–Mega-Parks (micro-formalisation to parks — the processing-ladder: India's ~10 per cent processing versus 60–80 per cent developed — the value-addition gap as the doubling-income lever).

Allieds (§9's sectors as income-engines): dairy (Operation-Flood-1970–96 — Kurien–NDDB–Anand — the world's largest milk (~230-million-tonnes) — the cooperative proof: smallholder-aggregated, professionally-processed — plus Gokul-Mission breeds, minus lumpy-skin-2023–25 biosecurity lessons); fisheries (PMMSY-2020 — the blue-economy push: inland-aquaculture (Andhra–West-Bengal) booming, marine-stagnant (overfished-nearshore → mariculture-deep-sea — the transition pending), 2nd-largest aquaculture producer); horticulture (production exceeding foodgrains by value — NHM-2006, APEDA-exports, GI-produce — Darjeeling-tea-2004-first-GI, J&K-saffron-revival — the high-value shift: fruits-vegetables-spices-floriculture as the smallholder's friend — per-acre returns multiples of cereals); pulses–oilseeds (NFSM, NMEO–Oil-Palm-2021 — the 55–60-per-cent oil-import dependence as the strategic vulnerability — palm-in-NE–Andamans ecology-critiqued — the atmanirbharta missions: Tur–Urad–Masoor self-sufficiency, §11); millets (IYM-2023 — MSP-included, PDS-piloted, the nutri-cereal rebrand — recipe-to-ration-chain building). Food security's spine (§10 — NFSA-2013: 75-per-cent-rural/50-per-cent-urban, 5-kg-at-₹3/2/1, Antyodaya-35-kg, free-grains-2023-plus, ONORC-portability, FCI-plus-Shanta-Kumar-2015 (privatised-storage, cash-transfer-pilots — the reform-roadmap half-taken), WTO-peace-clause — §14's international paragraph). The chain synthesis: aggregate (FPOs) → assay-and-auction (e-NAM) → store-and-process (AIF–Greens–PMFME) → insure (PMFBY) → entitle (NFSA–ONORC) — the mandi-to-plate chain with the leak plugged at each hop, the farmer's share rising as the chain shortens.

17. The Agriculture Answer Method: Anchors, Second-Revolution Frames, Essay Bridges

Sections 1, 11 and 12 supply anchors, current-checklist and Prelims hooks; this section builds the method — because agriculture questions span Prelims-fact to Mains-argument to essay-theme, and one preparation must serve all three. The anchor pantry (§1's six, refreshed per §11): 16–17-per-cent-GVA-versus-46-per-cent-workforce (the mismatch-opener — every agriculture answer's first line); 1.08-ha-average, 86-per-cent-small-marginal (the scale-constraint — behind FPOs, mechanisation-rental, and insurance-design); 1.45-billion-fed (the security-scale); rice-largest-exporter-plus-oil-importer (the trade-paradox — export-curbs-to-record-resumes-2024–25, §11's cycle literacy: wheat-ban-2022, rice-curbs-2023, easing-2024–25, sugar-quotas — the diplomacy-of-the-granary); kharif–rabi–zaid (the season-frame — every crop placed in season); FCI-buffer-margin (the stock-comfort). Second-Green-Revolution frames (§2's afterlife as answer-skeletons): eastward (Assam–Bihar–eastern-UP–Odisha — water-rich, yield-poor — the BGREI bet); millet-ward (IYM-2023 → MSP → PDS → plate — the nutri-cereal chain); protein-ward (pulses–oilseeds–dairy–fisheries — the protein-and-oil independence); precision-ward (drones–soil-maps–AI-advisory — Kisan-e-Mitra-bridges, §11's Dhan-Dhaanya-100-districts convergence, cotton-productivity and pulses-missions — the technology wedge); natural-ward (organic–natural-farming pilots — BPKP, the Sikkim-proof plus the yield-honesty caveat — the sustainability frame with eyes open).

Essay bridges (farmer-welfare themes — the host's flag): the agitation essay (2020–21 as deliberative-democracy case — repeal-as-lesson: consult-before-reform, federalise-before-centralise); the water essay (paddy-in-Punjab as incentive-ecology — price-the-electron thesis); the women-farmer essay (feminisation-of-agriculture — 30-per-cent-plus workforce, land-titles-missing — the gendered agrarian); the climate-farmer essay (NAPCC-agri-mission, heat-and-monsoon volatility, the adaptation imperative — insurance-plus-varieties-plus-water); the doubling-income essay (Dalwai-Committee arithmetic — productivity-plus-diversification-plus-terms-of-trade — the 2022-target's lessons for target-setting). Prelims banks (§12's seven, drilled): CACP-recommends–Cabinet-approves–1.5×(A2+FL); FCI-1965–buffer-norm-months; Flood–Greens (1970–96–Kurien–NDDB; 2018–TOP–TOTAL); NFSA-numbers (75/50, 5-kg, 3/2/1); PM-KISAN-₹6,000-2019; Ken-Betwa–Atal-Bhujal; PMFBY-2016-rates (2/1.5/5). Source discipline (§13): Survey-agri-chapter (the yearly skeleton), CACP-reports (the price-policy primary), FCI–Agri-Census (the stock-and-scale), NCERT-XI (the spatial base), Gulati (the critique-balance — every answer's honest second voice).
