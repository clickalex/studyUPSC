# Essay Topic Bank — 30 Prompts with Opening Angles

> Grouped practice bank for the Essay paper. For each topic: an angle line you can bend into a thesis. Rotate the 6-move framework (see `essay-frameworks/detailed-notes/essay-writing.md`).

## A. Technology & Society
1. **"Artificial intelligence will not replace humans, but humans who fail to govern AI will be replaced by those who do."** — agency vs governance; compute equity (IndiaAI Mission), skill-transition gaps, algorithmic accountability.
2. **"The smartphone made the world flat and the mind hollow."** — attention economy, education shifts, political polarisation, digital public infrastructure as counterweight (DPI example).
3. **"Data is the new commons."** — DPDP 2023 tensions, platform power, open-standards diplomacy (MOSIP, UPI links).
4. **"Space is no longer the final frontier; it is the next ledger."** — commercial space (Space FDI 2024, IN-SPACe, Skyroot/Agnikul), satellite data commons (NISAR), station ambitions 2035.
5. **"Quantum promises are cheap; responsible computation is expensive."** — NQM ₹6,003 cr, cryptography transitions (post-quantum migration), talent bottleneck.

## B. Gender & Society
6. **"Women's voices will be heard when women are present to speak."** — 106th Amendment rollback calendar (post-delimitation), SHG economies (Lakhpati Didi), care-work accounting.
7. **"The glass ceiling is actually a labyrinth."** — FLFPR ~37–41% with quality caveat; boardroom pipelines; returnship economics.
8. **"Marriage, migration, and the market: the three forces reshaping Indian families."** — NFHS-5 data anchors, joint→nuclear gradient, digital matchmaking marketplaces.
9. **"Safety is the infrastructure of equality."** — POSH compliance math, transport design audits, BNS 2023 offence modernisation.

## C. Environment & Climate
10. **"Growth without ecology is a self-defeating arithmetic."** — GBF 30×30, CCTS carbon markets, adaptation-as-development (HAPs 23+ states).
11. **"The monsoon is India's oldest economy."** — 2025 monsoon metrics (~105–107% LPA band), Mission Mausam, crop-calendar fragility.
12. **"Water disputes are federalism's stress test."** — Cauvery Authority dynamics, Ken-Betwa clearances, aquifer governance deficit.
13. **"Great Nicobar: strategy against the sea, or against the self?"** — ethics of mega-projects, tribal consent (FRA), cumulative impact assessment case.
14. **"The right against climate change"** — M K Ranjitsinh (2024) constitutional recognition, enforceability questions, insurance-fiscal links.

## D. Polity & Constitution
15. **"A constitution keeps its meaning by asking to be read again."** — Kesavananda lineage; 131st Amendment defeat (Apr 2026) as federal bargaining lesson.
16. **"Elections are won at booths, democracies are won in committees."** — committee decline narrative, Electoral Bonds judgment (Feb 2024) funding-transparency open question.
17. **"Federalism lives in the plumbing, not the plumbing bill."** — GST council patterns, FC transitions (16th FC), cesses vs divisible pool tension.
18. **"Reserved seats, represented voices?"** — constituency delimitation timing, women's-reservation rollout design, rotation mechanics.

## E. Economy & Development
19. **"Inclusive growth is not a slogan; it is a spreadsheet."** — DBT savings claims (~₹2.7 lakh cr), NFSA coverage, PLI asymmetries (14 sectors).
20. **"The future of work is a negotiation between code and care."** — Rajasthan Gig Workers Act 2023, Code on Social Security gig categories, platform-labour judiciary trends.
21. **"India's demographic dividend has an expiry date."** — working-age peak ~2040; skills pipeline (NSQF), female participation as multiplier.
22. **"Trade agreements are the new trade routes."** — UAE CEPA, Australia ECTA, EFTA TEPA ($100 bn pledge), UK CETA (Jul 2025), RCEP abstention logic.

## F. Ethics, Culture & Philosophy
23. **"Reality does not conform to the ideal, but confirms it."** — ideals as measuring norms; reform episodes (socio-religious movements, abolition paths).
24. **"The empires of the future will be the empires of the mind."** — soft power ledger (diaspora, DPI exports, G20 presidency narrative, AU induction in G20).
25. **"Tradition is the votes of the dead."** — Chesterton frame; heritage politics (Moidams UNESCO 2024, temple reconstructions), custodial continuity.
26. **"Peace is the only dignified technology of the 21st century."** — Ukraine/Gaza arcs, India as messenger state, mediation credibility.

## G. Rurban & Urban Futures
27. **"A city is not a place; it is a process."** — 74th Amendment fiscal starvation, sponge-city norms, heat-action equity.
28. **"The village is not vanishing; it is migrating."** — Census 2027 stakes, remittance economies, peri-urban informality.

## H. India & The World
29. **"Strategic autonomy is a method, not an ideology."** — multi-alignment evidence (QUAD + BRICS + SCO), connectivity diplomacy (IMEC, Chabahar).
30. **"Multipolarity without multilateralism is organised chaos."** — UNSC reform, UNSC deadlock episodes, WTO dispute-crisis, India's reform pitch.

## How to use this bank
- Pick 1 topic per family weekly; write in 70 minutes; self-score with the rubric in `essay-frameworks/pyqs/essay-frameworks-pyqs.md`.
- Keep a *topic-index card*: thesis + 3 dimensions + 3 evidence anchors + 1 counterpoint for each finished essay.
- Before mocks, refresh the anchor pantry in `essay-frameworks/short-notes/essay-writing-revision.md`.
