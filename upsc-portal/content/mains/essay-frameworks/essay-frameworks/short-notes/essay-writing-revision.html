Essay Writing — Revision Notes

Exam-day sheet: framework, discipline, rapidfills.

6-move framework (tattoo this)

  1. Decode the aphorism → identify central tension.
  2. Thesis sentence → "This essay argues that X is Y provided Z."
  3. 3×3 grid → 3 dimensions × (cause / consequence / corrective).
  4. Evidence anchors → 1–2 per dimension (dated, precise).
  5. Counterpoint → state strongest opposite fairly; resolve.
  6. Conclusion → thesis echo + forward frame (SDG/constitutional/future-facing).

Intro methods (rotate)

  • Anecdote/statistic (place + year).
  • Verified quote (Gandhi talisman; Ambedkar fraternity; Tagore).
  • Definition-turn ("The Oxford dictionary defines X as … but in Indian context…").
  • Contrast ("Between 1996 and 2026 Kerala's literacy slope …").
  • Question (ONE per essay max).

Dimension playlist (choose 4–5, never all)

Social · Political · Economic · Cultural · Ethical · Environmental · Technological · Geopolitical · Psychological/spiritual · Gender · Historical.

Paragraph pattern

Topic sentence → mechanism → evidence w/ counter-line → mini-case (scheme/judgment/institution/region) → linkage sentence.

Anchor pantry (fresh CA)

  • Tech: AI Mission ₹10,371 cr · NQM ₹6,003 cr · NISAR (Jul-25) · SpaDeX (Jan-25) · AI Safety Institute.
  • Polity: 106th Amdt (women 33%) · Electoral Bonds struck Feb-24 · 131st Amendment defeat Apr-26 (one nation one election pause).
  • Economy: GST 2.0 (Sep-25) · MPC 4±2% to 2031 · ~4th economy $4.2 tn · forex $650–700 bn.
  • Environment: M K Ranjitsinh climate right (2024) · GBF 30×30 · tiger count 3,682 · Great Nicobar ethics.
  • Society: NFHS-5 TFR 2.0 · FLFPR ~37–41% · Bihar caste survey 2023 · Census 2027 2-phase.
  • Global: Ukraine 2022→ · Gaza arc · BRI 2013 · Namibia/SA '90-'94 decol echoes.

Language discipline

  • Verify quotes: never fabricate attribution ("as the saying goes…" fallback).
  • Short sentences for argument pivots; variety otherwise.
  • Explain acronyms on first use; no jargon trails.

Pitfalls → fixes

  • Drift → re-read thesis before conclusion.
  • Fact-dump → max 3 numeric anchors/dimension.
  • Political balance → criticise designs not actors.
  • Quote inflation → 2 max; verified only.
  • Endowment load → drop trophy phrases; use structural argument instead.

Time structure (3-hour exam)

  • Selecting & thinking: 15 min
  • Essay 1: 70 min (outline 10 + write 55 + review 5)
  • Essay 2: 70 min
  • Final check: 5–10 min

Since both essays share 3 hrs, keep per-essay ~1,000–1,200 words.

Sample thesis templates

  • "Technology amplifies agency; whether it amplifies justice depends on governance." (for AI/automation prompts)
  • "Cities are engines of growth only if they are livable engines: the 'only-if' is this century's planning question."
  • "Constitutions endure not by the ink but by the institutions that breathe it."
  • "Climate adaptation is development by another name; equity decides which name sticks."
