# Essay Writing — Bullet Points

## ASCII mindmap

```
ESSAY PAPER (250 marks; 2 essays)
├── Examiner rewards: structure · content breadth · language clarity · originality · balance
├── 6-move framework: decode → thesis → 3×3 grid → evidence → counterpoint → forward conclusion
├── Intro methods: anecdote/stat · verified quote · definition turn · contrast pair · question (once)
├── Paragraph: topic → mechanism → evidence vs counter → mini-case → linkage
├── Dimensions playlist (choose 4–5): social·political·economic·cultural·ethical·environmental·technological·IR·psychological·gender·historical
├── Anchor pantry: PLI/GST2.0/DBT · 106th Amdt/bonds/quasicuster · MK Ranjitsinh/GBF/Nicobar · NISAR/SpaDeX/AI Mission · NFHS-5/Bihar survey/Census 2027 · Gaza/Ukraine/BRI
├── Pitfalls: drift · fact-dump · fabricated quotes · political bias · ornamental rhetoric
└── Time: 15 min plan · 70 min ×2 · final 5–10 min check
```

## Topic families → ready frames
- Tech/society → "agency vs governance"
- Gender → "norm → law → economy → care-work → voice"
- Climate → "equity stack (CBDR) → adaptation → tech+finance → life-ways"
- Constitution → "ideals → institutions → practice → future"
- IR → "order contest → strategic autonomy → responsibility chain"
- Education → "capability vs credentialism; NEP moment"
- Urbanisation → "migration → informal work → heat → planning reform"
- Culture → "continuity-with-change"; vernacular modernity

## Quotes w/ safe attribution
- Gandhi: "There is no path to peace; peace is the path." / talisman framing (poorest-face).
- Ambedkar: fraternity triad — "religion of liberty, equality, fraternity."
- Tagore: "Where the mind is without fear…"
- JFK-adjacent ("ask not…") — avoid; Indian anchors preferred.
- Kautilya-statecraft quips — paraphrase, don't quote.

## Golden sentence trains
- "X is not the problem; the *indifference* X enables is."
- "The question is never whether X — it is always *for whom* and *at what cost*."
- "Institutions decay twice: once in law, once in habit — reform addresses both."
- "The alternative to bad governance is not no governance; it is redesigned governance."

## Discipline bullets
- Max 2 quotes per essay.
- Max 3 numeric anchors per dimension.
- Every dimension ends with a handoff sentence.
- Counterpoint paragraph always present (10–15% of length).
- Conclusion = thesis + forward-looking frame + one institutional suggestion.
