One-liner frames for Mains

  • WWI "spark vs structures": Sarajevo was the trigger; the alliance blocs + imperial competition were the fuel.
  • WWII = "Versailles' unfinished business" + fascist ideological war (not just German territorial goals).
  • Decolonisation diversity: "Asia by statute (mostly), Africa by insurrection (often)" — with India shifting model.
  • Industrial Revolution's India link: raw cotton in, finished Manchester out (terms-of-trade reversal).
  • Cold War end: not a single cause — military overreach + economic stagnation + legitimacy deficit + Eastern European agency.
