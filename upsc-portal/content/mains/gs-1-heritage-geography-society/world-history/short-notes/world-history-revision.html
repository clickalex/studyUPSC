World History — Revision Notes

Fast recall sheet for Mains answers — cause-effect chains, not dates.

Industrial Revolution chain

Agricultural revolution + empire revenue + property rights + coal/iron + banking → textile tech (Jenny 1764 → mule 1779 → Watt 1769) → factory system → urban labour class → imperial realignment (deindustrialising colonies) → second wave (steel/chemicals/electricity, 1870–1914) → Fordism/Taylorism → mass consumption states.

Ideology quick-table

  • Liberalism: rights, limited govt (Locke, Mill) — revolutions of 1688/1776/1789.
  • Socialism: exploitation critique (Owen, Fourier) → welfare states.
  • Marxism-Leninism: 1848 Manifesto → 1917 Bolshevik → planned economies.
  • Fascism/Nazism: Mussolini (1922 march on Rome), Hitler (1933 enabling act).
  • Keynes: 1936 General Theory → post-war welfare settlements (Beveridge 1942).

Colonisation → decolonisation

Berlin 1884–85 scramble → WWII exhaustion → Asian wave (India 1947; Dien Bien Phu 1954) → Bandung 1955 → "Year of Africa" 1960 → Algeria 1962 (war) → Portuguese collapse 1974–75 → Zimbabwe 1980 → Namibia 1990 → South Africa 1994. NAM: Belgrade 1961 (Nehru, Tito, Nasser, Sukarno, Nkrumah).

WWI skeleton (MANIA)

Militarism, Alliances, Nationalism, Imperialism, Assassination (Sarajevo 28 June 1914). Verdun/Somme 1916 → US entry 1917 → Brest-Litovsk 1918 → Armistice 11 Nov 1918 → Versailles 1919 (Art. 231 war guilt) → League.

WWII skeleton

Appeasement (Munich 1938) → invasion of Poland (1 Sep 1939) → Barbarossa (June 1941) → Pearl Harbor (7 Dec 1941) → turning: Stalingrad 1942–43, Midway 1942, El Alamein 1942 → D-Day 6 June 1944 → V-E 8 May 1945; Hiroshima 6 Aug / Nagasaki 9 Aug 1945 → V-J 2 Sep 1945. Outcome: UN 1945, Bretton Woods 1944, decolonisation acceleration, Cold War bipolarity.

Cold War arc

Truman Doctrine & Marshall Plan 1947 → NATO 1949 → Korea 1950–53 → Warsaw Pact 1955 → Cuban Missile Crisis 1962 → détente/SALT → Afghanistan 1979 → glasnost & perestroika 1985 → Berlin Wall 9 Nov 1989 → USSR dissolution 25 Dec 1991.

21st century echoes

US hegemony → 9/11 (2001) → China rise; BRI 2013; Crimea 2014; Ukraine war 2022→; Gaza 2023–; Global South & de-dollarisation; India's strategic autonomy: QUAD + BRICS + G20 (2023, AU membership).

Answer frames

  • "Effects on society" answers: structure → classes → state capacity → ideology → geopolitics.
  • Colonisation comparisons: settler vs extractive logics; direct vs indirect rule (French assimilation debate).
  • Revolution comparisons: French (bourgeois-republican), Russian (party-seizure), Chinese (protracted rural).
