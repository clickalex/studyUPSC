├── Structure: unity-in-diversity + hierarchy/difference (Dumont/Betéille)
├── Caste
│   ├── Varna vs jati; Art17 → PCR 1955 → SC/ST PoA 1989 (+2015/2018)
│   ├── Quotas: SC15 · ST7.5 · OBC27 · EWS10 (103rd) · Women 33% (106th, 2023, post-delimitation)
│   ├── Indra Sawhney 50% ceiling → Maratha 2021 reaffirm
│   └── Caste census: Bihar 2023 → Census 2027 debate
├── Gender
│   ├── NFHS-5: TFR 2.0; sex ratio ~1020 (methods caveat); FLFPR ~37–41%
│   ├── Law: POCSO 2012 · POSH 2013 · DV 2005 · BNS 2023
│   └── SHGIF economy: ~9 crore women, Lakhpati Didi targets
├── Population & urbanisation
│   ├── Demographic dividend window to ~2040; median age ~28; elderly 2050 gap
│   ├── Urban share 15→31→35%+; 6 cities >10 mn; 74th Amdt fiscal weakness
│   └── Programmes: Smart Cities · AMRUT 2.0 · PMAY-U 2.0 · HAPs 23+ states

│   ├── Arc:Aligarh → 1909 separate electorates → Partition → digital hate economy

Quote bank (enrich answers)

  • "Unity in diversity" — Nehru, Discovery of India
  • "India lives in her villages" — Gandhi (use with nuance; cf. Amartya Sen counter)
  • "Caste is a state of mind" — Ambedkar, Annihilation of Caste (1936)
  • "The India that lives in cities cannot understand India of villages" — folklore of field sociology (attribute cautiously)
  • Dumont: homo hierarchicus concept tag for structured inequality

Sticky facts (write under answers)

  • 106th Amdt (2023) = women's reservation; actual rolls follow delimitation.
  • Bihar caste survey 2023: OBC 63% — lever for nationwide demand.
  • Census 2027 two-phase digital-first — first since 2011 (COVID delay).
  • POCSO gender-neutral protection; POSH internal committees mandatory (10+ employees).
  • Uttarakhand UCC 2024: first post-Independence state-level common code (Goa legacy excepted).

Answer-skeleton shortcuts

  • Caste question → structure (jati system) → change (reservation, market, migration) → politics ( Mandal 2.0, caste census) → conclusion: "institution adapting, not vanishing."
  • Urban question → migration → informal work · housing deficit · climate risk → 74th Amdt fix → conclusion: "livable-density governance."
  • Globalisation question → economic (remittance/IT) → cultural (food/marriage) → inequality (English premium/gig precarity) → conclusion: "hybridisation over homogenisation."
