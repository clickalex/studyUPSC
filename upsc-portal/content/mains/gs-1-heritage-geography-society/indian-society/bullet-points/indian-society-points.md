- "The India that lives in cities cannot understand India of villages" — folklore of field sociology (attribute cautiously)

## Answer-skeleton shortcuts
- Caste question → structure (jati system) → change (reservation, market, migration) → politics ( Mandal 2.0, caste census) → conclusion: "institution adapting, not vanishing."
- Urban question → migration → informal work · housing deficit · climate risk → 74th Amdt fix → conclusion: "livable-density governance."
- Globalisation question → economic (remittance/IT) → cultural (food/marriage) → inequality (English premium/gig precarity) → conclusion: "hybridisation over homogenisation."
