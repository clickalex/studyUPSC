Indian Society — Revision Notes

High-yield numbers + framing moves for GS-1 society answers.

One-liner anchors

  • Diversity framed by unity in diversity with hierarchy/difference twin reading (Dumont vs Betéille).
  • Untouchability abolished: Art.17; poles of enforcement: PCR 1955 → SC/ST PoA 1989 (+2015/2018 amendments).
  • Quota architecture: SC 15 · ST 7.5 · OBC 27 · EWS 10 (103rd Amdt 2019); women's reservation 33% (106th Amdt 2023, post-delimitation).
  • The 50% tableau: Indra Sawhney v UoI (1992) ceiling; Maratha judgment (2021) re-affirmed ceiling, states barred from breaching post-102nd Amdt.

Population numbers (NFHS-5 unless noted)

  • TFR 2.0 (below replacement 2.1): Bihar 3.4 highest; Kerala TN ~1.8.
  • Sex ratio: 943 (Census 2011); NFHS-5 ~1020 (methods caveat).
  • Median age ~28; working-age bulge peak ~2040.
  • Migration: ~450 mn internal migrators (2011 base); 2020 lockdown exodus.

Urban anchors

  • Urban share ~31% → mid-2020s ~35%+; cities >10 mn = Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad.
  • 74th Amdt municipalities — weak fiscally (own revenue ~1% GDP share, Tddf line).
  • Programmes: Smart Cities, AMRUT 2.0, PMAY-U 2.0 (2024), Day Zero Chennai 2019 (water-stress symbol), HAPs 23+ states.

Gender scorecard

  • Female LFPR ~37–41% (2020s) — improvement but quality questions (unpaid family work capture).
  • Legal stack: POCSO 2012 · POSH 2013 · DV 2005 · Dowry 1961 · BNS 2023 re-writes.
  • SHGs: ~9 crore networked women (NRLM "Lakhpati Didi" push).

Communalism toolkit

  • Historic arc: Aligarh-Movement + 1909 separate electorates → Partition episodes → post-92 environment + digital hate-speech economy.
  • Guards: Art. 25–28 floor, UCC debate (Art.44; Uttarakhand UCC 2024 pilot), NHRC/NCM, hate-speech Jurisprudence (Amish Devgan, suo motu warnings 2023–25).

Regionalism table

  • Linguistic states: Fazl Ali SRC 1953–56 (AP 1953; states reorganised 1956; later 2000 Grit trio + Telangana 2014).
  • North-East peace accords: Bodo 2020, Bru-Reang 2020, Naga framework pending; Manipur 2023 ethnic conflict.
  • Water federalism: Cauvery Authority 2018; Mahanadi tribunal 2018+.

Globalisation impacts (use as quotes-free anchors)

  • Remittances ~$100+ bn/yr (India #1 globally).
  • Platform/gig law experiments: Rajasthan Gig Workers Act 2023; Code on Social Security recognises gig/platform workers.
  • Glocalisation examples: McAloo, Domino's Paneer variants; whitewashed OTT multiplex culture.

Framing devices for answers

  • "Continuity-with-change" (Yogendra Singh's modernisation) as the default conclusion.
  • Structure → agency → policy three-beat pattern.
  • Data-facing openings: NFHS-5 for demography; NCRB/PLFS for gender/employment.
