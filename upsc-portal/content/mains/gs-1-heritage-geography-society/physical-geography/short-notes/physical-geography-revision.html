Physical Geography — Short Revision Notes

One-liner

From the crust core-ward (crust → mantle → outer core → inner core) to the atmosphere skyward (troposphere → exosphere), physical geography explains landforms through plate tectonics, rainfall through the monsoon machine (ITCZ + jets + ENSO/IOD), and ocean behaviour through currents, salinity and tides.

Earth interior (memorise order & discontinuities)

Crust (SIAL over SIMA) → Moho → Mantle (asthenosphere ~100–250 km) → Gutenberg → liquid outer core (S-waves stop here — the proof) → Lehmann → solid inner core.

Hotspot gift: Deccan Traps (~66 Ma, Réunion hotspot) — the world's largest basalt province in India.

Monsoon mechanism (5-step answer frame)

  1. ITCZ shifts north over the Indo-Gangetic plains (low pressure).
  2. Differential land–sea heating drives the cross-equatorial SE trades curving into SW monsoon winds (Coriolis).
  3. Subtropical westerly jet retreats north of the Himalayas; the Tropical Easterly Jet sets in aloft.
  4. Tibetan Plateau heat plateau reinforces the low.
  5. ENSO/IOD/MJO modulate the season (El Niño typically weakens; positive IOD can compensate; MJO creates active/break cycles).

Pattern facts to never miss

  • Onset over Kerala ~1 June; ~75% of annual rain June–Sept.
  • Mawsynram (Meghalaya) — world's highest rainfall; Cherrapunji historic second.
  • Western Ghats' windward (wet) vs Deccan rain-shadow; retreating monsoon = NE monsoon → Tamil Nadu coast (Oct–Dec).
  • Western disturbances — winter rain, NW India; vital for rabi wheat.
  • Seismic Zone V = most at risk (Kashmir, NE, Kutch).

Oceanography one-liners

  • Deepest trench — Mariana (~10,994 m).
  • Highest salinity — Red Sea (~40 ‰); lowest — Baltic Sea.
  • Cold currents on western continental coasts → coastal deserts (Atacama–Peru current; Namib–Benguela).
  • Warm + cold current meeting = fisheries (Grand Banks, Japan coast).
  • Tidal range hotspots in India: Gulf of Khambhat & Gulf of Kutch; Sundarbans tidal mangroves.
  • Tsunami: 2004 Indian Ocean (Sumatra); warning centre INCOIS, Hyderabad.

Biogeography quick palette

  • Soils: alluvial (Indo-Gangetic), black/regur (Deccan, cotton), red/laterite, desert, mountain, saline (usar).
  • Vegetation: tropical rainforests (Western Ghats, A&N), moist deciduous (teak/sal), dry deciduous, thorn (NW), Himalayan montane, Sundarbans mangrove.
  • 4 hotspots: Western Ghats, Himalayas, Indo-Burma, Sundaland.

Recent tools (show current awareness)

  • IMD Mission Mausam (approved 2024) — better forecasting & weather-modification research (cloud chambers at IITM Pune).
  • Monsoons 2024 & 2025 — above-normal back-to-back (~105–108% of LPA); early 2025 onset (~24 May).
  • Deep Ocean Mission (2021) — Central Indian Ocean polymetallic-nodule research.
