Gandhian Era 1919–1947 — Short Revision Notes

One-liner

From Nagpur 1920 to the midnight of 15 August 1947, Gandhi turned Congress into a mass movement through three great campaigns — Non-Cooperation (1920–22), Civil Disobedience (1930–34), Quit India (1942) — which broke the legitimacy of British rule.

Milestones between movements

  • 1923: Swaraj Party (Das & Motilal Nehru) — council entry.
  • 1927: Simon Commission — "Simon Go Back"; 1928: Bardoli (Patel → "Sardar"); Nehru Report.
  • 1929: Lahore session — Purna Swaraj (Nehru, youngest president); 26 January 1930 first Independence Day.
  • 1931: Karachi session — first Fundamental Rights resolution; Bhagat Singh, Rajguru, Sukhdev hanged (23 March).
  • 1935: Government of India Act — federation (never worked), provincial autonomy, three lists, Federal Court/RBI, Sindh & Orissa separated.
  • 1937: Congress wins 8 of 11 provinces; ministries in 6; resign 1939.
  • 1942: Cripps Mission fails; Quit India.
  • 1946: Cabinet Mission Plan; Direct Action Day (16 Aug); Constituent Assembly meets (9 Dec); Objective Resolution (13 Dec — Nehru).
  • 1947: Mountbatten Plan (3 June); Indian Independence Act (18 July); Partition & Independence (15 August).
  • 1948 (30 Jan): Gandhi assassinated.

Match: leaders to moments (prelims traps)

  • Dandi March (12 Mar 1930) — Gandhi + 78 volunteers; broke salt law 6 April.
  • Dharasana raid (21 May 1930) — Sarojini Naidu; journalist Webb Miller.
  • Peshawar 1930 — Khan Abdul Ghaffar Khan ("Frontier Gandhi") — Khudai Khidmatgars ("Red Shirts").
  • Chittagong Armoury Raid (18 Apr 1930) — Surya Sen.
  • Bardoli (1928) — Vallabhbhai Patel receives the title "Sardar".
  • Kakori (1925) — Ram Prasad Bismil, Ashfaqullah Khan (HRA → HSRA).
  • Parallel governments in Quit India — Ballia (UP), Tamluk (Bengal), Satara (Maharashtra).
  • INA / Azad Hind Govt (21 Oct 1943, Singapore) — Subhas Chandra Bose; earlier INA prototype by Mohan Singh.

One-line answers to famous quotations

  • "Do or Die" — Gandhi (8 August 1942).
  • "Post-dated cheque on a crashing bank" — Gandhi on the Cripps offer.
  • "Swaraj is my birthright" — Tilak (still the motto of the pre-Gandhian era's extremist wing).

PYQ favourites

  1. Nagpur session (1920) — what changed in the Congress constitution?
  2. Why was Non-Cooperation withdrawn? (Chauri Chaura + Khilafat fading + organisational strain.)
  3. Dandi/Salt Satyagraha — phases, regional variations (Dharasana, Peshawar, Chittagong).
  4. Poona Pact (24 Sep 1932) — reserved seats instead of separate electorates for Depressed Classes.
  5. Government of India Act 1935 — federation never came into force; provincial autonomy worked 1937–39.
  6. Quit India — "leaderless revolt," parallel governments, repression, erosion of British legitimacy.
