## Bullet facts
- **10 May 1857** — revolt begins at **Meerut**; sepoys march to Delhi
- **12 May 1857** — Delhi captured; **Bahadur Shah Zafar** proclaimed Emperor
- **29 March 1857** — **Mangal Pandey** at Barrackpore (34th Native Infantry)
- **1856** — Awadh annexed; **Religious Disabilities Act**
- **Doctrine of Lapse** — Satara, Jhansi, Nagpur, Sambalpur, Jaitpur, Udaipur
- **Enfield cartridge** — greased with cow/pig fat (trigger)
- **Kunwar Singh** — the 80-year-old zamindar of Jagdishpur (Arrah)
- **Begum Hazrat Mahal** — led Awadh in the name of son Birjis Qadr
- **Tantia Tope** — Nana Sahib's general; guerrilla fighter; hanged 1859
- **Rani Lakshmibai** — died fighting at **Kotah-ki-Serai**, Gwalior (June 1858)
- Suppressors: **Campbell, Havelock, Outram, Hugh Rose, John Nicholson**
- **1 November 1858** — Queen's Proclamation read by **Lord Canning** at Allahabad
