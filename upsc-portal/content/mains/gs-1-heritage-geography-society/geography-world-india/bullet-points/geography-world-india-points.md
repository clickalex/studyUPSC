## Bullet facts (prelims gold)

### India's physiography
- **Himadri** (Greater Himalaya, Everest 8,848 m) · **Himachal** (Lesser — hill stations) · **Shiwaliks** (youngest — *duns*: Dehradun, Kathmandu).
- **Bhabar** (stones, streams sink) → **Terai** (marshy, springs re-emerge) → **alluvial plain (khadar floodplain vs bhangar upland)**.
- **Western Ghats (~1,600 km, continuous escarpment)** vs **Eastern Ghats (discontinuous)**; highest — **Anamudi (2,695 m, Kerala)**; highest eastern — **Mahendragiri (Odisha)**.
- **Aravalli** — India's oldest fold mountains; **Guru Shikhar (1,722 m)**.
- **Narmada — rift valley between Vindhya & Satpura**; Sardar Sarovar on it.
- **Deccan slopes east** → peninsular rivers pour into the Bay of Bengal.
- **Barren Island (A&N)** — India's only active volcano; **Indira Point** (Great Nicobar) — India's southernmost (subsided after the 2004 tsunami).

### Rivers
- **Indus**: rises near Lake Mansarovar; **Panchnad** tributaries Jhelum, Chenab, Ravi, Beas, Sutlej; drains to the Arabian Sea via Pakistan.
- **Ganga system**: **Gangotri (Bhagirathi)** + Alaknanda = Devprayag; Yamuna is the largest tributary; **Sundarbans = world's largest delta** (with Brahmaputra's Padma/Meghna arms).
- **Brahmaputra**: **U-turn at Namcha Barwa**; braided; largest by discharge; **Majuli** among the largest river islands.
- **East-flowing deltas**: Mahanadi, **Godavari (longest peninsular, "Dakshin Ganga")**, Krishna, **Kaveri** (tribunal → CWMA 2018).
- **West-flowing estuaries: Narmada & Tapti** (rift valleys).

### Resources (India match-ups)
- **Coal** — Jharia (Jharkhand coking) + Raniganj (WB) + Talcher (Odisha) + Korba (CG).
- **Iron ore** — Odisha (Keonjhar), **Bailadila (CG)**, Singhbhum (Jh), Ballari (Kar).
- **Bauxite** — Odisha (largest). **Mica** — Koderma (Jh).
- **Oil** — **Mumbai High offshore**, **Barmer-Mangala (Raj)**, Assam (Digboi — first refinery).
- **Gas** — **Krishna-Godavari (KG) basin**, Tripura.
- **Uranium** — **Jaduguda (Jh)**, Tummalapalle (AP).
- **Thorium** — monazite sands, Kerala coast.
- **Steel** — Chotanagpur belt cities: Jamshedpur, Bokaro, Rourkela, Bhilai, Durgapur, Burnpur.

### World resources
- Oil reserves concentrated in the **Persian Gulf arc** (Saudi, Iraq, Kuwait, UAE, Iran); Venezuela's heavy oil largest single reserve.
- **Iron ore top exporters: Australia (Pilbara), Brazil (Carajás).**
- **Copper — Chile (Escondida, largest mine); lithium triangle — Chile/Argentina/Bolivia; cobalt — DR Congo; rare-earth processing — China.**
- Fisheries — Grand Banks (NW Atlantic), North Sea, Japan coast (Kuroshio+Oyashio), **Humboldt anchovy (Peru)**.

### Population & settlements
- **India overtook China as the most populous country (April 2023, UN).**
- **2011 Census: 53 cities >1 million; 7,935 towns; urbanisation ~35% (2023 est.).**
- **Mumbai–Ahmedabad bullet-train** under construction; **Vande Bharat** expansion.

### Transport & chokepoints
- **Golden Quadrilateral** + N-S/E-W corridors; **Bharatmala (2017)**; **Sagarmala (2015)**; **Gati Shakti (2021)**.
- Ports: 12 majors (Deendayal-Kandla, JNPT, Mumbai, Chennai, Kamarajar-Ennore, VOC-Tuticorin, Kochi, Vizag, Paradip...) — non-major ports already carry the majority of cargo.
- World chokepoints: **Malacca · Hormuz · Bab-el-Mandeb · Suez · Panama · Bosporus**; the **Red Sea re-routings (from late 2023)** showed chokepoint risk to India-Europe trade.
- **Atal Tunnel (2020)** — Rohtang; **Nathu La / Zoji La / Shipki La / Lipu Lekh** = Himalayan passes on the map list.

### Current-development hooks
- **Ken-Betwa river-link** foundation (Dec 2024).
- **India's G20-2023 IMEC corridor** idea links Indian ports to West Asia–Europe.
- **Mission Mausam (2024)** for weather research — connects monsoon geography to disaster governance.
