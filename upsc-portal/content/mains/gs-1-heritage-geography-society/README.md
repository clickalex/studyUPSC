# General Studies I — Heritage, Geography & Society

Six topic folders (Modern History carries six sub-chapters); every topic has the
standard 5-section set (`detailed-notes/` · `short-notes/` · `bullet-points/` ·
`diagrams/` · `pyqs/`) — 46 Markdown chapters and 11 SVG diagrams/maps in all:

- `modern-history/` — sub-chapters: `east-india-company`, `revolt-1857`, `socio-religious-reforms`,
  `national-movement-1885-1919`, `gandhian-era-1919-1947`, `constitutional-development`
- `indian-heritage-culture/` — architecture, sculpture & painting, music/dance/theatre, literature, festivals & fairs, UNESCO lists
- `world-history/` — industrial revolution, ideologies, world wars, redrawal of boundaries, decolonisation, cold war
- `indian-society/` — diversity, caste, women, population, poverty, urbanisation, communalism, regionalism, globalisation
- `physical-geography/` — geomorphology, climatology, oceanography, biogeography
- `geography-world-india/` — physiography, drainage, climate & soils, agriculture, resources, industry, world patterns, mapping

Practice questions with answer frameworks for this paper live in
`content/mains/practice/gs-1-practice/pyqs/gs-1-question-bank.md`.

After adding/editing files: `python3 cli/md2html.py && node cli/generate.mjs` from `upsc-portal/`.
