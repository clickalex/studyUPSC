7 · How the site stays HTML-only

  • upsc-portal/index.html — the SPA shell (dashboard, syllabus tree, search, tracker). Documents open inside it as real pages.
  • content/**/…html — 316 self-contained, print-ready document pages (also openable standalone).
  • content/index.html — printable catalog of every document (rebuilt by the generator).
